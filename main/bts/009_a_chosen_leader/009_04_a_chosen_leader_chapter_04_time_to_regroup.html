RSI Story #9A Chosen Leader

-4-Time To Regroup

As predicted, Britney and Leesh were not happy about having their teams broken up. Not happy at all.

"We're winning!" Britney said. "Why would you screw that up?"

"I'm not..."

"My team is finally coming together," Elisha said.

"Guys, this isn't..."

"I think this is a mistake," Britney said.

"If you would just..."

"Maybe your biggest one yet," Elisha added.

"HEY!" I yelled. I slammed my fist down so hard, it actually made the assorted junk on my desk jump. "I know you guys sometimes forget, but I'm still in charge around here! Now, SIT DOWN and SHUT UP!"

Both women froze and stared at me, unsure of what to do next. That made three of us, since I had no clue, myself. I hadn't intended to yell like that. Though it made them stop yapping, it had also ratcheted up the tension in the room. The goal of this meeting was to get Britney and Elisha on board with the idea of changing up the teams. What I'd just done hadn't helped.

"Do I need to come over there and separate you three?" Danni called from the neighboring office.

I closed my eyes and rubbed my face. "Shut up, Danni," I called back. "I'm trying to assert my authority over here."

"Oh wow," she said. "That should be exciting. Can I come over and watch?"

What choice did I have but to smile? I opened my eyes and saw that Britney and Elisha were smiling, too. I gestured them toward the open chairs as I said, "Sorry. This is a private performance."

"Damn," Danni said.

She actually sounded disappointed.

Britney and Elisha sat down, and I tried a less forceful approach.

"We're going to start this third team. I know it's going to stir things up a little, but I think it's the right move. We have a lot of talent on the roster that we're not putting out there."

They both went to speak, but I put my hands up and gently motioned them to hold on.

"I know," I said. "You hadn't expected this to have a big impact on the existing teams. Neither did I. But, when you think about it, it makes sense to take a look at everything. I want to put together the best teams we can, across the board."

The didn't look happy, but they were quiet. I took that as a win.

"Britney," I said, "Aly is going to be the new team leader. She's the one for the job. I think we can agree on that."

Britney nodded grudgingly. Aly was currently the light heavyweight competitor for Britney's team, and the move meant the less experienced Krystal Forscutt would be her replacement. Krystal was developing nicely, though, and I was confident Britney could live with the change.

"So now, we need to look at what we can do to give her a strong team, without leaving you guys hung out to dry. I don't intend to tear the teams apart. We just need to look and see where changes make sense."

The two team leaders looked at each other and shrugged in silent agreement. Then, they looked back at me.

"How do we do this?" Elisha asked.

"Get with Aly," I said. "Look over the roster and see what you come up with. Then, bring it to me."

They looked at each other again, then started to get up.

"Still not happy," Britney chirped.

"You don't have to be happy," I replied. "Just have to be willing."

Two days later, they were back in my office and Aly was with them.

"How'd it go?" I asked.

"Turns out," Britney said as she gestured toward Aly, "the new girl's not half bad at this."

"Yeah," Elisha said, "and she feels the same way about you as we do."

I dared not ask for an explanation on that, so I simply said, "Glad to hear you're all getting along."

Britney and Elisha sat down in my guest chairs. I looked to Aly, who was carrying a small bundle of papers with her.

"Whatchya got there?"

She handed the bundle to me and said, "These are the rosters we came up with." Then, she turned and half-sat on the arm of the chair Britney was in.

I'm gonna need more chairs, I thought to myself.

The bundle was comprised of three sheets of paper... a proposed roster for each team.

On my desk, I have a blue rubber ball. I keep it there because I have a weird habit of holding it in my hand and rolling it between my fingers when I'm thinking. As I looked over the rosters, I unconsciously picked it up and did just that.

As it turned out, Britney and Elisha had little to fear. The original rosters were almost completely intact. Aside from the Krystal substitution, there was only one other change.

"Why switch Danica and Jules?" I asked.

Britney fielded the question. "We were just trying to balance out the talent. I think Danica is ready to turn the corner, but Jules is already highly ranked. Leesh's team needed a boost, so this seemed to make sense."

I nodded, and perused the lists again.

"Not a lot of experience on Aly's team," I said. Almost none, actually.

"That's my fault," Aly said. "I kind of like the idea of bringing up my own team from scratch. Besides, Emma and Ashley bring a lot to the team."

I looked at her, and nodded approvingly. Spoken like a real leader. But I still had a monkey wrench to throw in the works.

"I can't let you have Jen Stano," I said.

Predictably, a minor uproar ensued.

"WHAT?" Aly asked incredulously.

"You can't be serious," Elisha said.

"I am," I said.

Britney tried the reasoned approach. "Richelle, she's the hottest younger fighter we have. You can't leave her on the bench."

In a stable with thirty-two fighters, a little less than half were rookies or slightly better. Of those, Jennifer Stano has been rising the fastest by a wide margin. I had no doubt Aly was counting on her to help form the backbone of the new team.

It was a hard call to keep Stano out of team competition. But, I had my reasons.

"Jen's been fighting out of her mind, and she's near the top of the division. But, she's still young," I said.

They started to protest again, but I cut them off.

"I know," I said, "that I've doubted her in the past... expecting that her bubble would burst at some point. I'm past that, I think. But, if she keeps this up, she's going to find herself wedged between Laura Vandervoort and Doutzen Kroes."

"She's already beaten Doutzen twice," Elisha said.

"You don't think Doutzen's adjusted her game?" I asked. "I'd be shocked if Doutzen isn't holding that belt again after this next event. And she'd love nothing more than for Jen to try and take it from her, just so she could stop her and prove that everything that came before was a fluke."

I looked back to Aly. "I need Stano focused on what's in front of her. Besides, J-Law can give you what you need."

Britney and Elisha continued to argue with me, as Aly quietly studied the rosters, again.

"This changes everything," Elisha said.

"Stano may be young," Britney said, "but she would have brought a lot to Aly's team. This really means we need to start over."

I started to respond, but Aly spoke first.

"Maybe not," she said.

"What do you mean?" Elisha asked.

"I think we can fix it with one move."

Britney and Elisha looked doubtful, and I motioned Aly to continue. She set the three rosters side-by-side on the desk. Then, she pointed to a name on Britney's page.

Predictably, Britney wasn't thrilled. "Oh sure," she said. "First I lose Aly. Then Jules. Now, this!"

She had a point, and I looked to Elisha.

"I actually think we need to move the new fighter to your team," I said.

She started to protest, but I kept going. "You have Julianne, now. And, you've already had success with Cobie as an injury substitute."

Elisha opened her mouth, then thought better of it. With a nod, she conceded my point. Britney nodded as well, and it appeared all three were satisfied.

But, there was still one person who needed to weigh in.

I took the rubber ball I was holding and threw it at the wall between my office and Danni's. The thud it made on impact was impossible to ignore. I caught it on the rebound and resumed rolling. A moment later, Danni appeared in my doorway.

"Did you just..." She paused, unsure of how to describe what I'd done. After a second or two, she gave up and said, "What the fuck was THAT?"

"You seemed to have an issue the last time I called out for you," I said.

"So, this is BETTER?"

"Just giving you options," I replied.

She smiled, likely already plotting her revenge.

"What do you want?" she said with some false attitude.

I gestured toward the trio already assembled in my office. "The ladies have come up with a plan."

She stepped forward and looked at the pages on the desk. They still showed the original plan the women had come up with, but I figured it would be best to bring Danni through it the same way the rest of us had done it.

After a moment, she nodded.

"This looks good," she said.

"I've pulled Stano back," I said.

Unlike the others, she didn't argue. "You want her focused on Doutzen and Laura."

I nodded.

"Makes sense," she said.

She looked at the rosters some more.

"Leaves Aly's team a little weak, though."

"We've already discussed that. Another move has been proposed," I said.

"Oh?"

I leaned forward and set my finger down next to the welterweight slot on Britney's roster.

The name in the slot read Danneel Harris.

"Oh, shit," Danni said.

I smiled and waited.

She looked again at Aly's roster. I couldn't tell how much of her expression was for show and how much was real. But, she didn't look thrilled.

"These are almost all kids," she groaned.

Aly stood up and squared herself in front of Danni. Danni pulled herself upright, and their eyes met.

"Come and help me make them into a team," Aly said.

It sounded less like a plea, and more like a challenge. Clearly, Aly knew Danni better than I'd realized.

Danni seemed to know right away she was done for. She made a few faces, but finally agreed. Sort of.

"This is gonna suck," she said.

Aly threw her arms around her stable leader and new teammate, and Danni responded in kind.

"Okay," I said after a few moments, "everybody out of my office."

Britney and Elisha rose, and all four started to file out through the door. Danni was last in line, and I simply couldn't resist. I took the rubber ball that was still in my hand and threw it at her, hitting her square in the ass. It made almost no sound, so the other three didn't notice what I'd done. Danni stood stock-still but didn't turn around.

When Aly looked back, Danni said, "I'll catch up."

After the others were gone, she slowly rolled her head around to me.

"How ya' doin'?" I asked with a big smile on my face.

"Better than you're gonna be in a minute."

I think the statement was supposed to sound threatening, but her smile said otherwise.

"What are you thinking?" I asked.

I didn't have to offer any further explanation.

She picked the ball up as she meandered back toward my desk.

"I think Aly's got a lot of hard work ahead of her. And, I think she's the perfect person to do it."

"And now she's got you to help," I said.

Danni gave a half-nod, half-shrug but said nothing.

"You two seem pretty close," I said.

"We've known each other a long time," she replied.

I just looked at her.

"We worked together on a movie a few years ago," she said. Then she shook her head and added, "A really BAD movie."

"Why didn't I know this?" I said.

She spread her hands out in front of her.

"Maybe because you didn't ask."

Couldn't argue with that, so I let it go.

"You'd better watch yourself," she said as she flipped the ball to me, "or I'm going to shove that ball up your ass."

I caught it and said, "I'd LOVE to see you try."

A wicked smile spread across her face.

"We'll see," she said as she turned and left.

Oh, boy...
