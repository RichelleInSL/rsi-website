RSI Story #9A Chosen Leader

-3-Challenge Accepted

The moment Aly walked into my office, she knew something was up. Ashley and Emma were still there, along with Danni and myself. I had considered that the proper thing to do was to offer Aly the leadership role privately, giving her the chance to consider it without outside influence. But Emma and Ashley were the reason she was here. They were the ones that had chosen her to lead them. I wanted them there so Aly could see that, though she was my third choice to lead the team, she was the first choice of her teammates.

Besides, I was tired of people telling me no, and I wasn't above applying a little pressure to make that stop.

"This can't be good," Aly said as she surveyed the situation.

"Not at all," I said. "In fact, I think you'll find it very good."

There weren't enough seats for everyone present. There were two guest chairs in front of my desk, and they were occupied by Ashley and Emma. That left Aly to stand between them. Danni was on my side of the desk, leaning in the corner to my left.

"As you know," I began, "RSI is planning to enter a third entry in the team competition."

"Uh, huh," Aly said, clearly not sure what that had to do with her.

"We're trying to figure out who should lead that team."

She glanced back and forth between Ashley and Emma. "Uh, huh," she said again.

I had decided beforehand that I would be totally up-front about the process that got us here.

"Emma and Ashley have each been offered the position," I said. "Each has turned it down for various reasons."

"Uh, huh," she said.

I knew Aly was going to be caught off-guard by what was coming, so I was trying to be understanding. That said, she was starting to get annoying.

"They volunteered to come up with a suggestion of their own, and I took them up on the offer."

Aly went to speak, but I cut her off.

"Please," I said, "don't fucking say 'Uh, huh.'"

She paused a moment and said, "Okay."

At least it was something different.

"Alyson," I said, "the suggestion they came up with is you."

Now, she looked truly lost, and she looked silently past me as she tried to get caught up. "I'm sorry," she said finally. "What?"

Everyone but Aly laughed. She just continued to look confused.

"Aly," I said more plainly, "Ashley and Emma have suggested you to lead our newest team."

"I don't understand," she said. "I'm already on Britney's team."

"I know," I replied. "And that's part of why I didn't think of you sooner." I gestured toward Ashley and Emma. "Your teammates have opened my eyes to what I was missing."

She shook her head. "I can't LEAD," she said. "I can barely keep myself competitive."

I didn't even get my mouth open to respond when I heard Emma say, "Bullshit."

Aly turned and looked at Emma in disbelief. I had to admit, I found the outburst a little hard to believe, myself.

"First of all, you're always competitive," Emma continued. "I've never seen you look overwhelmed in a fight."

Aly did little more than blink.

"And second, you can absolutely be a leader," Emma said. "Everyone in this building has seen the way you fight through one setback after another. You have exactly what it takes to lead this team."

Aly turned slowly to look at me. "What's with her?" she asked as she hooked a thumb in Emma's direction.

"I'm not sure," I said. "But she knows who she wants to lead her team, that's for sure."

Aly then faced Ashley. "What do you have to say?"

"You lead and I'll follow," Ashley replied. "We're starting from nothing, Aly. We need a strong leader to get us through."

Aly nodded thoughtfully. Then, she looked to Danni. "And what do you think?"

Danni shrugged and said, "I think it may be the greatest idea Richelle's ever had." Then, she smiled as she added, "Of course, that's probably because it really wasn't her idea."

I turned to her slowly and, with a smile on my face, I said, "You're fired."

"Yeah, right," she said.

"Hmph."

I looked back at Aly. "What do you say, Hellcat?" I asked, using the nickname Britney had given her way back when.

She thought for another moment, then said firmly, "Let's do this."

Both Ashley and Emma leaped from their seats and threw their arms around their new team leader, squishing poor Aly in the middle.

"Okay," Aly croaked. "That's enough of THAT shit."

Her teammates laughed and I glanced over at Danni, who looked back at me an nodded.

"Oh, does this mean I have to come up with a team name?" Aly groaned. "I'm terrible at that stuff."

I gestured toward Aly and Emma. "The Aly Cats!" they said in unison.

Aly looked impressed. "I actually like it," she said.

"Okay," I said. "You guys get outta here. Aly, I'll set something up with Britney and Elisha. We'll figure out how all of this is going to work."

She nodded and said, "This means a lot to me. Thanks."

I smiled broadly and said, "Don't thank me, yet. You haven't seen what lies in store for you."

The new teammates made their way back to the training area, while Danni stayed behind.

"You thinking what I'm thinking?" she asked.

"That Ashley's purple hair kind of reminds you of Plum Puddin' from Strawberry Shortcake?"

"Uh... no," she said. "I meant what I'm thinking about the teams."

I nodded. "If we're going to move Aly, that changes everything."

She shrugged. "Might not be so bad. We're probably better off spreading the younger fighters around."

I grimaced. "Britney and Elisha are not going to be happy."

"Ah, they'll get over it," she said.

"Fine," I replied. "Then YOU tell them."

She made a show of looking at the watch she wasn't actually wearing. "Oh, gee... look at the time. Gotta get down to training before Steph has a fit."

"Coward," I said.
