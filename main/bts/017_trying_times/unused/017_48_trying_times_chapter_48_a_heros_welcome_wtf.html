-48-A Hero's Welcome

Our time in Australia included very little tourist-type activity. Some nice dinners out, a couple of long, lazy visits to Kings Park and a whole lot of time spent in the hotel room being anything but lazy.

I read a theory somewhere that having sex with someone is always best the second time. When the first-time clumsiness was gone but the experience was still new and exciting. My limited research to this point supported that hypothesis... until now. Being with Danni was utterly fantastic and only getting better. I wanted her. All the time. In fact, I've been working hard to fight it. To train myself to resist. And I've discovered that I kind of like that, too. Fighting the pull is a thrill all it's own.

Today was going to be a real test, though. Having to wait an entire day?

I could do it. Easily. Waiting makes things better, and Danni is worth waiting for.

Danni bumped the Audi into the RSI lot, and put it in her usual spot. We got out and walked around to the main entrance. For no particular reason, I paused and looked around.

She's not much to look at, this gym of ours. Clean and well-cared for, but otherwise unremarkable. Nothing about it gave even the slightest hint of the things were happening here. The relationships. The titles. The triumphs and the heartbreak.

In her customary fashion, Danni read my thoughts without either of us saying a word.

"It's a helluva thing you've built," she said.

"We've built,"
