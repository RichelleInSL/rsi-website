-1-The Nightmare Scenario

Rod Laver Arena, Melbourne, Australia

Blood and sweat sprayed from Danneel Harris's face as her opponent's fist slammed into the side of her head. The force of the blow staggered RSI's stable leader, but she somehow gathered herself and slid left - barely avoiding being caught on the fence, yet again.

Cheryl Cole pressed forward cautiously, stalking Danni like a cat playing with its prey. But this was anything but a game to her. This was her chance. Her chance to prove that Danni was little more than a paper champion - one who had feasted on inferior competition for far too long.

Though both fighters could lay claim to outstanding careers, Cheryl's had been a constant war with Megan Fox and Catrinel Menghia for the ESL welterweight belt. She had shown time and again she was up to the task, and the three passed the title between them for years.

Danni, meanwhile, has enjoyed almost half a decade of relatively unblemished success as the queen of the DEF welterweights - a period that goes back to well before she signed on with RSI. During that span, she successfully defended the title on eight out of ten occasions, and she avenged the two losses the very next chance she got. It was the kind of run that few fighters ever experience, and it easily made her the most revered welterweight in the entire league.

Danni's fans believed she had easily proven herself worthy of that level of respect.

Cheryl's fans believed that Harris had simply taken advantage of a favorable situation. They felt things would have been much different if Danni and Cheryl were competing for the same crown.

Thanks to the league's recent franchise reorganization, now they are.

The change immediately gave the fans the rivalry they were dying to see, and the reaction was immense. The fact that people were excited was no surprise. What was surprising was how excited Danni was. Though the brutal and powerful Cole was a clear threat to her championship reign, she had gone on record regarding how much she was looking forward to the upcoming rivalry. Most people - myself included - thought she was crazy. But it was clear she wanted the competition and the challenge. Cole offered no response to Harris's comments, but her fans said Danni was going to be sorry.

In June of last year, the moment finally came. Harris vs Cole for the DEF Welterweight championship. Though both fighters mostly stayed out of it, the chatter surrounding the event was deafening. Cheryl's fans were chomping at the bit, eager to get to the moment they'd been waiting for. And they were right. Danni was sorry.

Sorry she had to go and crush their dreams like that.

There was no doubting the effort Cheryl had put into her preparation, including adding a few new tricks to her bag for the occasion. But Danni quickly proved that she was as sharp as ever and she was ready for anything. Energized and determined, she brushed Cole's early attacks aside and delivered an unceremonious thumping to the challenger. It was a win that reminded everyone - including the champion herself - that this was still Danni's division and her belt to lose. But, even in victory, she acknowledged that, at some point in the rivalry, Cheryl was likely to come out on top.

In part, it was the expected response from a fighter who knew better than to antagonize an opponent... especially one of Cole's caliber. The loss she'd suffered was all the inspiration Cheryl would need to make herself better for next time. It would be stupid to add fuel to the fire. But Danni's comments were also an honest and realistic assessment of the situation. She has a tremendous amount of confidence in herself, and I have no doubt she believes she's the better fighter. But even the best make mistakes, and Cole is too good to hold down forever. At some point, Danni would slip and Cheryl would be quick to take advantage.

Now, nearly a year later, Cole was getting another shot... and she was making it count as she brutally hammered the champion from one side of the cage to the other. From the moment the bell rang, the fight had been completely one-sided, and the problem was much more than just chance or bad luck. It was a total disaster. A nightmare scenario that had been building for months.

I was just too ignorant to see it until it was too late.
