RSI Story #4Nightmare Relived

Nightmare Relived

July 24, 2014

I can't believe it happened again.

Those familiar with Tiffany Mulheron's long road to the championship know the story of the terrible night when she was completely destroyed in the cage by nemesis Nadine Coyle. But, you also know the story of her amazing and courageous comeback from that night. You know how, with the help of her friends and teammates, Tiffany trained and prepared and steeled herself to return to the cage and face down Nadine, once more. And, most importantly, you know how Tiffany found a way to finally unseat the long-standing champion, even if it was short-lived. It was a glorious comeback that culminated in ultimate vindication for Tiffany and her teammates.

And, yet... somehow... here we are. Back where it all started.

I won't go through the blow-by-blow of tonight's championship fight between Nadine and Tiffany. If you want to know the details, you can check the official UMMA round-by-round commentary. Or, you can simply look back to the details of that terrible night that had seemed such a distant memory. Tonight, just as then, Nadine crushed Tiffany into a bloody, helpless wreck.

After the fight, I hurried back to the RSI locker room to talk with Tiffany. As I entered, I found Emma Watson, her training partner, sitting alone on a bench with tears in her eyes.

"Where is she?" I asked.

"She finished with the training staff and went straight to the press conference," Emma said softly.

"Did you talk to her?"

Emma shook her head. "She wouldn't talk to me. She just left."

Uh, oh.

Emma and I arrived at the press conference just as it was starting. Danni was already there, and we quietly slid in next to her. Tiffany took her seat, and didn't bother to wait for a question. "I have no excuse for my performance here tonight," she said with a cold, stony expression on her face. "I humiliated myself, my teammates and my stable. I know that I'm better than this, but knowing it doesn't mean anything. I have to show it when the pressure's on, and I didn't." She stopped and looked out at her audience... her stoic expression cracking only slightly as her gaze fell upon Danni and Emma, who have been by her side since she joined us at RSI. She swallowed hard, and continued. "I let myself down, and, more importantly, I let my friends down. I am disgusted and ashamed of myself. And I honestly don't know where I go from here." Her remarks finished, she pushed away from the table and left the room.

By this point, tears were rolling down Emma's cheeks as she tore off after her friend. I turned to Danni.

"What the hell happened in there, tonight?" I asked.

"I don't know," she said honestly, as she wiped away a stray tear of her own. "She didn't look right from the minute the fight started. She looked... well she looked like a rookie. Her head was never in the fight." Then, her jaw tightened and her eyes narrowed. "And Nadine..." she growled. "That fuckin' Nadine..."

"Nadine did exactly what you'd expect she'd do, Danni," I said. "But she's not supposed to be able to do it to Tiffany, anymore."

We made our way back to the locker room, where we found Emma sitting and stoically watching Tiffany unload her locker into her gym bag. Our youngest champion turned to me, and I motioned with my head for her to leave so I could talk to Tiffany alone. She and Danni stepped out of the room, while Tiffany just kept throwing her things in the bag... seemingly oblivious to my presence.

"You're not going to even talk to me?" I asked after a few moments.

"Not much to say."

"So, that's it?" I said, the frustration clear in my voice. "You're just gonna quit?"

"You were there. You saw what happened."

"I saw what you let her do to you, yeah."

She spun to face me, a flash of anger in those pools of green. "What's THAT supposed to mean?"

"It means where the fuck was your head, tonight?" I shot back at her. "Because it wasn't in that cage."

She turned away... once again fiddling with the crap in her locker. "I got my ass kicked. I'm not going to make excuses."

I reached out and touched my hand hand to her shoulder. "Tiff... what's going on?"

She shrugged me off. "Nothing." Slamming the locker closed, she snatched up her bag and headed for the door.

"Tiffany!" I called after her. "You can't just let it end like this."

She stopped, but kept her back to me. "I just need some time," she said finally.

I breathed a small sigh of relief. "Well... where are you going?"

"Home." And then, she was gone.

No sooner had Tiffany walked out when Emma flew in, followed by Danni. "What happened? Where is she going?"

"She said she's going home," I replied.

"Well... is she coming BACK?"

I shrugged. "It sounded like she was. She said she just needed time."

Emma looked silently back at the door for a few moments. Then she mumbled something softly to herself. I'm not sure of exactly what she said, but I'm pretty sure I heard things like "I'm sorry" and "I didn't mean it."

"What?" I asked her. The mumbling stopped, and she went back to silently studying the door.

"I'm going with her," she said finally, striding toward the exit.

"Emma, WAIT!" I called after her. "You're on the card for next week. You can't just leave."

She turned and looked at me, then looked back at the door. "I'm sorry, but I have to. I'll see you in a few days." And then she was gone, too.

I turned and looked at Danni. "Am I still in charge around here?" I said exasperatedly.

She just smiled. "What made you think you ever were?"

Danneel Harris, ladies and gentlemen. What a riot.
