RSI Story #16Haunted By The Past

-1-Ruining A Good Day

It was a bright and beautiful winter morning. Snow had fallen overnight... just enough to paint the world a pristine white. The wintry weather had ended a few hours before dawn, meaning nothing but clean roads and unbridled sunshine for the morning drive. I walked into the RSI facility feeling happy and energetic.

That feeling died about ten minutes later.

The moment Danneel Harris walked into my office, I knew something was very wrong. Her cheeks were red and blotchy, the coloring made all the more noticeable by the blanched appearance of the surrounding skin. RSI's team leader was wearing a black wool jacket... the same one she had worn the day before. Only today, it looked two sizes too big, as she seemed to shrink inside of it.

"What's wrong?" I said, no hint of uncertainty in my voice. For Danni to look as she did, there was no doubt something terrible had happened.

She didn't say a word. She simply extended her right hand... a simple, unmarked, white envelope grasped between her thumb and forefinger. I eyed it for moment, then turned my gaze back to Danni.

"What is that?" I asked.

"Just take it," she said.

I had no idea what had happened to put Danni in this state. I had no idea what thoughts were running through her mind. But, I suddenly knew exactly what was in that envelope.

I shook my head.

"No."

"Richelle," she said, "please don't make this any harder than it already is."

"I'm not taking it," I said, "until you tell me what's going on."

She started to tear up as she held it out more urgently. "Please just take the fucking envelope. I really can't talk about it."

I reached out, snatched the envelope from her hand, tore it in half and threw it in the garbage.

"Jesus fucking..." Danni yelled. "What the fuck is wrong with you?"

"You can always make another one, if that's what you want!" I yelled back at her. "But first, you need to tell me what's wrong."

She opened her mouth to speak, but no words came out. Instead, tears started to stream down her face.

Much like me, Danni is an emotional person. She gets angry, worried, frustrated... and she uses those emotions to drive herself. As long as there's a problem she can attack, Danni will be fine.

But, what I saw from her now looked alarmingly like hopelessness. Whatever was wrong, it was beyond her control, and she didn't have a way to go after it. I had seen this from her only once before. And the problem in that case had been me. In a poorly conceived attempt to help Emma Watson work through a bad period, I had stupidly gotten myself badly hurt. It landed me in the hospital, and it left Danni with nothing to do but wait and see how things turned out.

Wait and see is not something Danni does well.

I stood, came around my desk, and took hold of both of her hands.

"Whatever this is," I said, "you know I'm going to help you with it."

She shook her head.

"You can't help me," she said softly.

"You don't know what I can do, until you talk to me."

She looked sadly down at the floor.

"My past has come back to haunt me."
