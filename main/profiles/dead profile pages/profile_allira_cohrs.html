Bio

Even the most athletic of women often shy away from the fight game, and convincing them otherwise can be difficult. On the other hand, sometimes the right woman simply falls in your lap. A few years ago, we found Allira Cohrs tucked away in the pages of FIGHT! Magazine. Though she was clearly featured as a centerfold rather than a fighter, we couldn't help noticing the list of her favorite things. Nestled in there with dark chocolate and blueberries were weight training, boxing and UFC. We wondered just how serious her love of MMA was, so we figured we'd make a simple phone call. The next day, Allira was on a flight up from Australia's Gold Coast. She arrived at our facility a few days later, gear bag in hand.

That's the kind of excitement we like to see.

Since then, she's been a pretty steady fighter. She hasn't exactly set the world on fire and she's had a few too many bad losses, but she also has enough big wins to tell us she can do more. With superstars like Steph McMahon and Torrie Wilson around to guide her, we firmly believe there are big things on the horizon for Ms. Cohrs.
