The Undisputed Champ

Danneel Harris and Kristanna Loken have recently seen quite a bit of each other inside the octagon. Back in March, Danni narrowly escaped with a split decision... a decision many felt should have gone to Kristanna. In May, Kristanna not only defeated Danni, but pounded her badly, leaving no doubt that Danni had a challenge on her hands. Afterward, Kristanna stated that she felt she had earned the chance to take the welterweight title away from Harris. Danni didn't argue, saying only that she would be ready when the time came. Well, that time came tonight, and she was more than ready. In a performance that stunned even her own coaches and trainers, Danni literally brutalized Kristanna. She seem determined to show everyone why she's the champion. In what seemed like a concerted effort to humiliate the challenger, Danni even resorting to slapping her suffering opponent. Afterward, the champion was not exactly humble. "This belt hangs around my waist for a reason," Danni stated simply. "It's mine. Anyone who wants to take it is going to literally have to step over my broken body to get it." Still, she did offer Kristanna a measure of respect. "She pushed me harder than anyone has in a long time. Since she beat me last time, I've been focused on nothing but getting ready for this matchup." Having met the challenge tonight, Danneel decided to offer Kristanna a little motiviation of her own. "I stepped up my game to be ready for this fight. Let's see if she can step up her's for the next one."

Next up, we have the latest chapter in the ongoing saga of Elisha Cuthbert and Britney Spears. Seriously... isn't there anyone else in the middleweight division these girls can match up with? Why am I constantly being tortured with this? Anyway... while I may have hated it, the women put on the usual fantastic show for the fans. Once again, it was Elisha getting the best of Britney in a brutal slugfest, knocking the beautiful songstress silly with less than a minute to go in the first round. This makes three wins in a row for Elisha. Is Britney worried? "Worried? No. Pissed? Hell, yes," said a less than pleased Spears afterward. "I love Elisha. Everyone knows that," she continued, as a wry smile spread across her face. "But, honestly... I'm going to hand her her ass, next time." For her part, Elisha gave her friend and training partner her due. "I owe Britney alot. It's no secret that things were sliding the wrong way for me when I signed on with RSI. Working with Brit every day has really helped me get myself straightened out." Then a smile flashed in those mischievous blue eyes as she added, "Maybe it turns out I'm just naturally better than her, though." Awesome. No doubt this friendly rivalry is going to be fun for someone. And, it's certainly not going to be me.

Lastly, Holly Bricken looked pretty good against newcomer Maria Kanellis. With neither woman exactly sure of what to expect from the other, the action was not as crisp as UMMA fans have come to expect. But, Holly stayed patient, defending well and unleashing her fearsome power only whenever the opportunities came. On the other side, Maria looked confident and prepared. But, it was clear she had never come up against an opponent with Holly's strength and striking ability. A solid knee strike dropped the former WWE starlet a little more than halfway through the second round. So, Holly maintains that zero in the loss column, while her team is clearly excited about what the future holds. Holly, however, wasn't willing to spend any time patting herself on the back. "I'm happy with the win, and I'm mostly happy with my performance. But, I know I still have a lot to learn." Not a bad attitude to have. Winning in the heavyweight division is certainly no small accomplishment. But, the competition is going to get much tougher.

Well, it's a full slate for RSI at the upcoming event in Belgrade. Emily VanCamp will get a shot at longstanding lightweight queen Irina Sheik. Ashley Tisdale will look to prove her last victory over Lady Gaga was no fluke. And, Krystal Forscutt, who has been notably quiet for quite a while, will return to action against Gemma Arteron. Also, RSI rookies Jennifer Stano and Allira Cohrs will try to stay perfect as each faces off against far more experienced UMMA fighters. We've been telling these women over and over that the road to the top is a long and difficult one. We'll find out soon if Jennifer and Allira have been paying attention.
