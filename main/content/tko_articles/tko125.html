The Going Gets Tough

Another team event behind us, and that means another round table postmortem with the team leaders. For a change of pace, I asked each to bring with them the fighter they've selected as their Fighter of the Night. So, along with team leaders Britney Spears, Elisha Cuthbert and Alyson Michalka were Emily VanCamp, Julianne Hough and Emma Watson. Also along to share a little insight was fighter development coordinator, Stephanie McMahon.

And be forewarned... the discussion went all over the place, tonight. With talk of mismatches and the changes coming with the UMMA reorganization, I'm surprised we actually managed to cover the original topic, at all.
