FORSCUTT ON THE RISE

Aside from a tough loss for Ashley Tisdale, RSI had a great night at Gauntlet.

Ashley knew the score going into her match with Alizee Jacotey. She needed to stay on her feet and attack with her superior striking. Alizee was going to want to get the fight to the ground, where she has the advantage. Well, in the lightning fast world of professional MMA, things don't always go like you plan. Alizee tripped Ashley up early, and Ashley just never recovered. To be honest, the thinking here at RSI is that much of this is our fault. Ashley DOES HAVE the ground skills she needed to get herself out of trouble tonight. But, in the leadup to this event, her team continually stressed the importance of staying on her feet. So, when Alizee got her down so quickly, Ashley looked like the fight was already lost. She appeared to give up, and Alizee worked her over brutally until she finally scored the KO. Getting beaten like that will certainly be tough for Ashley to accept. But, if anyone can shake it off, she can.

Next up, Danielle Lloyd against Sophie Howard. Now, we'd love to simply gush about Danielle's hard-fought victory, but we can't help but feel she made it harder than it had to be. No offense to Sophie, but, when you have your opponent face down on the canvas, you need to finish her. Instead, Sophie was able to escape and survive the round. Credit to Danielle, though, for overcoming that failure and putting Howard away in the second round. Now, clearly we're nitpicking here. Danielle was basically in command throughout this fight, and it's a great win for her. But, the level of competition here in the Super Heavyweight division of IHY80 is very high. Less than 300 ranking points separates 5th from 11th, and Danielle is right in the middle of that pack. She just can't give her opponents any second chances.

OK, so... how about that Rhian Sugden? Clearly, she can make a statement when she wants, and she sure made one tonight. I don't know whether Jana Defi used to take Rhian's lunch money when they were little, or what, but she sure gave her a beating in there. Not only that, she was plenty nasty about it, too. On that point, referee Nikki Sanderson was calling this one a little tight, hmmm? In a league that allows victory by stripping, what's so wrong with a head butt or two? Speaking of victories, I don't think Ms. Sugden could have found a more humiliating way to finish this one. I don't where this nasty version of Rhian came from, but it certainly works.

When Krystal Forscutt signed on with RSI, she had all of one victory in thirteen fights. Three bouts and three wins later, she is easily RSI's biggest success story, so far. This one was never in doubt, as she completely dismantled Jessica Hart. Also, apparently she's been hanging out with Rhian too much, because she finished this one by demonstrating a certain flair for humiliation herself. Her sudden turn around has certainly inspired a lot of confidence. "I feel much more controlled in the cage, now," she said after the match. "I used to feel like every match was just a free-for-all. I never had a plan. Now, I feel confident I can handle whatever my opponent throws at me. I've been at the bottom of the rankings for so long, it just feels good to be moving in the right direction."

Well, the next Shift event is just around the corner. Julianne Hough will be in action against the always tough Joanna Levesque, while Alyson Michalka will get all she can handle from the top-ranked Brooklyn Decker. We'll see you then.
