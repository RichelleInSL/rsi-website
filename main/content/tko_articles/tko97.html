Reeling in Rio

Another tough night for the RSI group, and this one cost long-standing Welterweight champion Danneel Harris her belt. After months and months of trying, Kristanna Loken finally wrestled the title away from the RSI team leader. And this one was a classic one shot victory. After the two women spent the first few minutes feeling each other out, Kristanna unleashed a fearsome hook that Danni never saw coming. The blow landed flush, and that was that.

Afterward, Danni's teammates rallied around her, but Danni didn't seem to need much support. In her post-fight comments, she seemed to indicate that she knew something like this was coming. "Kristanna and I have faced each other many times, now. I've usually performed well and things have gone my way. But, she's proven time and time again that she's no pushover. I knew she was going to win one eventually. It was just bound to happen." But, when questioned about her emotions after the loss, Harris wasn't ashamed to admit the loss hurt. "I'm disappointed. Absolutely. I'm disappointed at the loss. I'm disappointed that I lost the belt." Disappointed as she may be, Harris offered no excuses. And, though it was certainly an option for her, Danni steadfastly refused to use the "lucky punch" defense. "No. I wouldn't do something like that. The bell rang, and she knocked me out. I won't try to take that away from her. She won and she deserves credit for that." Though she accepted her loss graciously, Danni had a bit of advice for her adversary. "Take good care of my belt," she said as a sly smile spread across her face. "I'll be looking to take it back soon."

Another poor performance from Danica Patrick, as she once again failed to stay on her feet. In fact, this match was almost a carbon copy of her last match with opponent Tila Tequila. Danica failed to execute her game plan, and once again found herself the victim of a humiliating submission. Obviously, there need to be some serious changes to Danica's training regimen.

Speaking of failed game plans, Holly Bricken suffered much the same fate as Danica, as she was forced into submission by Ana Beatriz Barros. Holly gets a little bit of a pass, as it's her first loss. Nobody stays perfect forever. But, if you're going to lose, at least be competitive.

If Danica and Holly need an example to follow, look no further than Cobie Smulders. Cobie's performance against Paris Hilton was textbook. She took the fight to her opponent, pressing her advantage and not letting up until Paris was out cold. That's how you do it.

I'm not even going to spend a lot of time talking about the ridiculous match Petra Cubonova had with Denise Milani. A round of what looked like dancing, a round of the two of them rolling around on the ground and then referee Autumn Reeser decides to end things because Petra got a boo-boo. I'm not even angry about the loss... Petra is capable of a much better performance than she showed tonight. So, maybe the loss will help motivate her for her next outing. I'm angry because the silly stoppage means her next outing is going to be delayed by the mandatory UMMA wait period.

All in all, this was just not a good night for RSI. Even the much hyped match-up between Britney Spears and Elisha Cuthbert didn't live up to the billing. Both women fought well, but it didn't take long for Britney, still clearly riding the wave of momentum from her performance at Open Fight Night, to take control of things... knocking Elisha to the canvas with a barrage of punches late in the first round. It looked like Elisha might be able to last until the bell, but nope... once again, Autumn Reeser was there to make sure that everyone went home disappointed. After the decision was announced, a clearly ticked off Elisha stormed off into the locker room. She was met there by Britney as the two shared a hug and, no doubt, a few well-timed barbs. I expect their next training session will be quite an event.

Well, two events in, and RSI is still trying to find it's footing here in 2014. Maybe things will get on the right track at the upcoming ESL event. No point in whetting your appetite with all the details of the scheduled matches, as there's really only one fight everyone is talking about. Emily VanCamp will be putting her title on the line against long-time champion Irina Sheik. No doubt Irina believes the title is rightfully hers, and she has the resume to back up that claim. But the days of Emily being the heavy underdog in these match-ups is behind us. I expect quite a show when these two women meet up. Make sure you don't miss it.
