Putting in the Work, Bitch

For the RSI group, Open Fight Night became a flurry of challenges and mini-tournaments. It got so out of control, I'm not even sure who fought and who didn't. I also have no clue as to the motive behind some of the challenges. But I was told that our overall record was 7 wins in 9 fights, and I'll take that any day. I think a few RSI fighters accepted fights they were not properly prepared for, but that's a matter we'll handle internally. In the meantime, there were definitely a few highlights that need to be mentioned, including a monumental performance by Britney Spears.

First up, though, Daniela Hantuchova looked magnificent in her fight against Erin Heatherton. Though this challenge came from the ILF camp, Daniela was eager to test herself against the top notch contender. And, while Erin definitely came ready to play, Daniela showed she was up to the task. She weathered a number of vicious attacks from Heatherton, and responded with precision strikes of her own. Though both women got there licks in, Hantuchova seemed to be getting the better of the exchanges. The fight was shaping up to be a great one. And then, as happens much too often, the referee stepped in and stopped things before anything could be definitively decided. Though Heatherton was still actively attacking and defending herself, Natasha Yi decided to intervene, declaring Erin incapable of fighting on. Once again, a bitter ending to a fight that could have been a classic. That said, Daniela was definitely ahead at that point in the fight, and Erin was in big trouble when Yi stepped in. So, the tennis star certainly earned the win. Still... it would have been nice to see this one carry through to a proper end.

So, why did I choose to highlight Daniela first? Because I didn't want her performance to get swept up in the debris of Superstorm Britney. In our last release, I mentioned that Ms. Spears had taken on far more than she should have for this event. Having already agreed to participate in the Top Singer Tournament, Brit also accepted a challenge from rookie Élodie Frégé. I'm not sure what Frege was trying to prove with that challenge, but the risk here was all Britney's. With the experience Britney has, anything less than total domination of her opponent would be considered an embarassment. Well, not to worry, as Britney took care of business in short order, sending an unconscious Frege to the canvas in less than 45 seconds.

With that settled, it was on to the mini-tournament, where the challenge was much greater. Not one fighter in the tournament had less than 25 fights under her belt coming in, and most had more than 40 fights behind them. But, the most experienced among them was Britney, and it clearly showed. Her first opponent was Rihanna, and Brit never let the R&B star get warmed up, knocking her cold in a little over a minute. Next was the talented Taylor Swift, and the young songtress definitely showed no fear of her more experienced opponent. In fact, there was a point in the first round when Taylor pounded Britney bloody while working her over in the clinch. Spears was in real danger, but kept her composure and survived the round. In the second, however, it was all Britney, landing a barrage of her own that forced Rebecca Loos to step in and put a stop to things. Overall, these women put on a fantastic show, and Britney was not shy about praising Swift afterward.

"Taylor really impressed me out there," said Spears. "When you see her on stage, she always looks like such a tiny little thing. But, she packs a lot of power, and she has the skills to back it up. I'm definitely glad she and I are not in the same division."

The win over Swift brought Britney to the championship match, where she met another experienced opponent in Jennifer Lopez. This time, though, Spears made it look easy, catching Lopez early and never letting up. A minute and a half later, Britney had the win and the tournament championship. A fine cap on an excellent event for Ms. Spears, right? Yeah... not quite. It seems Christina Aguilera still harbors some ill will toward her one time rival. Having lost a totally embarrassing snore-fest to Mya Harrison in the mini-tournament, Christina didn't get the chance to face Britney there. So, she decided to call out Spears directly.

As soon as I heard about Aguilera's challenge, I confronted Britney, pleading with her not to accept. At that point, Britney had already taken part in 4 fights at this event, including the slugfest with Taylor. Meanwhile, Aguilera had sleep-walked through a non-fight with Harrison. This gave Christina a clear advantage, and I was concerned that fatigue was going to increase Britney's injury risk. Of course, I was also well-aware that I was wasting my time. A challenge like this... especially from Christina Aguilera... I knew there was no way Britney would refuse. What I didn't realize was just how deep the hatred ran between these two.

When fight time came, Britney came out with a look of absolute fury on her face. And when the bell rang, there was no technique. There was no strategy. There was nothing but pure savagery. In what must have been the longest two and a half minutes of Aguilera's life, Britney absolutely shredded her, never giving the Voice coach a chance to mount any kind of offense. When it was over, Britney stood with her arms at her sides, staring down menacingly at her vanquished opponent like she was a piece of gum on the bottom of her shoe. It looked like her only wish at that moment was for Christina to stand back up so she could pound her some more.

"What a fucking bitch!" seethed a clearly pissed off Spears when asked about the last-minute challenge at the post event press conference. "She knew I had a lot going on at this event, and she knew I was hurting. To call me out like that was a bullshit move, and she knows it."

A reporter tried to follow-up, but Britney wasn't done.

"She knew the only way she could beat me would be to come after me when I was already hurt. And look at all the good it did her."

Done now, Ms. Spears?

"She's fuckin' lucky I didn't kill her," the proud blonde muttered, just loud enough for the microphone to pick it up.

Well, I guess that just about covers that.

Lost in the noise of Aguilera's poorly thought out challenge was just what Britney accomplished here. Five fights in one event is no small task, and the mini-tournament alone was packed with talent. And yet, Britney emerged victorious throughout, and she has a shiny new trophy for her efforts. So, I send a big congratulations to Britney on her performance here, and my most heartfelt condolences to Elisha Cuthbert. There is no doubt that Britney is going to be rubbing this one in her face for months to come.

Well, that about wraps up RSI's rookie season. It had it's ups and downs, but overall, I take a lot of pride in what our athletes have accomplished. We're hoping for more of the same next year, starting with the women of SCQ. RSI will have six athletes in action in Córdoba, including a title shot for Stephanie McMahon. Until then, I wish everyone a joyous holiday season, and a very happy new year. Take care!
