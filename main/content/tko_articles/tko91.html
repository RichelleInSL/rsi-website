Credit Where Credit is Due

No doubt, Danneel Harris and Kristanna Loken have been getting to know each other pretty well, lately. They've faced off in the cage at each of the last five DEF events. With the exception of a bad injury loss, Danni has emerged as the victor each time, though Kristanna has certainly given her some difficult fights. Tonight, the women met up for the sixth consecutive event, and the results were more of the same. Kristanna was tough and extremely slick, repeatedly taking Danni to the canvas, often surprising her with unconventional takedown maneuvers. Though she was never really able to do much to hurt Danni, Loken frustrated her opponent, refusing to allow Harris to exercise her own gameplan. In the end, however, it was not enough. While the ground is not her favorite place to be, RSI's team leader has been doing this long enough that she has the skills to handle every situation. She was able to control her foe on the ground, and she was her usual brutal self when she was able to get the fight back to standing. Though she was never able to finish Loken she gave her quite a beating, and it was enough for the judges to score this one in the champion's favor.

While pleased to be leaving with her belt, Danni mostly focused on her familiar opponent in her post-fight news conference. "I have to give her credit... I have beaten her down, bloodied her up and knocked her out. And, through all that, not once... NOT ONCE... has she given an inch. Say whatever you want, she's extremely tough." High praise from the woman who has held the DEF Welterweight belt for nearly three years. And, she wasn't done. "Everyone saw it, tonight. Not only is she one tough bitch, but she's getting better. She was able to make me do a lot things tonight I didn't want to do. This was a hard fight for me. And, I suspect it's going to keep getting harder."

Is it possible to score a disappointing win? Because, that's exactly what happened to Britney Spears tonight. Not that Britney was disappointing. On the contrary, she and opponent Josie Maran looked like they were getting ready to put on a fantastic show for the fans. But, just as the action was heating up, Britney surprised Maran with a front kick that caught her flush, snapping a rib in the process. Though happy to get the win, Britney was just as disappointed as the rest of us at the way things ended. "I hate when something like that happens," said the blonde songstress. "I wish Josie the best, and I hope she's back soon."

Well, on the bright side, Britney's quick victory allowed her to be cageside to shout words of encouragment at her close friend Elisha Cuthbert. Elisha, however, was not in need of moral support as she absolutely destroyed champion Malin Akerman. Akerman opened the fight with an ill-advised takedown attempted that Elisha thwarted with a swift kick to the face. She followed that up with a crushing kick to the midsection that had Malin on the verge of tears. From that point on, it was all Cuthbert, scoring with one kick after the other. The only thing that stopped this from being a dream fight for Elisha was the unfortunate way things ended, as Akerman suffered a tough leg injury and could not continue. Still, this was not a lucky win for Cuthbert. She was clearly the better fighter tonight, and definitely deserved this victory.

As is seemingly always the case, wherever you find one half of RSI's dynamic duo, you usually find the other. In fact, as Elisha made her way out for the post-fight press conference, she found Britney already sitting at the table, working the fans into a frenzy. As Elisha entered the room, Britney swept her arm in her friend's direction, looking much like an emcee ushering a rock star on stage. And, just as Britney had planned, the crowd went wild. So wild, in fact, that Elisha was even surprised. "You guys really need to chill," laughed the stunned actress as she took her seat.

I guess this is just how it is, eh? Though these two may be the bane of my existence, the fans love them. And, really, what's not to love? They're fun, charismatic and friendly. Though they take their sport seriously, they love to mix it up with their fans. Oh, and did I mention that they kick ass? Anyone needing proof of that should just ask Malin, as each of the RSI fighters has scored an impressive victory over the champion, recently. No doubt, behind the smiles and the laughter lurk a pair of fearsome fighters itching for a chance to get their hands on some hardware.

Unfortunately, this was not a great night for Danica Patrick. And, unfortunately, this is not the first time something like this has happened to a new RSI recruit. Danica came into this fight ready to put her new RSI training to work for her. Perhaps taking advantage of Patrick's eagerness, Tila Tequila scored an early takedown and Danica just wasn't prepared to deal with it. Next thing we knew, a frustrated Danica was tapping out to a humiliating legspread submission. Not a great start to her RSI career.

While I have my concerns about this, Danica refused to lay any blame at our feet. In fact, she felt this was more Tila forcing the win than anything else. "She was willing to take a lot of damage to make that submission happen," lamented Danica. "I made her pay a couple of times when she tried to score the takedown, and I pounded her as she tried to secure the submission. But, it wasn't enough. She just powered through it." There is quite a bit of truth in Danica's view of things, but I remain skeptical. We'll be reviewing our approach here at RSI to see if we can better protect our newer athletes from these sudden submission losses.

When we last checked in with Holly Bricken, we found her consumed with worry. Even though she had just scored an impressive victory over the more experienced Arisa Oda, Holly wondered aloud when her lack of experience would catch up with her. Well, keep wondering my dear, as the only answer I can give you is tonight was not the night. Holly was smooth as glass as she once again made short work of an opponent with that traffic-stopping right hand. The victim this time was the vastly more experienced Beyonce Knowles. That experience went for naught, as all of the action was generated by Holly. In fact, the only enemy Bricken seemed to face was herself, as she got her herself in trouble with a renegade takedown maneuver that failed miserably. When asked what she was thinking, Holly was blunt in her response. "I really don't know what I was thinking," the muscular blonde admitted. "I waded in to attack and she tied me up. I tried to take what was there, but I knew right away it was a mistake. The ground was not where I wanted to be. It was as stupid decision, and it almost cost me." Well, clearly, Holly is one of those people who's going to be her own biggest critic. For my part, while the takedown attempt was a bad idea, I try to look at the positive. Holly got herself in trouble, but she overcame it and scored another impressive knockout victory. That sounds pretty good to me.

It was simply not supposed to be this easy for Cobie Smulders, tonight. With very little time to acclimate herself to her new training regimen, Cobie stepped into the cage to face the most brutal and fearsome rookie in the DEF Welterweight division. I was worried. Her team was worried. And Cobie? She just went out and dropped Amanda Harrington like a ton of bricks. With one punch. I kid you not. Cobie is MY fighter, and even I wanted to have her gloves checked for illegal weights when this one was over. Afterward, Cobie refused to see it as anything more than a simple victory. "It was a lucky punch, really. Amanda came out aggressive. She swung and missed, and I didn't. Simple as that." While the punch may have been lucky for Smulders, it was highly unlucky for Amanda. No doubt, the RWB rookie will come looking for payback soon. But, suddenly, I also have no doubt that Cobie will be ready. Let the battle begin.

Yet another roster announcement for RSI this event, though this one is just a quickie. We're pleased to announce that Kelly Hu has agreed to join the RSI family. Kelly has maintained a decent record against some stiff competition, and we're looking forward to helping her take that next step. Also, the signing reunites Kelly with her small screen BFF, Ashley Tisdale. I'll leave it to you to figure out what the hell I'm talking about on that one.

Quite an event coming up for RSI's ESL athletes. We have eight fighters in action, including three title matches. Newcomer Daniela Hantuchova will look to keep things rolling after her big win at the Global Rivalry tournament as she tries to take back the Light Heavyweight crown from nemesis Olivia Wilde. Also, Tiffany Mulheron will get a shot at the Featherweight title. The last time she faced off with Nadine Coyle, Tiffany showed a tremendous amount of heart and not much else, as the champion brutalized her for more than two full rounds. But Tiffany has been working hard, and we're confident she'll be ready, this time around. And, of course, no one will want to miss the rematch of the rematch between long time Lightweight champion Irina Sheik and the vastly improved Emily VanCamp. Though both of the previous fights went Irina's way, Em had nothing to hang her head about as the women had the crowd begging for more. Be sure to get your tickets for this one.
