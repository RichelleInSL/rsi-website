Painful Victories

Overall, another great showing by the RSI teams. But, not all was sunshine and roses. Here are the team leaders with their report.

Elisha Cuthbert: You look a little down for someone who's team just won 7 of it's 8 matchups. What's up, Brit?

Britney Spears: No, I'm OK. Like you said, you can't do much better than we did. It's just...

Elisha: You're worried about Steph.

Britney: (shaking her head in denial) Don't be silly. Steph's been doing this for a long time. Look at her record. Nothing to worry about. She'll be just fine.

Elisha: (looking knowingly at her friend) I hate to tell you this, but you can't lie to me, hon. I know you too well.

Britney: Sigh. Fine. I'm worried. I love Steph and I absolutely want her with us. There is no one else I'd want to anchor this team. But I'm starting to worry she's lost faith in herself.

Elisha: What do you mean?

Britney: She's had a rash of bad losses, lately. She's right in the middle of a tough bunch of fighters, so some losing is expected. But tonight... this shouldn't have happened.

Elisha: Don't be hard on her. I'm sure she tried.

Britney: I'm not being hard on her. This isn't about my disappointment. Steph looked bad tonight, but it's happened to all of us. Sometimes, you just don't have it. I'm just worried about her, that's all. She's been having a tough time, and it seems to be getting worse.

Elisha: Awww... look at you. The momma bear, protecting her cubs.

Britney: Oh, shut up. Tell me your not worried about Daniela.

Elisha: Worried? No, but I feel bad about what happened. You get all pumped up for a big fight, and it's over before it even starts. I know what she's feeling, and I know it sucks. She even apologized, afterward.

Britney: Apologized? For what?

Elisha: You know how she is... she feels like she let us all down. I told her not to be ridiculous, but she just stomped off to the locker room. I was going to go talk to her, but I saw Aly was already headed that way.

Britney: Leaving it to Aly was the right move. She and Daniela are so close now, they're like two sides of the same coin.

Elisha: (clears her throat) Wouldn't know anything about what that's like.

Britney: (laughs) I guess there's just something about two people beating the hell out of each other every day that brings them closer, eh?

Elisha: (smiling) Guess so. Hey, listen... this has been a pretty depressing report, so far. Didn't we win?

Britney: That's what I hear. So, any ideas for how to move on?

Elisha: Well, I was thinking we could start something new.

Britney: Oh?

Elisha: What if we named a Fighter of the Night from each team?

Britney: Hmmm... I don't know. Our team had plenty of good performances tonight, but I don't know if I could pick one that was best.

Elisha: (just looks at her friend expectantly)

Britney: Ah... but I'm guessing you've already got one for your team.

Elisha: I do. But, it wouldn't look right if only I do it. So...

Britney: OK, fine... tell me who you picked while I think about it.

Elisha: Yeah... no way. If I go first, you won't do it.

Britney: You DO know me too well. And, you're SUCH a pain my ass, too.

Elisha: Awww... you're so sweet.

Britney: OK, well, Jules did a hell of a job stopping Ariana Grande. That kid has been on quite a tear lately, but Julianne was having none of it.

Elisha: Yeah. There's something about having a tough rival that gives you an extra edge out there. It's happened to Emily in her competition with Irina, and now it's happened with Jules and Vikki. I guess when you're used to having to be so perfect every time out... when you face someone who's not as experienced, it's not even close.

Britney: No doubt. Both Em and Jules took care of business in a hurry, tonight. But, I'll give the award to Julianne. I think her matchup was a little tougher. So, who do you have?

Elisha: Well, there were a lot of great performances tonight, including a tremendous victory by Amanda Seyfried over Catrinel Menghia.

Britney: Amanda was terrific. Catrinel is a dangerous opponent, but Amanda was ready for her. Catrinel seemed happy to let Amanda make the first move, and she sure made it count.

Elisha: That kick landed perfectly, and Amanda was relentless. She knew she'd gotten a break, and she wasn't going to give Catrinel a chance to recover.

Britney: She was great, and she'd make a great choice. But, I'm guessing from the way you said it that you're not giving the new award to Amanda. So... who then?

Elisha: (smiling from ear to ear) Do you even have to ask?

Britney: (shaking her head and smiling) Your really proud of yourself for this one, aren't you?

Elisha: Hell, yeah. Injury rules forced Petra Cubonova to the sidelines for this event, so I decided to pick one of the rookies to fill her spot. And, I picked a gem, didn't I?

Britney: (laughing) So, are you giving yourself the award, or what?

Elisha: No. But I could give myself a Leader of the Night award, if you like.

Britney: No, I don't like. C'mon... out with it.

Elisha: The Fighter of the Night for Leesh's Lethal Ladies is, without a doubt, Marzia Prince!

Britney: You'll get no argument from me. I thought you were nuts to put a rookie out there, but Marzia made you look like a genius.

Elisha: I am a genius, but that's beside the point. Marzia didn't look like a rookie at all tonight.

Britney: Nope. She wasn't nervous or overwhelmed at all. Which is a little surprising, considering that she didn't fair too well the last time she faced Traci Bingham.

Elisha: No. Traci smacked Marzia around pretty good last time, literally breaking her face in the process. But, Marzia has learned a lot since then, and she showed it tonight.

Britney: I can't deny it. She looked great. She was in command almost the entire time, and this was not an easy fight. She was much more polished than what you'd expect from a rookie. I was very impressed.

Elisha: (teasing) So, who did a good job?

Britney: Really?

Elisha: (nudging Britney with her elbow) C'mon... who did a good job?

Britney: Sigh. You did.

Elisha: I KNOW!!! God, I may be the greatest team leader there is.

Britney: Yeah... or not.

Elisha: C'mon now. Don't be grumpy.

Britney: I'm not. I just...

Elisha: You're still worried. Sigh. Winning should be more fun, shouldn't it?

Britney: Yeah. This is not quite what I expected when we signed on.

Welcome to management, ladies. Winning is great, but there's always something else you need to worry about. And, right now, that something is the upcoming Hit the Beach tournament. With 22 fighters in action... trust me, I'm going to be plenty worried. We'll see you then.
