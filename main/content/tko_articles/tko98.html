The Agony and the Ecstacy

In a disappointing and somewhat controversial decision, Irina Sheik beat RSI sweetheart Emily VanCamp to win the Lightweight title here tonight. For the fans, this one absolutely lived up to the hype. Both fighters had every ounce of their skill on display, and their performances were masterful. And, following the mantra of "always leave 'em wanting more," three full rounds of white hot action was not enough to decide this one. Though both fighters were great, neither emerged as the clear victor. In such cases, it's common for the decision to go to the champion. After all, it's the challenger's job to outperform her opponent and take the title away. That didn't happen in this fight. So, in the end, Irina enjoyed the unwritten "champion's advantage" as the decision went her way. The problem? Irina was not the champion coming into this fight. Em was.

As usual, there was no griping coming from Emily after the fight. "I'm disappointed the decision didn't go my way," admitted RSI's slender powerhouse, "but I'm proud of my performance. I thought it was a tremendous fight, and I love going toe to toe with Irina. I'm looking forward to the next go 'round." Nothing but confidence and respect from a proud warrior, as always.

Which is good, because it leaves the yelling for me.

What, exactly, were these judges looking at? What did Irina do to make them decide to take Emily's crown away? A decision that was unanimous, by the way, which only makes it more bewildering. This was Emily's championship to lose, and she did not lose it. And yet, it's gone. I recall a matchup early in this rivalry that ended in a draw. On that night, I felt Emily had been the better fighter. But, it was very close, and the judge's gave Irina the extra credit a champion deserves. Irina retained her title, and I had no issue with that decision. So now, I'm forced to question tonight's outcome. Do I believe the fix was in? No. I have too much respect for the ILF group and the UMMA as a whole to think that was the case. But, I do think Irina enjoyed an advantage tonight that she was not entitled to. Call it the "veteran's advantage." Call it a reward for the tremendous title run she had before Emily broke it up. Whatever you call it, it shouldn't exist.

My goal is not to get you to cry for poor Emily. Em's a big girl, and she been through tougher things than this. She handles her own business, and does it quite well. But, I'm not gonna let her get pushed around, either. I think what happened tonight is yet another example of Emily not getting the respect she deserves. The title belt entered that cage around Emily's waist. It should have left that way, too. Irina is a brutal and exquisitely talented fighter. She is now, and always will be, a tremendous challenge for Emily. So, Em doesn't need or deserve the additional challenge of overcoming the Irina mystique. These two have fought a number of times now, and Emily has proven to be Irina's equal in each and every fight. It's time she gets equal treatment from the judges, as well.

Well, as bitter a pill as Emily's loss was, the rest of RSI's night had all the sugar we needed to swallow it down. And, the cherry on top of this sundae came from a stunning performance by one of RSI's newer athletes. It also came covered in blood.

Tonight's fight was Tiffany Mulheron's third shot at Nadine Coyle's Featherweight title. The first fight left Tiffany brutally beaten and bloodied... unconscious on the cage floor after nearly six and a half minutes of hammering from Nadine's savage fists. She was Nadine's plaything... totally outclassed and outfought. The fight was so one-sided that Nadine decided to challenge herself by trying repeatedly for her first ever submission victory. In other words, in the middle of a championship match, Nadine decided to use Tiffany as a practice dummy. And the blonde was powerless to stop her. Tiffany was left destroyed and humiliated... her career in tatters.

In the events that followed, Mulheron looked stunned and listless. She was, literally, a fighter on the ropes. But, she was still one of the top-ranked featherweights in the UMMA, and she was one of the very few who were not already contracted to another company. So, when we went looking for a veteran fighter to help mold the young Emma Watson, Tiffany's name was at the top of the list. Though I agreed we needed to meet with her, I freely admit that I expected it to be a waste of time.

I decided to sit down and talk with Tiffany first before doing anything else. I needed to know where she was mentally. "I saw your champiosnhip fight with Nadine," I said simply. "I'm sorry to hear that," she quipped. "I'm sure you had a million better ways to spend that time." I smiled and nodded, then asked the obvious question. "Do you think you can come back?" And, with an honesty that I would later find both honorable and refreshing, Tiffany gave the wrong answer. "I don't know." The words hung dryly in the air, frozen there as I tried to decide how to proceed. The next reasonable step would have been to end the meeting. But, something about the look on her face made me think twice. There was no pain there. No anguish. Just the look of a young woman who honestly didn't know what was next for her. So, I pressed on. "If you're going to keep getting in that cage," I said, "you need to commit. If you can't do that, then it may be time to give it up." Her expression never changed as she uttered the words that would change everything for both us. "I can't quit," she said stoically. "Why not?" She simply shrugged. "I just can't."

I was still pretty sure this wasn't going anywhere, but something about the way she just laid her answers out there made me wonder. I needed help, and, as always, I knew who to call. Danni agreed to meet us in the gym and put Tiffany through her paces. After some early light sparring, Harris kicked things up a notch... attacking with fierce combinations. Tiffany offered no response, simply covering up as Danni hammered away. Showing no mercy, Danni pressed the blonde back against the steel fence... holding her there as she tested Tiffany's rib cage with a series of brutal knee strikes. Again, the Scottish actress did nothing more than curl up defensively. Danni, was attacking with everything she had. I had seen more than enough.

"That's enough," I said, looking to stop the brutal beating before Tiffany was injured.

Shockingly, Danni continued to pound her victim mercilessly.

"Danni, STOP!" I yelled as I moved toward the cage door.

Just as I moved, Danni halted her assault and flung Tiffany to the canvas like a piece of trash. The blonde just lay there... conscious, but unmoving. Danni stood over her menacingly, plotting her next move. And what she did next shocks me to this day.

Dropping to one knee beside the prone blonde, Danneel Harris - the heart and soul of RSI - started to berate her beaten opponent.

"You really do suck," she spat. "It's no wonder Nadine was able to wipe the mat with you."

Too stunned to move, I stood silently as Tiffany started to cry softly.

Still, Danni refused to quit.

"You don't even fight back. You just stand there and take it."

"Danni," I breathed, but Danni held her hand up, motioning for me to stay out of it.

Then, she leaned in close... close enough to nibble on Tiffany's ear... and whispered again.

"Maybe you like it. Huh... is that it? You like being humiliated like that? Did you like being Nadine's bitch?"

Quick as lightning, Tiffany was on her feet, charging at Danni like a bull. Seemingly expecting the move, Harris deftly steered the blonde aside. Tiffany stumbled into the cage then sprang off of it, charging at Danni again. Danni evaded her once more. The process repeated itself over and over. Each time, Danni easily avoided the attack, and each time, Tiffany came at her again. It wasn't a fight. It wasn't a brawl. It was... heart-wrenching. Tiffany had seemingly transformed into a wild animal, screaming and charging savagely as tears streamed down her face. When finally exhausted, the blonde simply let herself slide down the steel fence. She just sat there, burying her head between her knees as her crying advanced into full-blown bawling.

I locked eyes with Danni, who motioned with her head for me to leave. Though deeply disturbed by what I'd seen, I turned toward the door as Danni sat down heavily beside Tiffany. I had no idea what was going on, but it was obvious that Danni did. And there is no one in this world I trust more than Danneel Harris.

I do not know what happened in that cage after I left. I do not know what they did. I do not know what they said. It is very likely I will never know. Two hours later, though, Danni walked into my office. We looked at each other for a moment. Then Danni just nodded her head softly and left. A few days later, it was announced that Tiffany Mulheron was joining RSI.

Like I said, there is no one I trust more than Danni.

Tiffany took her place in the RSI training facility, pairing off as planned with Emma. And, as has become the custom here, they hit it off immediately. In fact, though the plan was for Tiffany to help Emma, it quickly became apparent that the help would go both ways. They became inseparable... training, sparring and strategizing inside the cage, and joking and laughing outside of it. The Tiffany I had seen that first day was gone, replaced by a woman who seemed thoroughly pleased with where she was in life. After a few days, I discovered there was a secret to that transformation.

As each day winds down at the RSI facility, the parking lot slowly empties. Eventually, there are only two vehicles left... mine and Danni's. Shortly after Tiffany signed on, though, I noticed a third vehicle that seemed to never leave. Determined to solve the mystery, I wandered the halls until I came to the gym, which is where I found Danni and Tiffany sparring in the cage. I watched for a short while... only long enough to realize that something very special happened in that cage on that first day. Something very special, indeed.

It didn't take long for the automotive trio to become a quartet, as Emma discovered the secret training sessions and horned in on the action. Though I'm sure they were all aware that I knew what was happening, we never discussed it. Whatever the outcome, they had come together on their own. There was no way I was going to interfere.

Meanwhile, given her divisional ranking, I knew it was only a matter of time before the inevitable came to pass. And, sure enough, only a few short weeks ago came the rematch between Tiffany and Nadine. As the day of the fight drew closer, it was obvious that Tiff was feeling the strain. But, at every turn, Emma and Danni were there to keep her loose with a joke or a well-placed prank. At one point, I emerged from the facility to find Tiffany's car covered from bumper to bumper in pink bubble wrap. The perpetrators of the crime were never apprehended.

On fight night, however, there was just no way to get Tiffany to calm down. As she approached the cage, the fear on her face was plain to see. She looked terrified, and I was terrified for her. Then, a funny thing happened. The bell rang and Nadine punched Tiff square in the face. And, Tiffany took the blow and punched right back. Before long, her training took over and the fear melted away as she moved into the flow of the fight. The war was on and both fighters found themselves in and out of trouble again and again. The script was set for a fairytale ending, but it was not to be. Maybe it was Nadine's relentless attacks, or maybe it was just exhaustion from the raw emotions coming into the fight, but Tiff ran out of gas in the third round. She hung on bravely, forcing a decision from the judges, but the champion would retain her crown that night. The challenger, however, did not walk away empty-handed. She learned that she was definitely worthy of her role. And, a new and re-energized Tiffany showed up to the gym the next day.

After that, the trio of terror redoubled their efforts to get Mulheron ready for the next rematch. And when that rematch came tonight, there were no nerves. There were no fears. There was only Tiffany, calm and confident, ready to fight the fight of her life. And, she was awesome.

From the opening bell to the stunning knockout, the action was non-stop. Nadine was ruthless and efficient as always, but Tiffany matched her step for step. The battle was so savage and brutal, I started to worry someone might be killed. In the end, the cage was heavily stained by the blood of both fighters, but it was Tiffany who had her fist in the air as Nadine was tended to by the cageside medical staff.

No words were spoken in the locker room after the fight, because none were needed. As she emerged from the trainer's office, bloodied but unbowed, Tiffany was nearly knocked to the floor as Emma hugged her fiercely, tears flowing freely from both women. Even the great Danneel Harris looked a little misty as she stood aside and let the training partners have their moment. And, of course, it wasn't long before Emma turned and hauled Danni into the fray. I, meanwhile, watched quietly from the shadows, shedding not a tear. And, if you believe that...

Tiffany was able to gather herself enough for the post-fight press conference, but not without a box of tissues to accompany her. She looked a mess, both physically and emotionally. But, the smile on her face clearly announced that she did not care. When asked about how she felt after her big win, the new champion just dabbed her eyes and waved the question away, letting the tears speak for themselves. She was then asked what she felt was the key to her unbelievable turnaround. Tiffany's eyes sought out Danni and Emma in the back of the room. "I just needed my ass kicked," she answered softly, giving an appreciative nod to RSI's team leader.

When asked her feelings toward the former Featherweight champion, though, Tiff had no trouble finding her voice. "Oh, I hate her," she said plainly. "I hate her, and I know she likes it that way. I'm sure she doesn't really care for me either, but it's all part of who Nadine is. She's a ruthless bitch. I'm sure my calling her that doesn't bother her. In fact, I'm sure she loves it." That's not to say Tiff doesn't know Nadine is more than just an act. "She's an incredible fighter. She's dangerous, and she loves hurting her opponents. And, she's one of the best there is. So, I respect her as a fighter. And, I know I'm going to have to work my ass off to keep this title. But, I still hate her."

I don't know what I'm supposed to say about all this. In RSI's short history, we've had a lot of drama, both good and bad. But, we've really never had anything like this. A fighter I had no interest in... a fighter who was beaten nearly to death by the reigning champion... has come back to take the title for herself. It's an incredible turnaround that defies description. And, I'm ashamed to admit, I never thought it could happen. Even as we were signing her, I had no faith in Tiffany. Not after what I saw that night. But, somehow, some way, Danni knew better. So, my only solace is that I trusted Danni. That's not much for me to hold on to.

I'm not happy with myself for not believing in you, Tiff. Though I made the right call bringing you on, it's not something I'm really proud of. What I am proud of, is you. Danni and Emma deserve credit for their parts in this story, and they'll get it. As long as I do this, I doubt I'll ever see teammates bond together that way in such a short time. But, when it came down to it, it was only you and Nadine in that cage. Just as it was on that terrible night. To have the dedication and courage to change the outcome so drastically... I am amazed. And I will never... ever... doubt you again.

Now, take your new friends and celebrate your win, champ. And, I don't expect to see any of you at the gym, tomorrow.

Next up, Daniela Hantuchova. And, I'm going to cut this one short for two reasons. One, I'm already way over my limit for this press release, and the editors at Total Knock Out are going to throw the whole thing in the trash if I'm not careful. Two... there's really not much to say. Last time out, it took Daniela about a minute and forty-five seconds to flatten challenger Olivia Wilde. This time, it took her less than thirty seconds. That doesn't leave me much to work with.

Let's get something straight... Olivia is no pushover. She's is a fearsome and talented fighter who has beaten some of the best fighters out there, including Daniela. But, since losing her title to Olivia back at the Tombstone event, the Slovakian fighter has been on the warpath. Last time out, she ripped the title right back out of Olivia's hands. And, this time, she didn't even give the popular actress the chance to dream about reclaiming it. Daniela is, without question, at the top of her game. And, considering the record she has amassed in the UMMA, that's saying something. Daniela may be new to RSI, but she is not new to success. It's a pleasure to have such a talented and experienced fighter join our little family.

Speaking of high speed performances, Amanda Seyfried made short work of Holly Valance in their rematch. Amanda just overwhelmed Holly with an early barrage of punches that left her out on the canvas. The win keeps Seyfried on track, though she'll have to stay laser focused if she expects to make any noise in the veteran-heavy Welterweight division.

Four fights, four wins as Ashley Tisdale once again dominated Lady Gaga. After her last win, Ashley had mentioned she was expecting a rematch soon with Alizee Jacotey. Well, whether it's a rematch with the French songstress, or some other new challenge, I think UMMA management needs to find a way to get Ashley up against someone other than Gaga. She seems to have made her point, there.

Krystal Forscutt got back to her winning ways with a dominating performance against Gemma Arterton. There was some question as to how Krystal would respond to having her winning streak broken, especially after getting knocked around so badly by Kaley Cuoco last time out. But, the answer she gave tonight could not have been better. The win puts her right back in the middle of things in the Light Heavyweight division, and it may set her on course for a rematch with Cuoco.

Jennifer Stano has performed very well thus far in her young career, but there was some concern about whether she was ready to handle the superior defensive skills of Jennifer Metcalf. No worry was needed, though, as Stano simply overwhelmed Metcalf with powerful, precise strikes. The win means Stano will likely be up against much more experienced competition next time out. Knowing her work ethic and drive to win, there's no question she'll be ready.

A nice performance by Allira Cohrs tonight, but another overprotective referee robbed the fans of the chance to see more. A minor cut to opponent Khloe Kardashian was enough to get referee Aubrey O'Day to call a stop to things. I have no problem with protecting a fighter from serious injury. But a small cut on a fighter's chin does not exactly seem serious, nor does it seem dangerous. It's a fight. The women are going to bleed, sometimes. I think we're gonna have to learn to just let it go.

Both women have shown they are willing to do whatever it takes to win, and these matches have been pretty brutal and bloody in the past. I don't expect much to change this time around.
