Frustration

Simply put, things have not been going Stephanie McMahon's way, recently. And tonight, despite all her work and preparation, she just didn't see the results we expected in her title matchup with Veronika Zemanova. When asked to comment afterward, a clearly disgusted Stephanie had nothing to say. Her team, meanwhile, is left scratching their heads. I guess things just don't always go the way you think they will.

On the other hand, everything seems to be going right for Marzia Prince. After suffering a total mismatch in the recent tournament, she bounced back perfectly with a dominating victory over Emily Deschanel. Marzia's boxing skills are clearly second to none, and her clinch striking is quite a sight to behold. She'll need to round out her game as she progresses, but her natural talents seem to be more than enough to get her MMA career started in a big way.

The UMMA continues it's tour of Mother Russia, as we prepare for the upcoming event in Moscow. Danneel Harris will put her Welterweight title on the line against Kristanna Loken. These two have fought some close battles recently, and Kristanna has been calling for a chance to get her hands on the belt. Danni, however, has been attacking her training with a renewed sense of urgency. There's no doubt she'll be ready when the bell rings. In other action, Britney Spears will take on Elisha Cuthbert in an all RSI matchup. Again. And, we all know how that always fills my heart with joy. Lastly, rookie sensation Holly Bricken will try to continue her perfect start as she faces off against former WWE Diva Maria Kanellis, who will be making her UMMA debut. Until then, do svidaniya.
