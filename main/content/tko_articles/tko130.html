One Win Situation

It's been a long time since we had a night like this.

With tough competition across the board, I was expecting the women of RSI to struggle tonight. But what happened here in Miami still came as quite a shock. A nauseating, vomitous shock.

Yes, I know nauseating and vomitous mean the same thing. Shut up.

First off, this was supposed to be Alyson Michalka's big night. With Aly on a roll and Brooklyn Decker moved on to the ESL, Aly was finally going to get her hands on the divisional title she's been craving.

Too bad Erin Heatherton had other ideas.

Aly and Erin are very familiar with each other, at this point. In their more recent fights, neither has proven herself to have the clear edge. So, with Aly coming in having won eight of her last nine fights and Erin having dropped five of her last nine, it seemed The Aly Cats team leader was primed for success. But Erin came on strong from the opening bell, and before Aly knew it, her big chance was gone.

Once upon a time, a loss like this would have sent Michalka into a weeks long funk. That's a tough way to be when you're constantly facing fighters with Erin and Brooklyn's talent. Unsurprisingly, it was only after Aly learned to just roll with the punches that she really started to excel, and her frank reaction tonight was further proof that the Aly of old is long gone.

"Fucking bitch," she mused, shaking her head and wearing a thin smile that revealed she wasn't really angry at Heatherton.

Truth is, Aly knows tonight's result wasn't all that shocking.

"Everyone knows what Erin can do," she said. "I never go in that cage with her simply expecting to win. I know I'm gonna have to earn it. Tonight, I just didn't."

I'm sure Aly's demeanor is helped by a number of things. She's proven herself against the best of the best these past couple of years, and her confidence is right where it needs to be. She's also shown she's got what it takes to lead a team of her peers in the team competition... a position she was drafted into by some of those same peers. But perhaps most importantly, she knows she's going to get a few more shots at Heatherton before being swallowed up by the rankings. So, back to work, blondie... and let's see if we can pull off a win next time.

Jennifer Lawrence hasn't had the greatest career, so far. She certainly hasn't been bad, but it seems like every time she flashes from brilliance, her next fight is a dud. With a history like that, even she was surprised when the franchise realignment suddenly pulled her into title contention. Actually, maybe I should say she was slightly panicked.

"When they told me, I was like... 'Are you fucking kidding?'" said the plain-spoken actress. "A title shot? I can barely keep my head above water."

We helped her calm down and see it all for what it was. The shift had cleared the more experienced fighters out of the top of the division, leaving J-Law and the similarly experienced Debby Ryan in the top two slots in the division. She still didn't feel quite ready, but at least she realized she wasn't going to have to face off against the likes of Doutzen Kroes.

Well, Debby may not be Doutzen, but the end result still wasn't very good for Jen.

Actually, that's not really true. Jen was solid tonight, and she fought an intelligent fight. This one was far less about Lawrence's failings than it was about Ryan's interesting strategy... which amounted to pure volume. She didn't focus on accuracy or on combining attacks or on anything remotely technical. Her plan from the outset was to unleash a constant barrage of strikes in an attempt to overwhelm Jen and wear her down. Jen toughed it out... and I'm betting it took Debby quite a bit longer than she'd hoped... but the game plan eventually paid off.

Not the result we wanted, but Jen was pretty level-headed about things when the fight was over.

"Sure, I'm disappointed," she said. "It was a big opportunity for me, and it would have been nice to get a hold of the title so early. But I really don't feel like I'm there, yet. I feel like my game is improving, but I haven't had the results. I don't feel confident enough to call myself a title contender."

Well, I have a real confidence booster for you. I felt the need to track Jen down after she was done with the medical staff to remind her that she's got the team event coming up. You see, The Aly Cats have an extremely difficult matchup with the ILF Runway team. And who did Jen draw?

"DOUTZEN KROES?!!!" exclaimed The Hunger Games star. "ARE YOU PEOPLE FUCKING CRAZY?!!"

After that, it was back to the treatment room for Jen... for some much needed oxygen.

The bad news for RSI didn't stop with the title matches. Featherweight champ Emma Watson faced an all new opponent of her own here in Miami. New rivalries are a common sight since the franchise shift, that really wasn't supposed to happen too much in the FAC. While the other franchises all had experienced fighters moving in to add to and replace the ones they previously had, the FAC is the spot where out was the only direction of movement. But a weight class change for Hellfire's Victoria Justice meant Emma had to contend with an experienced divisional opponent for the first time in a long time.

The results were not encouraging.

As with Jen, the end doesn't tell the whole story. Emma was her usual self tonight, and she established firm control of the action almost immediately. But being in control only means you're executing your game plan while stymying your opponent. That's good... in fact, it's great. But it doesn't mean you're not going to take your share of pain. So, Victoria may have been in trouble, but she did what every fighter does in that situation... scrambled like hell. She stayed focused, and saw her chance after Emma missed the mark on a kick. Her counter landed perfectly... cracking into Emma's chin like they were demonstrating exactly what an uppercut should look like. Just like that, Emma fell victim to every fighter's nightmare... the one punch knockout.

"Well, we know she can throw a punch," Watson said afterward. "I've already seen the video, and... wow!"

Hey... Watson. This isn't a movie screening. You just got your ass knocked out. Shouldn't you be a little more depressed?

"I don't like losing," admitted the soon-to-be Belle, "but I'd rather risk it all against a tough opponent than get an easy win."

When asked if she was looking forward to a rematch, Emma didn't have to think about her answer.

"I'm looking forward to MANY rematches," she said. "I know Victoria struggled when she was starting out, but she's been tearing through most of her opponents these past couple of years. She pushed Brooklyn all the way to a decision back at the Maxim tournament. She beat Roselyn. I don't care what the rankings say... she knows how to fight. It's going to be interesting."

Interesting, indeed.

Three fights. Three losses. This is getting ugly. And we haven't even gotten to the ugliest part.

Losing to Audrey Allen isn't the end of the world for Shelby Chesnes. Shelbs has certainly been solid, but Audrey's early career has been nothing short of sensational. So, this was going to be a tough one, and I'm pretty sure she was prepared to file away a loss under the heading of 'Learning Experience.'

What she was not prepared for was spending a few months on the shelf.

This one ended in the worst way possible when a hook from Allen not only ended the fight, but it also gave Chesnes a couple of cracked ribs for her trouble.

When I stopped in to check on her... after Dr. Leone had completed her duties... Shelby wasn't in the mood for a lot of talk. I tried to encourage her by telling her she'd be back on her feet and in the cage again before she knew it.

"That's what Dragon said, too," she replied.

Uh... what the hell was Dragon doing there?

"He brought those," Shelby explained, gesturing toward a glass vase jam-packed with roses and baby's breath.

I looked toward the flowers, then back to Shelby. I paused, did the back and forth again, then shrugged.

Well, at least Elisha will be happy to hear Dragon's found other pursuits.

Meanwhile... is RSI going to get any GOOD news tonight?

Thankfully, Dianna Dahlgren stepped up and saved us from total disaster. Facing off against the tough but struggling Emma Kuziara, Dianna worked the fight to perfection... patiently staying with the defensive game plan she and Steph had worked out. Though I think maybe she was a little TOO patient, her defense was rock solid, allowing her to grind Kuziara down and get the win.

I may give her a bonus, just for salvaging the night.

A quick personnel note: RSI is pleased to welcome aboard bantamweight Olivia Holt. Olivia and I have a little history together, which explains why she's not talking to me, right now. But I have a feeling she'll come around.

Well, that's the merciful end for this event. The team event is next, and RSI has officially gone crazy. As if the looming battle between The Aly Cats and ILF Runway weren't enough... ladies and gentlemen, we have civil war on our hands. Because my life is not nearly interesting enough, Britney's Brutal Beauties will face of against none other than Leesh's Lethal Ladies. While everyone else is dying to see how this one turns out, I'm literally just dying.

I get to follow up a disastrous event like this one with an event that will feature a team matchup where half of my fighters are basically guaranteed to lose?

I'm going back to Disney World. This shit blows.
