And The Crowd Went Wild

The kick was so fast, many didn't even see it. The impact snapped the champion's head around viciously... her eyes rolling as she lost consciousness. Her body stiffened, then seemed to collapse in sections as she fell woodenly to the canvas. The challenger stayed ready... her fists up high... waiting for another brutal blow from her nemesis. Slowly, she began to comprehend what had happened. Her opponent was down. The referee was there, frantically waving her away as she signaled for the medical staff. The adrenaline started to subside, and reality came creeping in.

That's when she heard them.

"Em-uh-lee! Em-uh-lee!"

The chant was deafening... the crowd so frenzied, it felt like the whole room was shaking.

"Em-uh-lee! Em-uh-lee!"

She blinked in disbelief. Had she really done it? Had she finally beaten the champion?

You're god-damn right, she did.

Go ahead. Try it. I dare you. Just try to wipe that smile off of Emily VanCamp's face. You've got no shot. That's a smile that comes from hours and hours of training finally paying off. That's a smile that comes from achieving your goal after spending months patiently reminding yourself that you CAN do it.

That, ladies and gentlemen, is the smile that comes from defeating a legend.

After two historic battles that did not go her way, Em finally took down champion Irina Sheik. And, though this fight was quite a bit shorter than the earlier ones, it was, once again, a war. These two warriors gave everything they had tonight, unleashing blow after crushing blow. Within just a few seconds, it was clear that the first to sneak past her opponent's defense would be the one to land the killer shot. And, tonight, that shot came from VanCamp's right foot.

As Emily emerged from the locker room afterward, she was immediately mobbed by her teammates. Hugs and smiles were shared all around. But, as she shared an embrace with RSI's team leader, Danni Harris whispered something in her ear that brought the newly crowned champion to tears. When questioned about what was said, Emily was tight-lipped. "It was just a private moment between Danni and I," she said with a soft smile. "We'll leave it at that." On the topic of her new championship, however, VanCamp had plenty to say. "It feels awesome. There's really no other way to say it. I was proud of the way I fought the last couple of times. But, that feeling was not even close to the feeling of actually winning this thing," gushed the slender actress as she set her hand down upon her hard won prize. "This is special."

Emily also had plenty to say about her opponent. "A lot of people are already talking about Irina in the past tense. And, I gotta be honest... it's the most ridiculous thing I've ever heard. She may be the greatest champion the UMMA has ever seen. She's not going anywhere." Em also gave Irina credit for making her a favorite with the fans. "I love my fans. But, I think part of the reason I have them is that they see me as a legend killer," continued VanCamp. "I don't know about that so much, but I'll say this: In order to be a 'legend killer,' you have to have a legend to kill. And Irina is, without a doubt, a legend." Em was also asked about the possibility of a rematch with the now former champion. "Geez... would you let me have the thing for five minutes before you start asking when Irina might try to take it back?" laughed the Revenge star. But, after a moment, she addressed the idea in no uncertain terms. "I'll fight her, anytime. These last three matches have been the best I've ever had. A real fighter wants to be tested, and there is no greater test than Irina."

Tough and disciplined. Resourceful and respectful. There is nothing not to like about Emily VanCamp. She may not be the icon that Irina is. And, it's unlikely she will have the kind of reign Irina had. But, she is absolutely worthy of the belt that is now wrapped around her waist. And, as I've said before, she is no fluke. Irina will be back, and Emily will be ready. The war will rage on. I simply cannot wait.

Congratulations, Em. No one deserves this more than you.

I think it's safe to say that Daniela Hantuchova is back and better than ever, as she reclaimed her Light Heavyweight crown in dramatic fashion. The tennis star was so focused and determined that Olivia Wilde never knew what hit her. Daniela opened this one with a crushing right hook that landed flush on Olivia's chin, and the Tron actress was immediately in survival mode. While Olivia scrambled to recover, Daniela wailed away, landing blow after unanswered blow until Wilde collapsed in a heap.

When it was over, there was simply no hiding the smile as the UMMA president returned the belt to Hantuchova. And Daniela freely admitted she'd missed it while it was gone. "When I have belt," began the Slovakian superstar, "I feel as thought it is mine, alone. It is my job to protect it. When I fail, I feel as though someone has stolen from me. So, I am very happy to have it back." Well, we're happy she has it back, too.

Speaking of smiles that couldn't be hidden, the normally reserved (and, let's face it, angry) Alyson Michalka was practically beaming as she celebrated the victory with her new training partner. "I had no doubt she would win tonight," said the former Disney starlet. "She's been so intense and focused in our training sessions, it can be scary. I knew she was ready for whatever Olivia threw at her." Truth be told, it seems this new partnership is working out even better than I'd hoped. When asked how training with Daniela has changed things, Aly seemed as excited as I've ever seen her. "Having Daniela here has been awesome! Every training session is brutal and competitive. I leave here each day knowing I improved as a fighter." But, as with another infamous RSI pairing, the competition stops at the cage door. "I have a ton of respect for Daniela, both as a fighter and as a person," continued Michalka. "We've made it clear to each other that we're in this together." "Yes," agreed Hantuchova. "Alyson is very good fighter and very good friend. I am proud to stand behind her."

And, we're proud to have you on board, Daniela. Congratulations on getting your title back.

With Emily now having attained the championship that always seemed just beyond her grasp, RSI finds itself in need of another fighter to take up the role of the long-suffering runner up. Well, tonight, Tiffany Mulheron made it clear tonight that she wants to apply for the job. The last time Tiffany faced Nadine Coyle, it was a totally one-sided victory for the champion. But, this time, it was a war.

By the time it was over, the women had beaten each other almost beyond recognition. Both corners definitely need to give their cutman a nice bonus for the work they put into this one. The women took turns brutalizing each other, and neither gave an inch. Both were bloodied before the end of the first round, and neither seemed to care. Punches, kicks, knees, elbows... these women threw everything they had at each other. The pounding seemed to take it's toll in the third, however, as Tiffany started to run out of steam. Nadine took advantage, controlling most of the round to make the final decision easier for the judges. But, overall, this was a fantastic battle, and we can only hope it's the beginning of another great UMMA rivalry.

While most fighters would be disappointed at missing the chance to win the title, Tiffany seemed pretty upbeat afterward. "Most people wondered if I could even compete with Nadine," said the heavily bruised and bandaged Mulheron. "I have to admit, after what happened last time, I had some doubts, too." As the fight went on, though, the questions started to disappear. "After a few exchanges, I realized she was hitting me with all she had, and I was still there. She was hurting me, for sure, but I was hurting her, too. I realized I could do this. In fact, I started to really enjoy it." I'm sorry... you ENJOYED getting your face punched in? "Yeah," laughed the blonde. "It's a fight... you're gonna get hit. But taking a punch from the champion and then hitting back with one of my own? That felt great." I love the attitude, Tiff. And, I'm excited about the possibilities I see in your future. But, next time, let's hit more, and get hit less.

Another win for the scrappy and determined Ashley Tisdale, and, once again, it came at Lady Gaga's expense. Though Ashley is now 3-0 against Gaga, the unpredictable singer has bounced back with a couple of victories to keep pace with Tisdale in the rankings. Not that the repetition bothers Ashley. "I'll fight whoever they want," said a confident Tisdale. "To get to the top, I'll have to beat them all, right? So, I don't worry much about it. I just concentrate on getting the win." Certainly a common sense approach. Still, even Ashley had to admit she knows what's coming. "I think the fans want to see a rematch between me and Alizee Jacotey. And, I think that UMMA management is going to make it happen, sooner or later." Indeed, Tisdale and her French nemesis have taken turns beating the living hell out of each other in their last two meetings. A rubber match certainly can't be far off.

When I introduced Amanda Seyfried as a member of the RSI family, I mentioned she'd been struggling, recently. Once the top contender for the ESL Welterweight crown, Amanda has been slowly slipping down the rankings over the past few months. She was desperate to turn things around, and the idea of training with RSI team leader and DEF champion Danneel Harris was certainly appealing. The plan sounded good to Danni, too, as she had previously bemoaned the lack of an experienced teammate to train with. Well, I'm glad everybody's happy, because the plan sure seems to be working. Amanda looked sharp and confident as she destroyed opponent Holly Valance here, tonight.

After losing her last couple of matchups, Amanda was happy to get back to her winning ways. "It felt good, for sure," said the actress. "Not just winning, but being in command of the fight. I felt relaxed and confident, which is exactly where I want to be." Certainly the kind of thing we want to hear, and certainly the kind of result we were hoping for from Amanda.

Well, it had to happen sometime, right? Since signing with RSI, Krystal Forscutt has literally reversed her fortunes, putting together a string of divisional victories that most did not think she was capable of. She has been impressive, to say the least, and it's been a great story. But, tonight against Kaley Cuoco, she looked... well, terrible. I guess that's not entirely true. Krystal tried to execute her strategy, but Kaley was just simply the better fighter in this one. And, no one knows it better than Krystal. "I couldn't hit her," lamented the Aussie in her post fight presser. "She was really on it, tonight. It seemed like she couldn't do anything wrong, and I just couldn't get anything right." I think that about sums it up, to be honest. Kaley had a great night, and Krystal was simply off her game. It happens.

A disappointing loss for Allira Cohrs tonight, as Kat Dennings sought a measure of revenge against the RSI rookie. Kat smothered Allira, not allowing the rookie to utilize her strong striking game. Allira did well, scoring repeatedly from the clinch, but she wasn't focused enough on defending herself. Kat scored an easy takedown, and that was pretty much the end of it. Allira fought back with everything she had, but her ground game is nowhere near that of Dennings, and there was just too much time left in the round for Cohrs to hang on. So, a tough loss, but not a disaster. Allira still shows the raw skills she needs to succeed. She just needs the time and experience to put those skills to work for her.

Jennifer Stano had dreams of making a big splash at the Rookie Gut Check tournament a month ago. And, after a couple of dominating early victories, she was convinced she could make those dreams reality. Then, the whole thing went up in smoke as Bianca Balti battled her way to a unanimous decision win over our girl. Clearly unhappy afterward, Jennifer returned to the gym, determined to take her game to the next level. And, it looks to me like things are going pretty well. Tonight, for nearly five full minutes, this one was The Jennifer Stano Show. And, the role of the helpless victim was played by the very unlucky Samantha Mumba. To put it bluntly, Jennifer was perfect, crushing Mumba both at distance and from the clinch. Samantha tried gamely to respond, but there was just nothing she could do. Jennifer was just too much.

Her success at the rookie tournament bumped Jen up the rankings a little faster than we'd expected. Her victory tonight will move her into contention with some of the most accomplished fighters in her division. Will it be too much, too soon? It's hard to say. Though Stano is still very new, she's shown she's capable of competing with just about anyone. It will be interesting to see how she handles the more experienced opponents.

It's off to London for our next event, and I'm going to have to hire a writing staff to prepare the press release for this one. RSI has a total of 10 athletes in action, including title defenses for Julianne Hough and Emma Watson. Sounds like I may be exploring the power of brevity in my next report. We'll see you then.
