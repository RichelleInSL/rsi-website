Cats On The Prowl

The RSI teams had a night for the record books here in Adelaide, including one that achieved a feat most would say was impossible. Team leaders Britney Spears, Elisha Cuthbert and Alyson Michalka are here with me. They're supposed to help me make sense of things, but instead they have wreaked havoc upon the process. Each was supposed to bring along her choice for Fighter of the Night, but Aly couldn't choose. She wanted to bring three fighters with her. That led to Britney asking if she could bring more, and the whole thing devolved into predictable chaos. The end result? Everyone is here. Everyone. I am surrounded by twenty-four fighters, seated at a table so large that the opposing ends are in different time zones. Among the group are RSI leader Danneel Harris and fighter development coordinator Stephanie McMahon. The two I am most counting on to help me keep this thing under control. Do you think it will work? Me neither, but here goes...

Richelle Winterfeld: No point in beating around the bush on this one. This event was a tremendous success for RSI, up and down the board. I have never been as proud of all of you as I am, right now. Leesh, that means you need to pick your head up right now.

Elisha Cuthbert: I appreciate you trying to help, but...

Richelle: I know. You dropped another tough one - to former stablemate Anna Silk, this time. But you used to train together. She knows you.

Elisha: I should know her, too.

Richelle: (shrugs) Somebody has to lose. It just wasn't your night.

Elisha: That seems to happen to me a lot.

Richelle: (sighs as she motions to Britney) Can you fix this?

Britney Spears: Eventually. Not right now, though.

Richelle: OK, we'll come back to it. But Alyson... I really have no choice but to lead this one off with your group. Many of the teams are made up of fighters of mixed experience, and you guys are no exception to that. With three relatively new fighters on your roster, things are always a grind for you guys. It goes without saying that I have been impressed with what you guys have accomplished from the very beginning. But nothing... absolutely nothing... could have prepared me for this. On a night when you were up against one of the most experienced teams in the competition, you accomplished the rare feat of absolute perfection. So, don't take this the wrong way, but... how in the hell did you do this?

Alyson Michalka: Wasn't me. I just did my part. Ash, Emma, Danni, J-Law... they all did their thing against tough opponents, too. But the key to victory was these three ladies, right here.

(Aly gestures to the three women to her right - Brittany Tacy, Marzia Prince and Allira Cohrs)

Richelle: Well, what of it, ladies? Each of you had to face an opponent with more than triple your experience, yet all three of you not only won but did so convincingly. What was the secret to this astonishing result?

(The three women exchange shrugs and unsure looks until Marzia takes the initiative)

Marzia Prince: Well, for me, I think it was fear of death.

Brittany Tacy: (laughing) I think that's right. I think we all just worried about staying in one piece.

Allira Cohrs: (nods) All I could think about was keeping Uma in front of me and keeping my guard up.

Richelle: Survival is a powerful motivator, I'm sure. But you guys didn't exactly look afraid out there. Any insights, Ms. McMahon?

Stephanie McMahon: Normally, a fight like this... you'll see the younger fighter come in keyed up. Like she knows she has to be perfect, and she's rehearsing all her moves in her head. But they weren't like that, at all. The came in loose, and they stayed that way. Tacy and Cohrs played close attention during the breaks, and they did what I asked. Prince didn't even get that far. The point is, they didn't let the moment overtake them. All three acted like they had nothing to lose and everything to gain.

Richelle: These were huge fights for you guys. How'd you stay so relaxed?

Allira: That was Aly.

Richelle: Oh?

Marzia: Yep. She's been working on us for weeks.

Richelle: Working on you, how?

Marzia: 'Fight your way, not hers.'

Brittany: 'Keep the fight in front of you.'

Allira: 'Stay smart, and take what she gives you.'

Richelle: (grinning at Aly) You said those things?

Aly: (shrugs) I didn't want them thinking too much.

Brittany: She wouldn't even let us watch film.

Richelle: That true?

Aly: (shrugs again) There was no point. They couldn't game plan for everything their opponents could do. It was better just to focus on making themselves better.

Stephanie: Which is why they didn't feel the need to be perfect. They just went out there and did their thing.

Richelle: What are you trying to say, Steph?

Stephanie: I'm saying the one who was perfect was Aly. She knew what her fighters could do, and what they couldn't. She kept them focused on the could, and she didn't let them dwell on the other stuff. They were as prepared as they could be for tonight. Both physically and mentally.

Richelle: (Turns to Danni in mild shock.) Did you hear that?

Danneel Harris: I did.

Richelle: You ever hear something like that from her before?

Danni: Never.

Richelle: You hear that, Aly? Never.

Aly: I am truly speechless.

Stephanie: You deserve it. These three matchups... the odds were not in your favor. But you pulled it off.

Danni: I agree. You kept your fighters focused and positive at a time when it was easy to slip into a panic.

Richelle: Well, I'm proud of all of you. Brittany, Marzia, Allira... what you did was amazing. I never want to sell a fighter short, but this was beyond anything I could have imagined. But let's not overlook the rest. Tiz, Danni, Jen, Aly... these were not easy fights for any of you, but you all came ready to...

Emma Watson: Pardon me, but aren't you forgetting someone?

Richelle: Yeah, yeah. You were good, too.

Emma: Nice. That's real nice.

Richelle: OK Brit, you're gang is up. Only one loss for you guys, so Kelly, we'll start with you against Kate Lawler.

Kelly Hu: It's a tough one to swallow. I was expecting a war with Kate, and that's what I was getting. Then she caught me with that right, and the blood was too much for the ref to take. I think it was crap. I mean, stopping it for a cut nose? How often does that happen? But whatever... it's just disappointing. I trained a long time to be ready, and I would have liked the chance to fight it out with her.

Richelle: I hear you, but, honestly, the nose looked pretty bad at the time. And it means you'll have to sit out the upcoming tournament.

Kelly: True, but I'm still more bummed about tonight. My last fight with Kate was great, but it ended pretty shitty. I was looking forward to getting another shot at her.

Danni: Yeah... I know you were. I'm sure this won't be your last chance, though.

Richelle: No doubt. In the meantime, why don't we run through the rest of the fights? Kristen... Annie Short isn't all that experienced, but she hung in there pretty well.

Kristen Bell: RWB is known for being tough, and full of surprises. I was just glad to get the win.

Richelle: Emily, Pippa Middleton may be near-royalty, but she's certainly not soft in the cage.

Emily VanCamp: Not at all. She put up quite a fight. I'm happy with the victory, but I feel bad about the way it ended.

Stephanie: Word is that her leg isn't too serious. She's gonna need some time, though.

Danni: Steph, how is it you know everything? Where do you get the 'word' from? It's not even our stable.

Stephanie: It's my job to know what's going on.

Richelle: And you do it quite well... even if a bit mysteriously. Amanda... Chloe Saxon gave you all you could handle, with nearly a full round spent on the ground.

Amanda Seyfried: Not my favorite place to be.

Stephanie: And yet, you turned it around on her every time. You're ground game is much better than you give yourself credit for.

Richelle: (Gives Steph a sideways glance) Alright, what is going on with you? I've never seen you this nice.

Stephanie: I don't think I've ever been in this good a mood before.

Danni: Yeah. Things went so well, she has hardly anything to complain about.

Richelle: This is unheard of. I don't even know what to say.

Stephanie: Just try not to ruin it.

Richelle: Fair enough. Well Mandy, Steph is right. Chloe was able to get what she wanted a few times, but it did her no good. And you did more than just tie her up, too. You did a little damage on the canvas.

Amanda: I was tired of my ground work being such a big weakness, so I've been working on it.

Richelle: Well, it certainly paid off. That brings us to you and Jennifer Metcalf, Brit.

Britney: Not much to say. I felt good going in, and everything just clicked. Some nights, you just feel unbeatable.

Richelle: True enough. Looked like you were feeling pretty good too, Krystal. You certainly put on a display against Jodie Gasson.

Krystal Forscutt: Jodie's situation is a lot like mine. She had her ups and downs. But when she gets going, she can rip your head off. I wanted to keep her off balance, and luckily I was able to do that.

Richelle: It looked like more than luck to me. Mac, that brings us to you. Keely Hazell hasn't had much success against the better fighters, and she didn't fare too well against you, either.

Catherine Bell: She's tough as nails, though. I mean, I beat the hell out of her, and she just kept coming. I have a lot of respect for that. Things may not come easily to her, but you can't say it's because she doesn't try.

Richelle: I agree. The way she kept getting back up was unbelievable. Well last, but never least, Ms. Stephanie. With Alice Goodwin sidelined, we didn't know until late who were going to draw. Getting Abi Titmuss was probably not a pleasant surprise, but it didn't seem to phase you, at all.

Danni: Yeah, Steph. You seemed a little... motivated.

Stephanie: Been doing a lot of losing, lately. So, it's possible I may have been a little bit pissed off, yeah.

Richelle: Emotion is usually a problem in that cage. However, it seemed Abi was the one with all the problems in this one.

Danni: Well, that's what happens when Steph gets pissed.

Stephanie: That had nothing to do with it. Abi is not someone you fuck around with. When I saw my chance, I took it. Yeah, it got bad for her, but she's been around long enough to know that's how it goes, sometimes. Last time we met, she dropped me like a sack of meat within seconds. So, she knows.

Richelle: (to Danni) Like a sack of meat, she says.

Danni: Her imaginative descriptions are almost like poetry.

Steph: OK, remember what I said? You're ruining it.

Richelle: Leesh, that brings us back to you. I know you had a tough night personally, but, as a whole, you're team was pretty good.

Elisha: They did. Which was good, since some of us couldn't pull our own weight.

Richelle: Of course, success should be expected against a younger team like the one you faced.

Tiffany Mulheron: Hey, wait a second. That's not fair.

Daniela Hantuchova: This is not right way to see it. They are always tough, these Bunnies.

Richelle: Aww, c'mon. Half the team is inexperienced, and they had to pull Capri Anderson off the bench to fill in for the more seasoned Kloe Kardashian. How tough could they be?

Julianne Hough: Are you kidding? Have you been paying any attention to the way they've been winning? I mean, if the team is so bad, then why are they ranked so highly?

Richelle: (shrugs) They've been feeding on the lower ranked teams.

Petra Cubonova: This is crazy talk. Is because they are good, is why.

Torrie Wilson: Damn right, that's why. Their prep work is second to none. So, you're right... they should be easy to beat. They shouldn't be able to win the way they do. But the Bunnies don't give a shit. They always think they can win.

Richelle: I don't know. I mean, you guys did well, but in the face of what Aly's group did... it feels like you're making a big deal out of nothing.

Roselyn Sanchez: How can you say that? ¿Has perdido la cabeza? ¡Un error, y esas chicas te va a matar! ¡Van a aplastar como a un insecto!

Richelle: OK, OK... there's no need to go all esta loca on me, Roz. I think you guys are making my point for me. I mean, why are you getting so defensive?

Cobie Smulders: Because you're basically attacking us.

Julianne: Yeah... I don't care who we faced. We worked our asses off to be ready.

Richelle: I'm not denying that. I'm just...

Elisha: ENOUGH!

(The conversation halts as all eyes turn to Elisha, who has her focus squarely and intently on Richelle.)

Richelle: Got something to say, Leesh?

Elisha: I'm not gonna let you talk to them this way. They've worked too hard for too long, and I'm not gonna let you trivialize that.

Richelle: (turns to Danni) Wow. 'Trivialize.' Big word.

Danni: Oh, no you don't. Don't you drag me into your little game.

Elisha: (ignoring the banter) You're right. This was one they were supposed to win. And they did. But don't make it sound like it was easy. Training is always a grind, and having to do it for a fight you're supposed to win makes it worse.

Richelle: You're going to whine to me about training, now?

Elisha: I'm not whining. I'm saying don't give me bullshit about easy. Jayde Nicole, Jessica Ashley... those girls have been making a career of winning fights they were supposed to lose.

Richelle: What's your point?

Elisha: That you're turning what my team accomplished into a footnote. I mean, why did they even bother? If they'd lost, it would have been a big disappointment. But they win, and all you can say is they weren't a disappointment?

Richelle: OK... I guess I really hadn't thought about it that way.

Elisha: Damn right, you didn't. And I'm really surprised. Stuff like this... when there's so much risk with so little reward... you usually do a better job of showing the fighters your appreciation.

Richelle: You're right. I'm sorry.

Tiffany: I have to be honest, Leesh... I don't think most of us had that hard a time getting ready.

Daniela: Yes... was not bad. Maybe because is not about us?

Elisha: Not about us?

Richelle: I think maybe Daniela is saying this one was about the team, and not about each of you individually.

Daniela: Yes... this is what I say. Is bigger this way.

Torrie: That's true I think. These team events... I don't focus on what I get out of it. It means more to do my part for the team. I don't care if people are impressed by my performance.

Julianne: Plus, you know your teammates are going to appreciate it when you win.

Petra: And be there when you lose, ya?

Britney: (nudges Elisha with her elbow) Is true, ya?

Elisha: Yeah, I guess.

Petra: (shakes her head at Britney) Do not do this. You're accent... it is terrible.

Richelle: It's funny that you guys feel this way. Even though it's a team event, your performances are still individual.

Cobie: That's not really true.

Richelle: How so?

Cobie: I don't know. It just feels different. I mean, yeah... you fight on your own, but you know it's part of something else. That you're part of something else.

Tiffany: Plus, you're not on your own. The whole team is out there. You see them during the round breaks. You hear them when you're fighting.

Richelle: So, you're telling me that while you were battling it out with Capri Anderson... a fighter with almost no experience and not that great of a record... when she was still standing there giving you all she had in that third round, having your teammates behind you made a difference?

Tiffany: Yes.

Richelle: You're a sitting champion. If you had lost that fight, it would have been quite an embarrassment.

Tiffany: Never thought about that. I was just worried about letting the team down.

Richelle: Seems like quite a tight-knit group. And I'm going to say it again... there's no reason why it has to be that way. You each have your own career to worry about. So, I'm wondering how this happened.

Torrie: It's always been like this. When these events come up, we meet as a team. Leesh brings us together to train as a team. We even watch film as a team.

Julianne: Actually, we do everything as a team. We all know who our opponent's are. We know what challenges each one us faces. We even help gameplan for each other.

Richelle: Sounds like quite a setup. (focuses on Elisha) Especially coming from someone who failed to pull her weight.

Tiffany: Ohhh... now I see what you're up to.

Elisha: Nice try. But you know what I was talking about.

Richelle: Ohhh, ladies... I think your leader needs a hug.

Torrie: (starts to rise) Awww... poor Leesh.

Elisha: No. No, no... I'm fine.

(The 7 members of Elisha's team get up from various points around the table and gravitate towards Elisha)

Elisha: Awww, c'mon... don't...

(Tiffany arrives first and loops her arms around Elisha's neck from behind. The others gather quickly, draping their arms around each other until the team is assembled en masse around their leader. Elisha puts on an exaggerated look of defeated surrender which does nothing to hide the little smile that has crept onto her face)

Richelle: You've been having a tough time, Leesh. Losing sucks, no two ways about it. But everyone in this room knows how it feels. In the meantime, you are the center of this team. You pull your weight and then some. So, enough of that shit, OK?

Elisha: If I say yes, will you get them off of me?

(The group laughs, then the women slowly head back to their seats.)

Richelle: Alright, ladies... Operation Snap Leesh Out of It was a success, but it took up a lot of time. So, instead of going through the fights in detail, just give me one word each to describe your opponent for the evening.

Julianne: Unstoppable.

Tiffany: Tenacious.

Roselyn: En fuego.

Cobie: Fast.

Elisha: Mistake.

Richelle: I assume you're aiming that one at me for letting her go. And I admit, you may well be right. How about you, Daniela?

Daniela: Confidence.

Petra: Future.

Richelle: Future?

Petra: Yes. I believe she will be much better, in future.

Richelle: Ah... so, like... 'future superstar.' I got it. And you, Torrie?

Torrie: Stupid.

Richelle: I'm sorry... I think you're gonna have to explain that one.

Torrie: As in, she's too stupid to know she shouldn't be able to do the things she's been doing.

Steph: I warned you about that. Ashley tore it up all of last year. Then, she opens this year with probably the biggest win of her career. So, not only does she have the skills, but she came in with all the confidence in the world. She doesn't give a shit what she should or shouldn't be able to do. A young fighter like that given the chance to knock off a sitting champion? I told you she was gonna make you earn it.

Torrie: She sure did. Jessica pushed the fight deep into the third round, and never showed an ounce of fear. She was really taking it to me up until those last few minutes. I was a mess by the time it was over.

Richelle: But a happy mess. And Trish and the medical team seem to have patched you up pretty good.

Torrie: They're good at what they do. Plus, winning does a lot to dull the pain.

Richelle: Well, a job well done, ladies. All the way around, I'm a very happy owner today. Meanwhile, that's all for this event. Next up is the Red Carpet tournament, where RSI has twenty-three fighters seeded. With the likes of Emily VanCamp, Tiffany Mulheron and Danni Harris...

Emma: And me.

Richelle: You've never won one of these things.

Emma: Then I'm due.

Tiffany: (with a wry smile) Unless your girlfriend flattens you, again.

Emma: Shut up.

Richelle: OK, then... with Emily, Danni, Tiff, Aly... RSI has quite a list of fighters capable of bringing home a title. And there's always at least one crazy result at these tournaments, so who knows? Maybe Emma can win one, too.

Emma: Now see, why can't you be nicer to me?

Richelle: Because annoying the shit out of you is so much fun?

Emma: That's just not right. How would you feel if I treated you this way?

Richelle: Uhm... you do treat me this way.

Emma: Oh, yeah... that's right. Well, carry on, then.

Richelle: That's all for this one, folks. We'll see you next time in Perth, as the UMMA's tour of the Land Down Under continues.
