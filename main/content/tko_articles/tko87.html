And A Cherry On Top

Julianne Hough has been having a run that most fighters only dream about. After getting absolutely annihilated by Keira Knightley back at the Hit the Beach tournament, she was left a broken fighter. With only 12 wins in 29 fights, Julianne was seriously considering just giving up. Since then, she has been nothing short of perfect. She has taken home the bantamweight crown from both the Blood on the Red Carpet and Lingerie Invitational tournaments. She has beaten some of the best fighters in her weight class, including Knightley. She has rattled off an impressive 13 straight victories. And, tonight, with the crown jewel on the line, she made it 14.

Ladies and gentlemen, allow me to introduce your Red Carpet, Lingerie and, now, FAC Bantamweight Champion, Julianne Hough.

During the course of this run, Julianne has fought just about every type of match you can think of. She's won in dramatic, one punch, fashion. She's won battles of strategy and technique. But, she has never experienced a fight like the one she had tonight. The match lasted a little under three minutes from start to finish. And, in that short span, both women were savagely bruised and bloodied.

This is the third time Julianne has faced Vikki Blows during this run. Julianne was dominant in both of their previous meetings. But, she knew right away that this one would be different.

"Vikki was a very different fighter, tonight. She attacked as soon as the bell sounded, and the look on her face was just intense concentration. She was like a wild animal."

Indeed, it was recently announced that Vikki had signed on with RWB, and it seems clear she's all-in with the RWB approach in the cage.

"The RWB strategy is simple: attack, attack, attack," said Jules. "They'll hit you anywhere, with any part of their body. They'll win by any means necessary. And, they love to make you bleed."

Hmmmm. For someone who's never faced an RWB fighter before in her life, Julianne sure seems to know a lot about how they fight. Care to share the source of your wisdom?

"I'm glad you asked" said Hough, a sly smirk appearing on her face as she motioned for RSI team leader, Danneel Harris, to come join her at the table.

Yes, indeed. The day after RWB announced the signing, Danni walked into my office and said simply, "Julianne's in big trouble."

I'll be honest... she had to work pretty hard to convince me. With only a short time to go before this event, I didn't believe the signing could have much impact. Danni believed differently. Having faced RWB juggernaut Cheryl Cole a few times, she knew the stable's philosophy and she was adamant that Jules needed to be prepared.

I still wasn't sure, but I also understood that this is why Danni is here. I don't need her to agree with me all the time. I need her to tell me what's what. Especially when I'm off the mark.

Once I relented, the question that remained was what to do. How could we get Jules ready in such a short period of time? But, Danni had come with a plan.

An hour later, Julianne showed up to the gym expecting to find training partner, Ashley Tisdale, waiting for her.

Instead, she found Danneel Harris.

Needless to say, Hough was more than a little surprised.

"Danni tells me I'll be training with her to get ready for my next fight. All I could think was, 'What did I do wrong?'"

After Danni explained the situation, Julianne quickly came on board.

"I didn't understand what the big deal was, but I figured training with Danni was only going to help me. So, I was all for it."

And how did the training go?

"I thought she hated me," laughed Julianne. "She was absolutely ruthless from the very start. I thought maybe she didn't want me to win the belt, so she was just going to kill me."

Crushing Julianne's confidence was part of Danni's plan.

"She'd faced Vikki a couple of times recently, so she thought she was ready," said Harris. "I needed her to understand that she wasn't."

Danni pounded her teammate in every way imagineable, and even a few ways you probably wouldn't think of.

"I went from thinking I was one of the best to thinking I had no idea what I was doing," admitted Julianne. "It was like starting over."

Well, Julianne must be a fast learner, because she sure looked like she knew what she was doing, tonight. And, she gave full credit to Danni for the win.

"As soon as the fight started, Vikki caught me clean with a right hand. I didn't need to check... I knew I was bleeding."

And her response?

"I just smiled at her," said the blonde, a wicked grin spreading across her face. "That was the hardest punch I'd ever taken from Vikki, and it was nothing compared with getting pounded by Danni."

Indeed, her prep work with Danni allowed her to shrug off Vikki's attack and respond with an uppercut that literally knocked the champion off her feet. And, that's when the new Vikki got to meet the new Julianne. Viciously stomping her downed opponent, Julianne served notice that she could play at RWB's game. A minute or so later, it was all over and a new champion had been crowned.

While Julianne is certainly enjoying her fairytale run, she knows it can't go on like this forever. And, she has a suspicion about where her next big challenge will come from.

"I think Vikki's going to come back strong," she said.

True enough. Tonight's loss is not going to sit well with the former champion, and it's not going to sit well with her new management company, either.

For her part, Julianne seems to relish the challenge.

"Being champion shouldn't be easy. I think Vikki and I declared war on each other, tonight. I'm sure there are a lot of brutal battles to come. And, when they do, I'll be ready."

I'm running out of ways to describe what Julianne's run has been like, so I'll just let Danni do the talking.

"Everything about what Julianne is doing is just ridiculous. Her turn-around, her championships, her attitude... she makes me a little sick, to be honest," Harris teased playfully.

But when she got down to her real feelings, there was nothing but admiration for her teammate.

"When this run ends, I will be disappointed," said Harris, turning to face her fellow champion. "Not only as her teammate, but also as a fan. She's generated a level of excitement that will be hard to match. I know she was thinking about quitting. And now, she's the champ."

Danni paused and shook her head in disbelief before adding, "You're not gonna find a better story than that."

From the depths of despair to the top of her sport in just a few short months... even if it ends here, this has been a run for the ages.

Congratulations again, Julianne. I just don't know what else to say.

After Alyson Michalka's big win at the Lingerie Invitational tournament, I made mention of just how difficult it is to succeed in the FAC light heavyweight division. Unfortunately, I was proven right tonight, as Aly suffered a heartbreaking loss to up and comer India Reynolds. Aly has mentioned the danger in facing off against a relentless fighter like India in the past. Last time they met, Michalka was able to weather India's early onslaught and strike back for the win. This time, though, India just kept coming, finally overwhelming Aly a little over a minute into the second round.

With so much competition coming from the ILF fighters at the top of the division, a loss like this one is a major setback. When asked about it, Aly seemed to shrug off the loss.

"I always knew this could happen. It's just the way life is in this division. Everyone is dangerous. There are no sure wins."

That's true, but I'm not buying into the passive resignation. Try again.

"It's tough to take, sure. But, I knew what India was capable of. I can beat her... I know I can. But, like all of the RWB fighters, she takes an all or nothing approach. I slipped up and she got me in trouble early. I just never recovered. I'm still confident in myself, though. I'll shake this off."

Confidence is good, but I still think there's more. C'mon, Aly... let's have it.

"ALRIGHT! I'm fucking PISSED, OK?!!" screamed a clearly frustrated Michalka. "When I'm done bullshitting with you, I'm gonna go back in the locker room and throw a fucking fit. Did I know this could happen? Yeah. India's a great fighter. She's done this to others. Am I OK with it? Fuck, no. I want that title, and a loss like this fuck's up everything. So, when you hear shit breaking in there, don't worry about it. It's just me. Satisfied?"

Not only am I satisfied, I'm just a little bit excited. I realize this loss is hard to take, but sometimes a fighter needs a setback to get focused. I know what Alyson Michalka is capable of. Now, it's time to find out what an angry Aly can do.

Will this be what propels her to the top? I honestly don't know. What I do know is that Aly pours her heart and soul into her training and into her fights. And as long as she doesn't quit on us, RSI won't quit on her.

Now, let's see your rage face, baby.

FAC featherweight champion Emma Watson has put together an extremely impressive start to her UMMA career. And, she's been on a nice little roll since joining us here at RSI. But, tonight, former champion Christobelle Grierson-Ryrie sent our little wunderkind a message: She may have lost her championship, but she's not going to go away quietly. Normally a stand up fighter, Christobelle took things to the ground and caught poor Emma completely off-guard. Trapped in a terrible position right from the start, the champion struggled mightily to get free. But, Christobelle knew she had the advantage, and she patiently maneuvered for the submission victory.

I'd love to rant and rave about how something wasn't fair, or that Christobelle cheated, or... something. Anything, really. But there's no avoiding the facts. Christobelle had a plan, and she worked it perfectly.

For her part, Emma made no excuses.

"She got me good," admitted Watson, unconsciously rubbing her neck. "There's really no other way to look at it. She beat me, and she made me look pretty damned silly, too. So, I have to give her credit. She was better, tonight. Plain and simple."

Then, a defiant Emma made it clear that she wasn't going to just let this one go.

"I know I still have a lot to learn. And, tomorrow, I'm heading to the gym to learn how to make sure this doesn't happen again."

I can't ask for more than that.

After an emotional victory in her rematch with Michelle Vawer and a stellar performance in her tournament match against the experienced Halle Berry, there was some question about how Jennifer Lawrence would motivate herself for her matchup with Misa Campo. And, to be honest, I don't know what she did. I just know it worked.

As the bell sounded to get this one started, Campo was overheard challenging Jen to "just bring it." Well, consider it brought, sweetie. Jen immediately hammered Misa with a flurry of powerful strikes. The Canadian beauty tried to stop the damage with a standing submission, but Lawrence just shrugged her off and threw her to the canvas. Campo seemed happy to have gotten the fight to the ground, but Jen showed she can handle herself, countering everything Misa tried and even taking a shot at a submission of her own. Once the fighters got back to their feet, Jen continued what she'd started, scoring easily with precision strikes, both at distance and from the clinch. Misa lasted to the end of the round, but she was clearly hurt as she wobbled to her corner. To her credit, she stumbled back out for more punishment in the second, and Jen was more than happy to oblige. Working her dazed opponent mercilessly, she landed the knockout blow just over a minute into the round.

With tonight's victory, Jen pushes her record back to the .500 mark. While she has struggled at times, she has been slowly creeping her way up the FAC middleweight rankings. It's been a rough ride, and Lawrence knows it's only going to get tougher.

"Tonight was a great win for me, but I'm already worrying about what comes next," said the young actress. "Things are going to start getting pretty serious, now. I know I need to step up my game."

That she does. Each of the three fighters ranked above her have more wins than Jen has total matches. So, I suspect she's in for some tough times in the near future. But, if anyone can weather the storm, J-Law can.

There's a reason the phrase "rookie mistake" exists. It's because rookies often have to learn things the hard way. Brittany Tacy's big mistake was thinking that, since she was able to survive her last match with Selena Gomez, she didn't have to change anything to get ready for their rematch. As you might expect, Brittany paid pretty dearly for her error. The fighters spent nearly the entire first round on the ground, which is definitely not where Britanny wants to be. Tacy defended herself well, and Gomez wasn't really able to do much damage. But, she was able to wear Brittany down. The fatigue showed up in the second, as Brittany's defense got sloppy and Selena tagged her good, opening up a gash on Brit's cheek.

Now, make no mistake: I think referee Lena Gercke's decision to stop the fight because of the wound was ridiculous. Brittany was clearly capable of fighting on. And, due to the UMMA guidelines on fighter safety, the injury stoppage means the Floridian powerhouse has to sit out her next event, which only compounds the stupidity of the decision. But, I won't go so far as to say Gercke cost Brittany this fight. Though neither fighter had really done much damage, Gomez was clearly in control, throughout. I didn't see anything from Brittany that made me think things were going to change. Still... it would have been nice to let her have the chance.

Afterward, Brittany had little to say about the premature stoppage, choosing instead to focus on her disappointment with her own performance.

"This was easily my worst fight, so far. She tangled me up early, and I just had no answer," said a dejected Tacy. "I don't actually mind losing. I mean, I DO, but I also know it's going to happen. I'm not going to win every fight. But this was just sad."

The Floridian beauty was also frustrated with what she felt were wasted hours in the gym.

"It's aggravating. You work and you plan... and then, nothing. I came out here tonight, and I really didn't accomplish anything at all."

Not quite. It sure sounds like you learned a tough lesson. And, I don't think you're going to let this happen again. So, just hang in there.

Well, all in all, a mixed night for RSI. But, with a shiny new title and a common spirit of hardened defiance among the fighters who came up short, I have nothing to complain about. These women put it all out there, and, as always, I'm damned proud of them.
