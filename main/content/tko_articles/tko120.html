A Fine Debut

Another team event behind us, and, though the points system seems to have bitten Britney's team pretty hard, overall it was a good night for RSI. We went with a roundtable format for the breakdown of the last team event, and it worked out pretty well. So, joining me again are team leaders Britney Spears and Elisha Cuthbert. Also, reporting on her very first team competition, our newest team leader, Alyson Michalka.

Richelle Winterfeld: Ladies, lose the long faces.

Elisha: We'll try.

Richelle: Don't try. Do.

Britney: It's not that easy, Richelle.

Richelle: Sure it is. We'll start with you, Brit. Your team won five out of eight, with it's leader on the bench against one of the toughest teams in the competition.

Britney: Yeah, but Steph...

Richelle: I don't want to hear a thing about Steph. I'm not the slightest bit worried about her.

Britney: Well... I...

Richelle: Look... let's start by recognizing this amazing metamorphosis that Kim Kardashian has undergone. She's suddenly the queen of submissions. Nobody thought much of it when she beat Torrie, but now that she's done it to Steph... we have to acknowledge that something big has happened there.

Elisha: Yeah... how did she do that?

Richelle: Don't know. Don't much care. The point is that there was no way for Torrie or Steph to know that be fighting a completely different fighter. By the time they meet up again, we should have more tape to look at so we'll be better prepared. My only hope is that the MMA world descends upon Hellfire to try and figure out how they did it. That'll give them something to do other than complain about us. So, at least we'll get something out of the deal.

Alyson: (looking to Britney and Elisha) I though you guys said she was going to be mad.

(Both Britney and Elisha shrug)

Richelle: That's twice now you guys have done that. What makes you think I'll be mad?

Britney: Well, I don't understand why you're not.

Elisha: Yeah. It's not like we came through for you.

Richelle: (shaking her head) You guys really had it easy in the beginning, huh? You thought it was always gonna be that way.

Elisha: That would have been nice.

Richelle: It would have been ridiculous, is what it would have been. Look, you guys have amazed me, so far. I'm very proud of what your teams have accomplished. So, don't be confused. Just because you're angry with yourselves, doesn't mean I'm angry with you, too.

Britney: Yeah, well... we're angry, alright.

Elisha: Speak for yourself. I'm actually going with 'fucking pissed.'

Britney: (nods) 'Furious', maybe?

Elisha: We could try to be more elegant. Something like 'irate' or 'livid.'

Richelle: Alright... both of you, shut it.

Britney: Wow.

Elisha: Yeah. Talk about a lack of elegance.

Richelle: (sighs) Alright, Michalka... what do you got to say for yourself?

Alyson: (shrugs) Not bad. A couple of hiccups, but...

Richelle: Oh good. Another perfectionist.

Britney: She learned from the best.

Richelle: Okay, let's get serious. Brit... it was a bad break for Steph, Jen Stano is getting a little dose of reality, and Danica... well, I'll talk to her, later. What else do you have?

Britney: Some pretty good performances. Emily was solid against Victoria Justice. Katy Perry has struggled, but she's been around a while. Cat handled it without a problem, though.

Richelle: You have a Fighter of the Night for me?

Britney: (nods) It's gotta be Krystal.

Richelle: Agreed. Give Debby Ryan credit for her toughness, but this one was all Krystal. That brings us to you Leesh. Kate Beckinsale was a little too much for Cobie Smulders, and a tough, tough break for Tiff against Grace Park. But a lot of good things to talk about, too.

Elisha: Definitely. Daniela was all business as she handled Alexandra Paul, and Torrie came up huge against Angelina Jolie... especially since Angelina was a late injury substitute.

Richelle: Uh, huh. But...

Elisha: But my Fighter of the Night has to be Jules. She was incredible against Kristin Kreuk.

Richelle: No doubt, Kristin is a force. But Jules made it look easy.

Britney: A great win. No doubt.

Richelle: Alright, Aly... you're up. Allira just didn't have it tonight against Holly Madison, and Marzia put up a helluva fight against the more experienced Veronika Varekova, but just couldn't get it done. Whaddya have on the positive side?

Alyson: I have a real hard time picking a Fighter of the Night. That's what I have.

Elisha: (nods) You had some big performances.

Alyson: I want to start with Danni, though. I talked to her after her fight, and she was pretty upset.

Richelle: (nods) I know. It's not her fault. These team events create bad mismatches, sometimes. This wasn't fair to Tiffany Fallon, at all.

Britney: No kidding.

Elisha: Wasn't this only her second fight? And she had to face Danni?

Richelle: I know. It's ridiculous. And with a mismatch like that, injuries are going to be common. That's what happened here, unfortunately.

Alyson: Tiffany shouldn't have been in that position, and Danni shouldn't have to live with this.

Richelle: They'll both recover, but I agree completely. But this is how it is, so...

Britney: It could happen to some of our girls, too.

Richelle: No, it couldn't. I won't let it.

Elisha: What do you mean?

Richelle: I'm all for letting fighters test themselves against better competition. I let Cobie go against Beckinsale, and I let Marzia go against Varekova. But if I see a bad mismatch, I won't hesitate to make a switch. For example, I wouldn't have let Marzia go against Louise Glover or Adriana Lima. She's not ready for something like that.

Alyson: You let Jen Stano go against Doutzen Kroes.

Richelle: That was a tournament. Jen advanced her way into that fight. There was nothing I could do. But for the record, if I could have stopped it, I would have.

Elisha: But she won!

Richelle: Yes, she did. And full credit to her, for that. But nobody would have predicted that, ahead of time. And quite a few people would have predicted that Doutzen would leave her crippled. As far as I'm concerned, part of my job is protecting my fighters. I know not everyone would agree, and that's fine.

Elisha: You can't keep them in a bubble, Richelle.

Richelle: Gimme a break. Who do I keep in a bubble, Leesh? I let the younger girls take their shots. But just because I don't keep them in a bubble, that doesn't mean I have to stand-by and watch them get pushed in front of a bus.

Alyson: Did you just call Danni a bus?

Richelle: (rolls her eyes) Just finish, would ya?

Alyson: Okay, well... my fighter of the night was a hard call. Brittany Tacy looked like the obvious choice.

Elisha: Big win for her over Anna Faris.

Britney: No question.

Richelle: But you picked someone else.

Alyson: I picked Emma. I know she's a champion, and I know Kate Moss has struggled. But that was a tough fight.

Richelle: I think it's a good pick. Kate's no pushover, and I was worried her game would give Emma fits. She handled it, though.

Britney: There's a lot of brutalness behind those innocent brown eyes.

Richelle: Pretty sure 'brutalness' isn't a word, but I get your point.

Elisha: (to Aly) By the way, you left somebody out.

Alyson: No, I didn't.

Richelle: Sure, you did. Just like your two clown friends here...

Britney: Hey!

Elisha: Clowns?

Richelle: ... you left yourself off the list. Amber Heard is a good fighter, and you've had problems with her in the past. That was a good win, for you.

Alyson: Thanks. But there was no way I was giving my first ever Fighter of the Night award to myself.

Richelle: (smiles) Fair enough. Well, that about does it for this event...

Britney: Wait. Hang on a second...

Elisha: Yeah, let's go back to that thing about the clowns.

Richelle: ... but be sure to join us next time, as the women of SCQ take to the cage. Roselyn Sanchez will try to make up for a poor showing against Ali Larter in their last meeting, and Catherine Bell will face a tough test in Tricia Helfer. But the big fight for RSI will be Stephanie McMahon finally getting another shot at Veronika Zemanova's title belt. Can she put tonight's setback behind her and grab hold of the crown? Be sure to get your tickets and find out.

Elisha: (to Britney) I'm starting to think she might be ignoring us.
