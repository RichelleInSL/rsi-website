Keeping Pace

The women of RSI didn't have much to gain at this event, but they had plenty to lose. The goal of the night was to take care of business and stay out of trouble. It didn't go as well as we would have liked, but the ladies avoided any big disasters.

With injuries sidelining both champion Vikki Blows and nearest competitor Ksenia Solo, Julianne Hough was left to face off against Hellfire's Lyndsy Fonseca. With the ongoing war of words between Hellfire and RSI, there seemed to be some special interest in this matchup. As usual, I left the training and prep work in the hands of stable leader Danneel Harris and fighter development coordinator Stephanie McMahon. My job was to keep the extra-curricular garbage off of Julianne's plate. Lyndsy's a skilled fighter, and beating her was going to be hard enough. Jules didn't need any extra stress.

Well, I don't know how good a job I did, but I can tell you that Danni and Steph did their part. Jules looked sensational tonight. She was calm, cool and in-command throughout the eleven-plus minutes of action. She defended well, and scored whenever her opportunities came. Try as she might, Lyndsy had no answers for Julianne, and the end result was a worn-out and frustrated Fonseca finally succumbing early in the third round.

This one was all about patience for Jules. Each of the first two rounds started off slowly, with Hough focusing on keeping Lyndsy at bay. As Fonseca started to tire, Jules would take her shots... pouring it on as each round came to a close. A particularly brutal counter opened up a cut on Lyndsy's forehead as the second round ended, and her corner wasn't able to do much to stem the bleeding during the break. As they came out for the third, it was clear that Fonseca didn't have much left, and Jules patiently picked her apart until the knockout came.

Afterward, Julianne admitted to being impressed with her opponent. "Lyndsy was able to get anything going tonight, but damn... she hung tough," said the Footloose star. "I was hitting her with everything I had in that second round, but she just wouldn't fall." True enough. Lyndsy has shown flashes of brilliance in her career. Combine that with her toughness, and she can be very dangerous. If she can find some consistency, she could be headed for something great. Even if she is a Hellfire girl.

Meanwhile, Emma Watson got to meet her new top challenger here in Las Vegas. With Christobelle Grierson-Ryrie taking a break from competitive fighting, Xenia Deli got a chance to show what she could do against the champion. Emma's not much of a gracious host in the cage, though, and she definitely didn't break out the tea and crumpets to welcome Xenia.

Truth is, though she's still relatively inexperienced, Deli has a solid record with some big wins over tough opponents. So Emma's only interest was taking care of business, and she did that here, tonight. Watson came out firing from the very start, and she didn't let up until she secured the victory nearly five minutes later.

Though she gave a dominating performance, Emma was anything but arrogant after this one was over. "Xenia is a tough opponent. She's beaten quite a few experienced fighters. I'm just happy I was able to stay with my strategy, and get the win." Emma also recognized that her current position isn't as advantageous as it might seem. "With Christobelle stepping away from competition, it's left a big hole in the featherweight division. There aren't very many experienced fighters, but I don't think that's really a good thing for me. Every time I get in that cage now, my opponent is going to have something to prove." Indeed, Emma now seems destined to face a rotating gauntlet of developing opponents, all vying to be the one to take the champion down. "I may have the advantage now," said Emma, "but it won't last. The others know what they're shooting for, while I'm just fighting to stay alive. Anything can happen in the cage, and it's going to start catching up to me."

Emma also had a special message she wanted to deliver. "I think there are a few reporters out there who need to mind their own fucking business," she said as the question and answer session closed. When asked to elaborate, she offered no additional hints as to who she was talking about. "He knows who he is. And if he'd like to come make some of his smartass remarks to my face... well, I'm always in need of a good practice dummy."

Woah.

Back on point... Emma couldn't be more right about the way her competition is shaping up. In fact, it's starting to look like her next opponent will be Daniella Monet, who carries an even more impressive record that Xenia. No doubt, the manufactured Hellfire/RSI rivalry will play into that one, as well. I have to give Hellfire this much... despite Travis's nutty quest to make RSI the enemy, his group does turn out high-quality fighters.

Meanwhile, we knew RSI's luck couldn't hold out all night, and it ran out suddenly as a new wrinkle from an old foe took down Alyson Michalka. Brooklyn Decker went away from her usual stand-up style and got the submission victory in this one, and the loss was a cold dose of reality for Aly after her title run at the Lingerie Invitational tournament. In fact, it served as a tough reminder that, though Aly has had quite a bit of success in recent months, none of it has come against Brooklyn. The two have had some close fights, but it's almost always Brooklyn that walks away with the win. That's a problem Aly's going to have to solve if she's going to make her title hopes a reality.

"I won't say she surprised me," Michalka said afterward. "With Brooklyn, you have to be ready for anything. So, nothing she does should come as a surprise." Aly also went on to say that, despite the improvement she's shown, she's well aware of her record against Brooklyn. "I've had very little success against her, and that needs to change. Now. The only way to get the belt is to go through Brooklyn."

Indeed, the loss combined with the ever-mysterious UMMA scoring system now puts Brooklyn into the top contender's slot in the FAC light heavyweight division. An injury to champion Erin Heatherton prevented her from defending her title tonight, meaning Aly never got the title shot she deserved during her brief run at the top. But Aly's not letting the missed opportunity bother her. "I'll never quit. I'll never give up. No matter how long it takes, I'm coming for that title."

That's all I needed to hear.

Not a great night for many of RSI's younger fighters, as Jennifer Lawrence, Shelby Chesnes and Dianna Dahlgren all lost. Both Jennifer and Dianna performed decently, but fell to a pair of slick submission moves. Shelby, meanwhile, needs to take a hard look at her approach, as she took a brutal beating at the hands of Sara Sampaio. I wouldn't want to be her when Steph asks her to explain what the hell happened out there.

Not all was a disaster for the younger set, however. Brittany Tacy was the beneficiary of a ridiculously early injury stoppage. Not much to be particularly joyous about there, but these things happen all too often. Better to just be happy with the win, because there's no question she'll end up on the short end of the injury stick sometime down the road.

Meanwhile, RSI's newest recruit debuted in fine fashion. One punch was all it took for Demi Lovato to stop opponent Leven Rambin. It's true that Leven hasn't had much success in the UMMA, but, then again, neither has Demi. She looked confident out there, and that's really what I was looking for. Turning her career around wasn't going to happen in a single fight, but that's the way it has to start.

Some Open Fight Night news to catch up on. First up, it seems the Hellfire group isn't likely to give up on their ridiculous vendetta anytime soon. Some good things are coming out of it though, and the challenges Travis announced after the Raging Rumble event definitely fall into that category. SCW lightweight champion Ali Larter has issued a challenge to Emily VanCamp that Em was all too happy to accept. "All of this rivalry nonsense aside, I know what Ali is capable of. I know what Roselyn Sanchez is capable of too, but Ali clearly got the better of her in their last fight. I look forward to seeing what I can do, and I have no doubt the fans are going to be into this one." Meanwhile, the second challenge Travis announced came from Karen Gillan, and it was leveled at RSI's Jennifer Stano. Jen was all over it as soon as she heard, and I have to admit that I'm pretty interested in this one, myself. Jen's stunning success against Doutzen Kroes may have rocketed her to the top of the standings, but she doesn't have a lot of experience against other fighters on that level. She does have a win over Karen at the Lingerie Invitational event, which is where all this "Hellfire hates RSI" crap got it's start. So, I have no doubt Karen has been itching for revenge, and it should prove to be an excellent test for Jen. I think the fans are going to enjoy it, too.

As if that weren't enough, the ILF has entered the picture in a big way. Britney Spears has been challenged by non other than the legendary Doutzen Kroes. Britney's response? "You don't turn down a challenge from the best of the best," she said. "You just don't." Indeed, Britney is going to put everything she has into this fight, and not just because she wants to be a worthy opponent for Doutzen. "I can't get my ass kicked in this one," she joked. "Stano will never let me live it down if that happens." Greater inspiration, there has never been.

Speaking of OFN, the date is now just around the corner. With Britney, Emily, Jen, Emma, Torrie Wilson, Catherine Bell, Sabine Jemeljanova, J-Law and Shelby all in action, this promises to be our craziest event of the entire year. You won't want to miss it.
