Middleweights Gone Mad

Stunning surprises, dramatic disappointments and a disturbing number of injuries. The Hit the Beach tournament was pure chaos for RSI. With so much going on, I'm not even going to try to report on it all. But, a couple of our middleweight fighters made quite a bit of noise at this event. One is the story of a veteran fighter who has been on the rampage, recently. The other is the story of a young rookie who, quite simply, did the impossible. One of these stories folds up very nicely inside the other, so I'll start things off with the vet: Britney Spears, who recently put on a tremendous show at Open Fight Night, kept right on rolling as she came away with the middleweight crown in this one.

Britney's quest started off with a matchup against RSI teammate Jennifer Lawrence. Jen's game has been steadily improving, but this was just an unfortunate matchup for her. Though I think she's better than she showed here, there's no question that she's not ready to take on someone of Britney's caliber. For her part, though, the young actress took the defeat in stride. "I wish it hadn't been Britney, ya' know? Anyone else, I could have at least deluded myself into thinking I had a shot. But, Britney... I know exactly what she can do. So, I knew I was in big trouble." Still, the outspoken Hunger Games star did manage to find some solace. "At least I can say I lost to the champ."

Next for Britney was Nicole Kidman, who has been on a little roll of her own, recently. With victories over seasoned veterans like Halle Berry and fierce newcomers such as Lindsay Ellingson (more on her later), Kidman has really upped her game recently. But, that mattered little to Britney, as she tore right through the auburn-tressed Oscar winner. Attacking relentlessly, Britney left Nicole no room to maneuver, finishing the fight in just over two minutes.

Then, it was on to the toughest matchup Britney would face during this tournament, DEF middleweight champion Malin Akerman. Both Britney and Elisha Cuthbert have been chasing Malin for quite a while now. When they last met, Britney used surgical precision and careful strategy to come away with the hard-fought win. But, here in the Bahamas, Spears just blew her out of the cage. The fight was over in less than two minutes, and Akerman never even landed a blow. Like I said, the Blonde Bomber's on a roll.

With the Akerman bout behind her, Britney moved on to her next challenge. Her name? Jennifer Stano. And, no... that's not a typo. RSI's very own rookie sensation had made her way to the semifinal round. How did she get there? The same way every RSI athlete ever accomplishes anything. The hard way.

Right out of the gate, Jen had her hands full with the immensely successful Paula Patton. Coming in with a record of 15-6, Patton has already repeatedly shown she can handle herself against just about anyone. So, it was clear to most that Jen was in for a tough fight. Well, apparently, nobody told Stano. And that did not work out well for Patton. After taking a few minutes to measure up her opponent, Jen blitzed Paula with a relentless barrage, knocking the actress stupid with just over 30 seconds to go in the round. It was a solid performance and a surprising win for Stano. And that was just the beginning.

Next up was the much more experienced Denise Richards. If Patton was a tough matchup for Jennifer, then Denise promised to be a nightmare. Richards has pulled out victories over some of the best fighters in the UMMA, including Misa Campo and even Britney. She recently went toe-to-toe with Malin, narrowly missing out on the decision. Once again, things looked bad for Stano. And, once again, Jen seemed to be the only person who didn't realize it. Stano was in complete control of the match within seconds, and Denise spent the entire fight looking for escape. Stano chased her all over the cage, landing crushing blows throughout. It was a miracle that Richards survived the first round, but it didn't do her any good. It was just more of the same in the second, and after another minute, the ref stepped in and saved Denise from further damage. Though the injury that officially ended things seemed relatively minor, it was clear that Denise was in no shape to continue. If Jennifer's win over Patton was a surprise, then this one was simply stunning. What else could she possibly have in store for us? Well, if you're having a good day out on the lake, you might as well take a shot at the biggest fish there is, right?

I mentioned earlier that Malin Akerman was Britney's toughest challenge of this tournament. That should have immediately raised a question for those familiar with the top contenders in the UMMA middleweight class. What about Doutzen Kroes? Didn't Britney have to face her? No, she did not. Because Jennifer Stano had already beaten her.

Go ahead. Take a moment and let that sink in.

Let's look at some numbers, shall we? Total matches coming into this tournament: Kroes-102, Stano-8. Total wins: Kroes-96, Stano-6. Some quick math tells you that Doutzen Kroes had lost a grand total of 6 matches in her legendary career. And now, thanks to Jennifer Stano, that number stands at 7. And, let's be clear, this was no fluke win. No lucky punch. No injury. No over-protective referee. Jennifer took the best Doutzen had to offer, even suffering a clean knockdown after a brutal combination from the UMMA's most experienced fighter. But the rookie refused to panic and she refused to quit. She picked herself up and got right back in the fight, defiantly continuing to believe in the face of seemingly insurmountable odds. And that's when things went nuts.

A kick to Doutzen's chest. A brutal exchange that saw both fighters take a pounding. Then, a vicious knee that caught Kroes off-guard, stunning the veteran fighter. A hook to the body and a straight right hand that landed square in Doutzen's face. Suddenly, Kroes was in trouble, and everyone watching knew it. No one spoke. No one moved. No one even breathed. What followed was the loudest silence I have ever heard in my life. Doutzen Kroes was on the ropes, and it was an honest-to-god rookie doing the damage. Surely, this couldn't be happening. But, it was. Stano continued her assault, working Kroes against the cage. Knees, kicks, punches... Jennifer just kept coming. Kroes tried to fight back, but it was just no use. Soon, the blood started to flow and a murmur spread through the audience. Yet the wave of excitement you might expect never came. The vibe in the arena was something closer to shock than joy. Most of the crowd were fans of Doutzen, and they could not believe what they were seeing. Back in the RSI locker room, even Jennifer's teammates were too stunned to speak, as if saying anything might somehow change the aura of the arena and let Kroes back into the fight. But, the truth was, it was already too late. Doutzen was trying desperately to hang on, but there was too much time left. Jennifer continued to pound her opponent mercilessly, blood and sweat spraying from the veteran as each blow found its target. To Doutzen's credit she did not give in easily, taking a vicious beating at the hands of her adversary. And, for just a moment, it appeared she might actually survive the round. But Stano knew she couldn't let that happen, and she unloaded with a savage right hand that sent the veteran to the canvas with less than 30 seconds remaining. Even then, the crowd still didn't know how to react to what they'd seen. But, the thunderous cheering emanating from the RSI locker room more than made up for the silence of the audience.

How do you respond to something like this? Well, I'll let RSI's most seasoned middleweight contender do the talking.

"She should get a fucking trophy for what she did," said Britney afterward. "Elisha can go to hell," continued the blonde songstress. "I'm training with Jennifer from now on."

Of course, she was just kidding.

I think.

Want more? How about a quote from the far-from-easily-impressed Danneel Harris?

"This was stunning," said RSI's team leader. "I've liked what we've seen from Jennifer from day one, but I had no idea she had something like this in her. I am massively impressed."

The fighter herself was much more reserved when asked to describe her accomplishment.

"This is MMA," she said sheepishly, seemingly uncomfortable with her sudden fame. "Anything can happen in that cage. I knew that if I went in there and stuck to my training, I had a shot. That was all I could ask for, and it worked out."

Jennifer Stano, mistress of the understatement. But Jennifer wanted to make it clear she still knows where she stands.

"I'm proud of what I did here, and I'm excited about beating Doutzen. But I know that I still have a long way to go before I'm any real threat to her. I want to get there, but I still have a lot to learn."

I have no words. I have no explanation. I have absolutely nothing. Up until now, Jennifer has shown me exactly what I look for in a rookie. She's talented, tough and smart. I've commented in the past about her ability to stay poised under pressure. But this was more pressure than any rookie could be expected to endure. And yet, she did. She took down the single greatest legend the UMMA has to offer. If Jennifer Stano never wins another fight, she will still go down in the annals of history for what she accomplished here in Nassau. And she didn't even win the god-damn tournament.

This will most certainly ramp up the pressure on Jennifer, as her win will send her shooting up the rankings. So, I want to be careful not to add to the new expectations. Though I can't deny this may have been the most impressive win I've ever seen, it's important to remember she's still only got about a dozen fights under her belt. There will be bumps in the road. But, there's no longer any doubt... she's got what it takes to be great.

At any rate, that brings us back to the matchup between Britney and Jennifer. As you might expect, the win over Doutzen took everything Stano had. She had nothing to offer Britney, and it showed. Still, her toughness shined through, as she was the only fighter to take Brit to the second round in this tournament. It did her little good though, as she endured quite a beating before a high kick dropped her almost 2 minutes into the round. Next thing she knew, she was lying on the canvas being revived by the medical staff. Once she came around, though, there was a surprise waiting for her. As the medics cleared away, Jennifer sat up and found herself staring into the smiling face of Britney Spears. Britney extended a hand and helped Stano to her feet. Then the two embraced and the crowd cheered wildly, mostly in belated recognition of what Jennifer had accomplished in her previous fight. With their time as opponents behind them, Britney and Jennifer quickly returned to their roles as teammates. They chatted in the center of the cage as if they were the only two in the arena, with Britney gesturing empathetically as she spoke. No doubt the topic of discussion was Jennifer's monumental defeat of Kroes. But, alas... Britney could not stay long. Her work at this tournament was not yet complete.

Her final challenge brought Britney face to face with the previously mentioned Lindsay Ellingson, who made quite a run of her own to reach the championship match. That run included a win over RSI's own Elisha Cuthbert. Unfortunately, that fight was ended much too early, as the overzealous (and interestingly named) referee stopped things at the first site of blood. Still, there was no doubt Lindsay was keeping pace with Elisha through the 3 minutes of action we did get to see, so I was very interested to see what she had in store for Britney. It seems we'll never know the answer to that question, though, as Britney simply overwhelmed the young fighter from the outset, never letting her do a damn thing. And that's not an exaggeration. In a bout that lasted a little more than 2 minutes, Lindsay never touched Spears.

Did I mention Britney's on a roll? Yeah... no shit.

After it was all over, the celebration was on back in the locker room. As she passed through the door, Britney was quickly mobbed by her teammates. Both of the Jennifers were there, and Britney shared a warm embrace with each, pausing to offer special congratulations once more to Stano on her spectacular run.

Next in line was Danni, who smiled broadly as Britney approached. With Britney now serving as a team leader, she's been slowly stepping up into management territory. That's brought she and Danni closer than ever, and it showed clearly as Harris touched her hand to the back of Britney's head as they embraced, speaking softly so that only Britney could hear.

And, of course, Jamie Lynn was there. Surprisingly, there didn't seem to be much in the way of the usual ribbing between the siblings, as Jamie looked genuinely thrilled for her big sister.

It continued like that as Britney made her way through the throng. Spears has long been among the most popular fighters here at RSI, and her new role as team leader has only enhanced that. However, as the crowd around her thinned out, Britney started looking around. At first, the look on her face was expectant... as if, at any moment, she would turn and find the person she was looking for. But, the look quickly advanced to confusion and then concern as that moment never materialized. She turned back, going from teammate to teammate.

Though I could not hear what was said, I knew what she was asking them.

Where was Elisha?

Each, in turn, shook her head. No one seemed to know where Britney's training partner and closest friend was. Why would she not be here to congratulate her friend? Had her injury been more severe than it seemed? Was she so disappointed by her oh-so-silly stoppage loss that she slipped off to deal with it alone? Clearly worried, Britney left the mob behind and started to search the locker room, looking up and down the rows as she went. Danni and I joined her, but it quickly became clear that Elisha wasn't there.

With Britney needing to get to her post event press conference, we devised a plan. Britney and I would head for the presser while Danni continued to look for Elisha. Filing out through the locker room door, I lead Britney to the right as Danni broke left.

And that's when we all heard it.

"...might be the most complete fighter I've ever seen."

We stood stunned as the dulcet tones of a voice we knew all too well drifted out of the press room. As we turned the corner, we could just make out Elisha at the front of the room, taking questions from the assembled media members.

"She never gets rattled. Never loses focus," the 24 actress continued. "Unless she's literally knocked out, she's dangerous. Trust me, I know."

As Britney stood with her mouth hanging open, I glanced over at Danni, who was struggling mightily not to laugh.

"The bitch hijacked my press conference," muttered the stunned musical artist as Elisha fielded another question.

"How serious is your rivalry with Britney?" the reporter asked.

Elisha paused a moment to consider her response, and I grimaced at the thought of what was coming. But, I could not have been more wrong.

"There is no rivalry," Cuthbert said. "I know we give each other a hard time, and that's part of who we are. But, losing to Britney never bothers me. I'm there with her, every day. I see the effort she puts into her training. I see the heart and determination she fights with. Just being able to compete with her tells me I must be pretty good at this."

Britney smiled softly as I glanced over at her.

"Maybe it wasn't a hijacking," I whispered. "Maybe she was just warming up the room."

A follow-up question asked if Elisha was feeling any jealousy now that Britney had won a belt.

"Not at all," Elisha said simply. "In fact, I've never been this happy for someone else. She earned every bit of this championship. I only hope she realizes how deserving she is."

With that, Danni gestured Britney forward.

"You'd better go get her before she starts to cry."

As Elisha caught sight of Brit emerging from the crowd, she went off like a bottle rocket. The table went sliding and the chair actually flipped over as she launched herself across the room, literally tackling Britney to the floor.

"Jesus, Elisha!" cried Britney as the two went ass over teakettle to the cold tile.

But Elisha wasn't listening.

"You did it! You did it! You did it!" she screamed as she ploughed into her friend.

As they rolled to a stop, Elisha ended up astride Britney, pinning her to the floor.

"You have no idea how many times I've imagined this day," said the clearly ecstatic actress.

"If you don't get off of me, it may be the last day you ever see," deadpanned Spears, her smile betraying her real feelings.

Elisha stood, hauled Britney to her feet and hugged her fiercely. The two shared a few whispers between them, and both came away teary-eyed. Then Elisha squeezed her friend again, and the singer's eyes pleaded with Danni to come rescue her. RSI's team leader dutifully obliged.

With the crazy Canuck secured, Britney made her way to the microphone.

"The press room is more dangerous than the cage," joked the new champion as she retrieved the upturned chair.

With the actual winner now running the show, Britney was peppered with all of the expected questions. And she gave the expected answers to almost all of them. But, when she was asked her feelings about being a part of RSI, she caught me by surprise.

"I could not imagine a better place to work," she began. "And, I could not imagine a better person to work for than Richelle."

I could feel the heat rising in my face as most in the room turned in my direction. I waved my hand dismissively, but Britney kept on anyway.

"She doesn't feel any need to control her fighters," she continued as she locked her honey-brown eyes on me. "We get to do our own thing, but we also get the safety net of having a whole team behind us. She's built a great organization, and, when she calls it a family, she's not just saying that."

Desperate for escape, I turned toward Danni and Elisha. Unfortunately, there was no help there as both just smiled at me and nodded.

"Besides," Britney added. "Everyone knows Leesh and I are the real brains behind the operation. Richelle is smart enough to just stay out of the way."

Everyone had a good laugh as my embarrassed expression flattened into mock disdain.

"Why do I put up with these two?" I asked Danni with a heavy sigh.

"Because you love them," she replied matter-of-factly.

I just rolled my eyes. Why must she always be so straight-forward? Can't she just tell me what I want to hear, once in a while?

So, another first-time champion for RSI. Must be backstory time. But, what can I possibly add to Britney's backstory that isn't already known? After all, she grew up right before our eyes. From her time as the cutest little savior a parent could ask for on the Mickey Mouse Club to her days as a parent's worst nightmare as she became more and more risqué on stage, Britney has always been in the public eye. And the ride has been anything but smooth for her. I've lauded Emma Watson and Jennifer Lawrence in the past for handling their fame with a maturity well beyond their years. But Britney wasn't able to do that. She did let fame get the better of her. For years, she was tabloid fodder. From relationship disasters to infamous hair(-less)styles, Britney had slowly become the train wreck that people expect from young starlets. But look at her now. While so many young celebrities disappear into that downward spiral, Britney managed to pull herself out of the spin. She stopped caring about what people said. Instead of rebelling against every expectation she started living for herself and her children. She is her own person, and she looks very comfortable in her own skin. And the end result is that she is now respected for her accomplishments, instead of ridiculed for her failings.

I won't get into which is harder: keeping out of trouble or pulling yourself out of the abyss. I'll simply say I've never seen anyone pull off a recovery quite like Britney has.

So, here we are, Brit. I knew this day would come, but, now that it's here, I don't know what to say. You and your Canadian cohort are, time and again, the bane of my existence. But you are also an irreplaceable part of what makes RSI what it is. I try to be the glue that holds things together. Danni is the rock that holds me up. And you two are the grease that keeps the wheels turning. You prod me. You push me. You force me into things I want no part of. And somehow, it always works out.

Congrats on the belt, Queen B. May it be the first of many.

Oh, and yeah... I called you a greasy bitch. Got a problem with that?

A few other notes before I end this parable. Alyson Michalka lost a heart-breaker to rival Erin Heatherton, but not before scoring a solid win over nemesis India Reynolds. The competition in that group will always be unbearable, with every match being a toss-up. But Aly continues to prove she's on that level, and I can't ask for more than that.

Though Catherine Bell looked spent in a one-sided loss to Louise Glover in the heavyweight final, she looked fantastic against Caroline Wozniacki the round before. Winning these tournaments often means having to beat numerous top-notch opponents. Mac couldn't get it done this time around, but she certainly showed she's ready for the big time.

There were a lot of silly injury stoppages in this tournament that proved costly for some RSI fighters. I won't get into the particulars, because there's no point. That's just the way it goes, sometimes. But, there were a couple of more severe injuries that really made my heart sink.

No shame in Ashley Tisdale's loss to Kristin Kreuk, but the broken cheekbone she suffered is going to set her back badly. I feel terrible for her, but I know Sharpay won't be down long. We'll get her back on her feet and back on track.

The most heart-wrenching injury, though, was suffered by Amanda Seyfried. I said earlier that you often have to face a number of the very best competitors to win a tournament like this. Not only is it mentally difficult, but it's also physically exhausting. Sometimes, that physical toll can lead to injury. After overcoming former FAC champion Gabriella Cilmi and current ESL champion Cheryl Cole, Amanda just didn't have anything left for current FAC champ Candice Swanepoel. Unfortunately, Amanda's competitive spirit does not allow her to see when she needs to give in, and she suffered a horrendous beating in the final before finally succumbing to a devastating rib injury. I don't know exactly what to say about this. In my head, I tell myself Amanda should have seen what everyone else saw. She wasn't in shape to deal with Swanepoel, and she should have been smarter. This injury will cost her dearly, and it comes just as she was poised to crack into the upper echelon of her division. But what's the one thing that separates the best from the rest? Heart. And Amanda showed plenty of it, fighting on until her body completely gave out. You just can't train that kind of thing. So, we'll deal with the situation, and move on. We'll get Amanda back in fighting shape, and get her back out there where she belongs.

Well, that's all for this update, but it's time for the women of SCQ to take the stage in the next event. There'll be plenty of action in this one, including rookie Marzia Prince facing what may be her greatest challenge to date in Trish Stratus. Marzia's big win over Traci Bingham created a lot of buzz at the recent team event. Can she pull off another upset here? Speaking of upsets, Stephanie McMahon lost in stunning fashion to the far less experienced Deborah Ann Woll at the team competition. Though Steph seems to have shaken it off, her RSI teammates have been showing a lot of concern. So, her upcoming match with the talented and experienced Abi Titmuss is looming pretty large, right now. Tune in next time to see if RSI's super heavyweight queen can get things back on track.
