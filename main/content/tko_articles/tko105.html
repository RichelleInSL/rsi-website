The Tiny Terror

I've said this before, and, no doubt, I'll say it again. What you see is not even CLOSE to what you get when it comes to Emma Watson. Never has something so lethal come in such an innocent looking package. I've said similar things about Emily VanCamp, and Emma fits into the same mold. Both so charming and sweet. And yet, if you look closely... if you look behind the smiles... there is a quiet intensity there. Just a tiny hint of something different. Something darker. Pity the poor soul who accidentally taps into that darkness, though. It's kinda funny that their names are so similar, too. I often refer to Emily as "Em," and I once considered referring to Emma as "little Em." I thought better of it, though. She might not like the nickname, and I don't want someone so dangerous mad at me. Think I'm exaggerating? Just ask Christobelle Grierson-Ryrie.

Don't get me wrong, I'm not saying Emma is invincible. She's taken her share of losses, including one to Christobelle, herself. But I'm talking about how quickly Emma can turn a single mistake into victory. Which is exactly what happened tonight. Christobelle took control of this one early, scoring with a few heavy shots before taking Watson to the mat. The ground game is not Emma's strong suit, but, unlike most stand up fighters, she doesn't just try to survive on the canvas. She fights. She squirms. She twists. She refuses to let her opponent get hold of her. Before Grierson-Ryrie even knew what was happening, it was Emma who had control. Though Emma scored with a few body shots, the most important thing is that she kept herself out of trouble. After some brief struggling between the fighters, the referee decided to stand things back up. And that's when the the mistake came. As Lena Gercke signalled for the battle to resume, Christobelle put the pressure on immediately. But Emma seemed to expect it, and she drove knee into the center of her opponent's face that staggered her badly. Christobelle pawed at Emma, trying to keep her at bay while she cleared her head. But Emma blocked it easily and unleashed a kick that nearly took Grierson-Ryrie's head off. The impact was so clean that it could be heard clearly throughout the arena. I really have no idea how Christobelle stayed on her feet. The Kiwi lashed out instinctively, but Emma just swatted the blow aside and landed a perfect hook to her opponent's jaw. Christobelle swung again and took another fist to her face for her trouble. With just under a minute left in the round, the young model tried to keep her distance and survive the round. But Emma pressed on, clinching and hammering away at her opponent. From that point on, it was just a question of time, and it ran out for Christobelle. A minute and a half after the referee restarted the fight, Grierson-Ryrie was bloodied and unconscious on the canvas. And that's what I meant when I said Emma was lethal.

Though she certainly looked like the better fighter in this one, Emma was, as always, quick to praise her opponent afterward. "Christobelle was terrific tonight," said the always poised Watson. "She and I have separated ourselves from the field a little bit, so she's probably going to get another shot at me, soon. I know she's going to be working hard to get her hands on this belt. My only goal is to make sure I'm ready to defend it." That's the thing about Emma. She never takes cheap shots... in or out of the cage.

Emma's win gives RSI it's first successful title defense in quite a while. And I really don't know where we'd be without her. She has proven to be a fine champion. She was instrumental in getting training partner Tiffany Mulheron... a fighter who's shown some championsip mettle of her own... turned around after her crippling loss to Nadine Coyle. She is EXACTLY the kind of person I want representing RSI. But though I've been trying to sing her praises as loudly and as often as I can, Emma still somehow manages to fly beneath the radar. And many blame that fact on me. After all... it was my decision to bring Tiffany in and place her on Britney's team instead of Emma. Hadn't she done enough to earn that spot? You bet she had. But I've also got to think ahead to RSI's future plans. And trust me... Emma is a big part of them.

Anyway... not a bad day's work for RSI's budding young superstar. And she didn't even have to use her wand. Congrats, Emma. You make me proud in more ways than I can count.

Now, where have I seen this story before? Julianne Hough and Vikki Blows stepped into the cage with the bantamweight championship on the line. And as has been the case every single time these women have faced each other, the challenger walked away with the title. The bad news for Julianne is that she entered as the champ in this one, so she was the one to leave empty handed. I'm sure I should be depressed about that, but I just can't be. The action is always blistering when these two meet up, and you just can't be unhappy after one of their fights. This one didn't disappoint either, as the two went back and forth throughout the entire round and a half of action. But Blows had a wrinkle that Jules just wasn't prepared for. After basically abandoning the ground game in their recent fights, Vikki made it the centerpiece of her game plan tonight. Julianne handled herself perfectly each time Vikki scored a takedown, but she just couldn't stay on her feet long enough to execute her own plan of attack. Eventually the grappling on the mat started to take its toll on Hough's stamina. Vikki opened up the second round with yet another takedown. Julianne fought her off once more, including a beautiful arm bar defense. But as the fighters got back to their feet, Julianne got sloppy and Blows popped her with a vicious punch that had the blonde seeing stars. Julianne struggled to hang on, but she couldn't clear her head and Vikki finished the knockout only seconds later.

As I said, it's hard to be too disappointed after witnessing a fight like this. Truth is, Vikki won because she came in with the right plan. Though Jules was amazing, as always, Vikki had her playing catch up throughout the fight. For her part, Julianne seemed to take the loss well. "Sure, I'm disappointed," said the leggy blonde. "I want that title just as much as she does. But let's face it... I'm getting pretty used to things going this way between us." In fact, Jules seems to enjoy these matchups just as much as I do. "I've said it before... I love fighting Vikki. Winning the championship should be a huge challenge, and Vikki never lets me down. I'm looking forward to doing this again, soon."

Wow. That's about the only thing I can think of to say about the show Alyson Michalka and India Reynolds put on tonight. I mean, the action is always itense and constant with these two, but they were so fierce tonight, it was absurd. As usual, India attacked as soon the bell rang, and she tore into Michalka pretty good. But Aly weathered the early onslaught and turned the tables with that patented high kick of hers. She followed that with a crushing hook that dropped the younger fighter to the canvas. Game over for Reynolds? Hardly. She got back to her feet and right back into the fight. The two exchanged blows, with both swinging for the fences. The crowd was on it's feet, sensing that the knockout could come at any moment. But somehow, both fighters were still standing when the bell sounded to end the round. No one sat down, though, as the audience was expecting more of the same in the second. And the women delivered. Back and forth they went, punishing each each other with heavy blows. It wasn't long before both were bleeding heavily, though it didn't seem to slow them down any. The war just raged on, with neither fighter really taking control. Suddenly, Aly connected with that brutal high kick again, and India finally dropped unconscious to the mat. Just like that, it was over.

This kind of action has become pretty typical of Aly and India, and the crowd ate it up with a spoon. However, you'd think getting hammered like that wouldn't really appeal to the fighters. Not so, according to Aly. "Holy shit... that was AWESOME!" bellowed the former Hellcats star as she sat down at the post fight press conference. "That may have been the most incredible fight I've ever had!" UMMA management seemed to agree, as the stunning finish earned Aly "Knockout of the Night" honors. But Aly was quick to remind everyone that it takes two to tango. "I'm sure India isn't as excited as I am about this fight," said the toned blonde, "but I think she'll look back on it and see what we did tonight. She was incredible, and everyone who watched knows it. I'm just sorry it took me so long to give her her due."

With the win, Aly inches ever closer to that title shot she's been craving. But I've talked about this in the past. That division is just too jam packed with talent to predict what may come next. Aly just needs to stay focused on what's in front of her, and not worry about the big picture. Of course, that's not a concern with Aly. Maybe before, but not now. She doesn't train to win championships, anymore. She trains to win fights. Put her in there with anyone, and Aly will be up for the challenge. I don't make much use of those nicknames Britney and Elisha handed out a while back. But Hellcat... yeah, I kinda like that one.

A nice piece of work from Jennifer Lawrence tonight, as she got herself out of some early trouble against Misa Campo. Misa was intent on pressing the action, and Jen looked a little lost in the early going. For a moment, it looked like Campo was going to score the quick victory, but Jen kept her guard up and fought through it. Then, a little over half way into the round, Jen slipped a bone crushing left from Misa and scored with a beautiful combination of her own. Having Jen get back in the fight seemed to take the wind out of Campo's sails and J-Law started to establish herself. Then, just when it looked like this one was going to go to a second round, Jen pulled a move out of Aly's bag... catching Misa with a vicious high kick that sent the Canadian model to dreamland.

The win sets Jen back on the right track after her recent loss to Melissa Giraldo. The race is pretty tight in the middleweight division, and Jen is currently caught up in a knot right in the middle. It's tough to predict who she'll face next, but there's no question she'll be ready.

The last time they met, Brittany Tacy was basically humiliated by Zaira Nara. While Brittany had the strength advantage, Zaira was clearly the better fighter as she pummeled Tacy for the full fifteen minutes, reducing her to a bloody wreck. Nara may not have gotten the knockout she wanted, but there was no question about who the winner was. Well tonight, Brittany finally got the chance for a little payback. And she did not let the opportunity pass her by.

The bell rang and Brittany immediately caught her opponent in a clinch. And she simply did not let go. What followed was 57 seconds of sheer brutality from the young fitness fanatic. Punches, kick, knees... blow after blow scored without answer. Brittany seemed intent on making a point as she unleashed her power on every part of the young Argentine's body. By the time Tacy was done, the knockout blow almost seemed like an act of mercy.

While this certainly seemed like Brittany taking revenge for the earlier humiliation, Tacy said it went much deeper than that. "This wasn't about Zaira at all. This was about me. It was bout establishing myself and fighting MY fight," said the Floridian powerhouse. "I'm tired of always trying to 'make it through' the fight. I wanted to push the fight and see what happened. And I liked what I saw."

Me too, Brittany. Me too.

Not a bad day for the rest of RSI's rookie group, either. Dianna Dahlgren proved to be a more than worthy challenger for the more experienced Johanna Lundback. An unfortunate injury to Lundback ended things early, but Dianna was terrific up to that point. The win keeps her moving up the rankings... and into an area nearly overrun with RWB competitors. Things are about to get pretty interesting for Miss Dahlgren.

After getting dropped with one punch by AJ Lee, Shelby Chesnes has been spending some time in the featherweight dog house. While no one expects perfection, teammates Emma Watson, Tiffany Mulheron and Kelly Hu were none too happy that Chesnes failed to even get her guard up. But Shelby has been working her ass off in training, and her "big sisters" have forgiven her for her mental slip-up. Still, I'm betting the pressure was raised a notch or two when she stepped in against true rookie Molly Quinn. And when she fell victim to an early takedown by the redhead, I feared for Shelby's safety at the next training session. But Chesnes kept a level head, scoring the reversal and even going for the surprise submission. Molly squirmed free, but the move clearly had her rattled. It was all Shelby after that. A quick combination... an outrageous spinning back kick... and then the thunderous punch that dropped the flustered Quinn to the canvas. This was much closer to the Shelby we expected to see. And with such a fearsome group in her corner, there's no doubt she's only going to get better.

Lastly, Sabine Jemeljanova kept her winning streak alive as she knocked out model Ashley Graham. And she did it with authority. After a few tentative exchanges between the two, Sabine unleashed a mighty uppercut that put the American fighter back on her heels. Ashley tried to keep the Latvian beauty at bay by going on the offensive, but Sabine stuffed the attempt and responded with a kick that damn near took Graham's head off. Ashley was clearly hurt by the blow, and Sabine went in for the kill... swarming her opponent with punches until she could simply take no more. The win makes it four in a row for Jemeljanova. If she keeps this up, she, too, will soon find herself in the thick of an RWB bottleneck. Somehow, that doesn't sound like much of a reward.

It's not easy being Stephanie McMahon. Though she recently received a richly deserved and long overdue management role here at RSI, she hasn't had much time to adjust to her new position. We recently lost three of our most talented young prospects in Leanna Decker, Holly Bricken and, most notably, Jamie Lynn Spears. As head of our fledgling Fighter Development Program, it would fall on Steph to recruit and develop new fighters to fill those voids. I strongly suspected that process would take weeks, if not months.

I was in my office yesterday, staring intently at my laptop screen as I considered strategy. If I could knock that pile of rocks loose, they would cascade down and destroy everything. I was pretty sure I could clear this one using only one bird, if I did it right. A small knock pulled me from my all important mission, and I looked up to see Steph standing in the doorway.

"Got a second?" she asked.

I guess the those diabolical pigs were going to have to wait.

"Sure," I said, motioning her into the office. She was carrying a small group of folders with her. "I like the folders," I chided. "They make you look very professional."

"They should," she said with a laugh. "Finding new stable members is my job now, right?"

"You filled one of the slots already?" I asked with surprise.

"Actually, I think I filled all three."

I eyed her skeptically. Finding athletes who fit into the RSI framework isn't easy. Chemistry is important, and I was sure there was no way she could have...

"The first is Anna Kendrick," she said as she handed me the top folder.

OK... she was off to a good start. To date, there are two signings I highly regret missing out on. I'm not going to spill the beans on who the first one was, in case we ever get another shot at her. But the second one was Anna Kendrick. She fits the Emma Watson/Jennifer Lawrence mold in the way she's kept herself fully grounded despite her fairly quick rise to stardom. But she's got a dash of Ashley Tisdale's grit, with a side of Aly Michalka's focus to go with it. And if nothing else, she'd be entertaining on long flights. All she needs is a cup.

It's impossible to predict whether or not a rookie will develop into an elite fighter. The best you can do is look for the athlete with the best raw skills and the right character traits. On paper, Anna is the complete package, and I was sure she was going to be highly sought after when Ginny's Academy announced they were folding. This signing is an absolute coup for RSI, and a nice opening salvo from our new development coordinator. As an added bonus, it makes me look like a genius for moving Steph into the role.

"How did you pull that off?" I asked her.

"I have my ways," she replied coyly.

I smiled and nodded, clearly impressed. "Well done," I said. I gestured toward the remaining folders. "What else do you have in your little pile of goodies?"

"The next one's a true rookie," she said. "We've had pretty good success with the fitness athletes we've brought on board in the past, and there was someone there that caught my eye."

No doubt, Steph was right. Like all rookies, Allira Cohrs, Brittany Tacy, Dianna Dahlgren and Marzia Prince have had their ups and downs. But there is no question that they are dedicated and talented. And of course, Jennifer Stano's recent travails have been the stuff of legend. All in all, these women have worked out perfectly in every possible way. I suppose that makes sense. Coming from a competitive fitness background, these women have proven their dedication and athletic ability before they even walk in the door. But that's not all it takes to succeed in the UMMA. Outside of physical ability, one of the most important skills a fighter needs is the ability to be entertaining. Many in the fitness arena are, understandably, hard-nosed and singularly focused. It's tough to find one of these women who have the type of engaging personality it takes to gain a following in the UMMA. There are a few who come to mind, but the list is very short.

"Her name is Michelle Lewin," Steph continued, eyeing me knowingly.

That, in case you're wondering, would be the name at the very top of said list. Michelle Lewin is a physical specimen who, arguably, has no equal. I'll leave the detailing of her physical features to the UMMA's male fans, but there can be no doubt that achieving such results takes the focused, hard-nosed approach I just mentioned. But she's also got personality to spare. She puts a lot of effort into connecting with her fans, often taking to Twitter and Facebook to chat and share pics. Speaking of which, her camera is a constant companion and she has no problem popping a "fan service" selfie at any given moment. In fact, she generally has an SLR camera handy, because, you know... that low-res camera phone just can't provide the quality her fans deserve. Her accompanying posts are always fun and flirty, but her very appearance is a testament to her drive and dedication.

Now, I'm sure you must be wondering... if she's so damn perfect, why haven't I signed her already? Simple. She said no. I have approached Michelle a number of times, from every angle I could think of. She was always polite, but also always direct. She had no interest in MMA. Eventually, I just gave it up. Now, here was Steph, giving me that look that said she was well aware of my inability to convince Ms. Lewin to join us. I could only guess that she came by that knowledge with the help of the lovely, yet disturbingly annoying, Danneel Harris. Clearly, I had yet another pairing on my hands that was going to give me indigestion. But if a little heartburn was what it took to land talents like Anna Kendrick and Michelle Lewin, then I could live with that.

We sat quietly for a while, the smirk never leaving Stephanie's face. Finally, I could take no more. "OK, smartass. How'd you do it?"

"I told you..." she began.

"Right, right," I cut in, waving dismissively. "You have your ways." I feigned annoyance, but I don't think the big smile on my face was very convincing. The truth is that these were big signings for RSI. Clearly, Steph was much better at this than I was. But that was the point, right?

"This last one's going to be something of a project," she said as she lifted the final folder from her lap. "She's a veteran fighter who's shown some talent, but she hasn't been able to put it all together."

"Oh," I said teasingly. "You don't have another blockbuster to drop on me?"

I took a sip of lukewarm coffee from my Goofy mug. With Mickey having been collateral damage in my recent, uh... spirited discussion... with Danni, I was forced to call the Goofster in off the bench for a little while.

"Her name is Kristen Bell."

The mug slipped from my hand as I struggled to keep the coffee in my mouth from spewing everywhere. I re-gripped and managed to secure it, but a healthy dose of liquid hit the floor. Careful, I thought. If you break two in the same month, they'll kick you out of The Club.

"Damn... that was hotter than I thought," I lied in an attempt to cover myself. Though I have quietly indulged my little obsession a few times in my role as head of RSI, there was no need to just throw it out into the open if I didn't have to. I pulled myself together and looked over to see if Steph had any appreciation for the real impact her little announcement had on me. Not surprisingly, the enormous "cat that ate the canary" grin on her face told me she knew EXACTLY what she'd done.

God. Damn. That. Harris.

Alright... so maybe I have a little... fondness... for all things Disney. I'm able to keep it under control... most of the time. And it's never had a negative impact on our stable. Sure... maybe Britney's MMC connection helped persuade me to bring her on board. But is she not one of the most capable fighters in the entire UMMA middleweight group? And yes... I knew who Alyson Michalka was before I'd even heard of the UMMA. But in a division where simply surviving is an accomplishment, Aly is a force to be reckoned with. And if anything, my prior familiarity with Ashley Tisdale gave me some insight into the "never say die" attitude that's helped her find her footing in her division. Oh, and the Kelly Hu signing was all business. Her connection to Disney - and the aforementioned Ms. Tisdale - is minor. At most, it serves as a little inside joke between the three of us.

Now, I love very one of these women, and I will protect them with every ounce of my being... as I would any member of the RSI family. But I'm not such a Disney fangirl that I treat them like superstars. They're entertainers, who have fought to make a name for themselves in the face of long odds. That fight brought them into the Disney family, and my love of Disney helped bring them into the RSI family. I love having them here, and I could not imagine RSI without them. I am not, however, starstruck by their Disney past.

But Kristen is different. Kristen is newly minted Disney royalty.

The idea that every little girl dreams of being a princess has become awkwardly dated as the years have gone by. But I personally think it still holds true. Certainly, characters such as Snow White, Cinderella and Aurora, while still cherished, are no longer the ideal for a world of fierce, independent young women. But as the world has changed, so has the Disney concept of what a princess is. And for the most part, they've gotten it right, as each new princess has been more daring and self-sufficient than the one that preceded her. But the more things change, the more they stay the same. Though Disney has created a couple of true "princess warriors," the more traditional princesses continued to have one thing in common with the royalty of old. They needed a man to save them.

Then came Princess Anna. She is a traditional princess in every way you can think of. She is sweet. She is kind. She is not a fighter. She is not trained in ancient martial arts. But she has no problem being exactly who she is, and she doesn't worry much about what others think of that. And if a problem arises, she can handle it. End of story.

I can go on and on about the depth and appeal of Anna's character. But I think my Disney side has had enough fun for one press release. The point is, as little girls grow up, maybe the dream changes. Maybe they don't want to be a princess, any more. But what woman would give up the chance to MAKE a princess? Who would pass up the opportunity to shape and define a character that would, in all likelihood, touch the hearts of millions of little girls around the world? Princess Anna is a character unlike any Disney princess that came before her. She is an instant classic. And it was Kristen Bell that made her what she is. Beyond that, Kristen has made it quite clear in her interviews and comments that she's going to own Anna over the long haul... that it was more than just a job for her. She put a little piece of herself into the character, and she's damn proud of what she accomplished.

So, yeah... this one's going to take a little getting used to, for me.

Back to the canary eater. While these thoughts were swirling around in my head, I struggled mightily to keep my face expressionless. I could tell Steph was looking for a reaction, and I'd be damned if I was going to give her one. Once I pulled myself together though, I did give her her due.

"You did a hell of a job with these," I said, holding the up the folders. "I have to admit: you're a lot better at this than I was. This is great work."

She smiled at the praise, but I caught the quick look of disappointment in her eyes.

Nice try, wiseass.

With no more folders to present, McMahon rose to make her exit.

"With the roster refilled," she said, "I guess it's time to get to work on a training regimen."

"That's been mostly Danni's area, up until now," I said.

Then I shot her a knowing look over the top of my glasses.

"I get the impression you two will have no trouble working together," I added sarcastically.

"None at all," she said with a wry smile. And off she went.

Alone in my office, I allowed my inner Disney geek just one more little moment in the sun.

"God damn," I said aloud. "We signed Princess Anna."

No sooner had the words left my mouth, when Steph's head angled back into the door frame.

"I heard that," she said with a satisfied smile.

"Go away, McMahon," I said, wearing a big smile of my own. "Go far, far away."

One more thing: remember when I said I was a genius for promoting Stephanie to this role? Forget it. A genius would have gotten her involved back at the beginning. I, on the other hand, am an idiot. And because of that, we almost lost Steph for good.

Now, if you'll excuse me, I need to go figure out what I'm going to wear when I meet Kristen. After all, when in the presence of royalty, one must dress appropriately.

Well, that's all from me for this event. But with the team event coming up, everyone at RSI is pretty busy. Among the busiest is Cobie Smulders, who has been tapped by team leader Elisha Cuthbert to fill in while Amanda Seyfried continues to recover from the rib injury she suffered at the Hit the Beach tournament. Now, I know Elisha prefers to keep things in house when she needs a substitute, and I love her for it. Though not every fighter at RSI is involved in these team events, we're all part of the same family. When one goes down, another should step up and take her place. But this is a tall order for Cobie, as her opponent is going to be the much more experienced Kate Beckinsale. I know Marzia Prince pulled off a stunning win when Elisha called her number not long ago, but it will be hard for Cobie to repeat that kind of success. Can she do it? I only know that Cobie is going to come in ready and put it all on the line for her teammates. Join us next time, and we'll find out if that's enough to pull out the win.
