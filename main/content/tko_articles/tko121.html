Deserving Diva

She works, and she slaves... and what good does it do her? Sure, she's been the key to victory countless times for her RSI teammates. A demanding taskmaster who squeezes every last drop of ability from her charges. They may hate her during training, but they always seem to love her when fight night comes around. But what good has any of this done for Stephanie McMahon?

Well, if the unwavering support of an entire stable counts for anything, then I guess it's done her a lot of good.

It's no secret that, while she's been instrumental in helping stable leader Danneel Harris and myself build RSI into a strong, solid stable, Steph has struggled in her own career. Once the clear champion of the DEF super heavyweight division, Steph did not handle the move to the SCQ well. Reunited with old nemesis Veronika Zemanova, Steph was unable to wrestle the senior circuit title away from the Czechian adult film star. Twice she was upended by silly injuries. The last time, Zemanova was just better. In fact, Veronika seemed to get stronger each time out. Meanwhile, Steph seemed to be coming apart. Difficult losses. Disappointments at team competitions. Suddenly, she was slipping down the rankings, and the title seemed to be slipping away.

But Steph fought through it, coming back with a renewed sense of confidence and purpose. Partial credit for that confidence has to go to Danni. She was the one who made me see what Steph could do for us... and how we could show her that we hadn't lost faith in her.

Still, the road to the top was long and littered with pitfalls. Sometimes she slipped. Most of the time, she didn't. But when a fantastic run at last year's Red Carpet tournament ended in a disastrous injury... no one knew what to expect. On top of that, once Steph finished her arduous rehab, guess who was waiting for her. Yep. Zemanova.

Steph's first event after the layoff was the team event, and her counterpart was none other than Veronika Zemanova. Talk about having to jump straight into the fire. Was the momentum she built at the tournament going to be enough to carry her, or had the layoff stalled her again?

Once the bell rang, the answer was clear. Nothing was going to stop Stephanie McMahon. She hit Veronika like a wrecking ball, leaving her laid out on the canvas before the end of the first round.

No doubt about it... Steph was back.

Now, here we were a few months later, and it was Steph and Veronika once again... this time with the individual title on the line. Would Veronika get her revenge and once again deny Steph the title she craved?

Not a chance in hell.

Steph smashed her way through Zemanova, needing less than two minutes to drop her nemesis this time. In fact, she never gave the Czechian the chance to do a damn thing. McMahon was textbook as the effort she had put into being ready for this fight came shining through. And every one of her students/teammates was watching and cheering her on.

"Did you see Steph torch Zemanova's ass?" said Anna Kendrick, a young woman who's known for simply saying what she's thinking. "She was amazing!"

"We all knew Steph still rocked," said Stephanie's team leader, Britney Spears. "Now, everyone knows."

Want more? How about a word from Steph's friend and stable leader, Danni Harris?

"We all knew Steph could do this," Danni said. "None of us doubted her for a second. She's the one responsible for the success RSI has been having, and there is no one more deserving of this title than she is. I only hope our support and encouragement had something to do with her win tonight."

Meanwhile, when her turn came, the newly crowned champion stuck a more humble note.

"I was just happy to get another shot at it," she said of being able to fight for the championship. "There was a time when it looked like I'd missed my chance."

Meanwhile, she was remarkably complimentary of her opponent. "It's no secret Veronika and I don't like each other. It's not really personal, though," she said. "Truth is, it's just what happens when two people are constantly battling for the same championship. Veronika is easily the toughest opponent I've ever faced, and she's shown it, time and time again. So, I may not like her, but I have to give her credit for what she can do."

For my part, I never lost confidence in Steph. She's been with us since the beginning here at RSI, and I had always intended for her to be one of the faces of the stable. I'm only sorry I didn't appreciate all she could do sooner.

You're the one who keeps things running on the training floor, Steph. Every one of your teammates owes you a debt of gratitude... a debt they are more than happy to repay. You have a lot of happy friends tonight. So, try to let it go and enjoy yourself, for once, huh?

The last time Roselyn Sanchez and Ali Larter met up in the cage, it was not a pretty picture for the RSI fighter. Ali was in control throughout the entirety of the first round, putting a beaten Sanchez out of her misery with just a few seconds left in the frame.

Could Roselyn recover from such a beating? Would she be ready when it came time for the rematch?

Well, when Emily VanCamp is your training partner, you don't really get a choice.

Em has been drilling Roselyn relentlessly since that day, refusing to let up and refusing to accept excuses. Let's just say it hasn't been a happy time for Ms. Sanchez. But when it comes to prep work, no one does it better than Emily. As the foremost rival to UMMA legend Irina Sheik, Emily works hard to constantly stay in top form. So, Roselyn may not have liked her torture sessions, but she was willing to suffer through it if it gave her the result she was hoping for.

And it sure as hell did.

This fight looked shockingly similar to the one that preceded it. Once again, one fighter dominated the action throughout. Once again, one fighter left her opponent bloodied on the canvas with mere seconds left in the first round.

The difference?

This time, it was Ros doing the dominating.

"She was terrific," Emily said. "She was so calm and focused. Everything looked just like we'd done it in training. She was perfect."

As for Roselyn, the biggest emotion she was feeling afterward was relief. "This is a huge weight off my shoulders," she said. "If I have any hope of winning a title, I know I have to go through Ali. I know she's going to come back harder and stronger next time, but now I know I can compete with her."

Roselyn still has a lot to prove. Her record has always been spotty, and, though she's risen to the top contender's spot in the SCQ lightweight division, she's still light years behind Larter in overall ranking. But tonight was a huge step in the right direction. If she can establish a little consistency, who knows how far she can go?

Catherine Bell reaffirmed her position as the champion of the heavyweight division with a decisive win over former title holder, Tricia Helfer. There was no joy in it, though, as the fight ended with a brutal injury to Helfer. Mac elected to skip the post-fight press conference, issuing only a brief statement wishing Tricia a speedy recover. All of us here at RSI echo that sentiment. Pain aside, we all know how an injury can derail a fighter. Hopefully, Helfer will heal quickly and get back on track.

Torrie Wilson had another tough night, tonight, as Kim Kardashian seems to have exposed a weakness in Torrie's game. After falling to a stunning Kardashian submission at Open Fight Night, Torrie was forced to tap out again here in St. Louis. Just like with Kardashian, Torrie looked to be establishing control over opponent Gabrielle Richens when things suddenly went wrong. Obviously, we need to take a hard look at Torrie's training regimen and shore things up, going forward.

A nice win for Kelly Hu tonight, as she took care of business against Demi Moore. With surprising wins over Grace Park and even Rachel McAdams on her record, Kelly has proven she has the tools to beat anyone. But much like Roslyn, she has yet to show the consistency she needs to climb the rankings. This makes two in a row for her after returning from an injury layoff. Hopefully, it's the start of something big, for her.

A pair of injuries left a hole in the heavyweight rankings for this event, and it was left to Marzia Prince to step in and fill it. Opponent Jeri Ryan was a huge step up for Marzia, but she's always made the most of the opportunities she's been given. That pattern continued here tonight, as Marzia put in a highlight reel performance against her veteran opponent. Though Ryan scored a pair of takedowns against the relatively inexperienced Prince, Marzia fought her off and kept her cool until the fighters got back to their feet. Whenever they were upright, Marzia put on a stunning fistic display. Jeri showed her toughness, pushing her way through the first two rounds. But the outcome was never in doubt, in this one. It was all Marzia from wire to wire.

Marzia's overall record may not be that impressive, but, with wins over Ryan and Traci Bingham, she's proven herself capable when the pressure is on. I'm confident she's is going to find her stride soon, and I'm expecting big things from her.

A little personnel news to catch up on... the multitasking Stephanie McMahon managed to make time during her training for tonight's fight to score a couple of big signings for RSI. Things have not been good recently for Anna Silk, but, thanks to Steph, I'm confident the 'Lost Girl' has found her way home. Her results may not be all that glorious, but Steph is confident she can turn things around. And when Steph is confident, I'm confident.

Steph wasn't finished, however... she then surprised me with the news that she had picked up the savior herself... the recently released Emma Swan. Er, I mean Jennifer Morrison. It's true that Jennifer has not had the kind of success she expected in the UMMA, but I think that's about to change. She's shown the kind of patience and determination she needs to get herself on track. Now, she's going to have the training she needs, too.

It hasn't worked out so far, but Petra's shown no signs of giving up. Will she finally break through? Danni's been pretty solid throughout the history of this rivalry, but Kristanna has certainly had her moments... including stealing the belt away from the RSI leader a little over a year ago. Will Danni maintain her dominance, or will the seasoned Loken surprise her again? Tune in to find out.
