Off to a Good Start

Both of RSI's new teams got off to good starts at this event, with each only dropping one match. The team captains gave a full report at the post event news conference.

Britney Spears: Tough match for you tonight, Leesh. A tough ending, too.

Elisha Cuthbert: Yeah, I really have a problem with the way it ended. Laura Vandervoort was really taking it to me out there, and I was bleeding pretty bad. But, Rebecca Loos decides that a little cut on my nose is the reason to end the fight? Totally ridiculous.

Britney: I agree. I've seen you come back from worse than that. Still... what happened?

Elisha: Gee, I dunno. I think it started when I let Laura punch me in the face.

Britney: Yeah. That probably wasn't a good way to start.

Elisha: She got me good, and I had trouble recovering. Credit to her for pressing her advantage while she had the chance. I don't agree with the stoppage, but she was definitely kicking my ass. Anyway, enough about me... let's get to the report. Things didn't start out too well for you guys...

Britney: No, not at all. Hayden Panettiere came out swinging against Julianne Hough, and Jules made her look silly. Hayden missed so bad, she literally fell on her ass. But then Julianne made the terrible decision to try and take advantage of the fall. Hayden's awesome on the ground... maybe the best in the UMMA. Julianne never stood a chance, and Hayden must have felt like she won the lottery. Jules paid for her mistake as Hayden just worked her over. Typical Hayden, she ended it by stripping the helpless Julianne... even slapping her tits in front of the fans. I guarantee Julianne won't do something that stupid, again.

Elisha: Being a little hard on her, aren't you?

Britney: Yeah, I guess I am. I don't mean to be. It just pisses me off, what happened to her. Hayden is such a bitch, and I know Julianne is better than that. I hope Jules knocks her teeth out, next time they meet. Anyway... things started to go our way after that. The matchup between Tiffany Mulheron and Melanie Chisholm ended in an ugly injury, but at least it was a win. Then, things really got rolling. I actually felt bad for Summer Glau, tonight. She's a decent fighter, but Emily VanCamp just took her apart.

Elisha: Well, that'll happen when you spend all your time training to compete with Irina Sheik. Em is basically a finely honed weapon, at this point. I wouldn't want to have to face her, I'll tell you that.

Britney: That's for sure. Next up was Danni. I expected a war between her and Brittany Daniel, but the overprotective Dita Von Teese stopped this one before it even started. I've been smart enough to never let Danni kick me in the head, so I don't know exactly what it feels like. I imagine it sucks, but I think Brittany could have recovered. Or, at least, let Danni finish things. Stopping it the way she did just left everybody disappointed.

Elisha: A win is a win, hon.

Britney: I know, but it's supposed to be entertaining. And Brittany deserved the chance to fight on. Anyway... I was up next, and I honestly don't want to talk about it. I got the win, and I felt bad for Naya Rivera. She wasn't ready for this. End of story. Next was Aly, and she took care of business against Simmone MacKinnon. Catherine Bell looked awesome as she took out Shannon Elizabeth, and Steph finished things up, winning yet another mismatch against Rhona Mitra. So, as a team, we had a great night. And, you guys looked great, too.

Elisha: We sure did. In fact, I'm pretty disappointed in myself for being the only one who lost.

Britney: Oh, shut up. Laura's a top competitor. Nothing to hang your head about.

Elisha: Awww. Thanks, hon. That's sweet. In fact, it's a little too sweet.

Britney: Besides, you did really well for someone as old as you.

Elisha: Ah... THERE it is. You do know I'm younger than you are, right?

Britney: Yeah, but I didn't get my ass kicked, so...

Elisha: Oh... funny. By the way, what's your record against me, again?

Britney: Touché.

Elisha: Damn right. Anyway, the rest of the team looked great out there. Danica set the tone early, knocking Victoria Beckham silly in just seconds. Kelly Hu had to survive an early takedown by Anna Kournikova, but she stayed cool and knocked Anna stupid once they got back to her feet.

Britney: That was a great effort from her. Kelly's not that great on the ground, but she hung in there with the best of them. Did I see her try for the submission?

Elisha: (nods) She tried the RNC, but she couldn't get it locked in. Still, she had Anna wondering what the hell would come next. Great work from her. Next came Roselyn, and she was almost as fast as Danica in getting the KO. Amanda Seyfried was very impressive as she dismantled Maria Kirilenko.

Britney: She even got a marriage proposal out of the deal.

Elisha: Oh, geez... did you see that guy? Blech! Anyway... after I blew my match, Daniela picked us right back up, taking care of business against a clearly unprepared Jenna Pietersen.

Britney: Tough ending, though.

Elisha: Yeah. Sending Jenna out there was not a good idea, and she paid a high price. But, Daniela did what she's supposed to, and that's that. Next came Petra, and she did a nice job against Jennifer Hawkins.

Britney: The fans didn't think too much of that one.

Elisha: Everyone's a critic. Petra's job is to fight whoever she's supposed to fight, and that's what she did. Besides, Torrie Wilson certainly made up for any disappointment the fans were feeling.

Britney: Holy shit, yeah! She was awesome!

Elisha: I was a little worried about Torrie in this one. Monica Bellucci's got a ton of experience, and... look, I know Torrie's much better than her record shows, but her record is still her record. So, I expected her to surprise some people tonight, but...

Britney: But nobody expected THIS!

Elisha: Not at all. Torrie was ridiculous! She absolutely ripped Bellucci to shreds. Monica never knew what hit her.

Britney: She was great. People are definitely going to start expecting bigger things from Torrie, if she keeps performing like that.

Elisha: No doubt. So, all in all, a great start for both teams.

Britney: Definitely. But, of course, we were better.

Elisha: What the hell are you talking about? Both teams had the same record.

Britney: Yeah, but our team captain came through for us. That has to count for something.

Elisha: (jumping to her feet and heading for the door) Oh, you BITCH! That's it! You and me... cage... RIGHT NOW!

Britney: (rising to follow) Bring it on, blue eyes!


Oh, good lord...

Well, anyway, I'm proud of the way both teams performed tonight. They certainly showed they came to play.
