Untouchable

Back at the start of the year, Catherine Bell suffered a difficult leg injury while facing Carmen Electra at the Kamikaze event. At the time, Catherine's plight was completely overlooked. It wasn't even reported in the TKO edition for that event. What has also been overlooked is the fact that, since returning from that injury, Catherine has totally destroyed everyone she's faced. Four fights, four first round knockouts. Not only that, opponents have barely laid a glove on the Army Wives star. And, tonight, she added the aforementioned Carmen Electra to her list of victims.

Carmen came out swinging, but she never even got close. A couple of vicious counters and a mighty high kick from Catherine, and the Baywatch beauty was laid out on the canvas.

When asked about her recent string of domination, Catherine stayed true to her professional nature.

"I come to win, every time. I work hard to prepare, and work hard to know my opponent. Sometimes it works, and sometimes it doesn't. But, it's really all you can do."

Well, it's been working pretty good, lately. Though she still has a long road ahead of her, Catherine has made it clear that she plans to be a force to be reckoned with in the SCQ Heavyweight division.

Stephanie McMahon has been having a real tough time of it, lately. When she joined us here at RSI, she had only 2 losses in 30 fights. With tonight's loss to Uma Thurman, she has since gone 5-6.

Doesn't sound like we're helping much, does it? What gives Steph?

"I think I have to face the facts… everyone around me has gotten better. It's especially difficult in the SCQ, but it's true all across the weight class."

It's hard to argue with her. Most of the superheavyweight contenders have signed on with management companies, and clearly they are all moving in the right direction. Even Veronika Zemanova, who remains staunchly independent, has stepped up her game. But, Steph knows what to do when the going gets tough.

"I just have to keep working to improve. Maybe I'm not as dominant as I was, but I know I can still beat anyone. My goal is still the same. I want to be the best. It's just getting harder to accomplish."

After she suffered a brutal beating at the hands of Nadine Coyle at the Global Rivalry event, I made a point of telling Roseyln Sanchez that things were going to start changing for her, right then and there. Well, clearly, the change has begun, as she was simply flawless in tonight's performance against rookie Kim Raver. After a quick feeling out, Roselyn simply hammered Kim, who looked badly over-matched.

While not exactly the equivalent of beating Coyle, this was a nice showing for Roselyn.

"I'm happy with how things went, tonight. I know I have a long way to go, but the way to get there is to just handle my business, one match at a time."

After a nice win over Sarah Silverman at the Harvest of Pain event, Marzia Prince celebrated with one eye on the future, wondering when her lack of experience would catch up with her. Well, that answer is still unknown, as she made short work of WWE legend Trish Stratus here tonight. And, as far as experience goes, Trish certainly has it. With 43 fights under her belt coming into tonight, it certainly seemed Marzia would have plenty to worry about. But, as in her previous fights, Marzia's fists were just too much for her opponent. Trish took what seemed a smart strategy, looking to control Marzia's power from the clinch. But, Marzia made her pay, brutalizing Stratus with her clinch striking game and dropping the retired wrestler in just under a minute and a half.

Though cautious of becoming too confident, Marzia certainly seemed pleased with her victory.

"I'm really happy about the way things went tonight," said the bodacious Texan. "Trish is on a different level from the girls I've faced, so far. My goal coming in was just to stay competitive in this one. So, for me, the win was just over the top."

No question, it was a performance to be proud of.

I have to give a little love to Torrie Wilson, tonight. Pulling out the win is not an easy task when you blow the game plan right out of the gate. Coming in to her matchup with Natasha Henstridge, the number one rule for Torrie was to stay on her feet. Fight time comes, and she's on the ground in less than 30 seconds. Not good. But, Wilson stayed with it, showing ground skills I didn't know she had. She held Natasha off for nearly 4 full minutes before Nell McAndrew finally decided to stand things up. With only seconds remaining in the round, Torrie served notice that she was done playing games, smashing her foot into Natasha's face. The bell saved Henstridge from further punishment, but not for long. She tried to regain control in the second round, but Torrie was too fast and too elusive, countering with brutal punches that floored her Canadian opponent.

Though happy with the outcome, Torrie was not exactly thrilled with her performance.

"When she took me down at the start, I was just pissed. There's no other word for it. My team warned me to stay on my feet, and I blew it right away. I was furious at myself."

Fury, however, can be a good thing.

"I was so angry… I just refused to lose. I was going to hold on. Signing with RSI was supposed to be a change for me, and, goddammit, things were gonna change."

And change they did. So, I give Torrie a lot of credit for what she accomplished here, tonight. But, next time, let's try to stick with the plan, OK?

Lately, it seems every press release ends with me introducing the new members of the RSI team. Well, this one is no different, as we have four new women joining the organization. And, boy… are they gonna liven up the party. RSI prides itself on being a place where talented, intelligent women can come and compete. So, a "nude glamour model" and a pair of Playboy models don't exactly fit into that equation. Add in "Miss Supercross," herself, and there's really no question that I've lost my mind.

Let's start with current Super Heavyweight rookie Sabine Jemeljanova. Though she's only been with the UMMA for a short time, we've been impressed with the skills Sabine has shown so far. With a little focus and direction, we're confident she can be competitive in one of the toughest divisions the UMMA has to offer.

Floridian Shelby Chesnes was the featured… performer? Model? Whatever. She was Playboy's Playmate of the Month for July 2011. Though it was a moment she's proud of, it wasn't all fun and games. First of all, there were the awkward moments when she had to tell her family. Chesnes had taken a couple of camera phone pictures in the mirror and uploaded them to Playboy's website, figuring if she didn't get chosen, no one would ever know. And then the call came… they wanted her to come to the Playboy Mansion - yes, the mansion - and have test shots taken.

So, she sat down to tell her mother she had some news. While her mother wasn't exactly thrilled, she was relieved that Shelby wasn't pregnant. Her father, meanwhile, was willing to let his daughter see it through, just as long as he never had to see the pictures. Her brother also refuses to look, though he has no trouble bragging to his friends about it. Then, there was her big sister, whom Shelby calls her "surrogate mom."

"Why can't you do something normal, like work in a restaurant?" she asked Chesnes.

And, while Shelby's loves the attention the shoot has brought, there have been some creepy moments.

"Oh, yeah… like the guy driving all the way from New York to take pictures of me," she said, laughing. "It's OK. We have bodyguards. They were like, 'Keep an eye on that guy.'"

For all her newfound fame, she still sees herself as the kid who was good at math and science - the reason she now studies psychology at Florida Atlantic University - and was a travel-team competitive cheerleader while at Jupiter High.

So, with a successful modeling career in full swing and highly sought after degree on the way, why try MMA?

"Though most people don't consider cheerleaders to be athletes, there's no question it takes a lot of athletic skill. And, I thrived on the competition. So, I think maybe that's what's behind it."

Or, maybe it's simpler than that.

"Maybe it's like my sister said… I can't just do something normal."

Clearly a woman of the modern age, Leanna Decker is Playboy's Cybergirl of the Year for 2012. I think that means that, while Shelby's pics need to be scanned in before they can be spread all over the internet, Leanna's come prepped and ready to go. Well, whatever it means, Leanna has the cyber thing down to a science. She is everywhere. You can look her up on Twitter, Facebook or countless modeling sites. And, if that's not enough, you can apparently sign up to have her send her pictures straight to your inbox.

Men of the world, rejoice.

Oh, and in case you're wondering, Leanna offers this insightful message.

"My boobs are real!"

Yeah. This is gonna be fun.

So, why risk her good looks in brutal cage fights against other women?

"I'm a competitive person, no question. So, that's part of it," said the West Virginia native. "But, I also love taking risks. And, cage fighting definitely has a lot of risk. I plan to train and prepare and be better than my opponent. But, what if I'm not? That question might scare some people, but I love it."

That sounds pretty good, but Ms. Decker wasn't done.

"I also have a mean streak in me. The thought of tearing up another girl in the cage… mmmmmmmm! It get's me hot just thinking about it."

Good God. Is there really any question that I'm going to live to regret this signing? Still, there's something about Leanna that made me decide to go for it. I'm pretty sure it's the hair.

So, what exactly does one have to do to be named "Miss Supercross," anyway?

"Well, you basically have to get yourself noticed by the company that sponsor's the Supercross circuit," laughed new recruit Dianna Dahlgren. "Then, they make up a title for you, and you're on your way."

And, while the gig has certainly paid dividends for Dianna's modeling career, it also allowed her to tap into her love for the sport.

"I'm a fan of Supercross because I grew up going to motocross tracks with my dad and brother, watching my brother ride and race since I was little. It really is a part of me!"

You'd think a successful career that allowed her to be a part of a sport she loves would be enough. But, Dianna was too competitive for that.

"As soon as Supercross ends each May, I get my ass in gear to prepare for a summer of fitness competitions."

So, why add MMA on top of all that?

"Competitive fitness is great, but a lot of it is subjective. I wanted to test myself against other women in a pure competition, and it doesn't get any purer than this. You either kick ass, or get your ass kicked. No in-between."

Well, at least she knows what she's in for.
