The More The Merrier

As usual, RSI has used the quiet time during the team event to make some roster changes. Only this time "some," means a ton. Between trying to balance the difference weight classes and planning for the future, RSI is proud to announce it has signed eight new women to it's roster.

As usual, we searched for women who would fit into the RSI culture. Women who show heart, determination and, most importantly, self-respect. In some cases, you have to dig into a person's personality to find these attributes. And, sometimes, you just know. When we went looking for an experienced fighter to add to our bantamweight group, no digging was required. As soon as Danica Patrick's name came up, my only thought was, "How have we not signed her already?" In her "day job," she competes in a sport where women are not generally accepted. Dogged with negative and often barbaric criticism on a regular basis, she faces it head on, determined to accomplish her goals. Clearly, she is a perfect fit for the RSI team. The only question was, would she find RSI to be the perfect fit for her? "I was cautiously optimistic when RSI contacted me," said Patrick. "I'm not really a big 'team player,' to be honest. I like doing things my own way. But, if there was an organization that could convince me, I knew RSI was going to be it." And, as usual, it was the existing members of the RSI roster that made the deal happen.

"After meeting with Ashley (Tisdale) and Julianne (Hough), I was sold," continued the diminutive driver. Then, she added with a laugh, "I mean... if they could do for me what they've done for Julianne... that would be AWESOME."

RSI will be behind you all the way, Danica. But you'll have to talk to Julianne about where she found her extra magic. We can't claim credit for that.

It's no secret that we're always looking for ways to push our fighters to the next level. And, when a fighter is already teetering on the edge, we make it our mission to find what we need to give her that little bit extra. You don't get any closer to the edge than where Emily VanCamp is right now, so we searched high and low for a fighter that could give her a challenge. Maybe someone who had a little bit of a chip on her shoulder, herself. We found what we were looking for in the experienced, yet consistently overlooked Roselyn Sanchez.

Both women seem pretty pleased with the idea of working together.

"I've been holding my own out there, but I just haven't been able to move ahead," admitted Sanchez. "I have no doubt that training with Emily will force me to up my game."

Emily seemed to be thinking along the same lines.

"Roselyn competes in a tough division. There's a lot of experience in that group, but she's been able to hang in there. Someone who can survive that way is definitely someone I want to learn from."

Next stop, the welterweight division. As I recall, a certain senior member of the RSI stable recently made a smart-ass remark about how her management company wasn't giving her the challenge she needed. Well, how does FAC contender Amanda Seyfried work for you, Harris?

"It's perfect," said the RSI team leader. "Someone of Amanda's skill level is exactly what I was hoping for."

Not only does Danni see a challenge as a fighter, she also sees a chance to flex her leadership muscles.

"Amanda's been having a tough time of it, lately. I'm hoping I can help her get things back on track."

Amanda, meanwhile, needed no convincing.

"I need to get myself straightened out. I know I need help, and Danni is just the kind of help I'm looking for."

Sure. No credit for your new organization or it's supremely intelligent owner. I see how it is.

I wasn't done with RSI's team leader quite yet, though. Since she's so eager for a challenge, we also picked up rookie sensation Cobie Smulders.

"Fantastic," said Harris, clearly looking forward to having a hand in molding the talented Smulders.

But, I still had one more surprise: we also decided to grab the recently released Jamie Lynn Spears.

"No fucking way," said Danni, shutting me down before I could get the word "Lynn" out of my mouth. "There is no way I'm dealing with another Spears."

Sorry, Danni. If I have to do it, you have to do it. Just look at it this way... Elisha's sister might want to get into the MMA business someday, too.

Another UMMA fighter who's been having a tough time lately is light heavyweight Daniela Hantuchova. And since RSI already has an aggressive young fighter who needs an edge, yet another match was made. Alyson Michalka has struggled in one of the most competitive divisions in the UMMA. And, though Krystal Forscutt has shown she's much better than her record indicates, Aly needs someone with more experience to help her push through.

When we told Aly about the plan to bring Hantuchova on board, she just smiled and made her way into the locker room with a spring in her step that I've never seen from her before.

Daniela, meanwhile, wasn't shy about praising the young Michalka.

"Alyson has had to face some very difficult fighters," said the Slovakian tennis star. "To win at all must be very tough. But, she has shown she can win against anyone. To have that kind of determination and heart... this is definitely someone I want to work with."

Hantuchova wasn't done, though, as she also offered a few words on the rejuvenated Krystal.

"To come back from where she was... this takes a lot of heart, as well. I definitely think the three of us can help each other become better."

Her English may be a little stilted, but I certainly like the things she has to say.

If Krystal Forscutt has taught us anything, it's that, just because a woman takes her clothes off for a living, it doesn't mean she isn't capable of something more. On the surface, Petra Cubonova appears to be famous simply because she was graced with good looks.

That doesn't seem to be enough for her, though.

"I am more than just beautiful woman," said Cubonova. "I want to be more. I have shown, here in UMMA, I CAN be more."

With a record of 24-19 in a division lead by the dominating Adriana Lima, it's hard to argue with her. And newly acquired RSI heavyweight Catherine Bell certainly liked the idea of bringing Petra into the fold.

"Working with Holly (Bricken) and Marzia (Prince_ is awesome. Both have shown a lot of talent, and it's exciting to help them develop," said Bell. "But adding someone like Petra will only make things better."

Sidelined by a bad injury, Petra is looking to come back strong.

"I have been very good, I think," said the Czech bombshell. "But, with RSI... now, I think I will be best."

Did you hear that? RSI is going to make her "best." You see, Miss Seyfried? That's how you do it.

Last, but not least, the super heavyweight division. Here, we had a problem. All of the fighters who seemed to fit would have to compete directly with Stephanie McMahon. Stephanie was flexible, but she made it clear she would have difficulty working together with many of her current foes. We never want to force this sort of thing, so we just kept talking. And, just when I thought we weren't going to find a fit, Torrie Wilson's name came up.

"That will work," said Stephanie, clearly excited at the prospect of finding a solution after such a long and difficult discussion.

I'll be honest... Torrie is not exactly who we were looking for, here. She lacks experience, and the experience she does have hasn't been too good. Still, Stephanie wanted to follow through, so we brought Torrie in to talk it over. And, we discovered a few things. First, her record may not show it, but Torrie is committed. Her struggle to succeed in the UMMA has been an uphill battle, at best. Where many would have given up, she flat out refuses. Also, after putting her through the motions, we see more in her than she's shown to date. With some guidance, we believe she can succeed. And, the most important detail, she and Steph have a good working relationship from their time together in the WWE.

I have a lot of questions about this one, but the women have sold me. We're gonna give it a shot.

So, eight new members added to the RSI family. Clearly, things are going to be busy around here.
