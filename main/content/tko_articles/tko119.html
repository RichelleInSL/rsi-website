The Pain of Victory

With eleven victories in fifteen fights, there's no question that Open Fight Night was an unqualified success for RSI. Yet, I am not happy. That's because one of the four losses was particularly costly for one of RSI's premier fighters.

The ever-popular Britney Spears had five fights on the card, tonight. She handled the first four with relative ease, leaving the one that was by far the most difficult for last. UMMA legend Doutzen Kroes had issued a challenge to Britney for this event, and Britney accepted without hesitation. She was excited for the opportunity, and I think she was honored that Doutzen felt she was worthy of such a challenge. However, I'm fairly sure she felt a lot less honored by the time it was over.

Britney fought well, and she should be proud of that. But I know she isn't. She got behind early, and she was never able to establish any sort of game plan. She showed her mettle, and hung in there as things started to get away from her, but I know she's disappointed she never really had control of things. Doutzen was dictating throughout, and that's an uncomfortable feeling for Britney. But the biggest disappointment is the way it all ended. Never one to give in, Britney kept fighting long after she had nothing left. As often happens, the reward for her toughness was a devastating injury that's going to keep her out of action for quite a while.

Her disappointment was clear in her post-fight comments. "I'm not happy with the way I fought, tonight," admitted the songstress. "Maybe I was too eager. Maybe I had psyched myself out a little. I don't know." She shook her head lightly as she continued. "I knew how hard it was going to be to beat Doutzen. And I knew it was going to take everything I had. But I really wanted to perform better than this. I'm disappointed in myself. On top of that, I'm disappointed I won't be able to be out there with my team at the next event."

In fact, that may be the most disappointing part. With the newly reformed teams preparing for their big debut, losing their leader is going to be a big blow for Britney's team. But she's going to be in the gym with them as they get ready, and she'll be right there at cage-side during the event. This is her team, come hell or high water, and nothing is going to keep her from that. Meanwhile, the middleweight duties will be in good hands, as I've tapped Jennifer Stano to fill in for Britney. And let's hope Jen is a fast learner, because she's about to come up against an opponent she's getting to know pretty well. And she did not fare all that well against her tonight.

Actually, that's not completely true. Jen gave an excellent account of herself against the more experienced Karen Gillan. But as usual, Jen let her opponent press the action early, and then tried to come roaring back. It's a strategy I hate, but it's worked for her in the past. This time, she was a little too reckless, allowing Karen to catch her in a painful submission hold. Jen was left with little choice but to give up... and to admit she's still got some work to do.

As expected, the first question Jen faced in the post fight press conference was about the manufactured RSI/Hellfire rivalry. When asked if losing to a Hellfire fighter worsened her disappointment, Jen pulled no punches. "Not at all," she said. "I could care less what stable my opponent is with. I'm disappointed in the way I fought. I'm disappointed that I let the fight get away from me." She paused a moment before concluding, "I'm disappointed that I lost a fight I felt I could win. And that's more than enough disappointment, as far as I'm concerned."

To make things even more interesting, Britney's team is scheduled to face one of the Hellfire teams at the upcoming event, and Karen is the middleweight fighter for that team. So, when news got out that I had tapped Jen to fill in for Britney, she was asked about having to face Karen again. "It doesn't matter to me," she said. "I'll fight whoever I have to. I just want to perform better than I did tonight."

Rounding out the bad news for RSI, both Shelby Chesnes and Torrie Wilson fell to slick submission moves tonight. Torrie's fight with Kim Kardashian had barely gotten started when it was suddenly all over. Shelby, on the other hand, was having her way with opponent Kara Monaco in the first round, only to have it all end in a hurry as the second round got underway. Shelby's still young, and submission defense takes a long time to master. So, I understand what happened there. But Torrie... well, the truth is she fell to a determined opponent. Kim was willing to absorb quite a bit of punishment to get that submission. You have to give her credit for that.

Strangely enough, Shelby emerged from the locker room after her fight holding a freshly cut bouquet of flowers.

"Where the hell did those come from?" asked teammate Elisha Cuthbert.

Shelby showed her the card, and Leesh literally laughed out loud.

"Good luck with HIM," she said as she walked away.

Meanwhile, this has been a very depressing press release for an event I said was an unqualified success. I've already mentioned Britney's 4 out of 5 victories. Now, let's get to the rest of the good stuff...

Despite her overwhelming recent success, Emily VanCamp has not fared well against Ali Larter in their two previous meetings. The first fight was an unmitigated disaster for Em, as Ali basically wiped the mat with her. More recently, the two faced off in the championship round of the 2014 Lingerie Invitational tournament. Em looked poised to get her revenge, as she was in command from the opening bell. But a perfectly timed, perfectly placed hook from the heavy hitting Larter dropped VanCamp in a hurry. That one was a particularly bitter pill to swallow. Top it all off with the fact that Ali basically owned Emily's friend and training partner, Roselyn Sanchez, in their most recent bout, and you can bet Em had some things on her mind as she was getting ready for this one.

Well, whatever her training regimen was for this fight, it sure worked out in a hurry for Em. After turning aside an early attack from Ali, Emily caught an off-balance Larter with a tremendous high kick, and that was the end of that.

Though she hadn't said much in the run up to the event, Emily looked pretty relieved when questioned later. "This win means a lot to me," she admitted. "Ali's had my number up until now. I knew I could beat her, but I needed to prove it." As with Jennifer, Em was questioned about whether she felt the win meant more with Ali being a member of the Hellfire stable. "No," she said simply. "Ali is a tough opponent... a champion who has proven herself repeatedly. That was all the motivation I needed to put my best out there."

Speaking of people who were going to need their best... Emma Watson has quietly become one of the most talented and tenacious young fighters in the UMMA. In fact, in a career that spans forty-five fights, there is no one who can claim a winning record against her. No one, that is, except for Kristen Stewart. Emma and Kristen don't face each other often, and they don't seem to have any particular animosity toward each other. But "Emma versus Kristen" is always a fan favorite, and you can bet they'll clamor for it when Open Fight Night rolls around. It happened again this time, and the fans got their wish.

This is the third time the two have met. In both of the previous fights, Kristen didn't just win... she destroyed RSI's youngest champion. Emma is great at keeping her focus... never letting any one thing get to her too much. But it doesn't take a genius to figure out this thing with Kristen was bothering her. She's not perfect, and has never pretended to be. But she's not used to coming in with a losing record against her opponent. She's not used to facing someone who has so clearly dominated her in the past.

She's not used to coming in as the underdog.

Well, though this fight was a very short affair, it was jam-packed with action. Both women gave as good as they got, and Kristen definitely had her moments. But as the second minute came to a close, Emma saw her chance and unleashed a terrifying kick that almost took Kristen's head clear off. The impact was heard throughout the arena, and Kristen dropped straight to the canvas.

Emma didn't try to hide her emotions after her win. "I wanted this," she said. "I don't have any problem with Kristen, and I don't think she has a problem with me. But we're both competitors, and this is what the fans want to see." She shrugged and gave that sly grin she's known for. "If she and I are going to keep getting thrown together like this... I knew I had to make a stand. I don't hate Kristen the way the fans wish I did, but I wasn't going to let myself continue to get beat like that. Not by her or anybody else."

When Catherine Bell received her challenge from Heather Graham, she wasn't really sure how to feel about it. "Here I was having all this recent success," she said, "but it was all a new experience for me. So, Heather's challenge came as a shock. Everyone wants to make a big deal out of my title, but Heather has a lot more experience and a lot more success. So, it was humbling, but also nerve-wracking at the same time. It left me wondering if I could really handle someone like Heather."

Well, wonder no more. Mac handled everything Heather threw at her, including dealing with a first round where they spent most of the time on the mat. Ground work is not Catherine's forte, but she managed to tie Heather up and keep her from doing any damage. And in the second round, she managed to stay on her feet... unloading on her opponent whenever she got the chance. The fight seemed destined to end in a knockout, as Mac continued to rain down heavy blows. But a pedestrian cut on Heather's chin gave referee Lena Gercke a reason to step in and rob the fans of what they really wanted to see. Still, there was no doubt Catherine had done enough to earn the win.

"Heather and I have never fought before," the SCQ heavyweight champion said afterward. "I don't think she knew what to expect from me, and I know I really didn't know what to expect from her." Not knowing your opponent makes training tough, and Catherine admitted that was the case here. "Winning like this was a great boost for my confidence. Heather is an experienced opponent, and I'm proud to get the win. But I think, if we meet each other again, she'll be harder to beat. I'm sure she thought getting me to the mat would get her the win, and I surprised her. After that, she wasn't sure of what to do with me."

Rounding out the RSI fights for the night: Jennifer Lawrence had an easy time with Amanda Righetti, Torrie Wilson got an early win over Laura Pausini before falling to Kardashian, and Sabine Jemeljanova made short work of Lucy Collett.

So, as I said... all in all, a good night for us. And stable leader Danneel Harris was just as happy as I was with our results. But Danni seemed to show a lot of interest in some of the other fights, too. She looked pretty damn happy after watching Briana Evigan dismantle Mila Kunis, and she gave a bemused grin after the ever-confident and supremely bitchy Olga Kurylenko made good on a particularly nervy title challenge against champion Rachel Stevens. But it was the wry smile she wore after watching her friend Sophia Bush overcome nemesis Elodie Frege that caught my attention.

"You're friend is looking pretty good," I said as I eased up next to Danni in the RSI suite.

"She sure is," she said with a nod, keeping her eyes focused on the cage below.

"You realize what you're doing to yourself, right?" I asked casually. "She's going to come for you."

She smiled. "I'll be waiting when she gets there."

I waited a moment before adding, "I don't always understand you."

"You understand me better than most," she said.

Another moment passed before I answered.

"Yes," I said with a nod. "I do."

As previously mentioned, the team event is up next. And with the exception of Britney, all three teams will come into the event miraculously whole. Britney's team will face a tough test against Hellfire Clique, while Leesh's team will look to continue it's success as they face off against the Raccoon-led Imaginary Heroines. But the real excitement at RSI surrounds the debut of The Aly Cats. With the unprecedented recent growth of the UMMA, it still remains to be seen who the new team will face. But with Alyson Michalka, Emma Watson and Danni Harris on the roster, you can bet it's going to be quite a show. Make sure you get your tickets, now.
