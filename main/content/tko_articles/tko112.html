Everyday Emily

Let's face it... RSI is loaded with characters of all types. We have a stable leader who is trusted and respected by everyone here. We have a training coordinator who works her ass off and takes every loss suffered by an RSI fighter personally. We have team leaders who wear their hearts on their sleeve, and their teammates love them for it. And lest we should forget, we have a stable owner who manages to continually keep the place going despite her best efforts to screw it up.

And then, we have Emily VanCamp.

Emily has a reputation for keeping her head down and just getting things done. Is she friendly? Absolutely. Well-liked? By everyone. She's a loyal and dependable teammate, and a respected role model for our younger fighters. She is also fun-loving and a little free-spirited. But above all else, Em is level-headed. And I mean, like... to a nauseating degree. Wins are great and losses are bad, but she never lets her emotions take her too far off center. Every day is a new day for her, and she approaches each one with the same confidence and determination as the one that preceded it.

That uncanny ability to shake off a bad loss may very well have won her the first-ever Maxim Top 100 title here in Las Vegas. Because she suffered a world-class spanking in the opening round that would have shattered the confidence of most other fighters.

It would be easy to make excuses. By the time she faced off against Jordana Brewster, Emily was already assured of a spot in the championship round of the tournament. Maybe she didn't try as hard. Maybe she got complacent.

Maybe you don't know Emily.

Em puts everything she's got into her fights. When she steps in that cage, she's always playing to win. So full credit goes to Jordanna, who took the fight to RSI's sweetheart early and just never let up. Emily has come up big against some of the toughest opponents out there, but Brewster made her look hopelessly over-matched. It was a soul crushing defeat... the kind of thing it should take months to recover from. But Em was ready and raring to go when the championship round started.

First up was the tough and aggressive Kaley Cuoco. Emily made it look easy. Then came Lydia Hearst, who came in as a complete unknown, only to pull out victories over the likes of the far more experienced Anna Paquin. Em was not impressed. Then, it was on to Stana Katic, who was fresh off a convincing victory over ESL middleweight champion Laura Vandervoort. The Revenge actress went right through her. That left Em with a championship match up that seemed destined from the very start.

Her opponent? The one and only Irina Sheik.

I mean, really... who didn't see this coming?

It doesn't seem like all that long ago that Emily was struggling just to survive against Irina. She knew she needed a new strategy if she ever hoped to realize her championship dream. So, she poured everything she had into improving herself, and the payoff came when she finally unseated the long-standing champion. Since then, the two have faced off in some of the most epic battles the UMMA has ever seen. Aside from their constant war over the ESL lightweight crown, the two also have a habit of finding each other in tournament competition. Even here, in a tournament where the weight classes were all thrown together in an all-out free-for-all, the championship came down to Emily and Irina.

The stage was set for what should have been the most exciting chapter in this momentous rivalry. Instead, it was snatched away by an injury decision that may haunt the UMMA for years to come.

Emily came out firing and caught Irina flat-footed at the opening bell. Em immediately took control, knowing she couldn't let up once she had Irina reeling. With Irina, you never know when she's going to reach down and pull out a comeback for the ages. But referee America Ferrera jumped in and put a stop to things at the first sight of blood from the supermodel, making sure the ILF fighter never even got the chance to turn things around. It was a devastating ending in a match up that had the potential to make history.

Be that as it may, it doesn't detract from what Emily accomplished here in Vegas. This tournament was one of the craziest the UMMA has ever dreamed up. Rookies seeded above champions. Heavyweights against featherweights. It was the kind of thing that had training coordinator Stephanie McMahon hyper-ventilating from the minute it was announced. But Em handled it all with her usual cool, quiet confidence. Because, no matter the situation, Emily will be ready. Every single day.

Congratulations, Em. I don't exactly where to rank this on your ever-growing list of accomplishments. But you had a lot thrown at you in this tournament, and you took it all in stride. Just like you always do.

Meanwhile, Ashley Tisdale made quite an impression here in Sin City. In fact, I think the judges stole away a chance for her to do even more.

Tiz had some impressive wins in the qualifying round, including a spectacular display against Morena Baccarin. Her run came to a screeching halt when she also fell to Jordana Brewster, but, like Emily, Ash had already done enough to secure her spot in the championship round.

By the way, I just realized I suddenly don't like Jordanna. At all. But I digress.

Anyway, her first match up in the championship bracket brought Ashley face-to-face with Brooklyn Decker, one of the UMMA's most experienced and accomplished fighters. On paper, things did not look good for Ash. But she's has made a name for herself based solely on her toughness and grit. She has consistently come out on top in matches when the odds makers said otherwise. And as far as I'm concerned, she did it again here in Vegas. Unfortunately, my opinion doesn't really matter, and the judges saw it differently.

By every measurable statistic, Ashley won this fight. She scored with more strikes, and she was far more accurate. She seemed to be doing more damage with those strikes, as well. Though she repeatedly fell victim to Brooklyn's take down game, even then, Ash seemed to be winning the battle on the ground. But the only numbers that mattered went against the fledgling producer, as the judges scores added up to a unanimous decision for Brooklyn.

I think you got jobbed on this one, Tiz. It's as simple as that. But there's no point losing sleep over it. As usual, I'm impressed with what you pulled off here young lady, and I'm hungry for more. So, bring it on Blondie Girl. Let's see what you've got.

Last up, young Jennifer Lawrence made a little noise here in Vegas. Because of the way the seeding worked, Jen found herself surrounded by far more experienced talent. She never really stood much of a chance of getting out of the qualifying round, but she sure did make a statement before she bowed out. Her opening match came against veteran fighter Vanessa Hudgens. With seventy-three fights to her credit, Vanessa's experience absolutely dwarfed Jennifer's twenty total matches. But three minutes and forty-nine seconds after the opening bell, Hudgens was flat on her back while Lawrence had her fist in the air. Though it would be the only win Jen would come away with, it was a fantastic victory that I hope can serve as the springboard for moving the young actress to the next level.

Next on the schedule: team competition. Elisha's group will be looking to keep their momentum going as they try to crack the top 5. Britney's crew, meanwhile, find themselves up against what may be the single most experienced team in the competition. If you think the women of RSI are fierce when fighting for themselves, wait until you see how they get when their teammates are depending on them. We'll see you then.
