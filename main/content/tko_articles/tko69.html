OPPORTUNITY DENIED

MMA fighting always has it's ups and downs. Even the most talented fighter can fall victim to a lucky punch or an unorthodox game plan on the part of her opponent. But, no matter how I look at it, Alyson Michalka's loss tonight is hard to swallow. Her opponent, Brooklyn Decker, is one of the most talented fighters the UMMA has to offer. With over 60 fights, and nearly 50 wins, she ranks among the most seasoned and accomplished fighters out there. But, when Aly saw that she had drawn Brooklyn for tonight's fight, she didn't even flinch. She knew what she was up against, and she knew she would need every ounce of her ability to prevail. Coming off her shocking loss to Holly Willoughby, Brooklyn was going to be looking to prove a point, and she was going to want to destroy Aly to do it. Aly trained and she studied. She prepared like she has never prepared before. And, for nearly 2 full rounds, she gave as good as she got. Did Aly dominate? Certainly not. But, she stood toe to toe with the champ, and put on the fight of her life. She weathered Brooklyn's assaults well, and scored with some of her own. Both fighters were tremendous, and the crowd certainly got their money's worth. That is, of course, until a highly controversial stoppage by referee Emma Roberts left a definite sour taste in everyone's mouth. I'm not sure if the sight of a little blood made her swoon, or what, but Aly was certain capable of continuing and was eager to do so. But, Roberts wouldn't allow it, and that makes this one hard to deal with. Let me be clear that I mean no disrespect to Brooklyn or the International Lingerie Federation. I have already lauded Ms. Decker's talents, and the ILF is a highly-regarded management group. Was a win stolen from Alyson? I can't say that. Both fighters were still in this, and Brooklyn was ahead on most cards. Alyson certainly had her work cut out for her if she was going to pull this one out. But, Aly WAS denied the opportunity to see this one through to the end, and that's really the shame of it. Alyson was not available for comment after the fight, but only because she was having trouble controlling her emotions. Her ability to speak was in no way effected by the small bandage needed to close the terrible wound that had Ms. Roberts so concerned.

Unfortunately, the controversy with Aly overshadowed Julianne Hough's terrific win over Joanna Levesque. Coming into the fight, these two fighters were separated by just over 2 ranking points, and it just doesn't get any closer than that. JoJo was looking to jump over Julianne in the standings, but Julianne was having none of it. In a commanding performance, Julianne brutalized her opponent right from the opening bell, finally putting her away with a decisive knockout. Credit to JoJo for hanging in there as long as she did, but this one was really never in doubt. When asked about the punishment she'd doled out, Julianne didn't seem sympathetic. "My job is to fight on until someone stops me. I never set out to deliberately cause an injury, but the idea is to destroy her before she destroys me. It's the ref's job to stop it, if one of us isn't defending herself." In other words, JoJo really needed Emma Roberts in there to keep her safe.

We conclude tonight with some unfortunate news. As with most organizations, RSI always signs new fighters to month-to-month contracts. The idea is to allow the organization time to get used to the fighter, and the fighter time to get used to the organization. And, though we're very pleased to announce that most of the current RSI fighters have now been signed to long-term deals, a few girls have decided to move on. So, effective immediately, Erica Rose Campbell, Danielle Lloyd, Rhian Sugden and Michelle Marsh are leaving our group. In each case, the decision came down to whether or not the fighter was comfortable with the RSI training philosophy, and these girls simply weren't. We applaud each for making what had to be a very difficult decision, and we wish them well in the future.

Well, no action for the RSI group at the upcoming team event, but that doesn't mean we won't have news. Big changes are coming at RSI, and the excitement is palpable within the RSI offices. With both experienced fighters and fresh-faced rookies coming on board, the future is looking bright. We'll have more details as they get finalized.
