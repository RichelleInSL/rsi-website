Taking the Long Road

I'm probably supposed to lead off this release by saying how disappointed I am. And, to be honest, I am a little disappointed. Back at the Reality Check event, Emily VanCamp fought the fight of her life and gave reknowned champion Irina Sheik an incredible scare. So, the question coming into tonight's rematch was simple: Could Emily do it again? Was it a fluke, or could she back up the performance she put in that night? And, we got our answer: Damn right, she could do it again. Once again, she stood toe to toe with the champion, and once again she proved herself worthy of being in there, fighting for that belt.

So, nothing to be disappointed about, right? Well, unfortunately, we let our minds wander a little, and a second question came to mind. Could Emily do more than put on a good show? Could she actually take down the long-standing champion? I still think the answer is absolutely yes, but it didn't happen for her, tonight. Tonight, the champion stood tall and retained her crown. And, that's where the disappointment comes in.

Among the most disappointed was the fighter, herself.

"This was a tough one, no question," said a dejected VanCamp afterward. "After our last fight, I thought I really had a shot. But, Irina proved that she's the best. Not me."

Sorry to do this to you, Em, but I'm gonna have to call bullshit on that one.

The only thing Irina proved tonight is that she's still a great champion. And, while she deserves all the credit in the world for a career that continues to be monumentally successful, that's not news. The news tonight is that Emily VanCamp is much better than people give her credit for. That she DOES have what it takes to stand up to one of the finest champions the UMMA has ever known.

The news tonight is that Emily VanCamp is here to stay. Believe it.

Nothing worth having ever comes easy. And, let's be honest... taking the title away from Irina is going to be hard. Excruciatingly hard. There will be setbacks. It will be frustrating. And, if you do manage to get it, there's no question she'll immediately come looking to take it back. So, this is going to be hard, and it's probably only going to get harder. But, your time is coming, Emily. You have legions of fans who know it. Your teammates know it. I know it. And, deep down, I think Irina knows it, too.

So, pick your damned head up, and get back to work. You have a long road ahead of you.

To move forward, sometimes, you need to go backward. Nowhere is that more true than in the MMA game. A few months back, Ashley Tisdale suffered a brutal loss at the hands of ground fighting specialist Alizee Jacotey. That fight was a totally one-sided affair, with Jacotey taking Ashley down only seconds into the fight and then just beating the hell out of her until a bloodied Tisdale finally lost consciousness. It was, at the very least, a total embarrassment for Ashley. When something like that happens, a fighter has two choices: quit or fight back. And, Ashley made it clear tonight that she's a fighter. Tonight's draw brought Ashley and Alizee face to face, once more. Given the chance to avenge the worst defeat of her career, Tiz put that painful memory exactly where it belongs. In the past.

For two full rounds, Ashley's performance was textbook. Superb defense, great countering and the patience of a Buddhist monk. Alizee attacked again and again, but Ashley waited her out, confident she would get the chance she needed. That chance came in the third, as Tisdale found a crack in the singer's defense, and just went to town. Blow after blow landed without answer from the young Frenchwoman. Ashley was a whirlwind of feet, fists and knees as she hammered Jacotey, finally rendering her unconscious with a little over a minute left in the round.

Since the loss all those months ago, Ashley hasn't had much to say in her post-fight interviews. Win or lose, her answers were curt... almost angry. Why so serious? A much more relaxed Tisdale came clean tonight.

"Ever since that night, I wanted this chance," admitted the former Disney darling.

Shades of angry Ashley returned as she continued, though.

"I like to think I'm a pretty confident person. I am who I am, and I'm happy with it. But, that night... that loss... it made me question everything about who I am as a fighter," said Tisdale, as a far-away look settled on her face. "I wasn't even sure I wanted to fight again, after that. But, once I made my mind up to fight on, I wanted this rematch. I don't have anything personal against Alizee. She was just doing her job that night. But, since then, I've been thinking of nothing other than getting even."

Tisdale's soft brown eyes narrowed as she added, "I didn't just want to beat her. I wanted to brutalize her. I wanted to destroy her. I got my wish, tonight."

Jesus. I might be wrong, but I don't think the blue fairy would approve of a wish like that one.

A fine debut for RSI newcomer Tiffany Mulheron, as she totally destroyed opponent Kayla Ewell. It started out evenly enough, with the two exchanging blows at the center of the cage. But, it took less than a minute for Tiffany to trap Kayla in the clinch. After that, it was all Tiffany, as she simply ground her opponent into dust.

Though it's only been a few weeks, it seems her new RSI team is already making an impact.

"I feel better... more focused... now that I have RSI behind me. My workouts are more organized. My training regimen feels more effective," said Mulheron.

Then, she smiled sheepishly as she added, "Of course, it helps to have a reigning champion kicking your ass in the cage every day, too."

A nice little nod to young champion, Emma Watson. Excellent. But, let's not get too cozy. I don't need another Britney Spears/Elisha Cuthbert saga on my hands.

At the recent lingerie tournament, Krystal Forscutt got her ass handed to her by UMMA veteran Melissa Joan Hart. I made my feelings known about Melissa's performance that night, and I won't rehash them here. Krystal, however, took the loss in stride, preferring to focus on her continued success against fighters on her level. Well, that success continued tonight, as she scored a blink-and-you-miss-it win over rookie Jessica Hart. A thunderous kick, a flurry of punches, and Jessica was lying beaten on the mat.

While clearly pleased, Krystal kept things even-keeled afterward.

"It was a nice win, tonight. It felt good to have the game plan go so perfectly."

You obliterate your opponent in a little over half a minute, and that's all you have to say? Where's the gloating? Where are the warnings to the other fighters in your division that Krystal is coming?

"I think, when you've been as low as I have, you gain a special appreciation for humility."

No doubt, Krystal knows of what she speaks. Just how low was low?

"I think I'm 5-2 since joining RSI, with my only losses coming in the tournaments. And, my record is still terrible. What does that tell you?"

It tells me I have a keen eye for talent.

You see how I took Krystal's moment and made it about me? Yeah. I'm good at that.

After suffering a bad loss to Charlotte Church last time out, the questions mounted as to whether or not Allira Cohrs could gather herself and shake it off. Well, it looks like she had no problem setting that one aside, as she looked confident and sharp in a win over Kat Dennings, tonight.

In the loss to Church, Allira's lack of a ground game was exposed, with Cohr's looking totally lost as Charlotte pounded her into the mat. Since that night, Allira has been concentrating hard on her ground defense, and it certainly paid off tonight. Dennings was successful in getting Cohrs off her feet, but Allira looked much more confident, weathering her opponent's attacks and damn near scoring a submission of her own.

"I was really happy with the way things went out there, tonight. I've been working on defending myself on the ground, and I felt I had improved a lot. But, you never really know how it will work out in a real fight. It went well, though," said a happy Cohrs.

Ground defense is one thing, but what was with the submission attempt?

"I took what she gave me. After I reversed her, she tried to roll free. Her back was exposed, so I took it. I just wanted to stay on top of her."

Though the submission attempt didn't really hurt Kat, it kept her guessing. Knowing when to adjust the game plan is the mark of an intelligent fighter. And, showing that kind of intelligence in the cage requires a confident, clear mind. Though Allira still has much to learn, she showed the composure of a much more experienced fighter out there, tonight.

Well played, rookie. Well played.
