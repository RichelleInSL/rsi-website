I found Elisha in the locker room again the next morning. This time, I was dressed for more rigorous activity.

"What's this?" she asked.

"You don't want to work with Britney," I answered, "so you get me."

"I didn't say I didn't want to work with her," she said. "I said I thought we needed a change."

"Well, we can discuss the significant differences between what you said and what I said some other time. I'm here and Britney's not, which is what you wanted. Now, let's go."

I made my way to the training room without waiting for a response.

I picked out Danni immediately, working with Britney in a cage on the far side of the room. Steph was also there, observing from the floor outside the octagon.

Elisha caught up to me a few minutes later.

"What's wrong with you?" she asked.

"Nothing," I said.

"Seems like more than nothing to me."

"You know what? Don't start," I snapped. "You made yourself clear yesterday, and you didn't seem willing to discuss it. So, we're trying to make it work."

"You don't want to work with me?"

"No, it's fine. But I can't be a full time training partner, for you."

"What about the others?" she asked.

"What others? You'll still work with Jen Stano, J-Law and Anna once in a while... same as you did before. But none of them are going to work out for you, long-term. You need someone with more experience."

The look on her face told me she hadn't really thought about what would happen once she and Britney parted ways.

"So, what's the next best choice?" I continued. "Pull someone from another weight class? I don't like that for a lot of reasons. The biggest one being that I don't want to break up any of the current pairings that are working."

"Well, I didn't..."

"Forget it," I said. "It's not yours to worry about. We'll figure something out."

I led her to the cage nearest the locker room door. I could hear Danni's voice carrying across the vast training area. I couldn't make out what she was saying, but I glanced that way and saw she and Britney were involved in a very animated discussion. They weren't so loud that it stood out from the general din of the room, but you could pick them out if you were listening for them.

I was listening. So was Elisha.

"That doesn't look good," she said, nodding in that direction.

I looked toward Danni and Britney for a few beats, appearing as though I hadn't previously noticed the skirmish. Then, I shrugged.

"Everyone will adjust," I said, softening my tone. "Besides, that can't be permanent, either. Danni has enough going on already, on top of her own training."

"So, what are you going to do?"

"We're working on it. Like I said, it's not your problem. If the training isn't working, then we have to make changes. That's on Danni and I... maybe on Steph, a little."

"I didn't really think about all that."

"You don't have to. Both of you were clear that a change is needed. We'll find a way to do it. I'm not going to force something that isn't working."

She held on the other pairing for a few more seconds.

"Yeah," she said. "I guess you're right."

As I ascended the steps to the cage, I picked up a pair of focus mitts I had set out on the apron, earlier.

"Oh," Elisha said. "Britney and I usually jump right into sparring. That's how we like to warm up."

"Things are going to be different," I said as I strapped the mitts to my hands. "That's what you wanted, right?"

"Right," she said, her voice lacking the conviction it had yesterday.

I got myself set and put the mitts up.

"You and I have never done this before," I said. "You're gonna have to go easy, until we get a feel for each other."

"Maybe I'll just let go, and see if you can keep up," she teased.

"You ever been slapped in the head with one of these mitts?"

"No..."

"Then, go easy," I said sternly.

We stared at each other for a moment. Then I gave her a smile, and she slowly returned it.

Unfortunately for her, that was the only moment of levity we had. From that point on, nothing I did was right. She didn't like the combinations I called. She didn't like my movement. None of it. We were out of sync, and her frustration hit its peak after only twenty minutes.

"Enough," she said. "Let's just... take a break."

Everything we were experiencing was common when a fighter trains with someone new. Fighting, especially on this level, is almost like dance. Your movements are fluid and graceful, each one leading to the next. Obviously, it doesn't look very graceful when your opponent is trying to stop you. But, with something like the mitts, you expect perfection. When everything is just right, you feel it... you know you're nailing it. And, for the first time in years, Leesh wasn't feeling it.

Because she was dancing with the wrong partner.

Elisha leaned back against the cage, then let herself drop to the canvas as she ran her fingers through her hair.

I glanced outside, and found Emma holding the heavy bag for her partner, Tiffany Mulheron. I gave her a small nod, and she then nodded at Tiffany. Emma let the bag go and Tiff continued to work solo.

"Off to a rough start, eh?" she said as she ambled up behind Elisha.

Elisha only grunted in response.

"Well, maybe it will get better once you start training with a real fighter, instead of Richelle," Emma said.

I narrowed my eyes at her. Though this moment was planned, the ad-libbing was all Emma.

"Long way from that," Elisha said sadly.

I noticed she made no effort to dispute the insult Emma had directed my way. I guess I should just consider myself lucky she didn't add to it.

"That's not what I hear," Emma said.

"What do you mean?"

"I heard Kirsten Dunst is on the verge of signing."

"Kirsten DUNST!" Elisha said, accentuating the last name as though it were a curse word.

"Where the hell did you hear that?" I asked angrily.

Emma looked at me, wide-eyed.

"From Danni," she said.

I shot a look over to where Danni and Britney were, then ripped the mitts from my hands and stormed out of the cage.

"What the hell's the matter with you?" I yelled when I was only halfway across the room.

Danni and Britney stopped what they were doing and turned in my direction.

Of course, so did everybody else.

"The thing with Dunst is nowhere near done," I said, zeroing my gaze on Danni. "Why are you telling people?"

"Dunst?" Britney said.

"They want to bring in Kirsten Dunst!" yelled Elisha, who, along with Emma, had trailed behind me.

Britney looked at Elisha, then whirled around to face me.

"For WHAT?" she said.

We had chosen Kirsten for two reasons. The first was that she's the highest ranked middleweight not currently signed. So, she's the most sensible choice, given that we were looking for someone who could keep pace with Britney and Elisha.

The second reason was that neither Britney nor Elisha liked Kirsten very much. There had been an incident a while back between Dunst and Jen Stano, where Kirsten had used some questionable tactics to secure the win. The reaction from our two senior middleweights... and also from myself... had been swift and furious.

Truth was, what happened wasn't a big deal. But, no one from RSI had any cause to interact with Dunst after that, so the anger and resentment lingered.

I ignored Britney's question and kept my eyes on Danni.

"I'm not telling people anything," she said.

"Then why does Emma know?" I shot back.

Danni looked at Emma with an expression that said you have a big mouth.

"You knew how sensitive this was going to be," I continued. "You shouldn't have told anyone!"

"Relax," Danni said. "It's not like they weren't going to find out, eventually."

"Why would you bring her here?" Britney asked.

I spun so that I could see both Britney and Elisha at the same time, and stared at them in astonishment.

"What did you two clowns think was gonna happen?" I said. "We need someone for both of you to train with."

"It doesn't have to be her!" Elisha said.

"Who else, Leesh? Everyone else is signed."

"We could have found some other way," Britney said.

"Geez. You guys..." I stammered. "I mean, really..."

"I told you this was gonna happen," Danni said.

I turned on her instantly.

"Well, maybe if we'd handled it properly, it wouldn't be so bad," I snapped. "Now, it's a mess!"

My expression left no doubt whose fault I thought the mess was.

Hers left no doubt that she didn't appreciate my tone.

"Don't talk to me that way," Danni said.

Her voice was so dark and cold, I had to take a moment to compose myself so I didn't laugh.

"Well, next time, don't shoot your mouth off!"

"Don't... talk to me... that way," she repeated, gritting her teeth and stretching her words out for emphasis.

I took a step in her direction, but Emma stepped in front of me.

Danni mirrored my movement. Suddenly, Steph materialized to stop her.

Danni and I glared at each other, and I hoped this would be the end of it... mostly because I wasn't sure I could keep this up with a straight face.

Luckily, I didn't have to.

"Stop," Britney said. "Just stop."

She descended from the cage and walked over to Elisha.

"This isn't going to work," she said quietly.

"It's only the first day," Elisha said.

"Look," Britney said, gesturing toward Danni and I. "It can't be like this. They can't be fighting because of us. And I don't want Kirsten here, and neither do you."

Elisha was quiet for a minute.

"We're not getting anywhere," she said.

"You're doing fine, Leesh," Emma said. "You've had a bad streak. It won't last forever."

"I don't care about that..." Elisha began.

"I know," I cut in. I nodded toward Britney and said, "You're more worried about her."

"And she's more worried about you," Danni added.

"You guys have been in this together for years," I said. "You're invested in each other, and neither of you is going to find a better partner to train with."

The two middleweights looked at one another, each with a small smile on her face.

Then Elisha's smile faded, and she was quiet again as she cast her eyes to the floor.

"I don't know," she said, shaking her head.

"Hey Leesh?" I said.

"What?"

"Laura called Brit a 'washed-up dinosaur.'"

It was like flipping a switch. Elisha's head came up slowly, and her eyes held on me.

"What?" she said. Not a question so much as an expression of anger and disbelief.

I nodded.

"Saw the quote this morning."

Elisha's head rotated very deliberately in Britney's direction.

"That true?" she asked.

Britney shrugged.

"It's not a big deal, Leesh."

"The fuck it isn't," Elisha replied. Then, she shook her head and asked, "That fucking cow said that?"

Nobody said anything, which Elisha took as confirmation.

"Let's go," she said to Britney, then marched up the steps without waiting.

"I think Leesh is back," Danni said with a smile.

"What about Dunst?" Britney asked.

"I haven't spoken to her," I said. "I'm sure she's doing fine, though."

"You haven't spoken to her?"

I shook my head.

"So, this was all bullshit," Elisha said from inside the cage.

"Yep," I said simply.

"And this little show you two put on?" Britney asked, indicating Danni and I.

"Just a little theater, for your benefit," I said.

"Screaming at each other was the best plan you could come up with?" the songstress asked.

"That was her idea," Danni said, pointing at me.

"Hey, it worked, didn't it?"

Britney shook her head as she started toward the cage.

"It's like working in the loony bin, sometimes."

That sounded a lot like a politician calling someone else a liar.

Of course, the irony of the statement didn't make it untrue.

Everyone else resumed their training, while Danni, Emma and I stood and watched as the blonde pair went back to doing what they do best.

Meanwhile, Steph had disapparated as quickly as she'd appeared. Maybe Emma taught her how to do it.

Speaking of our youngest champion... I brought my hand up behind her, and flicked the back of her ear.

"OW!" she yelled as she spun to face me.

"'A real fighter instead of Richelle?'" I quoted.

"I was trying to sound authentic," she said, massaging her ear lobe between her fingers.

Then, she pushed me with her other hand and whined, "Dammit! That hurt!"

I smiled at her, then turned to Danni.

"You were pretty good," I said. "You should probably think about doing a little acting, on the side."

"I'll keep it in mind."

We watched for another minute or two as the blondes sparred. Britney backed Elisha up to the cage with a series of combinations, but the wiley actress pushed her way inside and spun the singer around at the last second. She landed a quick combination of her own before Britney squirted free, and the two reset near the center of the octagon.

"Think they'll be okay?" Danni asked.

"Sure," I said. "It's Britney and Elisha. What could go wrong?"
