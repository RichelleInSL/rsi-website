"As a bona fide knight errant," I began, "shouldn't you be out in search of evil to battle, instead of gazing out the window, keenly alert for the presence of a short skirt?"

He didn't comment on my presumption, nor did he deny it's accuracy.

"Alas, fair maiden, it seems my prior efforts have been fruitful, for evil lurks not, this day."

"Splendid," I said. "And how goes the other pursuit?"

"It's too cold for skirts," he said.

"Bummer."

"I agree."

A thin smile spread across my face as the exchange briefly pulled me away from my troubles. I looked up at Danni, who appeared similarly amused, even though she'd only heard my half of the conversation.

"Hello, Spenser," I said into the receiver.

"Richelle," he replied. "If memory serves, we spoke just a few days ago."

"We did."

"And now, you're calling again... on the office line, this time."

"I am."

"Perhaps it's just my overdeveloped gumshoe intuition, but I sense trouble may be afoot."

My smile was long gone as the reason for the call came back into focus.

"Unfortunately, it seems evil does lurk this day," I said. "It's simply moved south."

Spenser patiently waited as I worked up the courage to get to the point.

"He's here, Spenser."

Once again, I could picture the scene as he sat forward and his expression turned serious. The time for humor was officially over.

"Tell me," he said.

Though we hadn't seen each other in years, Spenser and I spoke frequently by phone. He knew about RSI. He also knew who Danni was, and how she fit into things. I told him about the man who had approached her, and what he'd said about the King. Spenser asked if I'd seen Powers myself, or if he'd somehow made contact with me directly. I said nothing like that had happened, but he gave no hint that he didn't agree with me regarding the meaning of the royal reference.

"I have some things to tie up, here," he said when I finished. "I'll be down in two days."

"Only you?" I asked.

"I anticipate that Hawk will desire to be involved, as well."

I nodded.

"Good," I said. "I'll make the arrangements."

"There's really no need..."

"Spenser, don't."

My voice hadn't risen, but I could see the sharp tone of it had gotten Danni's attention.

I took a deep breath before continuing.

"Look, I'll never be able to repay you... either of you... for what you did back then," I said, "But, it's different now. I'm paying you, this time. I'm paying your rate... and Hawk's..."

"I'm not sure Hawk has a published rate schedule, per se..."

"...as well as expenses. I'm paying for everything."

He was quiet for a moment.

"I feel as though accepting remuneration may tarnish my shining armor."

"I think even knights have to eat," I said.

"We knights feed upon the nobility of our work," he said, "and are nourished by the gratitude of those we serve."

"I've been to dinner with you. I'm pretty sure part of the reason you love Susan so much is that you get to eat your meal and then most of hers, as well."

"I hate when she orders those fruity salads."

We exchanged goodbyes, with Spenser warning me to be careful. We agreed that Powers wasn't likely to do anything more, but there was no guarantee. If he was crazy enough to come for me after all this time, then there was no way to know for certain what he might do next.

I set the phone down, then observed the bemused grin on Danni's face.

"What?" I asked.

"Bummer?"

I shrugged.

"I think you had to hear the whole conversation."

"Uh, huh."

She nodded toward the phone and said, "So, he's the reason you're still here?"

"Well, it's not really that simple."

"It never is, with you."

I narrowed my eyes at her, but it seemed to have little effect.

"By the way," she said, "who's Hawk?"

"Right. Back to our story, then..."
