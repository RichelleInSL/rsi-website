I haven't always been this way.

As a kid, I was mostly the head-down, eyes-forward type. Not because I was studious and attentive, but because I didn't want to speak or be spoken. I didn't necessarily have what you'd call friends. There were a handful of people who tolerated me, and that was about it. And that was fine with me. Most people bored me to tears with inanity. Talk about who wore what, why science was gross and how to fake your way through gym class without getting all sweaty. Just shoot me.

Not that I had anything of greater import I wanted to talk about. Which sort of implies that I was part of the problem, but facts like that tend to distract from my narrative. So we'll just leave them be.

School tended to bore me, as well. The subject matter came easy to me, which likely indicated I was of high intelligence. I never saw it that way. I always assumed I was normal and everyone else was stupid.

Maybe if someone had told me. If some teacher had gotten me focused on something that really interested me. Maybe then I could have done something fulfilling and useful with my life.

But nope... that's not how this story is going to go.
