"First of all, I know this is kind of strange," I said. "I know both of you want this job, and it feels a little like I've dragged one of you in here to tell her she's lost. But we did it this way because..."

Given the delicate nature of the conversation, you'd think I'd have my thoughts organized ahead of time. But nope... only ten seconds in and I was already fishing for the right phrase.

"Because we don't think anyone is going to lose," Danni finished for me.

It was the perfect thing to say... mostly because it caused just the right kind of confusion. The kind that made both women wrinkle their nose.

"You're going to try and split the role?" Emma asked.

"I really don't think that will work," Emily added.

"Agreed," I said. "Only one of you will be Stable Leader. But that's not the only important role we have here."

"And one of you has actually been preselected for one of the others," Danni said.

Again, just the right note, with just the right amount of mystery to it. While co-stable leaders might not be a good idea, this co-owner thing seemed like it was going to work out just fine. Danni brought a very subtle kind of panache to the proceedings that I really liked.

"Preselected?" Emma asked.

I nodded, then opened the top drawer of desk and produced an envelope. It looked a little ragged because it had already been opened, and its stark white color had fade just a bit with age while it sat waiting for this moment. I held it so both women could see my name scrawled haphazardly across the front in blue ink. The penmanship was instantly recognizable to all of us.

I very ceremoniously removed the letter inside, and started to skim the contents aloud.

Dear Richelle... It has been an honor and a privilege to work with you, and to be able to help shape the women of RSI - both individually and collectively... your faith and trust have been invaluable... I could not have done any of this without you...

"That says, and Danni, there," Danni read over my shoulder.

"I'm just highlighting the important parts," I said dismissively.

She made an unhappy little noise, while I skipped down to the end of the letter.

I wish everyone at RSI the very best, now and in the future.

I omitted a few lines at the very end that were meant just for me, and concluded with the signature.

Thanks for everything, Steph.

I looked up to find a range of emotions playing across each woman's face. Some were alike. It had been a long time since we'd seen Steph, and reading the letter was a little like having her back in the room. It triggered a sort of melancholy smile from all of us as happy memories blended with a wistful longing to actually have her here.

But there was also a clear difference... because Emma possesses a magical ability to always have her finger on the pulse of this stable. She sees the relationships that form and she understands the way they drive RSI and make it what it is. So, she knew there was more to the letter, and she had a pretty good idea what it would say.

Emily, on the other hand, was mostly confused. She looked at me for a while, expecting me to connect the dots. Hearing the letter was nice, but what the hell did it have to do with anything?

Then she glanced at Emma and realized she was the only one who didn't get it.

"I don't... understand," she said.

With a flick of my thumb and forefinger, I separated the pages in my hand - revealing for the first time that there was more than one.

I set the first aside and read the second in detail.

Richelle, I don't know when you plan to find my replacement. Notice I didn't say "if" because you're going to have to replace me. By now, you must realize that the way RSI does things is what sets us apart. If not, then you're a bigger idiot than I'd realized.

I paused to see smiles all around. It really was like having Steph here with us.

That said, you don't need to actually replace ME. I know I had my way of doing things. I did them that way because it's the only way I knew how, but that's not the only way it can be done. What you need is someone smart. Someone who knows and studies the game, and can think ten steps ahead. Someone who can see the ways to win, even when it looks like the fight is over.

I paused again and focused on Emily, and saw the color rising in her face. Whether she believed what she was hearing or not, by now she knew where this was going.

I'm sure you know what I'm getting at, but just in case you're being extra thick today... you already have the person you need on the Rampage roster. Someone who has all these things. She's shown them in the gym, and she's shown them when she's under fire in a fight. She not only knows what to do and when, but she's actually done it, time and time again.

When the time comes and you realize you need someone new, the one you need is Emily.

I laid the page down on the desk, and looked at Emily. By now, she was on the verge of losing it. From the very beginning, she's always been what you'd call a student of the game. As interested in the gritty technical details as she was in turning those details into victories in the cage. And she has, quite frankly, been very good at both for a very long time.

Her "book smarts" weren't of much use to us in the beginning, though. RSI had a well-established training program that was defined, organized and executed by Steph. But Emily's been able to let her inner-Sensei out in recent years, and it all started with Zendaya Coleman.

Daya, Liv Holt and Dove Cameron all joined RSI around the same time, and all three found themselves floundering in the early going. Steph did what she could but she found the trio... resistant. Realizing she needed a new approach, she decided to tap into the wealth of established talent RSI already possessed. Instead of just barking at the new kids all day long, she turned them loose and let their teammates have a crack at them.

It was a stroke of pure genius that has changed the way we do things ever since.

At any rate... as old and new paired off, Z found the perfect partner in Emily. Because Daya has one advantage that was crystal clear from the start: she's got legs that go on for miles. Though five-foot-ten might not sound gigantic, it's well above the average height for the league. She needed someone who could show her how to make that work for her, and RSI's queen of the Muay Thai style was the obvious choice.

Emily's help gave Z everything she needed to find success... and it apparently gave Steph the pupil she didn't know she wanted. More and more, you would see the Steph and Emily together. In the cage, on the training floor or huddled off in the corner... they became almost inseparable. But I had never truly appreciated what was happening until Steph's letter made sense of it all. Apparently, she had seen the future in Emily, and had groomed her successor without telling anyone. Sounds weird, I know. But it also sounds like Steph.

You know what else sounds like Steph? Using a proxy to heap all this praise on her protégé long after she left the building - thereby avoiding the emotions she was now stirring up. I knew what Emily wanted to do, right now. She wanted to hug somebody. And not just anybody. She wanted to hug Steph... who wasn't here, and wasn't much of a hugger anyway. So, she just sat there silently for a long while. Packing down emotions she couldn't release.

After nearly a full minute, she said, "I can't do it. I could never measure up to her."

I gave her an almost apologetic smile. Then I spidered my fingertips across the page in front of me, spun it around and pushed it across to her.

She leaned forward slowly... rightly suspicious of what she was about to see. Then she looked down and saw the note Steph had written at the bottom of the page.

Emily,
Yes you can.
    - Steph

And then - as expected - she started to cry.

Despite the fact that we'd known each other for years now, I had never seen Emily cry before. No surprise, she did it in the most Emily way possible. After reading the note, her face twisted up as the raw emotion escaped for just a split second. Her hand came up instantly and covered her mouth. Like she was literally pushing her feelings back inside. Her eyes came up... traveling right past me and up to wall above my head. Then her expression smoothed almost magically. No problem. Situation handled. Everyday Emily had it under control.

Except that there were two things she couldn't control.

First, no matter how she tried, she couldn't stop the tears from coming. The steady flow running down her forcibly blank face was a strange sight.

Second... she forgot Emma was sitting next to her.

Anyone else probably would have given Emily time to pull it together. Instead, RSI's self-appointed emotional fixer dove right in and ripped the lid off. It started with a soft touch on the arm. Emily turned and saw patience, understanding and safety in Emma's expression. And then she let loose what she been fighting to hold back. She leaned over and started to cry for real, and Emma was there with a shoulder for her to do it on.

It wasn't the hug she'd been searching for, but it might well have been the one she needed.

"God, I miss her," Emily said wetly.

"I know, hon," Emma said. "We all do."

"And it's just like her to pull shit like this," Emily added. "Making me cry, and she's not even here to deal with it."

Sounded to me like Emily was already starting to recover, which made sense. We all missed Steph, but it's not like she was dead. If we missed her that much, we could just pick up the phone and call her.

Still... sitting there watching these two, I knew RSI's future was in good hands. Smart, skilled, caring and acutely aware... these were the women we wanted to lead us forward. It's why Danni and I decided to struggle on without a "Steph" for so long. Because we never wanted to choose between them. Never wanted either to feel like she'd been overlooked. The stable needed both of them... and now they were going to get them.

"So," I said eventually, "am I to take this as a yes, Ms. VanCamp?"

Both women sat pack, and Emily answered with a steady nod.

"Good," I said, "and just to be clear... Steph never had an official vote when it came to making decisions, but you can bet your ass she still made herself heard."

Danni made a little noise that translated to, and how.

"Obviously, we'll be formalizing that now," I continued. "Officially speaking, you'll have just as much say-so as anyone else, and we'll continue to make our decisions as a group."

"That's right," Emma said. "All four of us."

"I'm sorry," I said. "Did somebody offer you a job?"

"Aww, c'mon... is that really how we're going to play this?"

"It's not how I planned it, but I couldn't help myself. You walked right into it."

"Yeah," she sighed. "I know."

She smiled, and I smiled back. The good feeling I had was growing. These two were going to make a helluva team, and with the four of us together... I was expecting great things.

"Well, Ms. Emma Charlotte Duerre Watson..."

I paused to get a good look at the surprise on her face.

"That's right," I said, "even knew about the Duerre."

"You looked it up on Wikipedia," Danni said.

"Still knew about it. Now, shut up."

I brought my attention back to Emma.

"So, what do you think, Watson?" I asked. "You wanna be our new Stable Leader?"

"Can I take some time to think about it?"

I raised an eyebrow at her.

"Kidding," she said. "I'm in."
