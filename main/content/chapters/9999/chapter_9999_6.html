"Why are you so serious today?" she asked.

"Just got some things on my mind."

"Something you want to talk about?"

"Later."

She looked far from satisfied, but she also knew she wouldn't get any more from me until I was ready. It's a problem we both have, and there's no sense fighting it. So she just nodded, and let it go at that.

We stepped into the cage, with her heading right and expecting me to go left. The standard go to your corners prefight moment. But when she turned to square up, she found me right there, slamming a right hand into her gut. The unexpected attack left her bent over and gasping for air. I grabbed her arm, swept her feet from under her, and dropped her flat on her back.

"I win," I said.

She sucked wind for a few seconds, leaving her unable to speak. Which only served to piss her off more than she already was.

"What the fuck?" she spat when she finally found her voice.
