To call darkness my friend would be a lie, yet we cannot be considered enemies either. Though we were, once. Many years ago. When I was still afraid of what I couldn't see.

I stood before the mirror and studied my outfit in the unlit room. Simple, but effective. A deep red shirt tucked into dark jeans and covered with a black fleece jacket. The fleece was important. Stiffer fabrics like nylon were not an option. No sense being invisible if you can't be silent.

Poets and writers bring us many varied pictures of the night. Some try to pierce the shroud and reveal the beauty hidden away. Crafting images of near-motionless owls watching curiously from high branches and tranquil mountain lakes bathed in blue moonlight.

Others allow the darkness to do what it does best. To be mysterious. To hide things. Because we fear what we can't see.
