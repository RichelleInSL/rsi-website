May 2014 - QWEST Arena - Boise, Idaho

As the media conference came to a close and the girls of Dragons Den all stormed from the stage, stable leader Sophia Bush was quick to find her friend Briana Evigan, who had a series of outbursts while on stage.

"Bri, you okay? I haven't seen you snap at anyone before. Here I thought you were going to stab Olga and then every single member of the press."

"Yeah, I'm okay," sighed Briana. "I'm frustrated. And everyone else is frustrated. And they're all making me even more frustrated. Sophs, we have to do something about this stable. We're floundering. We're the laughing stock of all the stables. It's not good. Even Emmy said it's like we're cursed."

"I know we have been underwhelming, Bri, but sometimes that just happens. It won't last forever."

Briana was still visibly frustrated. "Well it's been happening a lot lately. And that tournament was weird. Even Josie lost her first bout. That's very not Josie. You know it's a fucked up day when that happens."

"True, but maybe that's all it was. One fucked day."

"That's been most days lately. And all the girls are pissed. We've got to do something about this. There'll be a mutiny or some shit."

Sophia was more concerned now. "Pissed? Who's pissed?"

"Everyone. And I mean everyone. Even Caroline's pissed. And you know she doesn't get pissed."

"Yeah, I know," Sophia frowned. "I was pretty scared when she left. She punched a locker on her way out. The door fell off it."

Briana's eyes widened. "I can't imagine Caroline punching anything that wasn't in the cage."

"So, what do we do about it?" Sophia was in serious thought mode now.

"It's got to start at training," frowned Briana. "We must be doing something wrong. Something's not working. Half the girls said they're not even coming in tomorrow. Sophs, we're getting desperate."

"You want me to mix up the training regime?"

"I really don't think that would do it. You got any friends?"

"Friends? I got you, right?"

"Well, yeah. I mean, you know, outside the stable. Someone that might know how to help."

Sophia stared off past Briana. There was only one name she could think of. The wiliest person she'd ever known. But, it had been so long. And could she actually help? Would she? There were bound to be complications. Sophia knew it was risky. But as they say, desperate times call for desperate measures.

"I might know someone," Sophia mused aloud.

Briana looked hopeful for a moment. "Sweet. A retired trainer from your youth or something?"

Sophia was still staring off into space. "God no. It's far more complicated than that."
