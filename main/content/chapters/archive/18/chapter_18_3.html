Rod Laver Arena, Melbourne, Australia

Tonight's bout had opened with the typical pawing and prancing as the two warriors settled in. Then, Danni saw an opening - flashing out the jab... once... twice... a cover for the big right hook that followed behind. I would never know what it what she thought she saw, but it was long gone by the time the punch reached it's destination. Cole had seen it coming from a mile away, and she deflected it easily. Eager to take the early advantage, Danni had gotten too caught up in landing the blow and failed to keep her form. Badly out of position, she was an easy target as Cheryl thudded a heavy right hand into her unprotected side. Danni tried desperately to stay close and avoid further damage, but Cole pushed her back with the jab, measuring her for a vicious right hand that caught the champion square on the cheek.

From that point on, the fight had been all Cheryl. What started with a few well-placed jabs from the challenger had quickly turned into a brutal trip to the woodshed for Danni. To most, it looked like Cheryl had finally discovered some secret. Some startling weakness that no other fighter had found in Danni's game. She was everywhere, pummeling the RSI stable leader with almost every strike she threw. She pushed Danni around, constantly outmaneuvering her. As the second minute drew to a close, Cole managed to catch her prey along the cage... pressing her against the mesh as she methodically worked her over. Punches, knees and elbows just kept coming, and Danni couldn't seem to escape. Before long, she was bleeding so severly that it was hard to tell where it was coming from. When she finally worked her way free, Cole chased after her, tagging her twice more before she got her defense together.

Now I am not so delusional that I believe Danni is invulnerable. She's lost her share of fights. Sometimes, she's even lost badly... like the times she's been run over by Candice Swanepoel. Stuff like that happens. When two elite fighters get together, that nasty element of chance is magnified. Sometimes, things can just get away from you, and there really is nothing you can do. That's life in the cage.

To most, tonight looked like one of those nights. Cole appeared to be unstoppable, and it seemed like Danni was just caught up in the storm.

But I knew better.

The woman in the cage tonight was not the Danni I knew. I could see her footwork was off. Her punches were slow and telegraphed. Defensively, she was sloppy... leaving openings she was usually so smart about closing.

To those who knew her best, Danni looked lost and unprepared. While it might look like Cole was tearing her apart, in reality, Danni was giving herself away. Cheryl was the seasoned and skilled fighter she always was. Danni was a shell of herself. And only a few people on Earth knew the reason for that.

Amanda Seyfried, Anna Kendrick and Cobie Smulders knew because they were Danni's regular training partners. They had witnessed how different her prep had been for this bout.

And they had warned me.

Steph McMahon knew because getting RSI's fighters ready for fight night is her job. Normally, Danni was not a fighter she had to worry about. This time, Danni's lack of focus had nearly driven her up a wall.

And she had warned me.

Emma Watson knew because she had been watching Danni and I like a hawk for months, and she was not happy with what she saw.

And she had warned me.

I knew... because I was the reason.
