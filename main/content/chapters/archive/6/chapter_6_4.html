My left eye was nearly swollen shut, and what vision it could still provide was robbed by the blood running from the gash on my forehead. My nose was also bloodied, and a mixture of blood and sweat dripped from my chin and darkened my top. My head was ringing, and my legs were rubbery and heavy with exhaustion. I sucked in huge gulps of air but still felt as though I couldn't breathe. Worst of all, my damaged side throbbed with such intense pain that it made me want to scream.

I was hurt bad. And I knew it.

I looked over at my diminutive adversary. She, too, was a mess. Blood trickled from the corner of her mouth, as she writhed on the canvas. Her soft brown curls were so matted to her neck with sweat, they looked like they were glued there. A huge, nasty looking mouse had formed under her right eye. Her narrow, muscled torso was covered in bruises. And, her eyes... those dark brown windows that were somehow able to perfectly display any mix of emotions you could dream up... were vacant and lifeless.

My heart broke just looking at her.

Though her body wasn't able, Emma willed herself to get up. She rolled over onto her stomach, and forced herself to her feet... only to stumble away into the far side of the cage. Still, she tried to stand... clawing her way up the mesh... using her hands to do what her legs had refused. She got herself upright, still using the side of the octagon to steady herself. It was clear she couldn't fight. She couldn't even stand. Keeping one hand on the cage, she slowly pivoted to face me. With dogged determination, she inched her feet apart until she found a stance that gave her balance.

And then, she brought her guard up.

I knew then that, if I wanted to stop Emma, I was going to have to kill her. And, let's face it... I was stupid to think it would be any other way. Yes, she was conflicted about her relationship with Tiffany. Yes, she had misplaced those emotions and taken them out on me. Yes... for the first time since I'd known her, there were cracks in the armor. She was vulnerable and even a little scared. But, she was still Emma. I've said in the past that Emma can be beaten, but she cannot be stopped. When she sets her mind to something, that's all there is to it. Knock her out... break her bones... it doesn't matter. As soon as she is able, she picks herself up, and she comes at you again.

She will never... EVER... give up.

As I looked over at the young, proud, beaten woman standing in front of me, my heart dropped right out of my chest. How could this have happened? What was I thinking? What had I accomplished?

How could I have DONE this?

"No more," I said softly.

She just stood there... wobbling unsteadily... her gloved fists balled in front of her.

"No more, Emma," I said again.

"NO!" she hissed. "We finish it."

I shook my head lightly. "No," I whispered. "I'm not going to hit you again."

"You don't get to quit!" she shouted, her voice cracking with emotion. "Finish it!"

Tears welled in my eyes. "It is finished, Emma. I'm done."

"NO!" She moved to charge at me, but she simply couldn't. She took one step and lost her balance... stumbling toward me, out of control. I caught her and fell to my knees... my arms wrapped around her head, cradling her to my chest.

"FIGHT ME!" she screamed as the tears started to flow. "FIGHT!"

She swung her left hand repeatedly... the weak blows landing harmlessly on my right shoulder. I unhooked my right hand from behind her, and gently pushed her arm down to her side.

Her body shook as he sobbed openly... the crying pausing only when survival forced her to take in precious oxygen.

"Shhhh," I whispered softly as she rocked against me. "It's over."

She made no attempt to speak, or even to control the crying. She just wept.

"Emma," I whispered as tears rolled down my face. "I'm so sorry."

"Oh God, Richelle," she moaned. "What did we do?"

"Shhhh," I soothed again. "You didn't do anything. It was all... AHHHH!"

Emma sat bolt upright, knowing before she even looked at me that something was terribly wrong.

I clutched at my side as the room started to spin. My body pitched forward... my head curling to the mat.

"RICHELLE!" Emma cried.

I tried to answer, but I couldn't form the words. I rolled onto my back, gritting my teeth against the nauseating pain.

"Oh my God.... RICHELLE!!"

Emma's voice floated away from me as the lights above the cage filled my vision with blinding whiteness. I could still hear her, but she sounded like she was in another room.

"Oh, God.... HELP! SOMEBODY HELP!!"

The last thought I had before I slipped into unconsciousness was, perhaps, the most depressing thought I had ever had.

There's no one else here.
