July 30, 2014

Enough is enough.

In the three days that have passed since Emma Watson last saw fit to speak to me, the situation has degraded from bad to totally out of control. I had hoped that a little time might help her come to her senses. Instead, she has completely snapped.

After being unceremoniously tossed from my office, Emma decided that she will not participate in any stable activities until she and I meet face to face. I have repeatedly asked her to come to my office so we can do just that. But, she doesn't want to come to my office. She wants me to come down there.

To the training room.

So far, I have refused. So, Emma has taken to sitting on a chair in the training area, making disparaging remarks about me to anyone who'll listen. She has even given me an ultimatum. Meet her or fire her. I like neither option.

The words "incorrigible brat" don't even begin to cover her recent behavior. She has me worried, because she has a fight coming up in just a few days. She has me angry, because I'm really getting sick of her shit. But, above all, she has me depressed.

I miss the old Emma, and I want her back. But I'm running out of options.

I sat in the board room at the RSI training facility, trying to come up with some way to resolve the issue. With me were Danneel Harris, Stephanie McMahon, Tiffany Mulheron, Britney Spears and Elisha Cuthbert.

That's right... I've called in Britney and Elisha to see if they can help. Can you say desperation?

"Alright, guys... we're running out of time. Who's got an idea?"

"There are no more ideas," Danni said in disgust. "We've tried everything. She won't talk to anyone, unless it's about you."

"Britney... Elisha... anything?"

Britney shook her head. "No,"she said quietly. "She's gone off the deep end."

"I even asked Emily to talk to her. Sweet, friendly, lovable Emily," Elisha added. "Five minutes later, she came back, threatening to snap Emma's neck if she didn't cut the shit. That's not easy to do."

"It's easier than you think," I said. "It wasn't that long ago we were talking about sweet, friendly, lovable Emma."

No one had anything to say to that, so we just sat sullenly. Truth is, the change in Emma has everyone at RSI in a pretty deep funk. Emma was something to everyone here. Friend... teammate... even inspiration, especially in the case of some of the younger fighters. But, all of that seems to be gone now.

Though I hate to admit it, I know something has to be done.

"Richelle," Danni said solemnly. "It's time to let her go."

"No."

"There's nothing more we can do. And she's disrupting everything."

"No, Danni. We're not there yet."

"Richelle," said Tiffany quietly. "We ARE there."

I slid my eyes in her direction.

"Emma's out of control, and I don't think she's coming back. She's not willing to listen to anyone," she said sadly. "Not even me."

Tiffany's words were tough for me to swallow. As Emma's closest friend and training partner, she's the one with the most to lose if Emma leaves. She was also, for the span of about a week, Emma's lover. That relationship has been on hold... both the root cause and the victim in this mess.

For Tiff to admit she'd given up... it meant my options were dwindling fast.

I gave a depressed sigh. "We'll give it one more day. Thanks, guys."

As the defeated group dispersed, I motioned for Danni to stay behind.

"I don't know what you're waiting for," she said after the others had left. "I don't see another way."

"There is one thing we haven't tried."

She paused a minute, groping for some other solution she hadn't considered. "Please, don't tell me you're thinking of..."

"I want to go down there, Danni."

She ran her hand through her hair in frustration. "Do I have to tell you that's a terrible idea?"

I knew she was right. But, I wasn't ready to give up. Not until we'd tried everything.

"I want to talk to her."

"She doesn't want to talk, Richelle. She wants to kick your ass."

"I know what she wants. But, I feel like I have to try."

She eyed me skeptically.

"C'mon, Danni... I know she's being a bitch, but it's still Emma in there somewhere."

She said nothing, but I could tell she wanted to agree. I made my final push.

"Danni, Emma has been everything for us. She's been a fantastic champion, representing the stable with pride and grace at every turn. And, now she's in trouble. We can't abandon her."

"Don't you think she's already abandoned us?"

"I think she's abandoned everything and everyone, including herself. She needs help."

More silence from RSI's team leader.

"I have to try. I'll never be able to live with myself if I don't."

"What happens if talking doesn't work?"

It was my turn to stay quiet. But, with Danni, I don't have to say anything for her to hear me.

"Richelle," she said sternly. "This isn't you and me letting off some steam in your office. Emma's been jacked up over this for days. She's not fucking around."

"I have to do what I have to do, Danni. I told Tiff I would fight for Emma, and I will if it comes to that."

I could tell she didn't like the idea. "What makes you think that'll work any better?"

"If it gets that far, it'll mean I'm down to my last shot. It'll be the end of it, one way or the other."

She blew out a sigh. "I'm going on record saying this is a bad idea. I still don't know all of your story, but I know there's a lot more to you than you let on. But, Emma's the real deal, Richelle. If you take her on, it won't be a game."

"RSI is family, Danni. I don't play games when it comes to family."

She shook her head, still clearly unhappy. But, she knew I'd made my mind up. "How do you want to do this?"

"Let her know I'll meet her tonight, after we shut down."

She nodded. "I'll make sure everyone else has cleared out."

"That includes you, Danni." I knew that one wasn't going to sit well.

"Oh, no," she said emphatically. "No way. I can't let you do that."

"This isn't a spectator thing. Emma wants this to be one on one. That's how I want it to be, too."

"And what if Emma loses it completely? Who's going to stop her?"

"C'mon... do you really think Emma would hurt me?"

"A few days ago, I'd have said 'no.' Now... I don't know."

"I'm willing to take the chance."

Again with the hand through the hair. "I don't like it."

"Me neither," I admitted.
