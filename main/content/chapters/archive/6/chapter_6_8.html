We stayed like that long after our tears had dried. As far as I was concerned, we could have stayed like that forever. I know Danni's not as tough as she pretends to be, but to see her this vulnerable... and to know I was the one who'd done it to her... it was too much.

If I could've just kept her there... safe and protected... that would've been fine by me.

My eyes drifted to the window, and I looked out as the calm, cool waters of the river below sparkled beautifully in the midday sun.

Wait... what?

"Holy shit," I said without thinking. "What time is it?"

Danni reluctantly sat up. "It's got to be sometime after noon. Maybe closer to one."

I quickly did the math in my head. "I've been out for almost EIGHTEEN HOURS?"

She smiled knowingly. "Closer to forty-two."

"WHAT?!!" I exclaimed. "Are you fucking kidding me?"

"Trish said it was a good idea to keep you sedated for a little while. She said that the ribs needed time to set. In fact, I think she's got a bunch of rules you're not going to like." Then she smiled again as she added, "And I'm going to make sure you follow every god-damn one of them."

That didn't exactly thrill me, but I was in no position to argue. I smiled back at her, and, for the first time, noticed the sheer exhaustion that weighed on her body.

"You've been here the whole time, haven't you?"

She shook her head. "Trish made me go home at night. Steph was here a lot. Britney and Elisha, too. But, I haven't slept much, if that's what you're asking."

I nodded and looked back to the window, trying to figure out how to even begin to apologize. Finally, I turned and squared my gaze with Danni's.

"I know 'I'm sorry' doesn't cover it," I said softly, "but it's all I've got."

She just looked at me, her eyes soft and caring.

"I'm not going to lie to you," I continued. "I knew I was hurt. I knew I was hurt bad."

I looked away toward the foot of my bed.

"I just never thought about it being this serious. I never thought I'd end up here. I was focused on Emma." I stopped and brought my eyes back to Danni. "I never considered what could happen... or what it might do to my friends."

I reached out and took her hand.

"Truth is, I'm not used to having people looking out for me."

"Keep it up, and you won't."

I smiled at her, happy to see a little of the usual Danni peek out.

"I'm sorry, Danni. I promise..."

She smiled weakly as she cut me off. "Don't make me promises you can't keep."

"What do you mean?"

"The two of us in your office... this thing with Emma... it's in your blood, Richelle. We both know it's true."

I went quiet for a moment, knowing she was right. My fists are no longer my first solution to a problem. But, they have always been a last resort... a fall back when I simply didn't know what else to do.

I shook my head slowly. "I can't do things like this. And, not just because of what it did to you."

I looked away again as the scene with Emma played through my mind.

"What happened in that cage... I can't believe we did that."

She brought her other hand over, enveloping mine between hers.

"I know it worked out in the end. But, there had to be another way. Seeing her like that... what I'd done to her... what we'd done to each other... it wasn't right."

She gestured toward my ribs.

"You weren't the only one who went too far."

"I know... but we both know Emma wasn't thinking straight. I was. I should have known better."

We were both quiet for a moment.

"This isn't the way to do things."

"You're probably right. But you were trying to do the right thing."

I stared at her silently while I considered the rapidly changing landscape of the conversation.

"What the hell is going on here?" I said finally. "First, you're slapping me. Now, you're defending me. Pick a side, Harris. I can't keep up."

"Well, let me clear it up for you... what you did was fucking stupid, and you scared the living shit out of me. If you ever do something like that again, I'll kill you myself."

"Yeah... that part I got."

"But, I understand why you did what you did. You were doing what you always do. Most owners would have just run away from this mess with Emma. Champion or no, she wouldn't have been worth the aggravation she was causing. But you never saw a mess. You saw Emma in trouble. And I knew from the start you wouldn't let it alone."

I just looked at her.

"You don't quit on people. You may not have any idea what you're doing, but you'll keep blundering around until you find some way to help."

I frowned. "You don't make it sound very elegant."

She laughed at me. "Pffft... elegant? You're like a disaster waiting to happen."

I frowned a little harder.

"But, that's what I love about you. It's what they love about you, too. They all know you'll do whatever it takes to protect your girls."

"They're not MY girls, Danni."

"Yeah, Richelle... they are."

My eyes watered again. I was starting to wonder why I hadn't already passed out from dehydration.

"Every one of those girls would go to the wall for you, because they know you'd do the same for them."

"Even Britney and Elisha?" I asked with a laugh.

"They'd be the ones leading the charge," she answered seriously. "And don't pretend you don't know it."

I did. I would probably never admit it to them, but I did.

"I don't want you to ever change who you are," Danni said as she lifted my chin with her hand. "I'm just asking you not to be an idiot."

I literally laughed out loud. "I can't make any promises."

The moment felt good, so we let it linger for a while.

"What about the other thing?" she asked eventually.

"What other thing?"

"Your, uh... secret need."

I rolled my eyes.

"It's not a need," I protested. "Though, I guess there is a part of me that still enjoys it."

"So, why don't you join?"

"Join what?"

"The UMMA."

"No," I said flatly. "Those days are over for me. Competing is not something I really want to do again."

She looked at me. "You're so full of shit..."

Ah... there's my Danni.

I sighed. "I can't do it, Danni. Trish would kill me, and I'm not kidding. She'd been after me for years to give it up because my body couldn't take anymore. If I went back to it now... well, let's just say I'd rather face Emma, again."

"Wow!" she said in honest surprise. "I never would have expected you to actually follow a doctor's advice."

"I do when I know the doctor is right."

That took the smile off of her face. "That bad?"

"Bad enough."

I could see the concern in Danni's eyes.

"I'm not dying or anything," I said. "I just... this thing with Emma is not the first stupid thing I've done..."

"I'm stunned."

"Shut up."

She laughed at me. So much for concern.

"Anyway," I continued, "there came a point when I had to decide whether or not I wanted to try and live long enough to be old..."

"Mission accomplished."

I shot her a look. "You know, you're very quick with the insults now that I'm in this bed."

"How is that different from before?"

"I don't know. I guess my inability to follow through on threats to slap you one is altering my view on things."

I saw a light come on behind her eyes.

"Uh, oh... do I want to know what's going on in that crazy mind of yours?"

"Maybe later," she said with a smile.

Yikes.

Her face softened as she spoke again.

"Listen... I'm sorry about dumping on you bef..."

I put my hand up.

"Don't even," I said with a smile. "I did a stupid thing, and you let me have it. But you don't get to make me feel even worse by apologizing."

She laughed.

"You were right to be pissed," I said plainly. "I put you through hell, and I can't tell you how sorry I am. I regret a lot of what I did, and that's all there is to it."

I held my arms out to her, and she stepped into me again.

"I'm not used to seeing you all warm and fuzzy," I joked as we embraced.

"Yeah, well... don't get used to it."

She forced herself to step back. "I need to go," she said gently. "I need to get some sleep."

"I think I'm safe now," I said with a smile. "Besides... I feel a nap coming on myself."

"Actually, you've got a few more visitors," she said as she turned for the door.

The way she said it gave me serious pause.

"Who?" Please don't let it be Britney and Elisha.... please don't let it be Britney and Elisha...

She stopped and gave me an ear-to-ear smile.

"Everybody."

She opened the door and in they came, one after the other. Britney, Elisha, Steph, Emily, Aly, Tiz... they just kept coming and coming. Before I knew it, the room was packed beyond capacity.

I craned my neck and looked over top of the crowd. Danni had walked a few paces down the hall as she headed for the elevator. She turned around and caught my eye... and stuck her tongue out at me.

I wrinkled my nose and narrowed my eyes at her, but I had no hope of hiding the smile that went with it. "Bitch," I mouthed at her. She just smiled and nodded, clearly pleased with the title.

"Jesus," Elisha said, "you look terrible."

"Yeah... even worse than usual," added Britney.

"Well, that's what happens when you try to act like you're twenty years younger than you are." Stephanie, now.

"Ah, geez..." I groaned. "Who the hell let you all in here?"

"That's not a very nice thing to say," said the blonde pop star.

"Yeah," echoed her fellow team leader. "We came to see how you were doing... show our support."

"Just for the record," I said as I raised an eyebrow, "what would it look like if you WEREN'T being supportive?"

"Keep up the attitude, and you might find out," sniped RSI's fighter development coordinator.

I was desperate for help, and I looked to the only person I was confident I could count on. And, as always, I was wrong.

"Did you even put your guard up," said Emily as she stared at my injured face, "or did you just let Emma wail on you?"

I drew my hands to my chest as though I'd been stabbed. "Et tu, Emily?"

Speaking of Emma... I looked around and found her standing at the foot of my bed... hand in hand with Tiffany Mulheron. I smiled and nodded as I realized what I was seeing. The others followed my gaze, and the young couple soon found themselves the center of attention.

"All is well then?" I asked.

A smile lit up Tiffany's entire face as she nodded. Even her eyes looked like they were smiling. Emma, meanwhile...

She reached around and put both hands behind Tiff's head... gently maneuvering the blonde until they were face to face. The kiss that followed was intense and passionate. And it was not just for show. In fact, I think Emma instantly forgot the rest of us were there. Her touch was firm, loving and confident. Whatever doubts she had, Emma had clearly made up her mind and gotten past them.

The cheer that went up in the room must have stopped everyone on the floor. As the two lovers carried on, I eventually grabbed the tissue box off the bedside table and hurled it at them.

"Get a room, you two," I joked.

"Wow, Richelle," Britney said. "It looks like you actually fixed something, for once."

"Hey!"

"Yeah," added Elisha, "but this time, she broke herself instead of just a door."

"HEY!"

"Be careful, guys," Emma said. "You don't want Richelle to take you out and beat some sense into you."

"Yeah, right," chirped the songstress.

"Pfft! As if..." her fundamental twin added.

It went like that for quite a while... wise-ass remarks ping-ponging all around the room, with myself the target of every single one.

I couldn't remember a time I'd ever been happier.
