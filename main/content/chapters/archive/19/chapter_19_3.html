The sadness of the morning's news had worn off, and things were in full swing when I got back to the gym. It felt callous. Demi was our friend and now her life was in tatters. But there was nothing any of us could do about that, and there was work to be done. The show had to go on.

The most noticeable change was the trio of champions I'd mentioned earlier. Emma, Tiff and Emily were starting to turn up the heat a little, with our resident power couple getting an up close feel for what Ms. VanCamp could do. If the three of them made this a regular thing... heaven help their future opponents.

I found Danni toward the back of the massive room, getting in a workout with Daniela Hantuchova. A classic matchup if ever there was one. Both great, well-rounded champions, but each with her own style of getting the job done. The Slovakian superstar is probably one of the most offensive-minded fighters in the business. If you want to have any hope of beating her, you need to do your homework. A solid plan and the ability to withstand unyielding pressure are absolute musts. Because if you get it wrong, it's very unlikely that you'll last long enough to make any adjustments between rounds.

Danni, on the other hand, is the mistress of defense and countering. She leaves no holes for her opponents to exploit. You might think you see one, but she'll have it closed before you can get there. And if you open yourself up in the process, she'll be ready to make you pay.

I watched for a few minutes, and it was incredible stuff. The kind of non-stop action people pay big money to see. Constant back and forth with neither gaining much of an advantage, and neither giving an inch. But maintaining either style for long periods takes a lot out of you, and both looked happy to take a break when Danni spotted me and waved off the spar.

She grabbed a towel and her water bottle, took a long pull, and then plodded her way over to me.

"Met your match, have you, Harris?" I asked.

"If anyone's going to help me get ready for Cheryl," she panted, "Daniela's the one."

There was no denying the truth of the statement. All-out offense was the hallmark of not just Cheryl Cole, but her entire stable. Some were better at it than others, and Cole was the best of the group. Daniela was definitely the best stand-in we had. Not quite as high pressure but a bit more precise and calculating, with a solid defense, to boot. She was probably more of a challenge than Cole could ever be.

"You weren't gone long," Danni said.

"Going was a stupid idea," I said. "She's doing well considering, but she was too wiped out to talk much. I shouldn't have gone."

"You say that because there was nothing you could do, but the fact that you showed up... I think that'll matter to her."

I just shrugged. If I'd made any difference, it would be minuscule in the face of everything else.

"We've got that meeting in an hour," I said.

She nodded.

"I'll get cleaned up and see you there."

I went back to my office to contact the league again and make Demi's suspension official. It would be a surprise to no one, but there was a bunch of official mumbo-jumbo and formal paperwork that we needed to get through. Better to get the ball rolling than procrastinate.

I was just finishing up when Danni walked in, and we waited together for our guest to arrive.

The process of bringing in a new fighter generally starts with Steph. She identifies the talent, let's Danni and I know the plan, and then makes first contact. At first glance, this might seem like a bad strategy. Steph is known for many things, the most notable of which is her abrupt manner. She's not exactly warm and welcoming. But her training skills rank right up there too, and that's the top priority for any new recruit. Can you make me better? The answer is always yes, and Steph's the one in charge of making that happen. Besides, Grouchy Steph is generally reserved for the people that already know her. She has a polished, professional side that she brings out for stuff like this. All in all, it works very well. Once a fighter has a good feel for what we can do and the hook is set, that's when Danni gets involved to reel them in. In some ways, her role is the same as Steph's. To show off what RSI can do. But where Steph was the perfect choice to talk training and routine, Danni was the one who brought across the personality of the stable. The one for all and all for one mentality that's gotten us where we are. It's easy for her to do because it's an attitude that feeds directly down from her.

As for me, I'm always available, but rarely get involved until this final signing step. It's not that I'm above such menial tasks. It's just that, I have Steph and Danni. And when you have people that are that good at what they do, the smart move is to stay out of their way.

Of course, there are also times when I sidestep the whole process and bring in someone my own way.

At any rate, since Steph is the one the new fighter is most familiar with, she's also the point person here at the end. After another good look around and a chance to ask any last-minute questions, the final stop is my office. Which is exactly where our newest recruit was arriving now.

Steph got things started with a quick knock, then she stood aside and let our guest enter. I stepped out from behind my desk and offered my hand.

"Ms. Gomez," I said as we shook. "A pleasure to finally meet you."

"Please, call me Selena."

"Selena it is, then. And I believe you know our stable leader."

I slid right and turned a bit to make some room for Danni to step up.

"Indeed, I do," she smiled as the two shook hands. "How are you, Danni?"

"Good to see you again, Sel."

I did a mental eye roll.

They'd met twice, and already it's Sel.

And that, right there, is Danni for you.

I gestured toward my guest chairs.

"Have a seat," I said.

Selena took one chair, and Steph took the other... which is weird behavior for her. Normally, she stands against the bookcase on the far wall and scowls at everybody. But I've asked her to make an exception in situations like this because I think it's best not to scare the new people too soon.

I retook my seat. Danni followed me around the desk and rested against the low filing cabinet behind me.

"I know Steph's shown you the facilities," I began, "and between her and Danni, I'm hoping you've gotten a good feel for our little operation."

"I can't think of anything they didn't cover," Gomez answered. Then she smiled and added, "I was particularly impressed with the trophy case."

"Buttering up the boss," I said with an approving nod. "I like your style already."

"You're not the one..." Danni began.

"It's still my case," I said, "now zip it, Harris."

Selena smiled at the exchange.

"Yeah... I've heard about you two."

"Oh Selena," I replied, "you ain't seen nothing yet."

"Not even close," Steph added tiredly.

I cut my eyes to her, and gave her a threatening look that had no impact. Selena's smile broadened.

Maybe not the most professional of moments, but the new kid might as well know what she's in for, right?

I gathered a pile of forms from the corner of my desk.

"Any questions before we finalize things?" I asked.

"Actually, yes," she said. "I hate that I have to ask this, but I need to know what Demi's situation means for me."

I set the forms back down.

"You get right to it, don't you?" I said. "That's two points in your favor, so far."

"I'm sorry," she continued, "but I know how you like to do things here. I know what you were hoping for."

I had expected this from the moment we got the news about Demi. One of the big things we were hoping to get out of this signing was to give both of them a firmer footing. She and Selena had known each other for a long time, and they seemed to have a strong respect for each other. Timing and commitments kept them apart most of the time. We wanted to see what would happen if we brought them back together. We've found things work best when our fighters invest in each other, and this seemed a perfect opportunity for that to happen. Most importantly, I was hoping a bond like this would serve as a launch point for Demi. She was good and she worked hard, but her fight career was one of the many things her demons have robbed her of. Success at this level is impossible if you're repeatedly destroying your body and sacrificing everything you'd gained in your training. Our hope was that getting her into a partnership of sorts with Selena would give her something positive to focus on, and maybe help her keep her eyes on the prize.

We'd kept none if this a secret from Ms. Gomez, and we had zero interest in sabotaging her career to benefit Demi's. This was going to have to work for both of them, and we were happy to find that Selena was very intrigued by the idea.

Unfortunately, the whole plan was nothing more than a pile of freshly broken dreams now, and she would've been a fool not to ask where that left her.

"There's nothing to apologize for," I said. "It's a perfectly fair question, and the short answer is that Demi is not the only reason you're here. Not even close."

I paused and let her gauge the look on my face. To make sure she understood that I meant every word.

"First, let me say I'm sorry about what happened. I know Demi is your friend, and I know this can't be easy for you."

She gave a little shrug.

"Unfortunately, we're not as close as we used to be. In fact, I was kind of looking forward to us getting back together and seeing how this worked out."

"I'm glad to here you say that because I'm not going to lie... I expect Demi to come back, and I'm still hopeful about how that angle will turn out. But that would just be a nice bonus. You're right about how we do things here, but everybody being buddy-buddy is not enough to get the job done. It can't make up for a lack of talent or commitment. Those are the first things we look for. So, while you've been getting a feel for us, we've also been getting to know you. I know Steph worked you out some. She's also studied a lot of film from your fights. If she didn't see potential, we wouldn't be here. At the same time, Danni's been feeling things out to make sure you'll fit in with our crew. And Sel, I think it's fair to say she's satisfied."

She smiled at the way I emphasized the nickname.

"And I don't mean to be blunt," I continued, "but the fact of the matter is that I really don't know if Demi is ever going to be very successful at this. She's one of us, and we're with her no matter what. But when stuff like this happens, it really undoes everything. So, the plain truth is that, right now, I expect much bigger things for you than I do for her."

Thinking about Demi's plight but a bit of a damper on things, but the truth is what it is.

"You're talented, you're committed and you're a hard worker. That's why you're here. You've had a very good career so far, but we think you can do more. We've got good people, and I'm certain there are many you'll get along very well with. I think you're gonna like it here, and I think we can help you do some great things. No matter what happens with Demi, I expect to see your name in that trophy case before too long."

"Alright, geez!" she said playfully. "Enough with the sales pitch! I'll sign, already!"

I smiled and pushed the forms across to her, along with a pen. She signed in the appropriate places, and pushed them back. I added my signature, then stood and reached across the desk.

"Welcome aboard," I said.

She took my outstretched hand in hers.

"I'm excited to get started," she replied.

"Us too. No reason to waste time, either. Steph will take you back and get you a locker, and give you an idea of what tomorrow will look like."

Selena rose as Steph said, "Meet you out in the lobby in just a second."

Once she was gone, Steph said, "Can you both meet me on the training floor in a couple of hours?"

"Sure," Danni said. "Why?"

"I have a surprise for you."

"You?" I said. "Have a surprise? For us?"

"Yeah," she said. "I can do surprises."

"I... uhh..."

But then Steph was gone.

I turned to Danni, who was staring at the now empty doorway. She was wearing the same dumbfounded expression I was.

"Do you have any idea what the hell she's talking about?" I asked.

She shook her head slowly.

"I'm just wondering if it's gonna hurt," she said.
