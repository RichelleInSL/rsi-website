The day was winding down by the time everything was finalized with Kat, and I found myself left with quite a bit of terribly boring paperwork sitting on my plate. One suspension and two new signings meant contracts, health forms, legal documents that contained more Latin than English... all of which would eventually end up in the league offices where some bean counter would go over them with a magnifying glass, then send them back for the obligatory i-dotting and t-crossing.

It was a chore I was more than happy to set aside when Danni crossed my doorway, dressed for battle. She never said a word, and she didn't have to. This was my favorite part of the day. The time when I get to suit up and throw down with the most challenging opponent I've ever faced. Battling Danni is an exhilarating part of who we are. We've done it many times, and we know just how to push each other... to literally exhaust ourselves, both mentally and physically... in a way that is also somehow safe and sane.

Only today, I just wasn't feeling it, and Danni picked up on it pretty quickly.

"You alright?" she asked when we stopped for a break.

"Yeah, I just... I don't know. I'm just tired I guess."

"You want to call it a day?"

I gave that a little thought, which was probably a sign all it's own. But I'm not one to give in easily.

"Let's go one more."

We did, but not much changed. I felt strange. I was fine, physically. And I really wanted to enjoy this. My heart just wasn't in it.

Danni didn't ask again. On the next break, she casually grabbed her water bottle and sat down against the fence like we were done, and I didn't argue.

"Busy day," she said.

I finished a long drink.

"Crazy," I said.

"Kind of excited about Kat, though. I'm trying not to have too many expectations, but that was quite a show."

"Selena, too," I said. "We've seen a lot more of what she can do, and we know she's a solid fighter. If we can sharpen her up just a little, I think we're going to have something."

She nodded her agreement. Neither of us said anything about Demi.

"Your place or mine?" she asked.

It had become a daily question. Rare was the evening we spent apart these days.

But I decided this would be one of them.

"I think maybe I'm just going to call it a day."

She nodded and tried to hide her disappointment, but I saw it anyway. I tried to make up for it.

"I'll come by in the morning and bring Tramp with me."

Bringing my scruff of a dog along signaled I'd be spending the night. Which was no real sacrifice. Like I said, rare was the evening. But bringing him in the morning took the question out of it, and also meant I wouldn't have to waste time going to pick him up later. And since tomorrow was Friday, I was able to sweeten the deal.

"We'll spend the weekend," I said.

She gave me a little smile. It wasn't full wattage because she was still disappointed about tonight, but having something to look forward to helped some.

After we showered - separately - I went back to my office. Danni found me there behind my desk a few minutes later.

"You're not leaving?" she asked.

"I've got all this paperwork to do."

"Leave it. I can help get it done tomorrow."

"I'd rather get at least some of it out of the way," I said. "Less for me to dread."

She studied me for a while, and I could tell she wanted to say something but maybe wasn't sure what.

"I feel weird, leaving you here."

I got up and came around to her.

"I'm a big girl. I can handle myself."

"That's not what I meant."

I moved to put my arms around her. She opened hers to let me in, and we kissed. Just the feel of her against me set off a voice in the back of my brain that said I was being a fool.

But sometimes, I just can't help it.

"I'm fine," I said. "Don't worry about me."

"Text me when you're leaving."

"Didn't I just say I'm fine?"

She wordlessly held her ground, and I honestly wasn't up for a fight on something so silly.

"Fine," I said.

We kissed again. The voice got a little louder, but I continued to ignore it.

After she left, I sat down to plod through the paperwork. I decided to get the documentation for Demi's suspension done first.

That was a mistake.

I didn't even finish filling in her name before my mind started to drift. The updates that had come out later in the day had only made the situation worse. Her condition had deteriorated since my visit. Nothing life-threatening, but she was experiencing what the official statement referred to as complications. I couldn't help but wonder if that really meant she was going through the symptoms of withdrawal. Which brought us to the other bit of news. Apparently, this was not a sudden fall of the wagon for Ms. Lovato. She and her friends had apparently known exactly what they were planning to do, and they had come prepared. It turned out the likely life-saving dose of Narcan she'd received had not been administered by the police. It had been given to her by her friends. The group had somehow gotten their hands on some, and brought it along just in case.

I thought about what that meant and how stupid you'd have to be for such a plan to make any sense. It didn't fit. The Demi I knew was smarter than that. Then I thought about the overgrown little girl lying in that hospital, with her raggedy stuffed animal at her side. I thought about how vulnerable she looked, and I couldn't square that with the Demi I knew, either. The one who was so confident all the time. In the cage. In the way she faces and embraces her flaws. Hell, she sang a fucking song about it. How did that woman dissolve into the pitiful mess I saw this morning? And when did it start? HOW did it start? She'd been down this road before, and knew where it ended. What happened that made her decide to throw away everything she'd accomplished since the last time? What could she possibly have been running from that made that first high worth it?

I surfaced from my thoughts and checked the time. Almost forty minutes gone, and all I had was a form that said Demi.

Time to give it up.

I sent the promised text to Danni and headed for home. Forty-five minutes later, I was sitting in silence in my living room with a book in my hand that I wasn't really reading, and Tramp curled up on my feet. Still thinking about Demi for reasons I didn't understand. Why couldn't I let it go? Why was I so busy searching for answers I knew did not exist? But I couldn't shake the memories of Demi and her mother. God, that poor woman. If I was thinking about it this much, then what level of hell must this be for her?

Music! I thought. I needed music. Something else to take up some space in my skull.

A few taps on my phone, and my streaming service was good to go on every speaker in the house.

Ain't technology grand?

I didn't know what I wanted to hear, so I decided to let the radio feature do the work. But what to seed it with?

The answer came to me instantly, and Come And Get It by Selena Gomez was playing seconds later.

Next came Levels by Nick Jonas.

Then Lie A Little Better by Lucy Hale.

The music was like magic. Picking me up and pushing me along. Leaving the sad thoughts in my wake.

I Love Me by Meghan Trainor.

There's Nothing Holdin' Me Back by Shawn Mendes.

Another RSI connection was made with Zendaya's Replay.

My musical tastes are eclectic, and these weren't the songs I listened to most often. But they were what I needed now. Happy music. Energetic. Youthful.

I managed to get through a whole eight tracks before I got kicked directly in the teeth.

Sober by Demi Lovato.

The moment it started, it sucked the life right out of me. It was like she was talking to me. Trying to explain what couldn't be explained.

For all of these goodbyes

'Cause I'm dying inside

I wanted to cry, but couldn't find the strength. The questions rushed back in.

God Demi... why? Why did you do this to yourself?

The chorus answered.

There was a knock at my door.

Tramp flew to the front window and started to do his happy dance. This told me nothing. Mailman, pizza delivery, murderer... they all get the same reaction.

I grabbed some tissues to dry my face, and was still stuffing them in my pocket as I opened the door.

I was greeted by a dog the size of a small horse. Winzig. Danni's massive Great Dane, with his owner behind him... the leash in one hand and a pizza in the other.

The tears came anew, and I was crying and smiling at the same time.

"You don't pay attention to a thing I say, do you?" I said.

"I really didn't feel right leaving..."

She stopped short when she heard the song that was playing.

"Really?" she asked as her eyebrows went up.

"I didn't do it. It just came on."

She let the leash go and the two dogs went bounding into the living room. Actually, Winzig is too stately for bounding, but Tramp was happy to pick up his slack.

Danni said, "Hey Google, stop the music," and even though the system is tuned to my voice, the music stopped. Because any control I think I have is an illusion, and even my Google is against me.

She put the pizza on the table - well into the center in case the dogs got any bright ideas - and gathered me into her arms. By that point, I was a disastrous mess of lunacy and tears. Crying because I was still sad, smiling because I was happy to see her and laughing at the way I was acting and the thought of how stupid I must have looked.

"You're kinda pathetic," she said.

"I know," I admitted as I nodded into her shoulder.

We stayed like that for a few long minutes. Then I pulled myself together and focused on what was really important.

"You brought pizza."

"I figured you'd be too busy wallowing to eat."

"I wasn't wallowing."

She tilted her head and gave me a look.

"OK, yeah... maybe a little."

We fussed around the kitchen for a bit as I dug out plates and she poured drinks. Then we took our food to the dining table.

As we ate, we talked some more about Kat and Selena. We talked about upcoming events and TV shows and the dogs... but we avoided talking about Demi. No need to force an issue we both knew I would find my way back to in due time.

After we cleaned up, we did what we always do and retired to the den to catch up on our TV viewing. It's a backlog we're never likely to clear because we can barely make it through a single episode before we get... distracted.

She was careful with me, tonight. Not cuddling quite as close. But that just meant I made all the moves, instead.

When I snuggled up next to her and kissed her neck, she got the message that I was up for a little physical activity.

"I thought you were exhausted," she said.

"I'm feeling renewed."

She laughed. One of my favorite sounds in the world. Then she looked at me and that was it for the TV viewing as we kissed and touched and stripped our way to the bedroom.

After we finished, I lay facing the wall with Danni nestled up behind me... and I couldn't help but smile. How did we get from me sitting alone in my office to the two of us together in this bed in just a few hours? My emotional armor is heavy and thick. It's been that way for decades, and I'm pretty sure that's never going to change. There are a few people that can work their way in with enough time and effort, but Danni is the only one who can pierce straight through. Like she was undoing a combination lock. Just put in the secret code and I spring right open.

Scares the shit out of me, sometimes.

Thinking about the way I tend to shut my emotions in made me think about Demi some more. See? I told you that would happen. Only it was easier now. Not nearly as dark and sad as before. I knew Danni was the difference, but I didn't know why. And that's what I thought about. The way our moods and attitudes can change without us being able to explain why. Danni being here made me happy, but why did it make it easier to think about Demi? And why was I spending all this time thinking about her to begin with? I didn't know. Why did Demi do this to herself, even though she knew how badly it would end? I didn't know that, either... and it was very likely that neither did she. She probably never would. Because human emotions are complicated, and none are more mysterious to us than our own. And yet those totally enigmatic sentiments are why each one of us does the things we do.

And so I had my answer. The solution to the problem was that there was no solution to the problem. Terrific.

Danni laid her hand on my hip, and gently kissed the back of my neck.

"Stop driving yourself crazy," she said. "There's nothing you can do for her."

Scares the shit out of me, it does.

I turned over and kissed her. I wrapped my arms around her, entwined my legs with hers and thought the same thing I've found myself thinking every day these past few months. That I never wanted to leave this bed. That we could just stay here together where it was warm and safe. Because there was nowhere I could go and nothing I could do that would be better than this.

I knew that wasn't possible, but just being here with her now gave me strength and clarified my thoughts. A quote popped into my head. Something I'd read a long time ago, during the darkest time in my life.

I understood myself only after I destroyed myself. And only in the process of fixing myself, did I know who I really was.

Demi would recover, and she would learn from that recovery. She would be stronger because of this. Maybe strong enough that it would never happen again.

Maybe.

All we could do is hope for the best, and be there for her if she needed us.
