Author's note: I'm going to give away the ending to this one right up front because the simple fact is that there is no ending. As you'll see, this story is loosely based on real-life events. My purpose here is only to introduce the storyline and show the initial reactions of all involved. But life has not yet written the ending. Early signs are good, but there's no telling where it's ultimately going to go.

I will say this: deciding to introduce this angle wasn't something I did on a whim. In fact, I'm still not completely sure I should have taken it on at all. But I recently expressed a desire to get away from the big, 50+ chapter stories for a little while. I want to write a little more about the everyday, and this, unfortunately, is a thing that many people have to face up to... every day.
