Author's note: As I'm sure many are aware, the real life Selena Gomez suffers from an autoimmune disease called lupus. While it's true that there are some professional athletes who suffer from the disease (soccer player Shannon Boxx comes to mind), it is uncommon. My guess (and it's only a guess) is that lupus makes it very difficult to compete on the highest levels. Though physical activity is absolutely possible - even encouraged - the lupus flare ups likely cause too many interruptions in training for the person to truly excel.

Anyway... it's no secret that, while I've always been clear that my characters are not supposed to be exactly like their real life counterparts, I like to cross what I know about these women over into my stories. But, this one... I've thought long and hard about it, and I think I'm going to stay away from it entirely.

I chose to add Selena because I think there's a lot to like about her. I'm always talking about the "turn" that child celebrities have to navigate as they grow up, and my roster is full of young women who've had to make it. Some cruised through it like experts. Others hit the rocks hard before they managed to pull themselves together. I've even got a few now where the jury is still out as to whether they'll make it, at all. Selena's journey has been... interesting... and I really like who she's become. I think she'll be fun to explore, and there's no doubt I'll repeatedly tie my character back to the real deal. But lupus is a disease where you can go from being fine one day to painful misery the next. And when I think about what that would be like for a professional athlete... I'm just not sure I can represent it in a way that would do it justice. And if I can't do it right, I'd rather not do it, at all.
