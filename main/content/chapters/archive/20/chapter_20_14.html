The break was a far different animal this time. Steph and Bri each did what they could to get their respective fighter focused for the winner-take-all round that lie ahead, but they mainly concentrated on staying the hell out of the way. Things in the damage department had gotten more than a little out of control, and the wound specialists had a lot of work to do in very little time.

Most of of my attention was on Danni as I tried to gauge just how bad things were. The way her face reappeared as Marcy mopped her up and treated her wounds was amazing, but much of the damage could not be hidden. Her damaged left eye was clearly swollen, and there appeared to be two or three small cuts in that area. Blood was also trickling from a small cut on the other side. Overall, I was left with the impression that things were bad, but maybe not as bad as they seemed just seconds before. She was hurt and tired, but also alert and responding as Steph poured out her final nuggets of advice. Whether it would be enough - or if it would even matter at all - remained to be seen.

Things were much more animated in Bush's corner. She too had wounds all over, but there was an energy to her that Danni seemed to lack. She was tired for sure, but being on the positive end of an extended ground and pound session is just the kind of thing that will put a little spring in your step, and she was still riding that wave. On top of that, she had just won her second straight round, making the count square. And while Danni had won rounds with a little veteran craftiness, Sophia had won hers by edging her opponent out toe-to-toe. The fight was just about as close as it could be, but even I was forced to admit the judges would likely lean Bush's way if it ended right now.

I found myself wondering how this had happened. Sophia Bush? Taking Danni to the limit like this? What in the hell was going on? And how had she done it? There had been no real turning point. No moment when she'd sprung some shocking strategy Danni couldn't counter. She was just... here. Once again doing things I didn't know she could do. Scratching and clawing and son of a bitch... winning!

Could this get any worse?

Yes. Yes, it could.

* * *

As they came out for the final round, both looked markedly better than they had going into the break. And yet, they still looked kind of terrible. They stepped to the center of the cage for one final tap of the gloves, both totally spent - the pain and exhaustion of the brutal battle evident in their every move as they willed themselves to go just one more round. I was particularly struck by just how off Danni looked. Dragging in a way that told me what I'd seen in the corner had been my own wishful thinking. With the blood staunched and the wounds treated, I had let myself be fooled. Of course she looked better... but she was not at all recovered. She looked like she could barely hold herself up, and I wasn't the only one to notice.

At the sound of the bell, Sophia went straight to work. First came a little testing with the jab. Trying to provoke a response and see just what Danni had left in the tank. Early signs were not encouraging. Danni plodded around, relying almost exclusively on her guard to protect herself. She showed little movement, and nothing of consequence in the counter department.

I was forced to confront the fact that I had never seen her like this... and to admit that I really didn't know how she would be able to mount any sort of offense in this condition.

Sophia slowly got more daring. Combos started to flow. Jabs followed by crosses. Right hands to the body. Danni stayed square, and continued to do what was honestly a pretty amazing job of staying behind her guard and blunting most of what came her way. She made effective use of her jab, too. Timing it nicely to break things up and keep Bush from really teeing off. But that all felt like survival instinct. Just her employing a little veteran savvy to stay alive in a bad situation that was getting worse. Bush may not have been landing everything, but damage was being done. Already, blood was seeping from the wounds Marcy had patched together. More importantly, this was not how Danni operates. Movement is the key to her game. Make 'em miss, then take advantage. Change angles. Make opportunities. She couldn't work her magic as a stationary target, and it wasn't long before the wheels started coming off completely.

Sophia swarmed in - a tornado of flying fists. Danni fended her off well early, but things quickly got out of control. She was forced to clinch, and that's when the grim state of the situation really came clear. As I watched Sophia muscle Danni to the fence with the ease of a hot knife cutting through butter, I knew things were well and truly fucked. Danni had nothing left.

Sophia pushed free and let fly with the punches again. As before, Danni's training and experience took over. Her guard came up, and thwarted much of what Bush tried. But now she had nowhere to go even if she wanted to, and Sophia was throwing punches in overwhelming fashion. More and more were getting through, with Danni offering little response. Reeser was right there, looking ready to call it at any moment.

As the situation worsened, my mind groped for something to pin my hopes on. A single positive thought floated to the surface: I was surprised how well Danni's damaged eye was holding up under the assault. A credit to Marcy's incredible talents, but otherwise not much to hold on to.

I couldn't pretend I was totally surprised. I'd seen enough true wars in the cage to know this is usually the way it ends. This fight had been everything it had promised to be, and more. Filled with surprising moments no one would have predicted. Both fighters had been within inches of victory more than once, only to have the other battle her way back from the brink. A roller coaster of a battle had mesmerized and thrilled everyone present, but now the ride was coming an end. The human body can only take so much punishment, and one of these fighters had finally reached her breaking point.

I just couldn't believe that fighter was Danni.

Which just goes to show how good an actress she really is. Even good enough to fool me.

I couldn't tell you how Miss Harris found her moment. Bush was all over her, but I saw no rhythm to the attack. No timing to pick up on. I only know that everything changed in an instant. Suddenly, the next right Bush threw found nothing but empty space where Danni's head had just been. Just gone. Slipped out and off the fence, disappearing like a ghost. Then she drove her own right hand into the side of Sophia's head. The strike came in at a sharp angle, with no chance for Bush to even see it, let alone block it. And it came with everything on it, the impact buckling Sophia's knees and turning the whole fight on its head.

Bush recovered her feet and tried to square up, but she looked shaky at best. Danni put two more punches into her midsection. Sophia tried to retreat, not realizing they were completely turned around and now she was the one backed up to the fence. Her heel caught for a second, throwing her off balance. Danni surged forward and pushed her deep into the mesh. A nasty elbow brought fresh blood from the wounds around Bush's eye. She brought both arms up to shield her head, so Danni immediately slammed a knee into her belly. It went like that for a while. An astonishing reversal of the situation that had existed scant seconds before. Now it was Bush covering up as best she could, but Danni was finding all the holes. Responding to the killer instinct coursing through her veins. Another knee brought Sophia's guard down, and Danni snuck a left into her face. Then she loaded up the right.

Sophia threw both arms up to fend off the blow. A very natural reaction. One it was clear Danni had been counting on. She abandoned the strike and grabbed Bush's left arm. Moving with a practiced speed. Leaving Sophia to realize what was happening only after it was too late to stop it. Panicked, the Dragon fighter took a big swing with the right hand. Hoping to convince her friend/opponent to let go by smashing her in the face. But the suddenly alert Danni was ready for that, too. Maneuvering the captured arm and using it to shield the incoming blow as she rotated to her left. Turning with purpose. Lifting the arm higher. Positioning for a judo throw. One of my favorites, mostly because it looks spectacular. Sophia quickly realized what was happening. She started to turn right. To try and set her feet and use her legs and body weight to fight off the throw. Exactly the right counter. And also exactly what Danni had expected.

Passing up the impressive visual of flinging Bush across the cage, Danni instead revealed what had been her true plan from the start. Putting a little pressure on Sophia's arm to lock the elbow, she was able to use it like a broom stick as she rotated back to her right. With a firm grip on the arm and a right hand flat on Sophia's chest, she forced her opponent back while simultaneously sweeping her legs out from under her. The move was simple, and that's what made it brilliant. Instead of tossing her around like a rag doll, Danni had quickly and elegantly put Sophia right where she wanted her while maintaining total control. Effectively eliminating all options for escape before Bush even had a chance to think of them. In the next instant, Danni dropped to a sitting position and kicked her legs out across Sophia's body and around the still trapped arm. Legs locked in tight, she leaned back and flexed the arm over her right thigh.

Game, set, match. Bush would be screaming her submission in seconds.

Reeser swooped in and got ready. It was just a matter of time. Quite a lot of time, actually. Much more than I expected. One second became five, and there was a bit of a struggle going on. Danni made a loud sort of growling noise. I thought maybe she was trying to say something, but her words were rendered unintelligible with her mouthguard in. The struggle continued. I started to worry that something was wrong. The armbar looked textbook. Continuing to resist put Sophia at risk for serious injury. Contrary to what people say, it's nearly impossible to break someone's arm this way, but it's surprisingly easy to destroy the elbow joint by tearing all the ligaments apart. So how was she withstanding it? Had she managed to squirm into some kind of position that relieved the pressure? Could she actually escape?

My worrying went for naught. After ten seconds that felt like a hundred, Bush finally tapped. Reeser signaled for the bell, and the place went crazy.

Danni released the hold and spun up to a sitting position. She looked exhausted and bloody and... kinda pissed, actually. My attention went to Sophia as I prayed the elbow hadn't given way. A quick assessment showed no sign of it. No writhing or cradling of the arm. She stayed on her back with both hands palm-down on the mat beside her. Looking bitterly disappointed as her mind slowly accepted all that had happened in the last thirty seconds. How a title victory she'd had within her grasp had been so shockingly ripped away from her.

The two of them made for an oddly disjointed scene amidst the pandemonium around them.

To me, it looked like the ending I'd been expecting. I couldn't predict who would play which role, but I knew all along there'd be no immediate joy for either of them.

The cage door opened and people streamed in. Rather than standing, Danni slid over to make room as the medics and the Dragon's Den staff hurried to Sophia. Soon enough, Team Rampage arrived. Steph first, with Amanda and Marcy behind. Their emotions ran the gamut.

Mandy was ecstatic. The simple and appropriate response after watching her friend and teammate win her title back in dramatic fashion.

Marcy was smiling, but contained. No surprise. I had never seen her otherwise.

Steph's expression was grim as she extended a hand and hauled Danni to her feet.

It was a look that told me plenty.

She and I had never discussed what the aftermath of this fight might look like. A big key to Steph's success as RSI's head fight strategist was her laser-like focus. Her job includes everything that happens both before and during a fight. What comes after is not normally something she concerns herself with, but the expression she was wearing now told me I wasn't the only one who knew this win was anything but normal.

The two shared a few words before Marcy - who had no tolerance for niceties when she had a job to do - shouldered in to look her fighter over. After toweling away the blood, she applied a little salve here and there, then finished her survey with a resolute nod. Good enough, for now.

Steph stepped back in and said something that finally made Danni smile. Then she nodded over to where Bush was now up and standing, looking bruised and bloodied, but otherwise okay. Steph said something else and I saw Danni nod. Then Bush looked over and the two locked eyes. The medics read the situation and got out of the way. Nothing was wrong with Sophia that couldn't wait a few more minutes.

Danni moved first, but Sophia was quick to match her as the two met in the middle of the cage. Not for a quick hug between competitors. This was a fierce embrace as they pulled each other close. Opponents no more, their friendship took center stage. They took their time with it, too. Congratulating. Consoling. Taking the first steps on the road to whatever came next. Both with tears in their eyes they dared not release. Because people wouldn't understand everything they were feeling, and they didn't want to have to explain themselves later.

At one point, Danni pushed back and hit her friend with a few pointed words, punctuated with some animated hand gesturing. I had no idea what the story was, but I was certain it had to do with whatever had her so angry in those first seconds after the fight was called off. Bush responded with an apologetic smirk, and a little what are you gonna do? shrug. Danni gave her a semi-playful punch in the arm. Then she seemed to let it go as the hugging resumed.

When they finally parted, another flurry of activity followed. The medical staff resumed their work with Sophia. A couple of them trickled over to check on Danni, with Marcy right there to see what came of it. It took less than a minute for both fighters to get the interim all clear. Each would get a full checkout later.

The formalities came next. The fighters and their teams lined up around Reeser for the official announcement. Danni's hand was raised, and the belt was brought over to the new champion. Sophia helped secure it around her waist. There was more hugging, and now the tears came. Because fuck it. They didn't have to explain themselves to anyone.

As I surveyed the scene, I tried to take stock of the situation.

For my part, it had been a long and often nightmarish experience, and I was just about wrung out emotionally. But I got what I wanted in the end. Danni had the title back, and no one suffered any permanent injury in the process. Hallelujah.

But what of the fighters themselves? They had done a lot to make this matchup happen, and it had been everything they could have hoped it would be. They'd left it all in the cage tonight, and they'd put on a show for the ages. Everyone else was more than satisfied, yet the fighters themselves didn't seem to be enjoying the fruits of their labor very much. Would that change later?

Maybe. It was too raw now to tell. Two close friends had just beaten the living hell out of each other, with blood still trickling from their wounds. Beyond that everything about the post-fight situation was a mess because the two were at opposite ends of the same seesaw. A coveted title had been won. An achievement Danni would usually look forward to celebrating with her friend. On the other side, a long sought and hard won title had now been lost, and Danni was definitely one of the first people Sophia would usually turn to for consolation.

Maybe when they looked back on it, it would all feel different. But right now, it was complicated, and both were left with a hole where the other should be.
