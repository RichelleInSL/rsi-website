I can't say off-hand when it had all started. A few months ago? A year? All I know is it felt like only yesterday. First there was one. Then another. Then another still. A trio of rookies brought into the Rampage Sports, Incorporated fold. Fresh, raw talent added to the roster for the first time in years. Wide-eyed and eager, they brought with them an air of excitement and limitless potential.

Then they each, in turn, had promptly wet the bed.

It was a disastrous beginning with plenty of blame to go around. We had been short-sighted in our plans. They had been recalcitrant and cliquish. Retreating into themselves at a time when they needed to open their eyes and soak in some of the knowledge and talent that surrounded them here.

The situation got ugly fast, and it led to some truly pitiful performances in the cage for all three of them.

My solution had been to knock down the wall they'd built around themselves, and I'd love to tell you I did it in some innovative and thoughtfully considered way. But that, to be quite honest, is simply not how I roll. Instead of knocking down some metaphorical wall, I had physically knocked all three of them on their ass. Not exactly a rational move, but it did prove effective. Sure, emotions bubbled over as the situation skirted the edge of disaster, but it also forced open the lines of communication. Things said in anger, sadness and humiliation turned out to be things that needed to be said, anyway. And once we finally got all the cards on the table, we were able to reshuffle the deck to everyone's benefit.

Life for the trio has been much better since then. With their teammates in their corner instead of in their way, each of them has been making some noise in her own way. But not all have found themselves on quite the same trajectory, and one even seems to have lost sight of what she's aiming for.

She turned out to be about half right.

Once Olivia Holt took the blinders off and found her place among her teammates, that talent she was so damn sure of started to show. Though she wasn't exactly the MMA savant she thought she was, the kid had all the raw materials she needed. Starting with one hell of a work ethic. Now that she understands the advantages her teammates can bring her, she's been spending every second she can steeping herself in their skills and knowledge.
