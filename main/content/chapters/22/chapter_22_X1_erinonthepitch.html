I walked out of the gym, clickety-clacked my way across the access road then finally dug my cleats into the perfectly manicured grass.

I closed my eyes as I took a deep breath, then reopened them as I slowly it out.

It all goes away out here.

My brothers could be on the moon, for all I cared. History, Science, Queens, Minions, ........ all of that was behind me.

In front of me was the lush green grass of the Oceanview soccer field. A hundred and ten yards long and eighty-five yards wide. Start me in one corner and I could walk the whole thing blindfolded.

For me, soccer was the perfect sport. It helps that I had some great coaching at the rec level that set me up and I think we've established how well I work with others.
