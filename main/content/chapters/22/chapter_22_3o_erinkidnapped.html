It all seemed to happen impossibly fast.

I had just gotten off the bus. I watched as it continued to the stop sign, then turned out of sight as I made my own turn for home.

Suddenly I was grabbed. Both arms nearly simultaneously. Daylight vanished into darkness as something went over my head.

"What the fu...?"

Something hit me in the stomach, and the air rushed out of me. I couldn't breathe. I couldn't talk. I couldn't even make a noise.

My arms were forcibly bent and my hands somehow bound behind me.

I was half-shoved, half-dragged for a few feet. I kicked and thrashed the whole way, but it was no use. I couldn't fight what I couldn't see.

My head bounced hard off something solid. I recoiled instinctively, but someone pushed my head down and forced me forward. I bounced off a few more things, then came to rest lying sideways across what felt like a couch or chair.

Strong hands grabbed me again from both sides and sat me upright. It was a struggle. Wherever we were, space was tight.

Then I heard the unmistakable sound of car doors closing. With the deep thunk and sudden silence you get when you're on the inside.

I finally got some air in me.

"What the fuck is happening?"

I twisted and tore, but the hands grabbed me and pinned me against the seat.

Someone hit me again.

Then I heard the engine start.

I fought frantically and screamed questions into the air.

The answers I wanted never came.

Every time I made a sound I got hit and fighting bound and blind was a useless effort. Meanwhile, no one else spoke a word.

I quieted down for a while. Right up until I realized the drone of the engine had been rock steady for at least ten minutes. Which meant we were on a high speed road with no traffic lights. In this area, that almost certainly meant the Garden State Parkway. From there, we could be going ANYWHERE.

I lashed out again, and my effort was rewarded with the same results. Someone punched me in the gut, and unseen hands pinned me back down.

I eventually realized I was going wherever they were taking me, and there wasn't anything I could do about it.

With no one talking and nothing to see, I had only the terrifying questions in my mind to keep me busy.

Who were these people?

Where were they taking me?

Were they terrorists?

An obviously ridiculous thought, but all possibilities somehow made sense in the context of this mad drama.

I tried to stay calm and sow seeds of hope.

It was broad daylight. Someone had to have seen them take me.

My backpack! I dropped it when they grabbed me. Someone would find it and start asking questions.

None of this made me feel particularly better.

An eternity later I became aware that the speed had slowed, and we were making frequent turns. Wherever we were going, we were getting close.

Time and distance were virtually impossible to track in the darkness. I was left with the feeling that we'd gone a long way, but I also felt like we were probably still in New Jersey.

After a few more turns, we eased to a stop.

Instantly, I was grabbed again. I tried to make their lives hard. Using techniques I'd used against my brothers. Alternating between pulling and thrashing like an animal and simply making myself dead weight. This can be a very effective strategy under the right circumstances — which these were not. One of them grabbed my left arm and started dragging me while another pushed me from behind. The coordinated effort had me out on the ground in seconds.

Again, I was dragged. Quite a ways, this time.

Then suddenly my hood was ripped off.

I squinted against light that wasn't there. Darkness had fallen during the journey.

I looked around at my attackers and immediately realized I recognized them all.

Chuckles, Shifty, Hoodie and Number Six were standing in front of me, while The Follower and Blondie kept hold of my arms. I was encircled by the whole Minion cast.

They were terrorists, all right. The terrorists of Oceanview High.

"What the FUCK?"

Nobody said anything.

"Why am I here?"

No one spoke or even moved, and it was immediately clear they weren't going to.

I squirmed a little, but Follower and Blondie held on tight and I still had no hands to fight with.

Besides... the big reveal wasn't over. Someone was very obviously missing.

I looked around at my surroundings.

We were in some kind of courtyard between two L-shaped multi-story buildings, with openings at the short end of each L just big enough to walk through.

It was the kind of place that could easily be made pleasant. A garden or at least some flowerpots. Maybe some benches. Simple touches that could turn it into a nice common space for the people in the buildings to enjoy. But none of that had happened. It was the blankest of blank slates. Just a cold, empty square of harshly lit concrete that rarely — if ever — saw any sign of life.

The buildings themselves looked disused. Every window was dark, and there were no curtains or shades. No flowers or decor. If there were people inside, there should have been something to see. Lamps or furniture or just old junk laying around. But there was nothing. They were either never used, or abandoned long ago.

The scene gave me the impression we were in a city somewhere, which told me very little. Even if I assumed we were still in Jersey, that left plenty of possibilities. The only new info I had was that we'd likely gone north, and even that wasn't a certainty.

They held me there in the middle of the square for a while. All of us waiting for the grand arrival.

It came quickly enough.

First, I heard the footsteps. Not the click of heels or hard-soled shoes, but the scuff of rubber against the stony surface.

I followed the sound to one of the openings.

After a few seconds, a shadowy figure appeared in the distance.

She walked toward us slowly, dragging her feet a little bit. Deliberately scraping her sneakers on the concrete to make the sound.

I didn't need any help figuring out who it was. The Minions were all around me, so their Queen had to be here, too.

The melodramatic anticlimax came just a few seconds later, when Christine emerged from the shadows and into the stark, fluorescent light.

"Are you shitting me?" I yelled. "This is because I bumped into you? A fucking year ago?"

"Of course," she said coolly. "I told you you would have to pay."

"Then take me out behind the school and beat me with a stick, you crazy bitch! You don't kidnap me and drag me to... where the fuck are we, anyway?"

If the name-calling bothered her, she didn't let it show.

"We're in my little playground," she answered. "Where we can have our fun without anyone bothering us. No cops. No people. No one around to hear you scream."

Well, now didn't that just sound delightful?

"Look, I don't understand. I tried to help pick up your books. I've apologized at least a dozen times."

"Which was all very nice of you, but the rules are still the rules."

Rules?

She gave a little nod. Follower and Blondie released me instantly, and whatever was binding my wrists was cut loose.

This only left me more confused.

"You may begin," she said.

"Begin wha..."

That's when Shifty stepped forward and buried her fist in my gut.

By the time I realized she was hitting me, it felt like her knuckles were touching my spine.

The punch had hit well below the ribs. In the area where there's nothing but muscle to protect the squishy stuff behind, and a lot of my squishy stuff suddenly felt badly damaged.

I fell immediately to my knees — struggling to breathe.

No one touched me. They just laughed and watched me struggle.

They were arrayed all around me now like the numbers on a clock. Follower was straight ahead at the twelve o'clock position with Blondie at the two. Then came Chuckles, Hoodie, Shifty and Number Six, with Christine circling the periphery.

After a few minutes, I got my wind back, then forced myself to my feet.

"What the FUCK?"

Blondie took her turn, aiming for the same spot. This time I saw the attack coming and got my arms down to stop it.

Christine spoke again.

"Teach her obedience."

Blondie took another shot while my back was turned, digging a punch into my side.

I turned to face her and Six kicked me in the other side.

I spun to her and Chuckles got her chance.

My battles with The Morons had taught me how to handle tough odds, but six to one was way outside my skill level.

The assault was relentless but very specific. No one hit my face. Every blow went to the body. Ruining and breaking things no one could see.

"Stop," Christine said. "Let her recover."

"Stop wasting your energy," Christine said. "You're going to need it."

"I'm sorry," I said again.

"Stand when you're ready to continue."

"You will either stand, or my girls will pick you up," she said coldly. "The lesson must continue."

"What LESSON?"

"Yours."

At least one fact had been established: Christine was fucking nuts... and the other six weren't far behind her.

"Pick her up."

Hoodie and Six stepped forward to do the honors, but I stood up on my own. The odds were already impossible. I didn't need the extra challenge of being restrained.

"Resume."

The attacks started from behind this time, and the blows came again in a seemingly relentless torrent. I no longer had any idea who was doing what.

It took longer this time. I was incapable of being a threat of any kind, so they had tightened the circle. Leaving me no room to fall. Allowing the torture to go on indefinitely.

The pain was unbearable. Tears streamed down my face, and I'd have screamed if I could have. Every strike found its mark, and terrible things were happening inside me. The middle of my body felt like liquid — sloshing back and forth as I careened around helplessly.

Eventually, the Mistress got bored.

"Stop," she said.

They all stepped back and I fell immediately. Just flopped over sideways in whatever direction the final strike sent me because there was no one left to catch me.

"Let her recover."

Oh god... no.

"Please," I sobbed. "No more."

Nobody said anything.

"Please! I'll do anything!" I screamed.

This had to be what they wanted, right? To have me terrified, broken and begging for mercy?

It had to be.

Again, there was no immediate response.

"Stand when you're ready to continue," Christine said eventually. "Do not make us wait."

A blood-curdling scream roared out of me as I surged to my knees.

I can't let them do it again. I'll die.

All of my focus went to finding a way to survive.

I have to get out of the middle. Out of the circle...

If I can just get them in front of me...

It was not a great plan. Six on one was still very unlikely to go my way, especially in the condition I was in.

But it was something.

A step.

A way to improve the odds, even a tiny bit.

"Pick her up," Christine commanded.

Hoodie and Six came again.

I decided on Six for no discernible reason. I just had to try SOMETHING.

With a burst of energy I didn't think I had, I lurched to my feet and ran at her.

The plan was not really a plan, at all.

I was going to run through her.

End of plan.

My move caught her off-guard, and she shied away on instinct.

The contact was light. Just a brushing of our arms as I blew by her. But the mission was accomplished.

I must have been a hell of a sight as I turned to face them. Half-dead and wobbling like a drunk. Struggling just to stand. But I was out of the circle and I could see them. All six, clustered right in front of me.

Maybe I was still going to die, but I would at least see the end coming.

I got myself steadied and brought my hands up in front of me. Drawing on the last precious drops of my resolve.

Come at me now, bitches!

Without warning, a white hot pain shot through my left leg.

Something had hit me hard near the midpoint between my ankle and my knee, and I went down again. Collapsed onto my right knee, with the newly injured left leg sticking straight out. It felt broken, and maybe it was. It was impossible to gauge the severity of this new pain against all the rest.

I got my hands down to secure my balance, then looked to see what had happened.

I turned to find Christine smiling wickedly as she drew back the foot she'd just kicked me with. She had circled around behind me while I was busy getting myself out of the center of hell.

The attacks did not resume. The Minions were waiting for a sign from their queen.

The lull gave me another chance to consider the hopelessness of my situation, which is probably exactly what the bitch wanted.

My body was destroyed. Beaten, sagging and utterly useless. My leg might be broken. And with Christine getting involved, the odds I'd just worked so hard to improve were suddenly worse than ever.

I struggled again to comprehend this nightmare.

Christine was clearly psychotic in a way I'd never seen outside the movies.

Kidnapping? A gang attack? Murder?

This wasn't a fight after school. This was prison-worthy shit. How could she even dream she would get away with it?

And what was with the others? I knew some people had no spine. That they just wanted to be cool. To belong. They needed it in a way I didn't understand, and they would do just about anything they thought would make it happen. But this? Did they have no conscience? What made them think this was OK? What gave them the right to just kick the shit out of someone?

What gave them the right to do this to me?

But what could I do? There were so many of them.

The core of my body felt like jello. Struggling just to hold all the other parts together, let alone help me fight.

My arms went slack, and my balance wavered.

I wasn't dead, but I wanted to be.

God it all hurts so bad.

There was no point in trying anymore. Whatever was going to happen was going to happen. Nothing I could do would stop them.

My eyes swept over my torturers. All of them indistinct. Just a blur of smiling, laughing faces, with Christine lurking behind.

So many...

My thoughts got hazy. Confused and indistinct.

How could they...

How could I...

Phrases started to repeat in fuzzy, incomplete patterns.

I can't...

How could I... fight...

The hopelessness started to take me... my body teetering on the edge of total collapse.

Then something else took over. Some emotion I didn't actually own. Suddenly drowning out every other thought.

The words in my head became a voice talking directly to me.

How could you NOT?

How can you quit?

The strength in my arms returned and I got my weight recentered above my feet.

The voice screamed at me.

Who gives a shit WHY?

They're KILLING you!

YOU HAVE TO FIGHT!

It was more than a voice. It was almost like another person, demanding that I let her take over.

So I did.

I rose to a crouch. Fingers flexing and relaxing rhythmically.

We can't let them get away with this, she said in a much more gentle tone. We have to make them pay.

I felt heat spreading from the center of my chest to the tips of my fingers and toes. Not radiating. Surging. Coursing through me like blood.

I was no longer worried about whether or not my injured leg would be able to do its job or whether my body would hold together.

No longer worried about the odds.

No longer thinking about the pain.

No longer thinking at all.

My feet seemed to get into position by themselves. My left planted on the concrete as the right slid back. Balanced and ready.

White hot fury twisted the expression on my face. Teeth crushed together like I was biting through a rock. Upper lip curled in a nasty snarl. Breath coming in savage rasps. Every muscle in my body quivering with rage.

My vision narrowed. The edges dark as night, but the center crystal clear. Like I was looking through a tunnel.

The voice spoke again. Warm, friendly and wicked. Like the devil on my shoulder. A dark partner, with whom I now shared a secret.

Pick one, she whispered.

I turned and found The Follower standing at the end of the tunnel.

I did it again and again. Over and over like a machine.

Something inside her broke. I felt it go. There was a sharp crack, and the area beneath my fists seemed to collapse. Then she started making a god-awful gasping, wheezing noise.

Nothing changed inside me. I wasn't shocked or saddened or horrified or pleased.

I just stood — left leg screaming but doing its job — and turned toward the rest of the mob.

Take another, the voice said.

Blondie appeared at the end of the tunnel.

I launched like I was shot out of a cannon. Zero to full sprint in what felt like an instant. But my assault on Follower had carried me away from the crowd. The distance between us was too great, and Blondie had time to prepare.

But not much.

The best she could do was get set, then launch her right fist in my direction.

It was a good punch. Not girly or flailing or slow. But every sense I had was working overtime and slowing the world to a crawl. So, I knew exactly what was coming from the instant she first moved. Read it like a book I'd already read a hundred times before.

My left arm came up as my head moved right. The punch sailed by — swept aside by the arm.

Instincts took over. Instincts I would never have previously claimed to have.

Everything I'd ever learned about fighting had come from dealing with The Morons. My brain had compiled a library of small lessons based on balance, leverage and position.

Now, my dark friend began to weave those simple techniques together in ways I'd never imagined in my darkest nightmares.

My right hand came across and grabbed her bicep. My left snatched her wrist, and I immediately flexed it backward. Bending the arm and locking the elbow. I twisted the wrist. The whole arm spun as far as it could until the shoulder reached its limit. Putting her wrist, elbow and shoulder all under my control.

I used the arm to maneuver her, putting her body between me and the others. Keeping them at bay.

Keeping the wrist locked, I brought my right arm up then slammed my forearm through the back of her elbow.

The joint flexed at least an inch in the wrong direction. Again, I felt the damage being done. The popping of tendons and what I thought was the snapping of bone.

She fell to her knees and started screaming.

I kicked her in the face and the screaming stopped.

By now, the others had started moving. Finally seeing the danger and taking action.

So, I didn't have to pick again. Number Six stepped forward and volunteered to be victim three.

As her shoulder made contact, I stepped back. Moving with the blow to lessen the impact.

My right hand flashed out and grabbed a fistful of her hair, then I spun and smashed her face into the wall.

I still had Six's head in my hand.

Hoodie skewed left and dropped like a dead person.

I put my arm out to my right and dropped Six like a worthless sack of shit as I advanced on the rest of the group.

She croaked out an awkward laugh then turned and ran, with Shifty right behind her.

Sensual.

Almost erotic.

She was getting off on this.

Oh, really? she cooed in a way that sounded keenly interested in this new challenge. Like she was also enjoying herself.

Get your revenge in first.

The fucking bitch had hit me.
