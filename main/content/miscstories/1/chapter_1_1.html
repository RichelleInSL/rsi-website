Again they circled, fencing with their jabs as they formulated their separate plans.

This time, it was Miley who committed to the first attack - leading with the jab, followed by a hook with the same hand. Looking for the element of surprise by staying on the same side.

Emma calmly absorbed both on her defense, then fired back.

Jab, Cross.

The left hand hit Miley almost dead center, but she swayed out of range of the right. Then she came back with a right hand of her own.

The punch smacked Emma in the mouth, but she just took it and responded again.

The buzz of the crowd rose and fell in waves as the two went back and forth. No attack went unpunished, and it soon became clear that neither fighter was going to back out of the skirmish. This was it... toe-to-toe until one of them fell.

The rumble became a thunderous clamor when Miley scored a clean one-two that staggered her opponent. Emma tried to set her feet and respond, but Cyrus was right on top of her - whipping her head back and forth with another strong combo. The punches were clearly hurting Watson, and she was forced to duck behind her guard and retreat to try and gather herself. But Miley had no intentions of giving her the opportunity.

A pair of strong body blows kept the actress reeling. Miley kept coming and turned up the intensity.

Jab-Cross-Hook-Cross.

The combo did no damage but forced Emma's guard way up. Sensing a set up, Watson started to slide left. Miley reloaded her left hand and pounded a hook into the brunette's side that stopped her cold.

On a roll and wanting to keep it that way, Miles came back upstairs and launched another flurry.

Jab-Cross-Hook.

The jab squeaked through but Emma ate up the follow-up punches on her guard. Still, Cyrus kept coming. She'd made up her mind that this was it. It was late in the fight and she had her opponent on the defensive. She knew she couldn't waste this chance. So, she threw caution to the wind and let it all fly... keeping the pressure on while she hunted for the opening that would let her put Watson away.

Punches seem to rain down on Emma from every angle and direction, and, once again, it was head movement that kept her alive. She bobbed and wove as she retreated... watching Miley's hands as closely as she could, and getting out of the way enough to stay on her feet. But Cyrus was still hurting her, and she couldn't keep this up forever. She mixed the jab in a few times, but Miley was oblivious. Hell bent on finishing things this time, she just kept punching and pressing forward.

And Emma was running out of room.

Tension filled the room as the crowd sensed the end. Some of Emma's fans screamed for her to get out of there, while others were silenced by worry as they feared the worst. Miley's supporters went wild, ready to finally celebrate the victory they'd been so tantalizingly close to multiple times before.

The only one not losing it was Emma. Despite the chaotic look of the exchange, she gathered her focus and started to sense a rhythm in it.

Though Cyrus was methodically probing for holes to score the knockout, Emma's tightened defense made head-hunting difficult. So, Miley would go low, pounding her body until she brought her guard down. That's when Emma would throw the jab in and try to get the space she needed to escape. But Cyrus would stop her by coming back up quickly... and she would always do it with the same combination.

Jab-Cross-Hook.

Sensing the fence was coming, Emma knew she was only going to get one shot at this. If Cyrus managed to push her against the mesh again... she didn't think she could take another beating like that.

As Miley pressed forward again, Em tried to stand her ground. Cyrus switched to the body, and a pair of hooks forced Watson back another step.

Emma let her guard slip a little as she felt the edge of the chain link dig into her foot. Miley took the bait and came back upstairs, and Emma brought her hands back up and sheltered herself... peeking between her arms to track her opponent.

Jab-Cross-Hook.

Emma made her move. Instead of simply blocking or dodging the last punch, she parried it... actually pushing it aside with her left arm as she rotated outside of it. The punch whipped by and momentum carried most of Miley along with it.

Emma took another step, then set her feet and swung. When Cyrus turned to get her opponent back in front of her, she spun directly into the path of Watson's right hand.

BOOM!

The punch ripped Miley's head sideways as blood and sweat exploded from her face. She staggered back against the fence and turtled up behind her guard as Emma poured it on. There were no fancy combinations. Just left, right, left, right in a never-ending stream. She changed levels and angles constantly. Miley managed to block some, but the punches never stopped.

Left-Right, Left-Right.

The crowd was in a frenzy, with both sides shouting and screaming in a tumultuous, unintelligible roar. The Cyrus side was screaming for her to move, while the Watson group went ballistic as their fighter found new life, once more.

Left-Right, Left-Right.

Miley did a good job of staying covered up, but Emma's relentless pounding made it impossible to even get a good view of what was happening. Every time she tried to sneak a look, she ate Emma's fist.

Left-Right, Left-Right.

She knew she had to do something. Anything. With no way to make a plan, she was getting ready to simply lash out. Take a swing and hope to get lucky.

Left-Right, Left-Right.

Suddenly, there was break in the rhythmic assault. Confused, Miley peeked around the left edge of her guard.

She had barely enough time to think, Oh, shit!  before everything went dark.

Emma's foot traveled from the floor to the side of Miley's head without the slightest interference. Like pummeling the heavy bag at the gym. It landed just below the singer's ear with a sickening POP!  that made the audience cringe.

Miley was out the second the kick landed. Her body stiffened instantly, and listed right - gathering speed as she fell like a tree that had been chopped down. She hit the floor with a hollow thump and did not move.

While her fans went crazy, Emma took a huge breath and fell back against the cage. Despite the fact that she'd just scored a thrilling win, there was no smile on her face. Cyrus had just given her the hardest fight she'd ever had. She was bloodied and sore and totally exhausted. So, this felt more like survival than victory. She didn't know how much of that was a result of Miley's skill and how much was her own fault for underestimating her opponent. And, at that particular moment, she didn't really care. She just wanted to stay there and let the fence hold her up while she recovered a bit.

Then, of course, there was the fact that her work here wasn't done.

She looked down at her fallen opponent and couldn't help but feel a little twinge of sadness. Cyrus had fought well. No... more than that. She'd had put on a phenomenal performance. And, despite her reputation, she'd fought clean, too.

She deserved better than to be left here like a piece of meat.

Emma stood up off the fence and knelt down beside Miley. She gently rolled the singer onto her side, then sat down on the dusty, wooden floor and waited.

Less than a minute later, she heard a pained groan.

Miles didn't know exactly what hurt. She just knew it hurt a lot.

Her body ached. Her arms felt like lead. And son-of-a-bitch, her head was killing her.

And all that fucking noise wasn't helping.

Her eyes fluttered open, and her brain began to process the scene.

I'm on the floor.

There's some sort of fence.

And who the hell are all those people?

Then it came to her. She was still in the cage.

She tried to sit up, but quickly discovered her body wasn't ready. She settled for rolling onto her back and studying the ceiling for a while.

A few seconds passed before she noticed something at the edge of her vision. She rolled her head in that direction and found Watson sitting next to her.

Why is she...

Then, it clicked.

"Awww, c'mon!" she moaned. "Now you're a fuckin' hero, TOO?"

Emma shrugged but said nothing.

"Couldn't you just let the medics do this, or whatever?" Miley continued.

Watson shook her head.

"It's not that kinda place. There's a doctor back there somewhere who'll patch you up, but no one's coming out here to get you."

Miley still looked annoyed. She started to say something, but stopped herself as she considered the situation. Waking up to find her rival waiting for her was a little humiliating, but it was probably better than finding herself laying here alone.

A few more seconds passed in silence. Then, with a Herculean effort, Cyrus managed to sit herself up.

A small pop rose as the crowd expressed its relief that Miley was okay.

"I guess I didn't win," Miley quipped.

That's when Emma's smile made it's first post-fight appearance.

"No," she answered, "but, hey... you tried real hard."

"Super," Cyrus shot back. "Maybe they'll give me a sticker that says Great Job!, or something."

"Yeah, maybe," Emma laughed.

More silence passed.

"You were good, Miley," Emma said eventually. "I've had a lot of fights here. I can't remember ever being pushed that hard."

Was praise from the woman who'd just beaten your ass supposed to be taken as a compliment, or just a further humiliation? Miley wasn't sure, but she didn't spend too much time worrying about it. Only moments ago, she'd been lying flat on her face, with her own blood dripping onto the filthy, cracked boards beneath her. As far as humilation went, it really couldn't get much worse, anyway.

"So were you," she admitted. "And I gotta be honest... after that, I'm finding you pretty hard to hate."

"What was that all about, anyway?"

"I don't know," Miley answered. Then, after a beat, she said, "I think it was the photos."

A few months earlier, Emma had been the victim of a hacking attack. The thieves had somehow gotten private photos of her that were taken during a photo shoot and posted them all over the internet. In truth, the photos weren't really anything for her to be ashamed of, but the media went nuts, anyway. And Emma only added to that when she spoke out about how ridiculous it was that women had to deal with shit like this.

No sooner had that furor died down when a similar scandal broke, this time with Miley at the center of it.

"I'm sorry," Emma said. "Some assholes invade your privacy, and you hate ME  for it?"

"No, I just... when it happened to you, everybody was like, Oh, those assholes! How could they do that to Emma?  Then, it happens to me and it's, What's the difference? Miley leaves her tits hanging out all the time, anyway."

"Oh, Miley..."

"I don't need your pity," Miley cut in. "Besides, I set myself up for it."

"No," Emma said sharply. "That's bullshit. You didn't deserve what happened. And that includes the reaction."

"Whatever."

Emma shook her head, but let the topic go. This wasn't the place for the conversation, anyway.

"I still don't understand what this has to do with me," she said.

"It just pissed me off," Miley said. "Why do I have to be like you for people to respect me?"

"I can't control what other people think, but I don't think you have to be like me."

"Isn't that what you're fighting for?"

"No," Emma said. "I fight so that all of us can be whoever we want to be."

Miley shrugged, but now Emma was the one getting pissed off. She didn't understand how people got these ideas in their heads, but she was getting pretty sick of it.

"Have you ever even heard anything I've said?"

"I've heard about  it."

"So, that would be a no. You're telling me you dragged me into this because you were mad at me about shit other people said about me, instead of what I actually did."

Miley shrugged again.

"Maybe I just needed to hit something," she offered.

Emma started to argue but caught herself. After all, hadn't Miley just hit on the same reason she came here to begin with?

"Yeah, well... next time just ask me. You don't have to make a fiasco out of it."

Miley smiled and said, "I kinda thrive on fiascos."

That sapped Emma's anger, and actually made her laugh.

"Sounds like you're okay with who you are, after all," she said.

"I'm pretty comfortable with it," Miley replied.

After a little more silence, Emma stood and held her hand out to her opponent.

"C'mon, let's get out of here."

Cyrus let Emma help her up, and the crowd applauded loudly as the two fighters exited the cage. Maybe this little show of sportsmanship wasn't really what they wanted. Maybe they'd prefer the fight end with the same level of hatred it had started with and promises of revenge. But they had come for a heavy dose of entertaining violence, and there was no question the two women had delivered.

Besides, she might not be driven by the same emotions as before, but revenge was still on Miley's mind.

"You know, I'm gonna take you up on that next time  offer," she said.

"I hope you do," Emma replied, "but, just so you know, it's gonna end the same way."

"Oooohh... that's some tough talk coming from everybody's little sweetheart."

"That may be what they think," Emma said, "but now you know better."

Miley just smiled. She did know better, but she also knew how close she'd come... and she was already thinking about what she needed to do put Little Miss Perfect on her ass, next time around.
