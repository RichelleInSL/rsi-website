TKO Issue: #109
Date: July 2014
Franchise: Diamond Edge Fighters
Event: Against All Odds
Location: Rogers Arena (Vancouver, British Columbia)

Missed Opportunities

Danneel Harris: With Richelle still on vacation, it'll be Alyson Michalka and Ashley Tisdale handling this week's update. Girls...

Aly: Geez, Ash... can you believe this? Danni's letting us do the press release for this one. Has she lost her mind?

Ashley: Maybe she couldn't find anyone else willing to do it. It's not exactly a happy day here at RSI.

Aly: That's true. This was a big chance for RSI to step up, but things just didn't go our way.

Ashley: Well, at least Danni came through. RSI's team leader is back on top where she belongs!

Aly: Yep. Everything feels right with the world, again. But Britney and Petra had title shots of their own here in Vancouver, and they just couldn't make it happen.

Ashley: I saw Britney afterward. She looked totally pissed off.

Aly: Yeah, I talked to Elisha a little bit. Britney doesn't feel like she fought on the level she's capable of. Losing is one thing, but you want to at least be able to say you gave it your best. Brit's pissed because she doesn't feel like that's what she did.

Ashley: She did look tight out there. She fought well, but she never really stepped up and took her shot. She let Malin dictate the fight, and she paid for it.

Aly: It was the same sort of thing with Petra. Adriana Lima is a tough champion, but Petra held her own just fine. She took the best Adriana had, but she never made a move of her own. Even the toughest fighters can only last so long with Lima pummeling them like that.

Ashley: Well, the good news is that neither Britney nor Petra suffered any injury. So, they'll both probably get another shot at it, sooner rather than later.

Aly: I guess that's true. You're talking to someone who would kill just to get that one shot. If it ever happens, you KNOW I won't be thinking about second chances.

Ashley: (laughing) I know you won't, baby. But, you just keeping doing what you're doing, and you'll get that shot.

Aly: Thanks, hon. But, let's get back to Danni, shall we? Tonight was not a good night to be Kristanna Loken.

Ashley: Not at all. Kristanna has built herself into a true rival to Danni, and she should be proud of that. But, geez... Danni just wasn't fucking around, tonight.

Aly: Nope. She was all over poor Kristanna right from the opening bell. Danni was pretty cool about it when she lost the belt to Kristanna, but I think we saw tonight that it pissed her off, and she made sure she wouldn't have to leave without it again.

Ashley: Yep. I think Danni's attitude is that the belt is now back home, where it belongs. I'm sure Kristanna will have more to say about that, though.

Aly: No doubt. Anyway... not a bad RSI debut for Princess Anna, eh?

Ashley: Don't you think she hates when people call her that?

Aly: (shrugs) I dunno... I think she kinda likes it. I think it bothers her coming from Richelle, because it comes out all kinds of stalker-type creepy when she says it. But, overall, I think Kristen's OK with it, for the most part.

Ashley: Whatever. If she keeps fighting the way she did tonight, people will call her whatever she wants them to.

Aly: Damn right. She took Natalie Portman apart. She couldn't get the knockout I know she wanted, but Kristen was in control throughout the whole three rounds. There was never any doubt which way the decision would go.

Ashley: She was outstanding. No question.

Aly: So, what's it like to train with her?

Ashley: I have to be honest. I had my doubts when she first came here, but it's worked out a lot better than I'd feared. Kristen's not the most well-rounded fighter ever, but she goes after it with everything she's got.

Aly: Gee... who does THAT sound like?

Ashley: (laughs) Yeah... I guess there's a reason we're getting along so well.

Aly: Anyway... a couple of other fighters were in action tonight. And, it was a tough loss for Cobie Smulders.

Ashley: Oh, I felt terrible for her. She was beating the shit out of Ursula Mayes, and all of the sudden Mayes catches her off guard with that rolling kimura. Cobie went from being in command to tapping out in a split second.

Aly: There's no question it sucked, but an opponent is always most dangerous when she's desperate. And, Cobie has shown in the past that she's vulnerable against a submission fighter like Ursula.

Ashley: Truth is, you have to give Ursula credit. That was a kick ass move. I just wish she hadn't pulled it off against one of ours.

Aly: Yeah. Well, last but not least, Danica Patrick is back in the win column with a quick and somewhat unfortunate win over Sienna Miller.

Ashley: Well, no one wants to win by injury, but Danica was really taking it to her before the ref stepped in.

Aly: True. Danica looked good tonight. But, this win-loss-win-loss thing she's been doing has to stop. She's not going to work her way up that way.

Ashley: I think she's going to get herself straightened out. She's too good not to.

Aly: I hope you're right. Well, that wraps it up for this event, but RSI has a lot on the line at the upcoming ESL event.

Ashley: Yep. Both Emily VanCamp and Daniela Hantuchova will look to defend their titles against some tough opponents.

Aly: I was talking to Em earlier, and she admitted she's a little lost in how to prepare for her matchup with Elisandra Tomacheski. She's so used to going to war with Irina, she's not sure how to train for anyone else.

Ashley: Well, she better figure it out, and soon. Meanwhile, Tiffany Mulheron will look to retake the featherweight title from her hated rival, Nadine Coyle.

Aly: Those two never show each other any mercy in the cage. By the time it's over, the WINNER usually needs an ambulance.

Ashley: They do beat the hell out of each other. Both are usually a bloody mess when their done. Their fights are definitely not for the squeamish.

Aly: We'll have those matchups and a whole lot more, including the debut of RSI's newest rookie, Michelle Lewin.

Ashley: It's going to be interesting to see how she does. She's one of the top fitness models in the world, but here, she's just another fighter looking to claw her way to the top. She certainly hasn't had a lot of time to prepare.

Aly: Well, we'll see what she's got soon enough. We'll see you then.

Ashley: Hey... how come you got the nickname "Hellcat?" We were both on that show.

Aly: I don't know. Ask Britney and Elisha. They gave it to me.

Ashley: Well, I want a cool nickname, too.

Aly: OK. Like what?

Ashley: How about "The Terminatrix?"

Aly: (just stares at her friend)

Ashley: Yeah, OK... we'll work on it.

Aly: Good plan. That's a real good plan.
