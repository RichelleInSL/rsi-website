TKO Issue: #127
Date: June 2015
Franchise: Steel Cage Queens
Event: Fortress
Location: Bojangles' Coliseum (Charlotte, NC)

Epic Battle

Sometimes, everything comes together just right. A look in the freezer reveals that you have everything you need to make that dish you're suddenly craving. A cloudy summer day at the Jersey Shore keeps the crowds away, but the skies never give way to rain... leaving the boardwalk as your personal playground. It's no different in the UMMA. Sometimes, you end up with the right fighters, in the right frame of mind. That's what happened here, tonight. Though it remains to be seen what the ultimate result of the franchise reorganization will be, the pairing of Kelly Hu and Michelle Rodriguez has certainly gotten things off on the right foot. After all, any fight that has both contestants bloodied by the end of the first round is sure to capture the attention of the fan base. The fact that the highly-contested bout then carried deep into the third round certainly kept them riveted, as well.

Let's not mince words... these women were amazing. There was not one second that went by when either was passive. Strong attacks, tough defense and stunning counters were the order of the day. How they managed to keep it up for nearly three full rounds is beyond me. But this was war, plain and simple. It was clear from the outset that neither fighter would let up until one of them fell.

Maybe it's easier for me to get excited. After all, when the dust settled, it was the RSI fighter who was still standing tall. But, I really feel like I'm speaking as a fan, here. This bout had a special quality to it from the second it started. And, if you don't believe me, just ask Kelly.

"That was intense," said the Hawaiian actress. "You could just feel it. After those first few exchanges, I think we both knew what was going to happen." Indeed, Kelly said the two seemed to make a silent agreement by the end of that first round. "We were staring each other down after the round, and I could see it in her eyes," she said. "It wasn't hatred. It was something... different. Something primal. That first round was an all-out brawl. She'd gotten a taste of it, and she wanted more. I wanted it, too. I think we were each just letting the other know we were up for it."

Though a frustrating injury kept Stephanie McMahon out of the cage at this event, it didn't keep her from fulfilling her duties as RSI's head of training. She, too, immediately realized something big was happening between Kelly and Michelle. "The first round set the tone," she said. "I think both fighters threw their strategy right out the window. It was going to be a slugfest. The fans wanted it, and so did the fighters. There was going to be no talking them out of it."

Tossing the game plan aside gave Steph a chance to enjoy this one more as a fan than as a trainer. "It was... fun," the normally fun-less McMahon shockingly admitted. "They agreed to lay it all out there, and it was incredible to watch," she continued. "Maybe I'd feel differently if Kelly had lost, but I don't really think so."

I'm with Steph on this one. This wasn't about RSI vs Hellfire. It wasn't about strategies or ranking or championships. It was about two warriors, relying on little more than guts and raw talent. It was, without apology, a fight for the fans. I don't where tonight's results are going to leave Kelly and Michelle, ranking-wise. I just hope things work out to bring them together again, soon. Because, if there's any chance they'll repeat a performance like this one, I know I'll be watching.

Heavyweight champion Catherine Bell reminded everyone tonight of why the title hangs around her waist. After a nightmarish performance against Adriana Lima at the most recent team event, I'm sure Mac was looking to make a statement... and, boy, did she ever. Opponent Elisabetta Canalis came out fast and aggressive, scoring quickly with a pair of hard body shots. But, she missed badly with the hook that followed, as Cat deftly slid out of range. The miss left the Italian fighter vulnerable, and Catherine made the most of her chance. She countered with a hook that landed completely untouched, and Elisabetta was bleeding almost immediately. From there, it was all downhill for Canalis as Cat went to work. Less than a minute later, referee Dita Von Teese had seen enough and stepped in to put a stop to things.

Afterward, Cat found herself wondering what's next for her. "There's so much talent vying for second place," said the JAG actress. "I never know where my next challenge is going to come from."

Well, as of right now, it looks like Cat's next opponent will be a familiar face... former champion Traci Bingham. No doubt, Bingham will be highly motivated to take back that title. So, back to the gym for you Mac. There's no rest for the weary.

Roselyn Sanchez has had an excellent career, so far. She's struggled from time to time, but she's also shown she can be a brilliant fighter, when she sets her mind to it. That brilliance was on display tonight, as she simply destroyed opponent Sarah Chalke.

One mistake. One mistake from Sarah was all it took. She made an ambitious call to go for an early takedown. Roselyn stuffed the attempt, and that left Chalke wide open. Roselyn did not waste the chance, obliterating her opponent in a mere thirty seconds.

Despite the one-sided nature of the fight, Sanchez refused to say anything negative about her opponent. "I don't think we learned anything tonight," she said. "She tried something, and it didn't work out. She wanted to fight the fight her way, and she took a big risk to make it happen. If she'd have gotten that takedown, we might be having a different conversation, right now." As for the beating Sarah suffered after the miss, Roz seemed to know a little about how that goes. "I don't want to say I feel sorry for her... she doesn't need my pity," said the Devious Maids actress. "I'm just saying I've had it happen to me. I followed through because that's how it works. That's what I'm trained to do. But I know what it's like to take a chance and have it cost you everything. I'm sure she's pissed about what happened, and I have no doubt she'll be training her ass off and looking for a chance to get even."

Sometimes, a route happens because one fighter makes a mistake. Other times, it happens because one fighter is just about perfect. Such was the case for Torrie Wilson, tonight. With Torrie firing on all cylinders... both offensively and defensively... Angelina Jolie never really had a chance. Torrie took command almost instantly and pounded Jolie with one relentless attack after another until the Tomb Raider actress finally folded a little over three minutes into the fight. Along the way, Wilson defended every attempt Angelina made to generate some offense of her own.

Having lost five of her last seven fights... including two in a row to Gabrielle Richens... Torrie needed a win like this to restore some of her flagging confidence. And don't think that wasn't on her mind in there, tonight.

"It's been rough for me, recently," said the former WWE star. "I'd really been rolling since signing with RSI. This recent patch reminded me of what it was like before, when I was out there on my own. Angie's a tough opponent, and I'm hoping this win will get me back on track."

Torrie's proven in the past that she's as good as anyone out there. So, hopefully, this is what she needed to get her out of her funk.

Some people just have it rough. Though Marzia Prince's record says she's little more than a middle of the road fighter, the fact is that many of her losses have come against far more experienced opponents. RSI's heavyweight group is markedly thin, so Marzia has regularly gotten the call to fill in when we needed an injury substitute at the team events. That's how she ended up in showdowns with the vastly more experienced Veronica Varekova and Traci Bingham. The recent addition of Dianna Dahlgren to the RSI family has given us some breathing room, which allowed Marzia to take up a permanent spot on our newest team entry. That led to a showdown with Dragon's Den powerhouse Caroline Wozniacki. That's like getting an early birthday present... from hell.

Meanwhile, Prince's divisional record has been nearly spotless, as she steadily climbs the rankings. Tonight was more of the same from her, as she evened the score with the more experienced Jenny McCarthy. While Torrie won her fight with a relentless attacking style, Marzia's secret was pure, unbridled power. The piece de resistance was a pair of brutal high kicks that landed only seconds apart from one another. The first seemed to leave McCarthy out on her feet. The second just left her out... period.

Prince... known more for her boxing skills than her prowess with her feet... seemed as shocked as anyone else at the way things ended. "I don't know how it happened," admitted the blonde fitness model. "The first one was almost automatic. I saw an opening, and I took it. That's what I'm trained to do." Then, she laughed to herself before adding, "The second was like, 'Well, hell... it worked the first time, so why not?'"

However, her look turned more serious when she considered why the second kick landed so easily. "She didn't seem right," Marzia said of McCarthy. "She didn't even try to defend herself. I think maybe the first kick did more damage than anyone realized. Maybe the ref should have stepped in sooner."

Well, luckily, reports on Jenny's condition seem fairly good. As for Marzia, tonight was yet another display of the talents that have her tangling so frequently with the big girls. So, we at RSI don't get too hung up on her record. We know exactly what she's capable of.

Now, THAT'S better. After dropping eight in a row and nine of her last ten, it was time for Anna Silk to show what she can really do. In her first official fight under the RSI banner, Anna was sensational. She came out full of confidence and took the fight right to Carla Gugino. For Carla, it had to be the equivalent of being hit by a hurricane. Anna landed eleven unanswered strikes in just over forty seconds. There are few women in the UMMA who can withstand such an onslaught, and it was clearly more than Gugino could deal with. So, a nice win to kick off Anna's RSI career. Hopefully, we'll be able to help her develop as she steps up the division, and have it be the first of many.

So, there you have it. A little slice of rare perfection from the women of RSI. But, don't expect a repeat, as the whole place is literally going crazy getting ready for the upcoming DEF event. Having ten fighters in action is a lot, but having five involved in title contests is simply insane. Danni Harris, Daniela Hantuchova, Tiffany Mulheron, Petra Cubonova and Britney Spears will all step into the cage with hardware on the line. As a result of the franchise reorganization, all of these women will be facing opponents they've seen little of, recently. Though some... and I'm talking to you, Miss Spears... will be reigniting old rivalries, others will be looking to start up new ones. Getting them all ready has been a difficult challenge, so Steph has been much more surly than usual. Hard to imagine, I know. Be sure to join us next time, and see how it all turns out.
