TKO Issue: #75
Date: May 2013
Franchise: Shift (later renamed to Fallen Angels Combat)
Event: Bad to the Bone
Location: Indira Gandhi Arena (New Delhi, India)

Rampage On A Roll

With nothing but victories to report for the second event in a row, the women of Rampage are definitely putting the UMMA on notice that they are a force to be reckoned with.

Last time out, Alyson Michalka suffered a controversial loss to Brooklyn Decker. We won't rehash all the details, but suffice it to say that Aly came out tonight looking to make a statement. And she apparently decided to abuse Rihanna to get her point across. She got so into it, in fact, that it nearly cost her the match. In her rush to punish her opponent, Aly's technique got sloppy, and Rihanna responded by dropping her on her ass. Having been properly reminded that she was facing a higly skilled fighter and not a heavy bag, Aly regrouped and finished what she'd started in dominating fashion. "I wanted to make sure I finished things cleanly," a determined Michalka said afterward. "I wanted to make sure the ref didn't have to make any difficult decisions." Indeed, the ref for tonight's bout was none other than Emma Roberts, who infamously stopped Aly's last bout after Brooklyn inflicted a vicious scratch to Aly's cheek. Interestingly enough, Miss Roberts chose to stay out of the action tonight, allowing Alyson to beat Rihanna into a bloody stupor for the KO win.

Speaking of making statements, I think RSI newcomer Emma Watson announced tonight that she would like a shot at Christobelle Grierson-Ryrie's Featherweight title. The two women certainly seemed to match up well in the first round of tonight's non-title fight. After Emma scored some damaging blows early, Christobelle managed to get things to the ground, where she has the advantage. Emma showed she's got some ground skills, though... gaining the top position and hanging on until the end of the round. The crowd seemed eager for more, but Emma apparently had someplace else she needed to be. A straight right to the face ended things in a hurry, leaving the champion crumpled on the mat. Afterward, Emma made it clear she wants the hardware on the line next time. "I think I earned a shot tonight, yeah. I beat the champion. I think that I deserve the chance to do it again and take her title." And what about those who think the more experienced Vanessa Hudgens should get a title shot first? "I know Christobelle and Vanessa have some kind of rivalry going. If people want to see them go at each other again, that's fine. I feel I can take the title from either one of them, so it makes no difference to me."

Five rookies, five debuts, five victories. Brittany Tacy had no trouble completing the perfect start for the RSI rookies. Her first round win over fellow rookie Emilia Clark was almost flawless, with Brittany in command right from the opening bell. Brittany, however, is not letting the early success go to her head. "Tonight was good. I felt confident, and I definitely feel I can hold my own in the cage against anyone. But, this is a very difficult division. I'm happy with tonight's win, but I know that winning here will rarely be easy." Indeed, the Shift Lightweight division is loaded with fast and brutally efficient athletes. How does Britanny plan to cope? "Training, training, training," she said. "I can handle it, though. I've always led a disciplined lifestyle, and I enjoy it. I will never lose because I'm not prepared."

On a less exuberant note, the showdown between the rejuvenated Julianne Hough and rookie phenom Alexa Vega turned horribly wrong right at the opening bell. A devastating kick from Julianne shattered one of Alexa's ribs, leaving the young submission artist writhing on the canvas. This is a devastating blow, considering that she had just returned from an identical injury suffered in the Hit the Beach tournament. Given the way things turned out, Julianne has chosen not to comment on the fight, saying only that she hopes Alexa is back soon. We join her in wishing this promising fighter a full and speedy recovery.

With the team event up next, RSI will turn over a handful of fighters to their respective team leaders, and hope to God they come out whole and healthy. In the meantime, we'll be concentrating on getting ready for the upcoming pay-per-view.
