TKO Issue: #93
Date: November 2013
Franchise: Fallen Angels Combat
Event: No Turning Back
Location: The O2 Arena (London, United Kingdom)

All Good Things Must End

We knew it couldn't go on forever, but that didn't make it any less depressing when it ended. After fourteen straight victories, Julianne Hough was finally stopped by Vikki Blows here tonight. Doubling the depression, the loss also cost Julianne her Bantamweight crown. 'Tis not a happy day in RSI-land.

Deploying the well known RWB "all-or-nothing" approach, Vikki attacked from the outset. She caught Hough with a vicious right-hand that immediately had Julianne back on her heels. Blows was relentless, and patently dirty, another RWB hallmark. Julianne simply never recovered, and Vikki just wailed away, leaving the actress a bloody mess. Julianne hung on bravely, nearly making it to the end of the round. But, it simply was not to be.

When it was all over, Julianne was simply unable to contain her emotions. "What a relief," said the long-legged actress. "It feels good to get that behind me." Wait. Say what, blondie? "I've said it over and over. There was no way I was going to keep winning like that. And, the streak was starting to take on a life of it's own. Now that it's done... I can't lie. It's a relief." Well... I didn't see that coming. Still, it certainly makes sense. Winning in the UMMA is tough enough as it is. The added pressure of the streak couldn't have been fun. That said, Julianne also admitted the loss did hurt, a little. "Sure, it's difficult. The loss cost me the championship. But, it's not crushing. In fact, it's inspiring." Care to elaborate? "As far as I'm concerned, Vikki just stole my belt," said Hough, a flash of defiance in her cool, blue eyes. "And, I want it back." Hot damn.

Another tough loss for Aly Michalka, and, once again, it came at the hands of India Reynolds. As with Julianne, the all-out RWB style had Aly's hands full from the start. She hung in well, exchanging heavy blows with India throughout the first round, but it was obvious that the first to land the big blow would take this one. Unfortunately, it was Reynolds who got there first, landing a thunderous kick early in the second. The blow left Aly badly dazed, and it was all over just seconds later.

Thoroughly disappointed, Aly decided against the usual post fight interview. I understood, and told her I would cover for her. My plan was another public pep-talk about RSI's unwavering support for Ms. Michalka. It seems that I was a little too slow, however. By the time I arrived, the interview had already started, and Daniela Hantuchova was running the show. "This disappoints my friend, Aly. I know it does. She wants badly to compete for championship," said the Slovakian tennis star. "She is going wrong way now, and it hurts her. But, I know she will never give up," said Hantuchova, slamming her fist down on the table. "She will never give up, because I will not let her." It seems my work here is done.

With so much bad news at the top of the RSI card, I really want to make a big deal out of Emma Watson's successful title defense. But, circumstances have conspired against me, as the fight ended in only 30 seconds due to a nasty leg injury to challenger, Christobelle Grierson-Ryrie. Certainly not an enjoyable outcome, and Emma was the first to acknowledge it. "I'm happy with the win," said the young actress, "but, I hate the way it ended. I know the fans would prefer if Christobelle and I hated each other, but it's just not that way. She's a skilled fighter, and she always fights me fair. I have a lot of respect for her, and I hate that this has happened to her. I hope she's back soon."

Sometimes a loss can be a good thing. After putting on a great show, but coming up short against Halle Berry back at the Lingerie Invitational tournament, Jennifer Lawrence has been on a little bit of a roll. She followed that with a quick, and nearly flawless, victory over Misa Campo at Fearless, which helped to cement her confidence. And, she continued her winning ways tonight, as she beat the much more experienced Melissa Giraldo. Unlike the Campo matchup, Melissa gave Jen a tough fight, including a great takedown by Giraldo to open the second round. But, J-Law stayed calm, cool and collected, tying her opponent up and preventing her from taking advantage. Once the fighters were back on their feet, Jen took control, ending things with a series of brutal knees from the clinch.

And so, what started as a slow creep up the rankings has become something more noteworthy. Tonight's victory leaves Lawrence ranked third in the division. Though she's still far behind the champion in ranking points, she can certainly see the top from here. But, Jen wasn't ready to talk championship, quite yet. "It's a long way to go, so I really can't think about it," said the Hunger Games star. "While I feel good about where I am, I haven't been in the UMMA very long. I still have a lot to learn." True enough. Jen's success makes it easy to forget that this was only the fifteenth fight of her career. And, Blake Lively is no pushover. And then, there's the highly successful Karen Gillan. So, this definitely seems like a case of so close, yet so far away. Still, Jen's recent performances have certainly been something to be proud of. That will have to be enough. At least, for now.

Well, that was easy. Jamie Lynn Spears made quick work of opponent India de Beaufort, dropping the British actress in less than a minute. The win keeps the younger Spears right in the thick of things in the FAC welterweight division. But, it seems her divisional competition was not forefront in the young actress's mind. "A few more wins, and I might have to take another run at Brit," giggled Jamie. "Fat chance, short stack!" called a voice from the rear of the room. By the time the crowd parted, the owner of the voice had moved on. But, there was no doubt who it was. And, there's no doubt I'm going to occasionally regret the decision to bring Jamie on board.

A tough, tough loss for rookie Brittany Tacy, tonight. She took quite a beating at the hands of Zaira Nara, and there's really not much else to say. Britt showed her toughness, sticking it out to the end. But, the decision was never in doubt. The fact of the matter is Brittany looked terrible tonight. I know it. She knows it. And, rest assured, we're going to do something about it.

A perfect start tonight for RSI's newest recruits. While Leanna Decker, Shelby Chesnes and Dianna Dahlgren all breezed to easy victories, it was Sabine Jemeljanova who scored the most impressive win... a single kick knockout of Alana Blanchard only eighteen seconds into the fight. All in all, a great night for all of them. The trick, now, will be to convince them it won't always be this easy. Most especially, Leanna Decker. "Did you see me tear Kelly Kelly apart out there?" cooed the sultry redhead. "It was like playing with a ragdoll. It was so.... mmmmmmmmmmmm... exciting." Ohhh... my head.

Well, once again, we conclude with big roster news from RSI. No new additions this time. Instead, we announce that RSI has finally given in to pressure from our fans... we are entering into the UMMA team competition. And, as with everything RSI does, we're going all in.. forming not one, but two new RSI teams. How did we get here? Well, the idea of forming a team had been out there for a while, but it took us time to get our roster filled out. Once I had the pieces in place, I sat down with RSI's in cage leader, Danneel Harris. Obviously, each team needs a captain, and Danni is, without question, the heart and soul of the RSI organization. When I needed someone to lead our new team into battle, I knew exactly who to ask...

Danni: "No."

Richelle: "Great. I... wait. What?"

Danni: "I can't do it. We have more than thirty women on the roster now, and ten of them are rookies. If you're going to have teams, you need leaders who can fully devote themselves. I don't have the time for it."

Richelle: "Hmmmm. OK, I guess you're right. And, I appreciate your honesty. But, I'm in a tough spot, now. I knew I was going to have trouble filling the second captain's spot. Now, I have to fill both. We have so many women who can do it..."

Danni: "No, you don't."

Richelle: "What do you mean? Even if we leave out Daniela because she's so new, there's Stephanie, or Julianne. Emma is young, but she can certainly do it. There's no question Emily would make a great leader. And, Aly's had a tough time, but she's got the kind of heart you need in a leader. How the hell can I choose?"

Danni: "Those are all good choices. And, you know I love every one of them. But, you know exactly who your team leaders are."

Richelle: (sweating a little) "I do?"

Danni: "Yeah. It's the two you deliberately left out."

Richelle: (swallowing hard) "Oh, geez..."

Danni: "Face it... you need leaders who are skilled and confident. You also need leaders who are popular with the fans. And, more than anything else, you need leaders who will work together to make both teams better."

Richelle: (on the verge of tears) "Oh, Danni... please don't say it..."

Danni: (as she gets up to exit the room) "I don't have to. You already know."

And, God help me, I did. I know this decision will cause me never-ending anguish. But, I also know it gives the new teams the best chance to succeed. And so, it is my distinct... duty... to announce the RSI team captains... Britney Spears and Elisha Cuthbert. I asked each to prepare a little announcement, introducing her team. In customary fashion, they ignored me and decided to prepare a joint statement, instead. That statement is included below. I haven't read it, because... well, let's just leave it at I haven't read it. I'm going to find some aspirin and lie down.

RSI Team Announcement

Both: First of all, let us say how honored we are to be chosen as team leaders. We know we both have a reputation for goofing around. And, make no mistake, that's not going to change. But, we take our new roles very seriously. Richelle has placed a tremendous amount of faith in us, and we will not let her, or our teammates, down. That said, it's time for some serious goofing around...

Britney: While I love Richelle to death, she does some silly things, sometimes. And, there is nothing sillier than her decision to let us pick our own team names. So, without further ado, let me introduce to you Britney's Brutal Beauties. First up, in the featherwweight slot, "The Deadly Dancer"... Julianne Hough! Next, at bantamweight, "The Scottish Slayer"... Tiffany Mulheron! At lightweight, a woman who needs no introduction, but I'm going to do it anyway... Emily "Vengeance" VanCamp! At welterweight, the long-time DEF champion, Danneel "Great White" Harris. At middleweight, your's truly, "Badass" Britney Spears! At light heavyweight, Aly "Hellcat" Michalka! Our heavyweight competitor, Catherine "The Good Bitch" Bell! And, at super heavyweight, "Sweet" Stephanie McMahon! Over to you, Leesh.

Elisha: Thanks, "Badass." Ladies and gentlemen, allow me to introduce Leesh's Lethal Ladies. At featherweight, the world's sexiest racecar driver, Danica "Downshift" Patrick! In the bantamweight slot, RSI's own martial artist extraordinaire, "Kickass" Kelly Hu! At lightweight, Roselyn "La Dueña de la Destrucción" Sanchez! At welterweight, "Amazing" Amanda Seyfried! At middleweight, the captain and easily the hottest member of the team, "Lethal" Leesha Cuthbert! Our light heavyweight fighter... "The Slovakian Sensation"... Daniela Hantuchova! At heavyweight... "The Czechian Obsession"... Petra Cubonova! And finally, at super heavyweight, "Torrid" Torrie Wilson!

Britney: OK, all kidding aside, there is no question that every one of these women can kick ass. We know they live to win, and we're both completely dedicated to making that happen. We're proud to have been given this responsibility, and we're proud to represent this amazing group of athletes.

Elisha: We're proud to have been given this responsibility, and we're proud to represent this amazing group of athletes.


Huh. That wasn't nearly as painful as I'd expected. Promises of dedication and seriousness? That doesn't sound like the dynamic duo I know. It looks like Danni may be wiser than I'd realized. Of course, they named the teams after themselves, and they made a ridiculous show of the introductions. So, the world has not gone completely crazy.

Oh, and about those nicknames... the terrible twosome came up with those on their own. So, don't hold the girls to them. Except for "Badass" Britney and "Lethal" Leesha. They did that to themselves, so have fun with those.

At any rate, our new captains will have no time for games, as the team event is up next. And, after an introduction like that, they have a lot to live up to. We'll see you then.
