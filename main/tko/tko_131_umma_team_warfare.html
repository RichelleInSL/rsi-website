Civil War

With so many amazing battles and more than a a few stunning results, this team event is one that the women of RSI will never forget. So much happened here in Columbus, I don't even know what to start with. As usual, team leaders Britney Spears, Elisha Cuthbert and Alyson Michalka are here to help me make sense of it all. Each has brought along her choice for Fighter of the Night, and I've invited a few extra guests to join in. So with us this time are Danica Patrick, Julianne Hough, Kelly Hu, Tiffany Mulheron and Emma Watson. And as always, RSI leader Danneel Harris and fighter development coordinator Stephanie McMahon are along for the ride, as well.

Richelle Winterfeld: Welcome ladies. Does everyone have a seat? Excellent. Let me start off by saying I have never been more proud of this organization than I am right now. Aly, this was the hardest test your group has ever faced.

Alyson Michalka: And we barely escaped.

Richelle Winterfeld: Don't even start with that. You're team was outstanding. Doutzen Kroes, Candice Swanepoel, Adriana Lima... that ILF team was stacked, but your team stood tall and came oh-so-close to pulling off some big upsets.

Aly: (nods) I know. I'm proud of them, too.

Richelle: Give us the rundown, then.

Aly: Well, Tiz and Emma got us started by getting the wins we absolutely had to have.

Richelle: (nods toward Emma) Good to see you back in stride.

Emma Watson: I knew we needed it. Bregje Heinen didn't make it easy, but I knew how important it was. I wasn't going to let the rest of the team down.

Stephanie McMahon: I did.

Steph: Tacy's won some tough fights in the past. Lost some close ones, too. I don't know if I expected her to beat Kerr, but I didn't expect her to roll over, either.

Aly: She put up a helluva fight. I really thought she was gonna steal it in that last round.

Richelle: It wasn't quite enough, but it was still fun to watch. Aly, what's next?

Aly: Let's see... bantamweight, featherweight, lightweight... that brings us to welterweight.

Danni: Don't you dare...

Danni: Can we just move on?

Richelle: You don't have anything to say about your performance?

Danni: (shrugs) I'm happy with it.

Richelle: Wow. Riveting.

Danni: Well, I'm not going to start talking trash about it.

Emma: I don't think anyone expected that.

Danni: Candice is good at this. Ask any one of her opponents. This one didn't go her way. Obviously, I know how that feels.

Julianne: Well, at least Candice has Erin to help her get over it.

Danni: Oh, blech!

Richelle: Excuse you? You have a problem with Candice and Erin?

Tiffany Mulheron: Careful with this one, Danni.

Emma: Yeah. Real careful.

Danni: That's not what I mean. It's just... they're a little too sweet for me.

Richelle: I don't think your opinion matters to them very much.

Danni: Nor should it. And I'm just kidding around. Who cares what anyone else thinks? If they're happy, then I'm happy for them.

Emma: (teasing) Maybe the problem is that you're just jealous.

Danni: Excuse me?

Emma: Candice has Erin. Who did you have after she beat you?

Danni: (narrows her eyes at Emma) I got the support I needed.

Emma: (teasing again) Oh, really?

Richelle: OK, then... this conversation is taking a weird turn. You two can sort out your relationship issues later. Can we refocus?

Julianne: I think maybe Candice and Erin need one of those 'shipping' names. You know, like 'Brangelina.'

Elisha: Yeah... something like 'Candin.'

Emma: 'Swanerton.'

Elisha: Yeah... that's it!

Tiffany: (to Emma) Don't get involved in this.

Emma: Why not?

Tiffany: Because they're going to make up a name for us.

Emma: Like what? 'Watheron?' 'Timma?' They're all stupid.

(Richelle and Danni share a knowing look, then turn back to Emma and Tiffany)

Richelle and Danni: (in unison) 'Emmany.'

(Emma's mouth hangs half open, then her head drops dejectedly.)

Emma: (to Tiffany) I'm sorry.

Tiffany: Yes. Yes, you are.

Aly: Actually, I have one more thing to add.

Richelle: Oh?

Aly: I'd like to name Danni as my Fighter of the Night.

Danni: What? No.

Emma: We expected no less.

Richelle: Sure, Tiff. Nice shiner, by the way.

Tiffany: Yeah, well... what are teammates for, right?

Kelly Hu: Happy to oblige.

Richelle: OK, we'll get to that shortly. What's your question?

Tiffany: I don't get to come to these little parties too often, but I'm wondering why someone always has to be forced into being fighter of the night.

Richelle: Oh, that's easy. It's because I have somehow managed to surround myself with people who pretend to be hard-working and selfless, while they are really just obstinate pains in my ass.

Danni: Wow. You did NOT just refer to others as obstinate, did you?

Richelle: I don't know what you're trying to say.

Steph: I think I can explain the 'somehow,' too.

Aly: Well, next up was Jennifer Lawrence against Doutzen Kroes.

Richelle: Another close one.

Britney: The way she was hanging in there, I thought maybe she was going to get it.

Richelle: What did you think, Steph?

Steph: This just shows what she can do. Wherever the fight went, she was up for it. Doutzen was too much for her in the end, but if she could just have a little more faith in herself... Jen has all the tools.

Richelle: She's a little frustrating, I know. If it helps, she seems to know it, too.

Steph: It doesn't.

Richelle: Aly, that brings us to you. Karolina Kurkova is a lot like Jen. She's had her ups and down, but she's capable of anything.

Aly: Definitely. I was hoping to get to her early, and it worked out. I knew that if I gave her the chance, she could make something happen.

Richelle: Solid, as always, Aly. So, who's left?

Aly: Marzia struggled against Adriana Lima.

Steph: Lima's been on the warpath since she lost her title. Marzia wasn't ready for that.

Richelle: Hey, Marzia's been good.

Danni: Yeah, but Lima is on a whole new level, recently. Just ask Mac.

Aly: That leaves Allira. And that was ugly.

Richelle: (sighs) What can you do? Injuries happen. It certainly wasn't Kate Upton's fault. It was a good move, and just... I don't know. Tough situation.

Danni: She'll be fine.

Elisha: Yeah, we know.

Richelle: Yes, well... humility aside, there was the potential for this to be a disaster. You guys kept everything light and everyone involved seemed to enjoy themselves.

Julianne: Some more than others.

Richelle: (laughs) Well, let's start with that, Jules. And a hearty welcome to you, Ms. Patrick.

Danica Patrick: Hey there.

Danica: To be honest, I really don't know.

Julianne: Don't let her fool you. She's had it in her, all along.

Danica: Well, it's easier when you get to train with one of the best.

Julianne: Oh, it's too late to be nice, now. I'm already planning on kicking your ass all over the place when we get back to the gym.

Richelle: Speaking of which... Tiff, anything you want to say to Kelly?

Tiffany: Nah. I'll let her have her moment.

Emma: (smiling) I have a few things I'd like to say.

Kelly: Oh, no... there's no way I'm taking on BOTH of them!

Richelle:Ah... Emma's not that dangerous.

Emma: Wow. Old age must be hard. Apparently, the memory is the first thing to go.

Danni: (sternly) I can serve both of you a reminder, if that's what's needed.

Richelle: (to Emma) It's really no fun when Danni's here.

Emma: (shrugs) I should have known better.

Richelle: Anyway... what do you have to say for yourself, Kelly? No one was giving you much of a chance, going in.

Kelly: Well, they were probably right. I think I was more lucky than good.

Tiffany: A win's a win. Don't sell yourself short.

Kelly: Got a little help from the ref, too.

Richelle: Nothing to be ashamed of. You've been jobbed by the ref a few times, too.

Tiffany: Yeah. At least it didn't feel so bad, losing to you.

Kelly: (exaggeratedly) Awwwww...

Tiffany: Don't get too mushy. I'm still going to make you pay.

Elisha: Yeah, geez... what the hell? Does she not like Roselyn, or what?

Elisha: I believe I did. And tonight, I proved my point.

Britney: Why you little fucking...

You've also shown all of us what it's like to compete with a friend or a teammate. You found a way to have a real rivalry, without ruining the friendship. That's not easy to do in a violent sport like MMA.

Britney: I have to admit, it's weird to be almost happy when you lose.

Richelle: They were very entertaining, and I just saw them in the locker room together before I came in here. So, apparently peace was restored one the war was over. OK, ladies... finish us up.
