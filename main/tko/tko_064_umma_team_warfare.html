TKO Issue: #64
Date: February 2013
Franchise: UMMA (League-wide event)
Event: Team Warfare
Location: Brisbane Entertainment Centre (Brisbane, Australia)

Team Play

Overall, Team Warfare was a good night for the RSI team, with one notable exception. In keeping with the team spirit of the event, we invited the individual team leaders to comment on our fighters. So, here goes...

"Elisha Cuthbert was simply a disgrace tonight," snorted Lindsey Anne Strutt, leader of No Satisfaction. "Our team is right there... so close to that number one ranking... and she goes out there and simply embarrasses herself. I don't know what those RSI people are supposed to be doing for her, but it's not working." Uh... yeesh. Well, we'd like to point out that we didn't feel Elisha was ready to take on someone of Laura Vandervoort's caliber. But, that said, we did expect she would be fairly competitive. Her performance tonight was a shock, and it's certainly going to mean a serious review of our gameplan for her. We still love what she brings to the cage, and we have total faith in her. But, she can't be taking beatings like that one.

Krystal Forscutt was simply outstanding in her match against Rihanna. RSI's plan when we brought her on was to round out her fierce striking skills with a strong ground game, and the plan worked to perfection, tonight. Club Athena team leader, Roxanne McKee, was clearly impressed with the changes. "Who the bloody hell was that?" she stammered. "We're used to Krystal being a weakness on our team. Then, she goes out there and performs like THAT against someone as experienced as Rihanna? Those RSI people clearly know what they're doing." Did you hear THAT, Lindsey?

Another slow-paced match, another victory for Julianne Hough. Patriots team lead, Gwen Stefani, was certainly happy with the outcome. "At this point, we'll take any win we can get," she quipped. Yep, clearly, she's ecstatic. Well, WE were pretty happy with Julianne's performance against Caitlin Stasey, anyway. Julianne weathered some early trouble nicely, and she stayed focused on controlling the action. We'd like to see her be more aggressive at times, but, all in all, she looked good out there.

When asked to comment on her match tonight, Britney Spears said simply, "Did you fucking see THAT?" Yes ma'am, we sure did. Facing someone with the skill and experience of Denise Richards is not an easy task. It is a test of your skill, your intelligence and even your courage. Not only did Britney show that, when she's at her best, she can beat anyone. She also showed she is not afraid to get in that cage, no matter how good her opponent is. Her last two matches have been against two of the toughest fighters the UMMA has to offer. She showed a lot of improvement against Laura Vandervoort, and she was tremendous here, tonight. Rest assured, whatever comes next for her, she'll be ready.

Well, the RSI team is extremely busy preparing for the upcoming tournament. All of these women are looking to show the improvements they've each made in their fight game. Julianne is out to prove she's better than the dead last seeding she's received. Danneel Harris, meanwhile, plans to show that she is, far and away, the class of her division. It should be an exciting event.
