Rampage Starts Strong

What a way for Rampage Sports to make its UMMA debut! Two fights, two wins. Of course, it helps that both fights featured women at the top of their game. Both Danneel Harris and Stephanie McMahon have displayed their impressive abilities in the past, and tonight was no different. And with those abilities comes a certain... confidence. Neither was shy in her post fight comments:

Harris: "I really wanted to put on a good show for the fans tonight, and I feel like I accomplished that. A big thank you to Kristanna Loken for being such a willing victim, too. Did you see the way I pounded her stupid throughout that first round? And yet, she hung in there, giving me the chance to really take her apart before putting her away. The fans ate it up."

McMahon: "I have to give my opponent credit... Veronika Zemanova never quits dreaming. No matter how many times I beat her down, she keeps coming back for more. She wants my title, and I have to respect the way she never gives up. Of course, I seem to recall her saying recently that she had plans to reduce me to a bloody smear on the cage floor. Well, maybe while she's washing her OWN blood out of her hair tonight, she can consider the merits of spending a little more time training and a little less time running her mouth."

Humility is optional here at RSI. Our only rule is that, if you talk big, back it up. And, both of these women took care of their business tonight.

Speaking of taking care of business, the RSI stable has a number of big matches coming up in Australia. Many of these fighters have seen their career stall, and this will be the first chance for each to see how their new RSI training programs are paying off. Specifically, Britney Spears is hungry for that Middleweight title. She hasn't had much success against Laura Vandervoort recently, but she's confident her new team has prepared her well for this fight.
