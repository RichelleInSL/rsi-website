SIGNS OF TROUBLE AT RSI?

First, Danneel Harris goes down at the Hit the Beach tournament. Now, Stephanie McMahon loses to Angelina Jolie in a fight that lasts little more than a minute and a half. Two of Rampage Sports' most prolific fighters losing in dramatic fashion. Are the wheels coming off at RSI? Not at all. We favor an aggressive style for most of our fighters. The goal is to press the action and force the opponent into doing things she doesn't want to do. The style is normally fast-paced, exciting and effective. However, on occassion, it backfires. Let's face it... Stephanie pressed too hard against a fighter of Angelina's caliber, and she paid the price. We're not the slightest bit worried about her. In fact, this fight simply showed us that we need to get a better leash on that power of hers.

Well, a light night for RSI in this one, but Never Again is just around the corner. Danneel Harris will look to show her last win against Kristanna Loken was not a fluke. Michelle Marsh let her mouth run a little bit after her last victory over Ivanka Trump. We'll see if she can back up her words in the rematch. And, after four losses in a row, Erica Rose Campbell will be looking for some signs of life in her fight game against Missy Peregrym. Another performance like her last will definitely lead to a serious conversation about her future with RSI... like whether or not she has one.
