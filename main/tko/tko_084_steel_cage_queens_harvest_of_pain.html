TKO Issue: #84
Date: August 2013
Franchise: Steel Cage Queens
Event: Harvest of Pain
Location: O2 World (Berlin, Germany)

Return To Form

After a couple of tough losses, Stephanie McMahon bounced back in a big way here in Berlin. Having to face off against the talented and experienced Uma Thurman is not probably not the easiest way to recover from a losing streak, but it may be the best way. Nothing in this one came easy for Steph, as the two traded blows for nearly three full rounds. Though I think she would have won even if the fight had gone to the cards, it had to feel better for Stephanie to score the decisive knockout. "After the way my last few fights went, I wanted this one to be a clear win. So, yeah... it felt good." A win like this one should give a fighter a nice shot of confidence. Surprisingly, though, it left Ms. McMahon questioning her approach. "I think maybe I need to try something new. Throughout my career, I've always just slugged it out with my opponent. No matter how hard they hit me, I'd just hit them back harder. I still feel like I can do that, if I have to. But, here in the SCQ... this division has a lot of women who hit pretty god-damned hard. Maybe I need to find a way to fight smarter." Well, what do you know? It looks like one of Uma's punches may have jarred something loose. It's not surprising, though. Any fighter would tell you that standing in front of the fists of Uma, Veronika Zemanova or Angelina Jolie is not a smart idea. So, if Steph wants to try a new approach, we'll help her find it.

An excellent performance from RSI newcomer Catherine Bell, as she thoroughly dominated Famke Jansen tonight. Unfortunately, though, Catherine got a little flashy at the end, and it resulted in a painful injury to Famke. "I feel bad about that. I really do. I got carried away there at the end, and it went all wrong. I hope to get the chance to talk to Famke about it, soon." That's all well and good, but what was with the attempted strip move in the first place? "Well, I think maybe the rookies are rubbing off on me a little," said a sheepish Catherine. I'm glad everyone is getting along, but let's try to remember who the veteran is in this arrangement, OK?

Speaking of the rookies, another fine showing for Marzia Prince tonight, once again taking out fellow heavyweight Sarah Silverman. Sarah got her licks in, but, much like last time, Marzia's iron fists were just too much. Her win tonight leaves Marzia at a perfect 3-0 in the SCQ heavyweight division. Is the success going to her head? "I've been surprised about how easy it's been, to be honest," the sultry 38-year-old said afterward. Uh, oh. "I don't mean that to be insulting. I know I've had some luck on my side, so far," continued the Texan beauty. "In fact, I sometimes feel like I'm waiting for the other shoe to drop." Phew. Though it's good to know she hasn't gotten too cocky, I'd also say her success is due to a lot more than luck. Marzia works hard, and her fight game is improving rapidly. So, humility is a good thing to have, but so is confidence. In the meantime, we'll work on some shoe avoidance techniques to get her ready for the what lies ahead.

Well, that's all for this week, but there'll be plenty of action coming up in Prague, as the women of DEF take to the cage. It'll be a busy night, with four titles up for grabs, including Kristanna Loken getting another shot at Danneel Harris's welterweight crown. We'll see you then.
