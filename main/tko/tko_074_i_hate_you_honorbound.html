TKO Issue: #74
Date: May 2013
Franchise: I Hate You (later renamed to Elite Starlet League)
Event: Honorbound
Location: Shanghai Oriental Sports Center (Shanghai, China)

Disappointment Deleted

After a string of recent disappointments, RSI had quite a night here in Shanghai. Four fights, four wins... a clean sweep in the Orient for the women of Rampage.

Ashley Tisdale has been having a tough time of it recently. Her match against Bai Ling went so well for two rounds, only to have it all go wrong in the third. The dejection of that tough loss seemed to carry forward to her next match, as she showed zero heart while suffering a brutal, bloody pounding at the hands of Alizee Jacotey. We knew we needed to get things turned around, before she lost herself completely. She has the skills. She puts in the work. So, what was missing? "I needed to get nasty," said a reinvigorated Tisdale. "I was too busy worrying about technique and strategy. That stuff is for training. When fight time comes, I needed to execute. I needed a 'kill her before she kills me' attitude." Where did we turn for "nastiness" inspiration? We looked for something we knew Ashley could relate to. So, we asked her what Sharpay would do. Her initial response was to laugh herself stupid at the idea of Sharpay Evans, cage fighter. "Sharpay?" said a hysterical Tisdale. "She wouldn't be caught dead in there. She'd be terrified that she might scuff her manicure." But, after a little explaining, the light came on for her. "They asked me what Sharpay would do if she wanted something. I said, 'She would just take it.'" Click. "I want wins. So, instead of trying to figure out how to get them, I'm just going to take them. And, I'm not going to let anyone stand in my way." Hot damn. I think we may be on to something. It certainly paid off tonight, as Ashley scored a terrific victory over Lady Gaga. Still, it seems even Sharpay has a heart. "I'm sorry about the way it ended," said Tisdale with a shrug. "Injuries happen, but it's never something you want to see."

Next up, after a slip up at the recent team event, Krystal Forscutt got back to her winning ways with a dominating performance. Credit to Ciara for hanging in there as long as she did, but it really was all Krystal in this one. How is Krystal enjoying her newfound success? "I'm loving it," beamed Forscutt. "I never feel overmatched in there anymore. Even when I lost to Alexandra, I was in that fight right up until the end. I can't win them all, but I'm never going down without a fight. My opponent will have to earn it."

Don't look now, but the RSI rookies are making things look easy. So far, every one of our new recruits has won her UMMA debut, and Allira Cohrs and Jennifer Stano added their names to that list, tonight. Not only that, they both won in such overwhelming fashion, they barely broke a sweat. Jennifer was first up, facing fellow rookie Julia Voth. Seeking to make a statement, Jen picked her opponent apart early with precision strikes. Overwhelmed by punches, kicks and brutal knees, poor Julia never managed to mount any offense, at all. Then, after basically rendering her opponent defenseless, Stano decided to add insult to injury by tearing away Julia's meager fighting attire, leaving her assets on full display. "I don't expect any mercy in there, and I certainly don't grant any," Stano said afterward. "If she didn't like it, she should have stopped me." We certainly like Jen's attitude, but we're probably going to have to talk to her about rubbing salt in people's wounds.

Allira, meanwhile, faced a tough task in the much more experienced Kat Dennings. At least, that's how we thought it would go. But, Allira basically made Kat look silly, working in close and hammering her with vicious strikes from the clinch. Kat showed her mettle, refusing to wilt in the face of Allira's withering attacks. But, Allira just would not let up, and she eventually broke through Denning's defenses... a series of brutal knees leaving her sprawled out on the canvas. "I've waited a long time to find something like the UMMA," said a thoroughly pleased Cohrs. "I've got my shot, now, and no one's going to take it from me. I plan on making my way to the top, and I'm going to crush anyone who gets in my way." Apparently, a serious case of supreme confidence has broken out among the RSI rookies. That's fine, provided we can keep it from evolving into overconfidence.

Speaking of fresh faces at RSI, we'd like to welcome some new competitors to our roster. Both Emma Watson and Jennifer Lawrence approached us a few weeks ago regarding the possibility of joining the Rampage family. Two strong, fiercely independent women, both of whom are very comfortable with who they are and refuse to compromise their values to satisfy anyone. No doubt, they'd fit right in with the culture at RSI. Unfortunately, both women were with Valkyrie Wrestling United at the time. RSI is not in the business of stealing competitors from other stables, so we were forced to tell each of them that, though they would be welcome here, we could not negotiate with them while they were under contract with another organization. By the way, did I mention that these were two strong, independent women? Apparently, they both left our offices and set to work in securing their releases from VWU. When word leaked that the women were available, I immediately made the requisite calls and the deals were done. So, why the desire for change? "I like what's happening here at Rampage," said Watson. "I enjoyed my time at Valkyrie, but I really felt I needed a change. I've worked hard, and VWU has certainly helped me achieve a lot. But, I'm coming into my prime, now. I really wanted to make sure I had a solid team behind me to help me get to the top of the division and stay there. RSI can give me that." Jennifer's reasons were more urgent. "I feel like it's make or break time, for me," said a determined Lawrence. "I've gotten my feet wet, but I don't really feel like I'm achieving as much as I can. And now, this injury has got me sidetracked... I just feel like, when I come back, I want a fresh start." And, why RSI? "I like how this operation is run. I like the respect they show their athletes. I didn't come here to be some sideshow. I came here to win. I feel like I can do that with this group behind me."

And, since I already had my good pen out, I decided to reach out to another fighter I've had my eye on for some time. Emily VanCamp is in a very crowded division that includes one of the most experienced fighters the UMMA has to offer. Despite that, her strength and tenacity have allowed her to mostly separate herself from the field. And, she's done it all on her own. She calls to mind another RSI fighter who has recently shown me the value of heart and determination when it comes to success in the cage. I had no doubt we could help her reach the next level in her career. Emily, however, had her reservations. "I'd been on my own for so long... I really wasn't sure I wanted to be part of a stable," she said. I have my ways, though. "Richelle brought me in and sat me down. She made it clear that RSI wasn't going to step in and take over my career. They were going to get behind me and give the extra push I need to make myself better."

Unfortunately, Emily suffered a minor injury in this evening's match against Irina Sheik. Though Emily was not officially fighting for Rampage Sports tonight, rest assured that her recovery will be managed by the RSI team. As for the match, I think it perfectly encapsulated what we see in Emily. As I referred to earlier, Irina is one of the most seasoned women out there, and Emily gave her all she could handle. RSI seems to be specializing in tough, talented women who find themselves ranked behind wiley, experienced veterans. We're proud to have Emily on board, and we're excited to see how much more she can accomplish with RSI in her corner.

Next stop, India, as the women of Shift make their way to the cage. The only RSI rookie still waiting to debut, Brittany Tacy will get a chance to show what she has as she faces off against fellow first-timer Emilia Clarke. Julianne Hough will attempt to continue her climb up the rankings, stepping in against surprising rookie sensation, Alexa Vega. And, Alyson Michalka will look to rebound from her disappointing injury loss to Brooklyn Decker, taking to the cage to face Rihanna. Rihanna will certainly be hoping to show she should be in the mix for the Light Heavyweight title, so Aly is likely to have her hands full. See you then.
