TKO Issue: #65
Date: February 2013
Franchise: UMMA (League Wide Event)
Event: Hit the Beach Tournament
Location: Adelaide Entertainment Centre (Adelaide, Australia)

Learning Curve

Everyone at RSI was really looking forward to the Hit the Beach tournament. We felt each and every one of our fighters had shown serious improvement over these past few months, and were eager to see where each one stood. And, honestly, the results were mostly decent. One truly poor showing, and a few fights that just didn't go our way. However, an unfortunate injury... one that came at the hands of another RSI fighter... overshadowed everything.

To begin with, Danielle Lloyd has the dubious honor of laying claim to that truly poor showing. Caitlyn Wynters is a brutally skilled stiker, and Danielle's training team spent a lot of time preparing her to deal with that. Apparently, that time was wasted, as it took less than 30 seconds for Caitlyn to reduce Danielle to a bloody mess. Danielle looked totally bewildered when the attacks she was trained to expect came at her. In short, she looked like a rookie out there, and that is unacceptable.

Well, what can we say about Julianne Hough? When you're seeded last, you start out against the best, and Julianne is simply not at Keira Knightley's level, right now. We would like to have seen a better performance, but the truth is that Julianne needs to just put this behind her and concentrate on winning against competition she's more evenly matched with. She needs to improve her ranking and improve her seeding for future tournaments.

After the Ultimate Force event a few weeks back, we alluded to the fact that Alyson Michalka was simply overmatched by someone of Amber Heard's caliber. Well, unfortunately for Aly, the situation hasn't changed much, and she was unlucky enough to draw Amber again, tonight. Amber wasted no time reinforcing what we learned at Force. As she did last time, Aly fought gamely. But, much like Julianne, Aly needs to improve her ranking by winning against fighters who are closer to her level.

We really, really want to say nice things about Michelle Marsh. She fought hard and showed a lot of skill against the much more experienced Mariah Carey. The goal was for Michelle to get the fight to the ground, where she has the advantage. Now, Mariah is a tough fighter who defends the takedown well, so we knew it was going to be difficult for Michelle to get the position she wanted. And, that leads us to the source of our disappointment. She had Mariah right where she wanted her... TWICE... and couldn't close the deal. I don't think doing everything right and still losing can qualify as any kind of success.

Ashley Tisdale's result hit the RSI team pretty hard. She performed well, but everyone here felt she was much better than her seed, and we really expected her to emerge victorious against Bai Ling. That's not to take anything away from Ling. She's a tough fighter, but we felt Ashley had made great strides in her training, and that she was ready for this. Afterward, a clearly disappointed Ashley tried to put a brave face on things. "I'm trying to concentrate on the positives from this one, but it's tough to deal with," she said, wiping away a stray tear. "I thought I fought well, but I knew it wasn't enough. I was good, but she was better. I just have to accept that." We disagree. Ling was better tonight, but you don't just have to accept it. We'll see you in the gym.

Speaking of things that hit the team hard, let's get to the disaster that was the Elisha Cuthbert/Britney Spears bout. From the moment the brackets came out, we knew this matchup was likely to happen, and it was not something we were looking forward to. Any time you have two of your own fighters facing each other, it can't end well for everyone. Unfortunately, it turned out much worse than we had anticipated. Elisha was outclassed in this fight. We knew that going in, but we were interested to see how she handled herself. Now, her left ankle is shattered, and her career is on hold. She faces a long recovery from injury at a time when she was just starting to really get her fight game on track. Heartbreaking.

As for Britney, we're certainly happy for her, and, clearly, no one blames her for what happened. No one, that is, save for Britney, herself. She was clearly concerned for Elisha after the fight, and she appeared shaken well after the match was over. Indeed, it is our firm belief that her mind was still not in the right place when she came out to face Doutzen Kroes, and it clearly showed. She failed to defend, and she failed to press the fight the way she normally does. In the end, she fell victim to a wild punch that came with almost no setup from Kroes. Clearly, Britney was not focused, and it resulted in a disappointing loss.

Lastly, we have Danneel Harris. Now, looking at the big picture, Danni's performance in this tournament was exceptional. She beat three top-notch, experienced fighters in Salma Hayek, Jessica Alba and Emmy Rossum. And, she didn't just win. She won big, and it was exciting to watch. But, the number one seed comes with certain expectations, and getting your clock cleaned in a minute and fifteen seconds does not meet those expectations. No doubt, Cheryl Cole is among the greatest fighters in the UMMA. There is no shame in Danni's losing this fight. But, the way she lost it was tough to accept. This was a higly anticipated matchup, and the crowd was expecting a war. So, the big picture... Danni was awesome. But, the way it ended definitely left a bad taste.
