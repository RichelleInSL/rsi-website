Heavy On Top, Light On The Bottom

Danneel Harris: With the extended layoff, this event got pushed into yet another of Richelle's seemingly endless vacations.

Stephanie McMahon: Yeah, does she have a trust fund or something we don't know about?

Danni: (laughs) I don't know, but she sure seems to be enjoying herself. In the meantime, I couldn't find anyone willing to take on the press release duties this time, so it looks like I'm stuck with it.

Steph: And, because I'm so lucky, I got stuck with you.

Danni: Hey. There are worse things.

Steph: Name one.

Danni: Nothing springs to mind, but I'm sure I'll come up with something. In the meantime, let's get things started. Emma Watson made it back in plenty of time for this event, and she and Tiffany Mulheron seem to have, uh... resolved whatever issues they were having.

Steph: (clears throat) Uh, yeah... that's for sure. And, I think that's about all we can say.

Danni: (nods) Indeed. We're just gonna call that one a personal matter, and move on to the fight itself. And, what a fight it was!

Steph: Before we get too far into it, I want to take a second to congratulate Emma's opponent, Daniella Monet. She and Emma have never faced each other before, and most first timers don't fare too well against the featherweight champion. I think Daniella showed a lot of heart and skill tonight. A performance like that will get you the win, most of the time.

Danni: I totally agree. But, it wasn't enough tonight, as Emma was absolutely sensational.

Steph: She was terrific, there's no doubt. Emma's always precise, but her accuracy tonight was off the charts. Almost everything she threw found it's mark. She was practically perfect.

Danni: Practically?

Steph: (shrugs) I saw a few opportunities for improvement.

Danni: (laughing) Always working on your fighter development strategy, eh?

Steph: I can't help it. I feel responsible for everything that happens in that cage.

Danni: Yeah, well... you're a loon.

Steph: Gee, thanks.

Danni: I've seen the work you put in with these girls. Every one of them has improved since you starting training them.

Steph: Yeah, sure... just take a look at the rookies, tonight. They looked like punching bags.

Danni: They looked like ROOKIES, Steph! That's why we call them that!

Steph: Yeah, well... Brittany Tacy needs to work on her takedown defense. This is not the first time an opponent has made her cry out her submission in the cage. Maybe being stripped and humiliated will make her pay a little more attention. And I can tell you this: Dianna Dahlgren is going to have a new training partner, starting right now. And you're looking at her. I don't know how many times I've warned her about that half-assed takedown maneuver she loves so much. I'm going to beat that instinct right out of her.

Danni: And what about Shelby Chesnes?

Steph: There's really not much to talk about in that one. But, Shelby deserves credit. She did what she was supposed to do, and she got the win.

Danni: Wow. That sounded almost like a compliment, coming from you.

Steph: (shrugs) It's as close as we're getting, so take what you can get.

Danni: (shaking her head) You're something else, you know that? I've never met anyone who was a pain in her own ass before. It's stunning, really.

Steph: Whatever.

Danni: Oh, and Dianna? It's been nice knowing you.

Steph: (laughs) Alright, alright... let's just move on.

Danni: No, let's not. Let's go back to Emma. You said yourself that Monet was impressive. But, Emma was still better. You don't think you had something to do with that?

Steph: I dunno. Maybe it was the new training regimen she and Tiff have got going.

Danni: Oh, hey... we said we weren't going to talk about that.

Steph: Yeah, they didn't do much talking, either.

Danni: OK, fine, fine... you're right. Let's just move on. With usual nemesis Vikki Blows out with an injury, Julianne Hough showed she's capable of beating anyone, as she literally destroyed Ksenia Solo.

Steph: Jules did everything right in this one. Ksenia's still young, and Julianne was just too much for her.

Danni: Aly Michalka was impressive tonight, too, as she made it three in a row over her persistent tormentor, India Reynolds.

Steph: India's kill or be killed approach used to give Aly fits, but she's much better at handling it. Truth is, though her record doesn't really show it, Aly is a terrific fighter. It's just tough to get ahead when you're in there with that pack of wolves.

Danni: In another rivalry matchup, Jennifer Lawrence evened things up with Melissa Giraldo.

Steph: Those two were absolutely rocking the cage, tonight. And Jen was taking the worst of it, really.

Danni: Yeah, she looked like a beaten, bloody mess coming out for the third round. Melissa was way ahead on points, and I thought for sure she had this one. But, Jen just kept coming and coming. She caught Giraldo with a brutal high hick, and the Colombian model was badly dazed. She tried to punch her way out of it, but Jen scored again with that kick, and it was all over.

Steph: It was a tremendous comeback, but it's clear Jen still has a lot of work to do. She needs to be able to control the fight better. She was lucky to get the win tonight.

Danni: Does nothing make you happy?

Steph: (thinking) Puppies. Puppies make me happy.

Danni: I seem to remember that your buddy Jerry Lawler is a big fan of puppies, too.

Steph: (rolling her eyes) Not that kind of puppies.

Danni: Last, but not least, we have Sabine Jemeljanova. Her opponent, Kate Upton, has been on quite a roll in these divisional matchups. But, Sabine stopped her cold.

Steph: This was a nice win for Sabine, who's been on a pretty good roll of her own. Upton was a tough test for her, and she handled herself well.

Danni: What? Nothing to complain about?

Steph: Well, there was that one takedown...

Danni: Never mind. I'm sorry I asked. Next up, we have the special Maxim 100 tournament.

Steph: Did you see this thing? The UMMA ranked them based on that stupid Maxim list, instead of by their UMMA ranking. Jennifer Lawrence is ranked fifth. She's in the same pool as Irina Sheik, Candice Swanepoel, Brooklyn Decker... it's going to be a NIGHTMARE to try get her ready.

Danni: It certainly should be interesting, so be sure to check it out. And, if anybody needs me, I'll be in the training room, giving Steph oxygen.

Steph: It's gonna be a fucking disaster, is what it's gonna be. They must be out of their minds. They'll be lucky if somebody doesn't get killed.

Danni: Wow. You are just a ray of sunshine, aren't you?

Steph: Fuck off.

Danni: Being stuck with you.

Steph: What?

Danni: You asked me to name something worse than being stuck with me. Being stuck with you is definitely worse.

Steph: Fuck off. Again.
