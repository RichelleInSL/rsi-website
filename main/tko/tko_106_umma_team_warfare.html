TKO Issue: #106
Date: May 2014
Franchise: UMMA
Event: Team Warfare
Location: John Labatt Centre (London, Ontario, Canada)

RSI Teams on the Move

Team time again, which means I get the week off from handling the press release duties. Take it away, ladies.

Britney Spears: Well, here we are again, Leesh. Another successful team event that's left a bad taste in my mouth.

Elisha Cuthbert: I hear you, Brit... though, I got a little spoonful of sugar that sweetened things for me.

Britney: You sure did, and we'll get to that in a minute. But, I guess we'll start things off with Aly.

Elisha: Yeah, that was a tough break for you guys. It cost you a lot of points. And Aly's always so dependable, which must have made it harder for you.

Britney: I'm not worried about how it effected the team. Heather Graham has been around a long time, and she knows how to win. She got to Aly early, and Aly seemed to be scrambling a little bit to get things under control. Heather slipped a clean shot in there, and that was that. But, everyone has bad days, and it's up to the rest of the team to deal with it. We had Aly's back tonight, and I'm proud of every member of the team, Aly included. The only thing that's bothering me is how it effects Aly's championship hopes. She needs every point she can get in that division, and this is going to set her back quite a bit.

Elisha: I agree, it's a bad situation. But, Aly seems to have a new attitude on these things. She doesn't seem to get caught up in the championship hype. She just takes it as it comes. Besides, I think Daniela was secretly happy to be the one to do the consoling, after Aly helped pick her up last time.

Britney: I saw Ashley in there, too, so I think she has all the support she needs. And, you're right... Aly does have a new attitude. She's changed a lot these past few months. You called her "dependable" before, and you couldn't have picked a better word. But, there's more too her, now. She's starting to lead. Have you seen her at these team things? She's everywhere. Cheering people on... getting in people's faces. She's all about the team.

Elisha: Yeah... she'd make a great team leader.

Britney: Yeah, and... HEY!

Elisha: (smiling) What? I didn't say she'd make a BETTER team leader.

Britney: You didn't say she WOULDN'T, either!

Elisha: I guess that's true.

Britney: Oh, you're so gonna get it when we're done here. But, truth is, she might make a better leader.

Elisha: Oh, stop...

Britney: No, no... I'm not looking for sympathy or fishing for praise. I'm just saying... Aly would be good at this.

Elisha: She would. But, not better than you.

Britney: Awww, thanks hon. Too little, too late, though. You're still getting your ass kicked.

Elisha: (yawns)

Britney: Cute. So, not all was sunshine for you guys tonight, either.

Elisha: No, it wasn't. I knew coming in that tonight's matchup with Kristin Kreuk was going to be a tough one for Danica. But I knew she could do it, if she could just get through the first few minutes.

Britney: I'm sure Kristin was thinking the same thing, which is why she was all over Danica the minute the bell rang.

Elisha: Yeah, Danica's been improving, but she's still trying to figure things out. I think she's got what it takes, but she's not ready to handle someone like Kristin.

Britney: True. And, speaking of not being ready...

Elisha: (nods) Kelly Hu. Grace Park seems to have a personal vendetta against Kelly, and Kelly has not responded well to the challenge.

Britney: Yeah, I'm not sure what it is between them. I only know that Grace is looking like the better fighter, and Kelly needs to step up.

Elisha: Kelly fought well tonight, but she just couldn't keep up in the second round. Like Danica, she's shown a lot of improvement since joining RSI. But, you're right... Grace has thrown down the gauntlet. Kelly needs to step up if she doesn't want to be embarrassed.

Britney: Well, anyway... beyond that, things went pretty well for both teams.

Elisha: They sure did. So, should we do the Fighter of the Night awards?

Britney: (laughing) Oh, geez... you seem a little excited.

Elisha: And you know damn well why. So, stop messing with me and pick out your winner.

Britney: OK, OK... well, I had a lot to choose from, once again. No let down from Stephanie in this one, as she handled her business beautifully against Pamela Anderson.

Elisha: I don't know whether she was was trying to prove something or what, but she sure didn't leave any doubt.

Britney: Nope. Danni, Emily, Julianne... all big favorites with everything to lose. But, they came through, as they always do.

Elisha: They did a great job of staying focused.

Britney: So, that leaves two... Catherine Bell was really taking it to Jenny McCarthy, but a brutal injury cut that one short. So, that makes things easy for me. Our Fighter of the Night has to be Tiffany Mulheron.

Elisha: Amy Smart certainly gave her all she could handle, but Tiff never let things get away from her.

Britney: Nope. She had to grind it out on the ground for almost the entire first round, but Tiff's defense was perfect. And, once they got back on their feet, it was all Tiffany.

Elisha: I can't argue with you. This was not an easy fight for Tiff, but she stayed on top of things. She was always in control.

Britney: She sure was. So, your turn...

Elisha: (clearly excited) Do I have to go through everyone first, or can I just skip to the end?

Britney: Geez.. you're really losing it. Here... let me help you out. Daniela bounced back from her disappointment last time out, with a textbook performance against Alexandra Paul. Petra, Roselyn and Torrie all steered well clear of upsets with crisp, clean performances. But, without a doubt, your Fighter of the Night is...

Elisha: (emphatically pumping her fist in the air) ...Cobie Smulders!

Britney: (laughing) I got to hand it to you, Leesh... you know how to squeeze every ounce of talent from these rookies when you need them. Cobie looked like a savvy veteran against Kate Beckinsale, tonight.

Elisha: Thanks, hon... but, I really can't take credit for this. When I asked Cobie to fill in for Amanda, I knew I was asking a lot of her. But, she took it as a challenge, and she worked her ass off to be ready. And, tonight's performance left no doubt that she was.

Britney: I think it's clear that RSI's rookies have a lot of talent, and they're eager to prove themselves. I can't wait to see how they start to develop, now that Steph's got hold of them.

Elisha: It's going to be awesome. I wonder if Richelle will consider starting up a third team.

Britney: I hope not. I like having them there when we need them.

Elisha: Awww... that's not fair.

Britney: I'm just kidding. Besides, Richelle has already said she has plans for Emma. I'm thinking we may soon have a third contributor for these press releases.

Elisha: You think Emma would fit in with us?

Britney: Have you seen her fight? I think Emma would fit in anywhere she damn well pleases.

Elisha: A fair point. Well, I guess that's all for this release.

Britney: Yep. We're out of here. (kicks Elisha in the ass as they exit)

Elisha: Hey!!

Britney: Told ya I was gonna kick your ass.

Elisha: Oh, you fucking BITCH!!

Oh, my fucking head... well, that wraps up another successful team event for RSI. All kidding aside, I'm proud of every one of these women. And, yeah... I'm most proud of my team leaders. Both teams are moving up the rankings faster than I ever could have expected. Doubling down on my humility, I not only have to admit I'm proud of those two jokers... I also have to admit they're right. Early discussions are in the works for RSI to form a third team. And, as usual, things are not going the way I expected.

More on that down the road. For now, though... just about everyone at RSI is busy getting ready for the upcoming Blood on the Red Carpet PPV. Every time the UMMA has one of these events, it seems like more and more RSI athletes are involved. I'm sure I should be excited about that, but, right now, it's just making me crazy. We'll see you then.
