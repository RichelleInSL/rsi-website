TKO Issue: #108
Date: June 2014
Franchise: Steel Cage Queens
Event: Tomorrow Never Dies
Location: MTS Centre (Winnipeg, Manitoba)

Wilson on the Rise

Danneel Harris: With Richelle away on vacation, I've asked Emma Watson and Jennifer Lawrence to handle the update duties this week. Take it away, ladies.

Emma: Well Jen, not a bad event for RSI, right?

Jen: No. Though, it was a tough one for Roselyn Sanchez.

Emma: Yeah, Ali Larter strikes again. She caught poor Emily with a solid shot at the Red Carpet event, and now she came away with the victory in an all-out war with Roselyn.

Jen: Yes, indeed. I'm sure the fans really enjoyed that one. But, tough luck for Roselyn.

Emma: I don't think there's anything to worry about, though. Roselyn put up a great fight, and she's still finding her feet after joining RSI. I actually think this was a pretty good outing for her. If she keeps improving, I think she can make a lot of noise in that division.

Jen: Geez, I hope so. She was a bloody wreck by the end of this one.

Emma: Oh, stop. We've all done our share of bleeding in the cage. Sometimes, things just don't go your way.

Jen: I know. Just telling it like it is.

Emma: Yeah, well... I think you need to lighten up.

Jen: You damn Brits... always so casual.

Emma: You wanna go a round or two in the cage? See how casual I can be?

Jen: Settle down, champ. Settle down.

Emma: That's what I thought. Anyway... another terrific outing for Marzia Prince.

Jen: No question. The big blonde is punching way above her weight class now, but that doesn't seem to phase her, at all.

Emma: Nope. She's got her mind set on her goal, and she doesn't let anything get in the way.

Jen: And, speaking of not letting things get in the way, how about that Torrie Wilson?

Emma: After her big win at the Red Carpet event, Torrie just kept things rolling right along with a beautiful, text book win over Abi Titmuss.

Jen: Yeah, Abi hung in tough, but Torrie just kept coming.

Emma: Torrie's striking was incredible, but it was really her defense that was the story. She really never let Abi get anything going.

Jen: Like you said... text book. Torrie had a plan, and she made it happen just like she wanted.

Emma: No doubt, the other girls in the SCQ super heavyweight division are paying attention to Torrie, now. Her road's about to get a lot harder.

Jen: Yeah, but I think she can handle it.

Emma: Me, too. And, speaking of handling things. RSI has a lot on it's plate, with three title opportunities at the upcoming DEF event.

Jen: Yep. Danni gets the chance to reclaim her crown from Kristanna Loken, Britney gets another shot at Malin and Petra Cubonova tries to put an end to her title drought against Adriana Lima.

Emma: Petra's had a tough time against Adrianna in the past, but this is her first shot at the belt as a member of RSI. We'll find out if it makes a difference when we see you there.

Jen: You know... I think I'll take you up on that offer to go at it in the cage.

Emma: (crooks a finger at Jen) C'mon, Yankee... I'll show you how the Brits do it.

Jen: Oh, pish-posh.

Emma: (laughing) Yeah... you're ass, bitch.

Jen: Oh! Such a foul mouth on you, Miss Emma!
