RSI UP TO THE CHALLENGE

Originally, RSI was scheduled to have the week off for Open Fight Night. But, a couple of surprise challenges could not be ignored, and the RSI fighters stepped up, as always. Of course, that was only after a great deal of hand-wringing on the part of the RSI staff.

The first challenge of the night was aimed at Britney Spears, and it came from a pretty surprising source. Actually, I guess it shouldn't be too surprising, considering how fierce sibling rivalry can be. But, to see kid sister Jamie Lynn come after Britney this way... it just seemed like a bad idea. Where we worried that Jamie Lynn might upstage her sister and steal the win? Not at all. We were actually terrified that Britney might totally destroy her sister, and what the psychological consequences of such a victory might be. It's been nearly two months since Britney shattered stablemate Elisha Cuthbert's ankle at the Hit the Beach tournament. And, while we're pleased to report that Elisha has made a full recovery, Britney is still struggling with the effects of what happened. Seeing Elisha back in the gym has certainly helped things, but we were nervous about what might happen if Britney seriously injured her sister. Still, a challenge was issued, and Britney has never backed down from a challenge. And, as it turns out, she made quick work of her over-matched sibling without doing any serious harm. Even so, Brit was clearly still edgy after the fight. "I don't know what the hell she was thinking," she spat. "She had to be out of her mind to challenge me. She was lucky something bad didn't happen." True... Jamie-Lynn simply wasn't ready for a fighter of Britney's skill level, and Strikeforce management might do well to try and reign her in before she does get hurt. That said, you have to admire her confidence. We here at RSI wish her well in the future.

The next challenge was even more surprising to us, but, again, I guess it shouldn't have been. The UMMA is extremely competitive, and not everyone is able to hack it. So, while it was surprising for rookie Holly Bricken to receive a challenge, we should have realized there are many fighters desperate to get a win any way they can. Chyler Leigh has had a rough time of it so far, and Raccoon management apparently thought they had found an easy opponent that would help her get back on track. With the benefit of hindsight, we wonder what in the world they were thinking. Chyler took on a fighter who was a total unknown to her, and she stepped way out of her weight class to do it. But, to be honest, when the challenge came in, we were terrified. Holly had yet to set foot in the cage for real. We knew she had the skills she needed, but you never truly know what kind of fighter you're dealing with until you see her on fight night. A loss to someone on Chyler's level would have been a crushing blow to a newcomer like Holly. But, once again, our concerns were rendered moot by an excellent performance. Things started off with some initial trash-talk and a quick, painful lesson for Holly in where your elbows should be when your guard is up. Then, one swift kick from Holly had poor Chyler stumbling around the octagon like a drunken sailor. Holly's legs are like steel cables, and Chyler just never stood a chance. "She thought she was going to get an easy win against the new girl," a clearly insulted Bricken said afterward. "She thought wrong." Well, it wasn't much of a show for the fans, but it turned out to be a great way for Holly to make her debut in the cage. Thanks, Chyler.

Well, the RSI group is back on the schedule at the upcoming Maximum Risk event. Newcomer Marzia Prince will make her debut against Sarah Silverman, and Stephanie McMahon will step into the cage against rival Veronika Zemanova in a matchup that will determine the Corrupt Souls super heavyweight championship. Steph has had a lot of success here in the UMMA, including an excellent 11-1 record against Veronika. But, she came out flat in her Corrupt Souls debut against Angelina Jolie and had to be helped out of the cage after the Tomb Raider actress leveled her with a stunning combination of kicks. Can she pull herself together and regain her championship status? We'll find out next week. See you then.
