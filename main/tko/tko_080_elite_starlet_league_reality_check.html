So Close

Let's start off with a quick review of the facts: Irina Sheik is easily one of the most experienced and dominant fighters in the UMMA. She has been the ESL lightweight champion for as long as anyone can remember. She has taken to the cage nearly 70 times, with over 50 victories. Her record is simply incredible. Emily VanCamp is a tough, tenacious fighter. Though she has put together an impressive resume of her own, it is not on the level of Irina's. With over 30 fights, Emily is certainly experienced. But, with fewer total fights than Irina has wins, she does not have Irina's experience. However, she already has nearly as many losses as Irina does. So, on paper, Emily trails Irina in every way. In the cage tonight, she was her equal. In every way.

I won't waste time saying I think Emily was robbed, even though I think she was. Did she dominate Irina? Certainly not. But, I think she was the better fighter tonight, and I think a lot of people will agree. For her part, Emily has been silent on the subject, refusing to comment afterward. I think that's probably best. No doubt she's disappointed about how things turned out, and she should be. But, this is not the time for sour grapes. Not every decision will go your way. That's just how it is. But, the result really has nothing to do with what Emily accomplished tonight. She took the best Irina had to offer, and she paid it back with interest. She turned heads, and undoubtedly gained fans. And, more than anything else, she did herself proud. In the history of the UMMA, a fight that ends in a draw will not be all that memorable. In the history of Emily VanCamp, however, I think tonight's result will mark a turning point. Emily will be back, and she'll be better. For years, Irina has been the best by far. That distance has closed. When she looks over her shoulder now, Emily will be standing there. And RSI will be standing right behind her.

Next up, we have another step in the rejuvenation of Ashley "Sharpay" Tisdale. And yes... she hates when I call her that. But, if pissing her off serves as motivation, so be it. It certainly paid off tonight, as Ashley looked crisp and confident in her rematch with Lady Gaga. "I felt good, tonight," she said afterward. "I was able to the things I wanted to do, and keep her off balance. So, I got the job done, and now I need to get ready for my next challenge." So much for stopping to smell the roses. But, this new take no prisoners approach seems to be working for her. She's winning, and she's hungry for more. We like her like that.

Another fight and another win for Krystal Forscutt... this one a first round dismantling of Gemma Arterton. To watch her control and command the action tonight, you'd never believe she came to us with only 1 win in 13 fights. She's 5-1 since then, and there's just no stopping her now. "I feel like a different person, as far as who I am as a fighter," said a relaxed Forscutt. "I really can't tell you exactly what the problem was before. I just know it's behind me, and I'm not just saying that. I'm really starting to believe it." Well, if seeing is believing, then she really has no choice. She's looking great out there.

Allira Cohrs certainly gave it all she had out there tonight, but she just could not keep pace with Charlotte Church. Allira just could not keep the fight standing up, and Charlotte's ground game was simply too much for her. While I'm proud of the way she battled out there, her team is definitely going to have to rethink their approach. She can't be that inept on the ground and hope to compete at the upper levels.

Not much to be said about Jennifer Stano's fight tonight. What was shaping up to be an exciting battle for the fans ended all too quickly with a nasty injury to Nathalie Kelley. Jennifer chose not to comment, citing her respect for Nathalie as a competitor. Injuries like that are tough to watch, and we wish Nathalie a speedy recovery.

The FAC is up next, and RSI will have plenty of athletes in action. At the top of the board, Emma Watson will get the chance she was calling for as she faces off against Christobelle Grierson-Ryrie for the featherweight title. Emma was victorious, though not exactly dominant, in their last matchup. No doubt Christobelle has been training hard for this fight, so I'm sure Emma will have her work cut out for her. In other action, Julianne Hough, fresh off her amazing run to the title in the last tournament, will be looking to make a statement as she faces bantamweight champion Vikki Blows. Her tournament success has given Julianne a hunger for more, and she'll be calling for a chance to put that belt around her own waist if she wins this one. Also, Alyson Michalka will try to keep cool as she faces the brutal and relentless attacks of upstart rookie India Reynolds. Jennifer Lawrence will face a tough test of her own in rookie Michelle Vawer. And, Brittany Tacy will step up in class to face the much more experienced Selena Gomez. As always, it should be an exciting night. We'll see you there.
