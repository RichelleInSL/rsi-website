Date: September 2013
Franchise: None (League-wide Event)
Event: Global Rivalry
Location: Bizkaia Arena (Barakaldo, Spain)

Greatness Reclaimed

Right off the bat, I have to admit that victories like this one make me nervous. We bring in the talented Daniela Hantuchova because she's "been having a tough time lately," and she goes out and wins a tournament that is absolutely stacked with talent. The way I see it, there's no way to improve on this. The only thing RSI could possibly do now is screw things up.

Stacked with talent? Surely, I'm exaggerating, right? Well, let's put it this way... if I told you that Daniela had beaten SCQ Super Heavyweight champion Veronika Zemanova, you'd be impressed, right? If I said she'd beaten FAC Heavyweight champion Caroline Wozniacki, that would be impressive, as well. A win over DEF Super Heavyweight champion Maria Sharapova? Certainly, that would be outstanding.

But, what if I told you she beat every single, solitary one of them?

Most likely, you'd say you didn't believe it. Well, neither do I, but that doesn't mean it isn't true. Here in Spain, Hantuchova fought like a woman possessed. No opponent was too strong or too experienced. No one was more formidable than Daniela, herself.

So, what was the secret to this historic run? To hear the Slovakian tell it, it sounds like maybe she recently got some worries off her mind.

"I think, maybe, I was worried too much about what to do with my career. Should I stay alone? Maybe join with a company? I did not know what to do. I only knew things were not going good."

Once she made the decision to sign with RSI, though, the weight of her indecisiveness was behind her.

"Now, it is done. I make my decision, and I live with it."

So, with her questions answered, it seems the young tennis star decided to take a few chances.

"When I fight against Veronika... I know she is great champion and great fighter. But, I tell myself not to worry too much. Just go after her. What will happen will happen."

Well, what happened is that Zemanova did not know what hit her. Veronika came out swinging, as she always does. But, Daniela was ready and able, dodging and countering with ease. After avoiding a few vicious kicks from the Czechian powerhouse, Daniela scored with a tremendous hook that stopped Veronika in her tracks. Zemanova was never the same after that, and Daniela just went to work, destroying the champion with a barrage of punches and kicks.

Though thrilled with the victory, Hantuchova was respectful of the injury her opponent suffered.

"I am sorry she was injured. But, it is a fight... I do what I must to win."

With the win over Veronika behind her, it seems Daniela remembered what made her great in the first place.

"After win, I think to myself, 'To hell with it. Why can I not beat anyone? I am as good as anyone here.'"

Damn right. Just ask Caroline and Maria what they think. Though neither fight was easy, Daniela left no doubt who the best of the tennis triumvirate was at this tournament. And, it's no surprise that some cross-sport rivalry factored into the mix.

"On the court, perhaps I am not as good as Caroline and Maria. They beat me many times," Hantuchova said.

Then a broad smile spread across her face as she added, "But here... I kick some ass."

Here you kicked a lot of ass, my dear. A LOT of ass.

I don't really know how to put this win into perspective, to be honest. Our relationship with Daniela is so new, it's hard to take any credit for what she did here. Though we're glad we were able to help her make her decision and settle her worries, Daniela really won this tournament on her own. All I can do is sit here and drool over the possibilities the future holds.

Congratulations, Daniela.

I'm not going to get too caught up in what happened to Roselyn Sanchez here. When I announced that we had signed Roselyn, I made reference to the stuggles she's experienced. Being matched up with the nasty, brutal and, unfortunately, talented Nadine Coyle is not the way to end such stuggles. I've already met with Roselyn and told her to just put this one behind her. A new chapter in her MMA career starts today.

It was a tale of opposites for Emma Watson in the Rookie/Prospect tournament. A quick, decisive victory over Katerina Graham in the opening round gave way to a one punch loss to the much heavier Ewelina Olczak in the second.

Afterward, Emma was both disappointed and impressed.

"I really hoped to go a lot deeper in this tournament. But that punch was... wow!"

Graceful, as always, Watson was also realistic about what had happened.

"I won't call it a lucky punch. She set it up well, and caught me clean," said the young champion with a shrug. "Sometimes you lose because you're not prepared, or you don't have the right game plan. And, sometimes you lose because it's just not your night."

Watson also paid homage to Olczak's fearsome power.

"I'm just glad she and I aren't in the same division. That's all I can say." Then, she added with a laugh, "With a right like that, Richelle should think about signing her. At least, then she'd be on my team."

Hmmmmm.

Continuing the Jekyll and Hyde theme, things started well and ended disasterously for Allira Cohrs in the Gut Check tournament. After putting on a superb display in her opening round matchup with Arianny Celeste, Allira was simply annihilated by RWB's Lucy Becker in the second round. Becker nailed Cohrs with a barrage of punches right at the opening bell, and Allira just never fully recovered. With her opponent staggered from the opening onslaught, Lucy gave her a clinic in how to destroy a fighter with your fists. Not much for Allira to do but lick her wounds and hope to learn from an experience like this one.

Nothing for Jennifer Stano to be ashamed about here. She looked terrific in her early round matchups against Nikki Bella and Mayra Suarez, dispatching each with ease. But, in the third round, the ILF's Bianca Balti fought back and fought back hard. To her credit, Jennifer stood right in there, going toe-to-toe with her opponent. The fans in attendance were treated to two rounds of great back and forth action. But, Bianca took control in the third, scoring early with a high kick. Bloodied and exhausted, Jennifer wilted but refused to quit, holding on to the end of the round. Though the decision rightfully went to Bianca, I think Jennifer showed the kind of heart and determination that's needed to be a great fighter.

Well, that's all for this update. Next up, the women of SCQ. In the past, the SCQ events have been relatively quiet for us here at RSI. But, with a roster that has expanded from two fighters to five, we're going to be very busy over this next week. And, since SCQ remains our smallest division, it seems like I may spend the rest of my life here at this desk, writing one press release after another. Joy.
