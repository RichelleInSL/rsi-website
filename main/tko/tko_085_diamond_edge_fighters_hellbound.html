Harris Stays On Top

A word to the wise, folks. Don't aggravate Danneel Harris. A few months back, Kristanna Loken gave Danni all she could handle at Never Again, with Danni barely escaping with the split decision. Shortly after that, Kristanna defeated the champion at Stacked, beating her quite badly. After that loss, I called Danni out publicly, because I knew she could do more. Well, I don't know if that worked, or if Danni had just had enough of Kristanna. But, either way, these last two matches have been all Danni, all the time. That includes tonight's championship fight, in which Harris pummeled poor Kristanna from wire to wire. Loken tried valiantly to get herself into this fight, but Danni's defense frustrated her constantly. In the past, defense has not been a priority for RSI's in-cage leader, but she and her team have been focusing on it recently. That focus has certainly paid off, as Kristanna scored with an abysmally low percentage of her shots. I give Loken credit for her toughness, though. Though she was never really able to hurt Danni, she showed no quit. In fact, it looked like she might even make it through to a decision. But, Danni was just too much, dropping Kristanna just as the final bell sounded.

After the fight - seated with her belt on the table next to her - Harris talked about how she stays focused.

"I just concentrate on getting better. I don't necessarily plan specifically for my next opponent. I work on making myself the best I can be."

To make that happen, Danneel has been working hard in areas she used to ignore.

"I've been concentrating on defense and ground game, recently. I still prefer the stand up game, and I always will. But, I realized I need to become more well-rounded if I want to continue to be successful."

When asked what techniques Danni uses to improve herself, she hesitated.

"Well... what I'd like to do is train with someone who could push me. But, that kind of thing isn't really available to me."

Wait. Did she just call ME out?

Congratulations on another fine performance, Danni. You might make it look easy, but we all know it isn't.

After yet another disappointment at the recent lingerie tournament, Britney Spears spoke publicly about her failing confidence. With the next DEF event only a couple of weeks away, Britney didn't have much time to shake those blues. Compound that with the fact that she drew champion Malin Akerman as her opponent, and things were not looking good for the blonde songstress. What's a girl to do when faced with such long odds? Just win, of course. And not just win, but look absolutely fantastic while doing it.

Not that things were looking so good at the outset. Malin scored early with a barrage of heavy blows, and it looked like this one might be over before it even started. But, Britney had other plans.

"She tagged me early and opened me up. I could feel the blood trickling down my face," said Brit afterward. "Someone like Malin rips your face open 20 seconds into the fight... I probably should have panicked. But, instead, it felt like something clicked into place. I was totally focused, after that."

Focused is one word for it. Possessed seems more fitting, though. Responding by driving her knee into Malin's face, Britney basically took control from that point forward. While she didn't necessarily dominate, she came as close as possible when facing a fighter of Akerman's talent.

What was her secret?

"I had simply had enough of losing."

Good enough for me.

While Britney looked massively relieved after her big win, friend/nemesis Elisha Cuthbert looked like she might have to be sedated.

"I knew she could do it! I fucking KNEW it!!" bellowed an ecstatic Cuthbert as she invaded Britney's press conference.

After grabbing a seat, because.. you know... why wouldn't she?... Elisha heaped praise on her training partner.

"I know she's had a hard time lately. Sometimes a losing streak can get out of hand, and I was a little worried about that. But, she's back. I mean, big-time back."

Not to be denied, the nemesis side of Elisha also made an appearance, as she cooly added, "Of course, maybe it's just me you can't beat."

Britney just shook her head and smiled. "I'm SO gonna put my Yankee foot up your Canuck ass."

So, it looks like I have to admit it... I was wrong on this one. Very wrong. A few months back, Elisha suffered a serious injury in a bout against Britney. Afterward, I basically panicked. For me, the only thing worse than an RSI athlete suffering a debilitating injury was to have the injury come at the hands of another RSI fighter. Based on their rankings, it was obvious that Britney and Elisha were going to face each other quite often. I felt I couldn't risk additional injuries. At the time, I had every intention of releasing Elisha. I hated the idea, but I felt it had to be done. These two women were having none of it, though. The story of how the blonde duo tag teamed me in my office is well known, at this point. Britney telling me I couldn't cut Elisha, she was too good. Elisha telling me I had to give things a chance. It was gut wrenching. I couldn't stand it. I caved. And, thank god I did.

I don't really know how this happened, and maybe that's because it's something that can't be explained. But, it's obvious these two women have forged something special. There is a kinship between them that I wouldn't have thought possible for such fierce competitors. They have each other's back, no matter what. Even as opponents, there is a clear mutual respect between them. But, anyone that's seen them fight knows they never go easy on each other. Not long ago, I asked them how they make it work.

"We talked about that early on. We agreed that, when we're in the cage, we both do everything we can to win," said Elisha. "I'd know if she was pulling punches. But, I also know she'd never do that."

Britney agreed completely.

"All in, all the time. That's what we said. I have too much respect for her to 'let her win.' She wants to earn every victory. Same as me."

And, that may be the key to everything.

"When I lose to her, I come back hungry for revenge," continued the Canadian. "Not in a vicious way. I'm just driven to get better... to keep pace with her."

Again, Britney nodded her assent.

"She pushes me. Every day."

Then those pearly whites flashed as she added, "Most women... they have to worry about being ready for their next fight. With Elisha, I have to worry about being ready for tomorrow."

So, there it is. Two friends and rivals who are not afraid to go at it, full bore, every day. They push each other. Each improving herself, which, in turn, improves the other. Together, they are more than either could be alone.

Sometimes, I get this shit right by mistake.

Holly Bricken is the only RSI rookie who's record remains perfect. She also hasn't had the unwelcome opportunity to have her head handed to her as the bottom seed of a tournament, but we're sticking with the positives here. Tonight, she stepped up in class to face the more experienced Arisa Oda. Could she handle it? No question. What Holly lacks in experience and training she makes up in pure, raw power. And, my god... is this woman strong. Arisa seemed to come in with a gameplan to get the fight to the ground, but Holly shattered that plan with a crushing left that staggered the Japanese fighter. Arisa tried to shake it off and refocus, but she just couldn't gather herself. After a few more exchanges, Holly turned the lights out with a bone shattering uppercut

After the win, Holly was not overly excited.

"I just keep telling myself I have to get better... I have to get better," she said, looking more like she'd lost the fight than moved to 4-0. "Every time I win, I move up the rankings. Pretty soon, I'm going to be in there with women I can't simply overpower. Then, what?"

Easy does it, rookie. You won. Enjoy it, for a little while. You have a team behind you to worry about the big picture. Just focus on getting back to the gym, and making yourself better.

That's all from us for this event. Up next, the women of ESL. A number of RSI fighters will be in action, but the headline attraction will be the rematch between Irina Sheik and RSI's Emily VanCamp. Irina has easily been the best lightweight fighter in the ESL for a long time now. Last time out, though, Emily matched her toe-to-toe, their fight ending in a draw. Was it just a fluke, or can she stand up to the champion, once again? Be sure not to miss this one!
