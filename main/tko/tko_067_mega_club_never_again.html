TKO Issue: #67
Date: March 2013
Franchise: Mega Club (later renamed to Diamond Edge Fighters)
Event: Never Again
Location: Marina Bay Floating Stadium (Singapore)

TOO MUCH TALK

After a surprisingly bad loss to start things out, things improved quickly for RSI tonight. Let's get right to it.

I'm not sure what to say about Michelle Marsh's performance against Ivanka Trump tonight. Michelle certainly did not perform well, but we sent her in there with the wrong game plan, apparently. The plan was for Michelle to stay away from Ivanka's dangerous striking abilities and get the fight to the ground, where she has the clear advantage. What no one expected was for Ivanka to be the one to take the fight to the ground. Once she had the advantage, Ivanka seemed quite please to stall and control. Michelle, on the other hand, looked quite foolish in her total inability to even attempt an escape. Michelle didn't have anything to say when asked for comment. Strange, when you consider how much she had to say after defeating Ivanka in their last meeting. Well, regardless, this certainly was a blow to the confidence she showed then.

Next up, Danneel Harris against Kristanna Loken. This was billed as a matchup between two of the best fighters Mega Club has to offer, and they put on quite a show. Kristanna gave Danneel all she could handle, especially in the first round. But, Danni showed why she's the champ, weathering the storm and responding in the later rounds to eke out the win. We acknowledge this one was close, and Danni didn't disagree. "I felt like I fought a good, smart fight tonight," gasped a clearly winded, but happy, Harris. "I felt like I was able to do the things I wanted to do, for the most part. But, she just kept coming back for more, and she definitely hurt me a few times. This is getting tougher, for sure." If Danni wants to stay on top of this division, it's clear she's going to have to up her game. The competition is definitely fierce.

Last, but FAR from least, we have Erica Rose Campbell. There's no doubt Erica has struggled recently, and we haven't been shy about telling her so. She knew she had to get her game together and show us something, and she responded nicely against Missy Peregrym, tonight. I'm not going to spend too much time picking this one apart. The fight had it's ups and downs, but the important part is that this is far and away the best effort Erica has put forth in quite a while. We're pleased, and so is she. Now, let's do it again.

That's all for this one folks, but, with the Gauntlet event fast approaching, the RSI's I Hate You fighters have a lot to prove. Ashley Tisdale, Krystal Forscutt, Danielle Lloyd and Rhian Sugden will all be in action against some tough competition. We'll see you then.
