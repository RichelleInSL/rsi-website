TKO Issue: #96
Date: December 2013
Franchise: Steel Cage Queens
Event: Sheer Disrespect
Location: Orfeo Superdomo (Córdoba, Argentina)

A Bad Trend

After an up and down 2013, Stephanie McMahon has definitely started the new year off on the wrong foot. After throughly dominating rival Veronika Zemanova early on, Steph has now lost four of the last five matchups with the Czechian brawler, including the last three in a row. Tonight's loss was particularly painful, as Zemanova simply destroyed her. After busting her open within minutes of the the start of the fight, Veronika pounded Stephanie into a bloody wreck by the time it was over. Stephanie fought back hard, landing some powerful blows of her own. But, she was no match for Zemanova's brutal assaults. Thoroughly dominated, Steph hung on as long as she could, until she finally collapsed in a ruined heap less than a minute into the third.

McMahon was unavailable for comment after the fight, as she was still being attended to by the RSI medical staff. But, there really wasn't much left to say. Veronika made it quite clear tonight that the tide has turned in this rivalry. And, it's up to Stephanie and her RSI team to get things turned back around. I freely admit that we're bewildered as to what's behind this dramatic shift, but, rest assured, we won't quit until we find the answers we need.

A tough start to 2014 for Marzia Prince, as well. After a sterling rookie season, Marzia suffered her first divisional loss at the hands of Jenny McCarthy. Prince made a nice showing, but she just didn't seem to have it tonight, and Jenny made her pay. Afterward, Marzia admitted to feeling a little bit of relief. "I've said before that I felt things were going a little too well, for me," said the bodacious blonde. "My early success had really launched me up the rankings. I felt like I was getting in over my head, and tonight made me realize that might just be true." That's not to say that Marzia is simply going to accept this defeat. If anything, she plans to use the loss as motivation. "Another thing I learned tonight is that I can handle myself at this level. I didn't have a good fight tonight, but I didn't embarrass myself, either. It left me feeling like I can get past this with a little more focus."

Not all the news was bad for RSI here in Argentina, as a number of our athletes scored quick and decisive victories. Leading things off was Kelly Hu, who bounced back from her disappointing loss at Open Fight Night to score a lopsided victory over Tina Fey. Fey never even got her gloves up in this one, as Kelly was clearly in charge throughout. Though clearly skilled and dedicated, Kelly's recent performances had been choppy and her results showed that. Tonight was her first divisional fight under the RSI banner, and she looked fantastic. More performances like this one will definitely put her back on the championship track.

Roselyn Sanchez smashed her way to a lopsided victory over Renee Zellweger, and, much like Kelly befor her, she never gave her opponent a chance to do anything. The win continues a nice little winning streak for the Puerto Rican beauty, and tightens the standings in the Lightweight class. No one is running away from the pack in that division, and Roselyn certainly finds herself right in the thick of things.

Hard to get too excited about Catherine Bell's victory here, given the unfortunate way things ended. Not that Mac did anything wrong. On the contrary, she looked fantastic. And, the win gives her seven in a row, a trend we would love to see continue. But a brutal injury to opponent Jeri Ryan put a damper on things. We here at RSI extend our best wishes for a speedy recovery.

Last, but certainly not least, it seems Torrie Wilson is out to prove she's far better than her record indicates. After a masterful performance against veteran Monica Belluci at the team event, Torrie backed that up with a dominating victory over Rhona Mitra here tonight. Torrie knew all eyes would be on her. Had she really turned a corner, or was she just lucky against Belluci? Well, in what looked like a scene from a movie, Torrie waded out, tied Mitra up in the clinch, and just beat the living daylight's out of her. Rhona never knew what hit her. In fact, she probably still doesn't know.

Afterward, Miss Wilson was all business. "I felt good out there, but I can't get too excited about it. I'm near the bottom of my division. I know I earned that spot, and I'm not complaining. But, it's a long way from where I want to be." A hard, but certain, truth. Still, Torrie is taking a positive outlook as she prepares to climb the mountain in front of her. "I feel like this is a new beginning, for me. Things are finally coming together, and it's showing in the cage. That's all I can ask for, right now."

Next stop, Brazil, as the DEF takes center stage. And, it's another busy night for RSI. We have seven fighters in action, including yet another title matchup between Danni Harris and Kristanna Loken. Kristanna hasn't fared too well against Danni in the past, but she's recently signed with one of the new UMMA management companies. Will that be the change that puts her over the top? Oh, and much to my heart's delight, it appears the Britney and Elisha show is ready to come off of hiatus. I can't tell you how excited I am about that. Well, actually, I could. In fact, I have in the past. But, nobody seems to care very much, so... there you go. Anyway... we'll see you then.
