TKO Issue: #126
Date: May 2015
Franchise: UMMA
Event: Global Rivalry
Location: Mellon Arena (Pittsburgh, PA)

Em and Em

Always one of the craziest events of the year, the 2015 edition of Global Rivalry did not disappoint. For RSI, the event was decidedly mixed. A finals appearance by Petra Cubonova and another deep tournament run by Sabine Jemeljanova were tempered by a nasty injury to Stephanie McMahon, who was making a run of her own. With what amounts to four tournaments going at once and all of the weight classes mixed together, I've decided I have no hope of sorting out the chaos. So, I'm going to surrender before I start and go another route, entirely.

Stemming from an idea I have to credit to the Dragon's Den manager, RSI is going to occasionally do interview style releases featuring our various athletes. Today's lucky contestants are Emma Watson and Emily VanCamp.

And, no... I didn't tell them what I was going to title the article. But, really... they should have been able to guess.

Richelle Winterfeld: Welcome ladies. I'd like to thank you both for doing this.

Emma Watson: I don't remember being given a choice.

Emily VanCamp: Well... she DID ask me.

Emma: Huh. Really? And why might that be?

Richelle: I like her.

Emma: I see. And how do you feel about me?

Richelle: I find you absolutely insufferable.

Emma: Excellent. My plan is working.

Richelle: So, I guess we should start with the Global Rivalry event, which you were both involved in. What'd you guys think of it?

Emma: Uhm, I hated it.

Richelle: Gee, could getting your little butt whipped in the first round have anything to do with that?

Emma: By an opponent five weight classes heavier, in case you didn't notice.

Richelle: I noticed. What about you, Emily?

Emily: It was okay, I guess. I had a win over Gabriella Cilmi, which was a little bit of a surprise.

Richelle: Hmm. I'm not seeing too much excitement from you two. I take it this is not your favorite tournament.

Emma: Definitely not. I feel lucky just to survive these mixed weight class events.

Emily: I have to agree. For the lighter women, winning an event like this is very difficult. Just take a look at the championship matchups here in Pittsburgh.

Emma: Yep. Only the big girls left at the end.

Richelle: Your protests are duly noted. Meanwhile, the other big news coming out of the UMMA is the realignment of the franchises. Reactions?

Emma: The FAC was already the largest of the franchises, and it was also growing the fastest. Something had to be done, so I think it's a good thing. Of course, that's easy for me to say. It doesn't have much of an immediate impact on me.

Emily: I agree, it's a good thing... and necessary.

Richelle: It certainly has a larger impact on you, though.

Emily: Definitely. With Ashley Greene moving up into the ESL, Irina Sheik and I have a new power to contend with.

Richelle: Still think Irina is your toughest competitor?

Emma: Well, Irina and I have a lot of history now, and it's been back and forth the whole time. So, you won't get me to say I don't see her as a tough opponent. On the other hand, Ashley and I have met twice, with her winning both. So, it's impossible for me to rank them. I just know I have my work cut out for me.

Richelle: Fair enough. Next up, I'd like to chat a little bit about your training regimens. You're both leaders out there on the training floor, and I'd like to give everyone a feel for how it works here at RSI.

Emily: Well, let's start by establishing that everything we do stems from Steph. She and Danni are real the leaders out there.

Emma: That's right. They're the ones who set the priorities. They do a great job of knowing what each of us have coming our way, and they set the training accordingly. Sometimes, they can see a tough matchup coming two or three events ahead of time.

Emily: I honestly don't know how they keep track of it all, yet still find time to train themselves.

Richelle: I'm never shy about giving Steph and Danni credit for all that they do. They're the ones that keep the wheels turning, and I never lose sight of that. I've also told them both they can never retire. But, I want to hear a little about where you guys fit in.

Emily: Well, they like to have the weight classes train separately, and the roster is really set up to make that work. Each weight class has at least two experienced fighters, and they generally run the training for that group. Danni and Steph float around and keep an eye on things.

Emma: (points at Richelle) Don't forget her.

Emily: That's true. You've been out there a lot, recently.

Richelle: Just trying to help where I can.

Emma: Don't bother with the act. We all know you just like being a part of it all.

Richelle: I won't deny that's true. But, I hope I'm helping, too.

Emma: Yeah, well... I guess you know a few techniques that aren't totally outdated.

Richelle: Pretty sure I can still show you a thing or two.

Emma: Nah... you ain't got nothing left I haven't seen.

Emily: Now, now children. Play nice.

Richelle: (looks to Emma and hooks her thumb toward Emily) Miss Goody Two-Shoes, over there.

Emma: She spoils all the fun.

Emily: Anyway... sometimes Steph and Danni want to make a change to something the group is doing. Other times, they want to do something for a specific fighter. But, they always include the veterans in the discussion. For the lightweights, that's Roselyn and I. We're the ones who will do most of the actual training... making whatever Steph and Danni come up with into reality. We work with Brittany and Demi, trying to help them improve. From our side, helping them makes us focus on our technique. So, we learn a lot, too.

Emma: For the featherweights, it's a little different. We have mostly experienced fighters in our group, with Shelby Chesnes being the only one who's really new. So, that poor girl never catches a break. She's either going to be the greatest featherweight fighter in the history of the UMMA, or she's going to die trying.

Richelle: That brings us right into the next topic... what are your impressions of your newer teammates?

Emily: I think both Brittany and Demi are terrific. Brittany has a work ethic that actually makes me nauseous. So, there's no question she's going to have success. Meanwhile, Demi has had a tough time of it, but it hasn't shaken her confidence much. She believes she can straighten it out, and, the more I see of her, the more I think she's right.

Emma: I feel the same way about Shelby. Given who she is and what's made her famous, you'd think she might be used to just getting by on her looks. That's not the way it is, though. She wants to be great at this, and she puts in the work to make it happen. We're not easy on her, but she doesn't seem to mind.

Richelle: One more training question before we move on. Steph and Danni came up with the idea to do some inter-stable training with the women of Dragon's Den. How do you feel that's working out?

Emily: I think it's... interesting.

Richelle: Not exactly a ringing endorsement.

Emma: I think it's just a different experience. We're very comfortable with the way we do things at RSI, so working with them has been a little awkward, at times. But, overall, I think it's good. It lets us get more experience with different styles of fighting.

Richelle: For example?

Emma: Well, the featherweight group has just been outstanding. There's a lot of experience between the stables, and we have fun working with the newer fighters. On top of that, Rachel McAdams has so much experience, she has things she can teach every one. I would say working with her has been my favorite part of the whole thing.

Richelle: And you, Em?

Emily: Well, the Den does have a very experienced lightweight, but she hasn't shown much interest in being a part of this. That works out good though, because it balances things out. We have Roselyn and I from RSI, and Joss and Evangeline from the Den. Between the four of us, we have a lot to show the newer women... of which there are a ton.

Emma: We have that, too. Briana is kind of caught in the middle, but she's shown she has the talent to step up and tangle with some of the more experienced women. After that, a lot of fresh faces. It's fun, though. Everyone has a good attitude about it.

Emily: Yep. A lot of good ideas come out of it, too.

Richelle: Emma, you mentioned Briana Evigan. She's been on a tear recently, and she's certainly got the attention of the fans.

Emma: For good reason. Like I said, she's capable of big things. Every time she gets the chance to show it, she does.

Richelle: The realignment has put her in a tough spot, though.

Emma: You're talking about Nadine Coyle. Well, Bri's not going to have an easy time of it. That's for sure. But, if anyone can help her get ready, it's Tiffany.

Richelle: You seem to think pretty highly of Briana. I hate to do this, but I'm going to put you on the spot. How do you think she compares to you?

Emma: First of all, you don't hate to do this.

Richelle: That's true, but I felt like I had to say it.

Emma: As for Bri... I'll just say I'm glad she and I don't cross paths too often.

Richelle: Fair enough. Well, I think that's enough shop talk for today. MMA is not the only career for either of you, so let's talk a little bit about what you've got going on, acting-wise. Emily, much to the dismay of your fans, Revenge has come to a close. What's next?

Emily: I'm just as upset as they are. Trust me. But, it was a good run, and I really enjoyed the whole experience. In the meantime, I have a new movie coming out soon called The Girl in the Book. I've had success on television, which I'm very grateful for, but this is basically my first movie role where I play the main character.

Richelle: I'm happy for you, and I'm looking forward to it, but let's cut to the chase: when are we going to get to see more of Agent 13?

Emily: She'll be back in Captain America: Civil War, which we're filming now. It should be out next year.

Richelle: Does she kick butt?

Emily: You'll just have to wait and see.

Richelle: Aww. No fair. I pay you a lot of money. I don't even get a hint?

Emily: You pay ME to kick butt. Not Agent 13.

Richelle: Okay, I guess. At least I know you're up to the challenge, should Agent 13 need to get physical.

Richelle: That brings us to you, Miss Watson. You wouldn't happen to be working on anything, would you?

Emma: You know perfectly well what I'm working on. You and I have already discussed it, at length.

Richelle: I wouldn't say it was 'at length.' I still have lots more questions about this live-action version of Beauty and the Beast you've got going.

Emma: I'll bet you do. You're so into it, sometimes I wonder if you'd rather be the one directing it.

Richelle: No, I'd just screw it up. But, if they need any expert feedback along the way, I'm TOTALLY available for that.

Emma: Expert feedback? Like what?

Richelle: Like the fact that the woman they cast as Belle is kinda short for the role.

Emma: Oh, kiss my...

Richelle: ...'foot,' right? You were gonna say 'kiss my foot.'

Emma: Yeah. That's what I was gonna say.

Richelle: I mean, how tall is the Beast supposed to be? Seven feet, maybe? And Belle is shoulder height to him. So, that must put her close to five foot ten. At least.

Emma: So, you're comparing me to a drawing.

Richelle: If it aggravates you, sure.

Emily: I knew I should have asked Danni to come in here to keep an eye on you two.

Richelle: Oh, really? And what was Danni gonna do?

Emma: She'd make you show me a little respect.

Richelle: Respect, huh? Okay...

Richelle: Emma, you made a big impact with your involvement in the UN's HeForShe gender equality campaign. I also saw video of the live chat you did on FaceBook. I know you've got my attention. How has the response been, elsewhere?

Emma: Uhm... wow. I wasn't expecting you to be serious.

Richelle: I admit it's rare, but it happens occasionally.

Emma: Well, the response has been overwhelming, really... especially from men. I feel like maybe we can actually have a meaningful dialog about this. If we can do that, I think there's hope we can make real progress.

Richelle: Well, you now how I feel... both about the movement and your part in it.

Emma: But you're not going to say it, now.

Richelle: Of course not. I wouldn't want to give people the idea I like you or anything.

Emma: Oh, no. We wouldn't want that.

Richelle: Exactly. And, just to set things straight...

Richelle: So, Emma... what's this I hear about you secretly dating Prince Harry?

Emma: Oh, shut up.

Richelle: There, see? That's much better.

Richelle: Emily, I think I speak for fans everywhere when I ask, how much of Emily Thorne is the real you?

Emily: (smiles wickedly) More than you think.

Emma: Wow... I kinda like evil Emily.

Richelle: Me, too. She's going to need that kind of edge, if she wants to keep pace with Irina and Ashley.

Emily: I've come too far to let them take it all away from me, now.

Richelle: That's exactly what I wanted to hear.

Richelle: One last question for both of you: Tell us something about you that not many people know.

(Emily and Emma share a knowing look)

Richelle: What?

Emily: (to Emma) Devrions-nous lui dire?

Emma: Uhm... Je pense que vous venez de faire.

Richelle: I say again... what?

Emily: We both speak French.

Emma: Emily speaks it much better than I do. Which is sad, because I actually lived in France for a few years.

Emily: I don't think that's a fair comparison. I grew up speaking the language in Canada. I even lived with a French-speaking family for four years while I attended ballet academy.

Richelle: So, you both sp... wait. You dance ballet?

Emily: Oui.

Richelle: Wow. You're just full of surprises. Are you good?

Emily: Well, let's see... I was accepted to École supérieure de ballet du Québec... a school so prestigious, I was willing to leave my family and go live with strangers for four years. So, I guess I'm not bad.

Richelle: (to Emma) You been teaching her how to be a smartass?

Emma: Nope. Turns out she's secretly good at that, too.

Richelle: Lovely. Well, ladies, that's all I have. Thanks for your time, and I have to say... this went pretty well. I might just be tempted to try it again.

Emma: That's a great idea. I'm sure Brit and Leesh would love to do one of these.

Richelle: Huh. Well, I'll just let Danni host that one.
