TKO Issue: #63
Date: February 2013
Franchise: Shift (later renamed to Fallen Angels Combat)
Event: The Ultimate Force
Location: Allphones Arena (Sydney, Australia)

A Peek Into The Future For RSI

Tonight's results mostly showed what happens when you have too much to do and precious little time to get it done. None of tonight's fighters really had the chance to gain much from RSI's guidance and training. Yet, despite one glaring failure on our part, what these girls showed tonight has the RSI staff doing backflips.

First of all, we take full responsibility for what happened to Rhian Sugden tonight. We knew she simply wasn't ready for this fight. She has such tremendous physical gifts, but her fighting skills are lacking in almost every area. We brought her on because we are convinced we can help her develop the skills she needs to put those gifts to use. But, we simply haven't had enough time with her, yet. We discussed this with her before the fight, and we even discussed the possibility of pulling her out of this one. Rhian, of course, was having none of that, and we let her convince us to go forward. She's a fighter, and she wanted to fight. It was our job to look out for her, and we didn't. The RSI medical team will be working hard to get her back to 100%, and we will make this up to her.

Aly Michalka is another case where we simply didn't have enough time to get her ready. Her skills are not yet where we want them, and we really weren't able to set her up with a good game plan for dealing with the likes of Amber Heard. "I'm really disappointed in myself, quite frankly," Mihalka said when asked for comment. "She totally outclassed me. I just couldn't do anything with her. I expected better." And that, folks, is why we love her. Despite our failures, Aly put up quite a fight here tonight. Her performance simply reinforces our belief in what she can accomplish. We see big things for this tough girl.

What Krystal Forscutt lacks in technique, she more than makes up for in raw striking skills. And, perhaps, a little unbridled ferocity. "She (Lauren Budd) wouldn't shut the fuck up in there. So, I slammed my fist into her big mouth," said a still seething Forscutt. "It might not have made for a great show, but I sure as hell enjoyed it." We're not sure what Lauren's strategy was tonight, but it certainly didn't work out too well for her. Meanwhile, while pure anger has it's uses, we're looking forward to developing Krystal's real fighting skills. Much like with Aly, Krystal showed everyone tonight why RSI is so excited to have her on board. Her record in the UMMA is simply not reflective of what we believe she can do.

Julianne Hough face a difficult challenge tonight in Ariana Grande. The girls were evenly matched, and both had been struggling coming into this one. And, that's pretty much how the match played out. "A win is a win," said Julianne afterward. "I can't say I felt too comfortable out there tonight, but it was nice to pull this one out." That about sums it up, in our opinion. We're proud of Julianne's victory, but the whole fight had a rookie feel to it. We're looking forward to helping her step up her game in the month's to come.

Coming into tonight, Ashley Tisdale was looking to prove her recent victory over Katerina Graham was more than just a fluke. And, with her facing the red hot Joanna Levesque, it was going to be a difficult task. But, Ashley made it look easy, overwhelming Levesque with her striking ability and incredible speed. "I felt great out there, tonight," she said. "I knew JoJo'd been on her game recently, and I knew she'd be tough. My goal was to attack early and often, and not give her the chance to execute her game plan." This was quite a showing from Ashley. In fact, we're not afraid to say it was a little surprising. We knew she was a very capable fighter. But, we didn't see this kind of performance coming so soon. Our expectations for her just went way up.

Well, there's no rest for the weary here in the UMMA, as the Team Warfare event is coming up soon. Julianne and Krystal face a quick turnaround from tonight's action, as they will be back in the cage for that one. Also, Britney Spears will be looking to show how quickly she is developing under RSI's direction, and Elisha Cuthbert will try to build on her impressive showing at the Clinic event. It should be a great night of action.
