Nightmare Relived

I can't believe it happened again.

Those familiar with Tiffany Mulheron's long road to the championship know the story of the terrible night when she was completely destroyed in the cage by nemesis Nadine Coyle. But, you also know the story of her amazing and courageous comeback from that night. You know how, with the help of her friends and teammates, Tiffany trained and prepared and steeled herself to return to the cage and face down Nadine, once more. And, most importantly, you know how Tiffany found a way to finally unseat the long-standing champion, even if it was short-lived. It was a glorious comeback that culminated in ultimate vindication for Tiffany and her teammates.

And, yet... somehow... here we are. Back where it all started.

I won't go through the blow-by-blow of tonight's championship fight between Nadine and Tiffany. If you want to know the details, you can check the official UMMA round-by-round commentary. Or, you can simply look back to the details of that terrible night that had seemed such a distant memory. Tonight, just as then, Nadine crushed Tiffany into a bloody, helpless wreck.

After the fight, I hurried back to the RSI locker room to talk with Tiffany. As I entered, I found Emma Watson, her training partner, sitting alone on a bench with tears in her eyes.

"Where is she?" I asked.

"She finished with the training staff and went straight to the press conference," Emma said softly.

"Did you talk to her?"

Emma shook her head. "She wouldn't talk to me. She just left."

Uh, oh.

Emma and I arrived at the press conference just as it was starting. Danni was already there, and we quietly slid in next to her. Tiffany took her seat, and didn't bother to wait for a question. "I have no excuse for my performance here tonight," she said with a cold, stony expression on her face. "I humiliated myself, my teammates and my stable. I know that I'm better than this, but knowing it doesn't mean anything. I have to show it when the pressure's on, and I didn't." She stopped and looked out at her audience... her stoic expression cracking only slightly as her gaze fell upon Danni and Emma, who have been by her side since she joined us at RSI. She swallowed hard, and continued. "I let myself down, and, more importantly, I let my friends down. I am disgusted and ashamed of myself. And I honestly don't know where I go from here." Her remarks finished, she pushed away from the table and left the room.

By this point, tears were rolling down Emma's cheeks as she tore off after her friend. I turned to Danni.

"What the hell happened in there, tonight?" I asked.

"I don't know," she said honestly, as she wiped away a stray tear of her own. "She didn't look right from the minute the fight started. She looked... well she looked like a rookie. Her head was never in the fight." Then, her jaw tightened and her eyes narrowed. "And Nadine..." she growled. "That fuckin' Nadine..."

"Nadine did exactly what you'd expect she'd do, Danni," I said. "But she's not supposed to be able to do it to Tiffany, anymore."

We made our way back to the locker room, where we found Emma sitting and stoically watching Tiffany unload her locker into her gym bag. Our youngest champion turned to me, and I motioned with my head for her to leave so I could talk to Tiffany alone. She and Danni stepped out of the room, while Tiffany just kept throwing her things in the bag... seemingly oblivious to my presence.

"You're not going to even talk to me?" I asked after a few moments.

"Not much to say."

"So, that's it?" I said, the frustration clear in my voice. "You're just gonna quit?"

"You were there tonight. You saw what happened."

"I saw what you let her do to you, yeah."

She spun to face me, a flash of anger in those pools of green. "What's THAT supposed to mean?"

"It means where the fuck was your head, tonight?" I shot back at her. "Because it wasn't in that cage."

She turned away... once again fiddling with the crap in her locker. "I got my ass kicked. I'm not going to make excuses."

I reached out and touched my hand hand to her shoulder. "Tiff... what's going on?"

She shrugged me off. "Nothing." Slamming the locker closed, she snatched up her bag and headed for the door.

"Tiffany!" I called after her. "You can't just let it end like this."

She stopped, but kept her back to me. "I just need some time," she said finally.

I breathed a small sigh of relief. "Well... where are you going?"

"Home." And then, she was gone.

No sooner had Tiffany walked out when Emma flew in, followed by Danni.

"What happened? Where is she going?" asked Tiffany's distraught training partner.

"She said she's going home," I replied.

"Well... is she coming BACK?"

I shrugged. "It sounded like she was. She said she just needed time."

Emma looked silently back at the door for a few moments. Then she mumbled something softly to herself. I'm not sure of exactly what she said, but I'm pretty sure I heard things like "I'm sorry" and "I didn't mean it."

"What?" I asked her. The mumbling stopped, and she went back to silently studying the door.

"I'm going with her," she said finally, striding toward the exit.

"Emma, WAIT!" I called after her. "You're on the card for next week. You can't just leave."

She turned and looked at me, then looked back at the door. "I'm sorry, but I have to. I'll see you in a few days." And then she was gone, too.

I turned and looked at Danni. "Am I still in charge around here?" I said exasperatedly.

She just smiled. "What made you think you ever were?"

Danneel Harris, ladies and gentlemen. What a riot.

I'll be honest, the situation with Tiffany really ruined my night, and it certainly wasn't the best way to come back from vacation. But the rest of RSI's ESL competitors did everything they could to soften the blow. So much so that it's hard to decide where to start. But, when you bounce back from a bad injury and defeat one of the toughest women in your division, that'll get you noticed in my book.

Megan Fox is one of the most difficult challenges the UMMA has to offer. It's not often you see a UMMA fighter so dedicated to the art of submission, and it's even more rare for such a fighter to have the kind of success Megan has had. It doesn't matter how fast you are or how hard you hit... if Megan gets her hands on you, it's over. So, Amanda Seyfried had one mission in their matchup tonight... don't let her take you down. And, she accomplished that mission... for all of about six seconds. The sound of the opening bell was still echoing through the arena when Amanda found herself on her back in the center of the octagon. Moving swiftly and confidently, Megan calmly locked Amanda up in a guillotine choke. Game over, right? Nope. Amanda slipped loose and even managed to secure the top position. No matter. A few silky smooth moves later, the wily Fox was positioned to break poor Amanda's arm with a kimura. Surely, Seyfried was done for. But, again, Amanda slipped free. Fox stayed with it and caught Amanda with a stunningly quick armbar attempt. Seyfried yanked her arm free and scrambled away again.

It went like that for almost the entire first round. Fox seemed to be in complete control, like a cat stalking her prey. But, somehow, the mouse kept finding a way to escape. And, let me tell you... the cat was getting pretty pissed off.

About three minutes into the round, Amanda squirted free of yet another submission attempt. Only this time, she chose to take the fight right back to Fox, keeping things on the ground. She worked patiently and methodically... grinding her way past Megan's guard and into full mount. And that's when she just unloaded. A barrage of brutal elbows tore into Fox's face, and the swelling was immediately visible. Megan eventually managed to cover up and stop the damage, but Amanda kept control and rode out the rest of the round.

The second round opened with yet another stunning takedown by Fox, and I was sure that was going to be the end. But, again, Amanda stayed focused and showed no fear in engaging Megan on the ground. She patiently worked to improve her position, always mindful that keeping Fox at bay was her top priority. She wasn't able to do any real damage, but she tied Fox up long enough for the referee to decide to stand things up. Unfortunately, that only led to another slick attack from the Mistress of the Takedown. By this point, the sweat was pouring down MY face. With the girls grappling this way, I knew Megan could end things at any second. Still, Seyfried refused to fold, and the frustration was clearly starting to wear on Fox. Again, Amanda patiently worked her way up to full mount. Fox struggled mightily, a mixture of confusion and anger playing across her face. But Amanda held firm... and then down came the elbows once more. This time there was no escape for Fox, and a particularly brutal shot sent the beautiful brunette to la-la-land before the referee could pull Amanda off.

Now a win is a win, and a win over Megan Fox is nothing to sneeze at. But, I can't exactly jump for joy over this one. I'll let Amanda tell you exactly why. "Not one thing that happened in that cage was part of my game plan," the blonde actress admitted. "I'm happy to get the win, but I'm not at all comfortable with the way it happened. Megan was really in total control, and I just survived long enough to get lucky." While I think Amanda did more than just get lucky tonight, I'm right there with her on the rest of it. Megan's submission game is a lot to handle, but this was not the way to do it. Still, when nothing was working, Amanda adapted and ground out a win against a lethal opponent. There's no denying she was impressive.

Of course, if you're looking for an example of how to win a fight on your own terms, Emily VanCamp can certainly help you there. With top contender and eternal rival Irina Sheik sidelined with an injury, Elisandra Tomacheski stepped up to take a shot at RSI's lightweight champion. Outside of the special events, Emily almost always looks across the cage to find Irina standing there. Even in the tournaments, those two have a habit of finding each other. So it was more than a little unsettling for Emily to try and prepare for someone different. Well, I'm not sure what she did, but it worked to perfection.

Emily was as ferocious as always, and it seemed like almost every blow found it's target. Realizing that the stand up game wasn't going to work for her, Elisandra tried desperately to get Emily to the mat. She eventually succeeded, but she had to give up top position to make it happen, and Em never let her get anything going. Once they were back to their feet, it was all Emily once more. Elisandra made sure that no one would question her toughness, lasting until almost halfway through the second round under a heavy barrage from VanCamp. But, in the end, there was never any doubt in this one.

I'm proud of the way Emily handled herself in this fight, as I know the pre-fight training regimen was not what she is used to. Have no fear though, champ... I suspect you'll be seeing a familiar face across the octagon at the next ESL event.

Sometimes, you just take what you're given, and you go home. Daniela Hantuchova had a seemingly difficult battle ahead of her, facing off against rival Olivia Wilde for the light heavyweight title. True, Daniela has had a lot of success against Olivia in the past. But, it's rarely an easy win. Only, this time, it was.

Credit to Daniela for coming out strong in the opening round and putting Wilde on the defensive early. But, I think the real credit needs to go to referee Nikki Sanderson, who jumped in at the first sight of blood to make sure Olivia never got the chance to recover. I mean, who wanted to see a real fight, anyway?

Daniela didn't have too much to say, afterward. "What is there to say?" chirped the frustrated tennis star. "Fight was basically canceled." Truth is, winning is great, but no one wants to win like this. Still, Daniela used the few brief moments there were to show her game is as good as it's ever been. But, no doubt Olivia will recover from her vicious cut and come looking for another title shot very soon.

After a quick comeuppance from Daniela at the Red Carpet event, Krystal Forscutt got herself back on track by scoring her first ever victory over rival Maria Ozawa. And, if you don't think that matters to her, guess again. "Getting a win over Maria has been a goal of mine for a while now," admitted RSI's comeback queen. "It's no secret that my early career was horrendous, and I lost to just about everyone you can think of. But Maria was the one I had to face most often, and it feels great to have it go my way for a change."

Though her record is still abysmal, Krystal's more recent results have her creeping up the rankings. Though I doubt Daniela is breaking into a sweat over it, a few key victories for Krystal could bring the two into the same stratosphere sooner rather than later. Another divisional rivalry for RSI. Won't that be a hoot.

Recent acquisition Anna Kendrick made it two in a row over opponent Holly Valance, and this one was even more impressive than the last. Anna has shown a lot of potential in her young career, and she unleashed all of it on Holly... emerging virtually untouched from a bout that lasted barely more than two minutes. With this win, Anna is really starting to separate herself from the other young fighters in the division and move her way up with the big girls. Of course, division champion Cheryl Cole recently flattened our girl in no time at the Red Carpet event. So there's no question she still has a long way to go.

With a couple of tough losses to rival Kat Dennings recently, Allira Cohrs needed to pick up her game and prove some things to herself against Marina Diamandis tonight. Well, I think it's safe to say her confidence is definitely improved, as she looked extremely impressive in this one. Demonstrating the kind of precision you rarely see from a rookie, Allira picked Marina apart, never letting the young songstress mount any kind of offense at all. Allira currently finds herself in a tough spot in the rankings, surrounded by a lot of talent. Hopefully, tonight's win will help jump start her in preparing for the challenges ahead.

Finishing off on a downbeat, Michelle Lewin's rookie debut was not exactly a coming out party. To be honest, Michelle fought a decent fight, and she seemed to get stronger as the fight went on. Unfortunately, she let herself fall too far behind, and the decision rightfully went to opponent Chrissy Teigen. Fact is, Chrissy is also a true rookie, and no one really knew what to expect from her. That made prepping for the fight a tough task, and I think Michelle gave a respectable performance under those circumstances. That said, I have high expectations for our newest rookie, and I'm hopeful she can kick it into a higher gear for her next fight.

Well, that's all for this event. I want to thank Danni for holding down the fort while I was away, and I want to thank Emma, Jennifer, Aly and Ashley for filling in on the press releases. I also need to talk to Aly over her misuse of the word "stalker", but we'll handle that privately. Just to clarify, the term she was actually looking for was "devoted fan."

Be sure to tune in next time, as RSI has... uh... absolutely no title matches coming up? Wow. That's a switch. Still, with eight of our women in action, there'll be plenty going on. That includes a tough non-title matchup for featherweight champion Emma Watson, and a dangerous challenge for bantamweight contender Julianne Hough. Both are used to stepping into the cage with hardware on the line, and neither can afford a let down just because the stakes are smaller. And, with Emma currently MIA, well... who the hell knows what's going to happen?
