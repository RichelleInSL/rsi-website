TKO Issue: #73

Franchise: Mega Club (later renamed to Diamond Edge Fighters)
Event: Stacked
Location: Yokohama Arena (Yokohama, Japan)

Scratch That Rivalry

Another event, another sorry looking stoppage. I can't say I necessarily minded this one, though. But, we'll get to that later.

First up, a disappointing loss for Danneel Harris. Once again, we are faced with a questionable injury stoppage. But, in this case, there's no doubt Kristanna Loken was scoring well and damaging Danni badly. Danni was fighting back, and certainly scoring, as well. Truth is, it was a great battle. But, as far as I'm concerned, Danni is simply better than this. She's been atop the Mega Club welterweight division for quite a while now, and I think she's been coasting just a little in her training. Her last battle with Kristanna ended in an extremely narrow victory via split decision. And, still... it didn't seem to be enough to make her change. Well, her biggest rival just punched her square in the mouth, literally. Danni said she could have fought on, and we agree. But, there's no room for excuses in the UMMA. Maybe now, she'll get more serious. She's certainly saying the right things. "I guess I had this one coming," admitted Harris, her eye badly discolored from the damage she suffered. "Kristanna has been stepping up her game. I know she wants my title. And, I know what I need to do to keep it. I'll be ready for her, next time." Who knows? Maybe a better training approach will help her remember to keep her guard up when Loken is swinging at her face.

Next up, we have the disappointment of the night, at least from the fans point of view. Personally, I made no secret of the fact that I didn't want to see the match between Britney Spears and Elisha Cuthbert happen, in the first place. So, a quick stoppage after Brit suffered what appeared to be a tiny scratch on her forehead... well, that works just fine, in my opinion. The fans, however, were bitterly angry over the decision, and the women agreed. "I'm not happy," admitted Elisha. "I wanted to win, but not like this. I wanted to earn it." After treating the horrific wound with the latest release from the Mickey Mouse fashion Band-Aid collection, Britney took the loss in stride. "It's bullshit, but what can you do?" she said. Imagine my excitement when she gestured toward Elisha and added, "There's no question we'll meet up, again. No one is satisfied with this." Oh, joy. Feeling a little chippy, Elisha couldn't resist putting a shot across Britney's bow. "You know, if I take the next one, I'll outrank you." A wicked smile crossed Britney's face as she shot back, "Go fuck yourself, kid." In the words of the immortal Charlie Brown, I just can't stand it.

Last, but clearly not least, Holly Bricken continues to impress, this time in her divisional debut. Though the far more experienced Laetitia Casta made the RSI team nervous, Holly was rock solid in the cage. She stayed focused and fought through as Laetitia repeatedly frustrated her in the early going, and her perserverance paid off with a big win. "This felt really good," Bricken said afterward. "I know Casta has been at this for a while, now. But, I felt that if I could hang in there with her, I'd get my chance. Once we got through the first round, I knew she didn't have anything I couldn't handle. I just had to keep working my game." I like Holly's confidence, and, clearly, she has what it takes to succeed. But, the road only gets tougher, from here. She's one of the hardest workers in the RSI stable, and she'll need to keep it up if she wants her winning ways to continue.

Up next, we'll be keeping things here in the Orient, as the IHY women prepare for battle at Honorbound. Ashley Tisdale will step into the octagon to face the unfathomable Lady Gaga. Last time out, Ashley suffered a brutal loss at the hands of Alizee Jacotey, and she'll be looking to show she's learned from that experience. The rejuvenated Krystal Forscutt will try to avoid a slip up against newcomer, Ciara. And, a pair of RSI rookies will see their first action in the cage. Jennifer Stano will match up against fellow first-timer Julia Voth, while Allira Cohrs will have her hands full with the more experienced Kat Dennings. It should be another great show. And, I'll even be able to watch all the matches this time. It just doesn't get any better.
