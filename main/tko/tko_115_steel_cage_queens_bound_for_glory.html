TKO Issue: #115
Date: October 2014
Franchise: Steel Cage Queens
Event: Bound for Glory
Location: Honda Center (Anaheim, CA)

The Quiet Conqueror

The women of Rampage Sports are not known for running their mouths. They know it's hard enough to win in the UMMA when everything is equal. There's no need to get their opponents fired up with trash talk. In other words: Don't say it. Just do it. Up until now, it's been Emily VanCamp that has lived that mantra most effectively. As Em worked her way to the ESL lightweight crown, she made an art of always knowing what to say and when to say it. Well, don't look now hon, but I think we have a new Queen of Quiet. Because Catherine Bell took home the SCQ heavyweight title here in Anaheim... and I don't think I've ever heard her say a word.

Even I was surprised when I saw that Catherine was up for a title shot tonight. She goes about her business so smoothly and efficiently, she almost becomes an afterthought. Divisional fight against a tough opponent? Mac's got it covered. Need a victory to save the day in team competition? Look no further than Catherine. Even in the tournaments... though she's never been able to overcome phenom Louise Glover, Catherine always pushes deep against top ranked competition. In her drive to the title, she's beaten the likes of Jeri Ryan, Tricia Helfer and Caroline Wozniacki. And now, you can add Traci Bingham.

This wasn't Mac's first shot at a UMMA title. Almost three years ago, she had a shot against then-champion Ryan. The result was a total disaster, as Catherine was beaten bloody before being forced to tap out. She never even put up a fight, and the humiliating defeat sent her into a tailspin. She would go on to drop nine of her next thirteen fights, and a once promising career was left in ruins. But Mac refused to quit. She battled back, taking three in a row. She started to feel that competitive fire once again, and she went looking for a stable who would help her find her way. By sheer luck, RSI was also on a hunt at that time... looking for an experienced fighter to help us mold our young heavyweight recruits. A match was made, and the rest is history. Since then, Catherine has won eighteen of her last twenty-one fights, including tonight's title win.

With the memory of her last title fight lingering, Bell came ready to play in this one. There was no way she was going to be shut out again. Traci pushed the action from the opening bell, but she had no luck getting to Mac. Catherine's defense was picture perfect, and her counters were textbook. Bingham became frustrated and launched a reckless uppercut that Mac deftly sidestepped. The fail left Traci badly out of position, but she decided to press forward. Catherine dodged the next attack with ease, and her counter exploded into the center of her opponent's face. Amazingly, Bingham kept her feet and came again. Mac shunted her aside and the next punch finally put the champion away for good. Bingham collapsed to the canvas, unconscious. It was a commanding win, but it was marred by a devastating injury. The cageside doctor immediately determined that the knockout blow had shattered Traci's cheekbone, meaning a long layup and a tough recovery lay ahead for her.

The injury took some of the wind out of Mac's sails, and it was a somber Catherine that sat behind the microphone at the post-event press conference. "You never want to see something like that," responded the seasoned actress when asked if the injury had detracted from the joy of her win. "I feel good about my performance. I felt very confident out there," she continued. "But I feel terrible about what happened. So, yes... it's taken a little something away." When asked if she was disappointed that the injury clouded her big victory, Catherine was pragmatic. "I wish the injury hadn't happened. I wish I was in a more celebratory mood. But hey... shit happens, sometimes. I just hope Traci recovers soon." Couldn't have said it better, myself.

It's really not fair that I haven't lauded Catherine for her accomplishments sooner. She's one of the most consistent fighters on the RSI roster, and I never give her any credit for it. But I totally blame Mac for my oversight. After all, she never says a damn word. Never complains. Never gets involved in any drama. The only thing she ever does is win.

Well, fear not, Mac. I see you now, for sure. Truth is, I knew you were there all along. RSI needed a leader in it's heavyweight group, and you were the answer we were looking for. No doubt it's working, too. Marzia Prince is developing well, and she's come up huge for us in some big spots. And Dianna Dahlgren is off to a red-hot start, including two stunning victories over former champion Veronica Varekova. So the kids are all right, and their mentor has a shiny new belt they can drool over. Not too shabby.

So, you just keep doing what you're doing Cath. I'll take care of all the talking for you.

Catherine's big win wasn't the only good news for RSI at this event. Fighter development guru Stephanie McMahon continued her march back from the brink, scoring a no-doubt-about-it victory over former champion Uma Thurman. And the aforementioned Marzia Prince took another big step forward, as she badly outfought the more experienced Carmen Electra.

On the other side of the ledger, both Torrie Wilson and Roselyn Sanchez were dominated in one-sided losses here in Anaheim. I think it's time for both of these women to take a long, hard look in the mirror. I certainly haven't lost faith in them, but both have been struggling a bit, recently. It may be time for them to regroup and get back to basics.

A quick roster note: lightweight fighter Demi Lovato has joined the RSI family. Stephanie approached me a few days ago about the idea of adding Demi, and I'll admit I had a few reservations. Thus far, Demi's UMMA tenure has been... well, it's been shitty. But Steph is convinced she sees something, and I don't question Steph when she's got a hunch. I'd keep an eye on Demi, if I were you.

Well, that's all I've got for this event. Join us next time as stable leader Danneel Harris puts her welterweight title on the line against Kristanna Loken. Danni's been the class of the field in that division for a long time, but Kristanna's been chipping away... even snatching the title away from Harris for a short period. Loken has since signed on with the Pye's Gorgeous Grapplers stable, hoping that will give her the extra push she needs to catch up. For her part, Danni has long acknowledged that Kristanna has been steadily improving. Will she have what it takes to rest the title away again? Or will Danni prevail once more? Meanwhile, Petra Cubonova hopes to improve on last time as she gets another crack at heavyweight champion Adriana Lima. Meanwhile, another badly timed injury has forced middleweight champion Malin Akerman to pull out of her planned title matchup with Britney Spears. So, instead Brit will be facing... oh joy... Elisha Cuthbert. How fuckin' lucky could I get, right? Truth is, the fans haven't seen these two face off in quite a while. And when they do, they always put on a show. So, as long as everybody comes out of it with all their parts still working and in the right places, it should be awesome to watch. We'll see you then.
