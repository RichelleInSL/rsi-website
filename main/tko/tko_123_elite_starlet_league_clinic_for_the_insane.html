TKO Issue: #123
Date: March 2015
Franchise: Elite Starlet League
Event: Clinic for the Insane
Location: American Bank Center (Corpus Christi, TX)

Foot Flashback

There goes that foot again!

Yet another battle has ended in the war that rages between Emily VanCamp and Irina Sheik. And, as has become the custom, the challenger came away victorious. In a mirror image of their last fight, it was Emily who came out fast and took it to her opponent right at the opening bell. Irina tried to fight back, scoring with a few brutal shots of her own, but there was going to be no denying Emily tonight. Every punch she landed seemed to kick her energy level up another notch as the blows rained down on the champion. Oh, and speaking of her striking game... if you thought Em looked practically perfect out there, that's because she was. She landed every single thing she threw from distance, including the awesome finishing blow. In a flashback to the win that started it all, Em caught Irina flush on the cheek with a dazzling high kick that had the Russian supermodel in dreamland long before she hit the canvas.

"Guess it's my turn, again," said a smiling VanCamp afterward. Then, she gestured toward her newly reclaimed belt and added, "It's nice to have it back. I missed it a little while it was gone."

As the question and answer session progressed, the topic turned to the uncanny respect she and Irina seem to share for one another.

"I think respect is the right word," she said. "Irina and I don't know each other very well. Almost everything I know about her comes from inside the cage. And, let's face it... it's impressive. How can you not respect someone with the skills and the resume she has?" Respect was also the word Irina used recently to describe her opinion of Em. How does that make the Revenge actress feel? "Awesome," she said. "I don't think she liked me much to begin with. I took away a title she'd held for a long time. Since then, I've been preventing her from reclaiming that dominance. So, I'm sure she had some nasty things to say about me, even if she kept them to herself. But I think she's starting to realize we have something special going on. There aren't a lot of women who can stand up to Irina Sheik. I'm proud to be one of the few who can."

As for the back and forth nature of this rivalry, even Emily admits it's too ridiculous to comprehend.

"It's just insane," she said with a smile. "I know the fans go crazy for it, and I get it. It's exciting to be in these fights with her, so it must be great to watch." Still, it seems Em would like to see a change in the pattern. "I'd like to keep it for a while this time," she said, laying a hand on the belt. "I'm sure Irina has other plans, though."

You can count on that, Miss Emily.

For me, I throw in with the fans on this one. I'd love to see Em hang on to the belt for a while, but this feels like one of those rare situations where the rivalry is worth more than the hardware. I hope these two NEVER stop going at it.

Another successful title defense for Daniela Hantuchova, as she once again made things look easy against Olivia Wilde. Olivia seemed to have a new wrinkle in mind this time, repeatedly trying to mute Daniela's power in the clinch. It had little effect, as Hantuchova powered her way free and hammered the young actress again and again.

Much like the situation with Danni Harris and Kristanna Loken, I think Daniela is in Olivia's head at this point. Though they've faced each other over and over again, it's been nearly two years since Olivia has come out on top. Still... she has beaten Daniela in the past. So, as dominant as Hantuchova has been, there's always this feeling that Wilde is one adjustment away from victory. And no one knows that feeling better than Daniela.

"I know she has struggled, but also know what she can do," the Slovakian tennis star said. "She has given me very hard time in past. Even she has beaten me few times. She is having hard time now, but still must I be careful. She has ability to change fate."

Olivia's going to get another shot... maybe even two... at Daniela's title before the field catches up. But, with Amber Heard signing on with the Bad Ass Bunnies, it's almost a certainty she's going to be coming. Her one-sided destruction of Ciara tonight is undoubtedly a sign of things to come. Whether or not Olivia can change her fate before Amber catches her remains to be seen.

It was not a great night for Amanda Seyfried. A quick start by opponent Megan Fox had the Big Love actress reeling from the outset, and overprotective referee Freida Pinto sealed Amanda's fate only a minute later... declaring a cut above her eye too severe to allow her to continue.

Seyfried... who required a 2-inch band aid to close the near mortal wound... didn't dodge the hard questions afterward. "She caught me good," she said of Fox. "I was in a lot of trouble, and anyone who was watching knew it. But, I would have liked to have had the chance to fight my way out of it. I'm not going to stand here and say I was robbed, but I don't think I was finished, either."

The competition between these two women has all the makings of an epic rivalry. They've had some terrific battles in the past. Add to that the fact that they're good friends away from the cage, and you can see why many believe that something special could be brewing here. So, with Amanda's star on the rise and former-champion Megan recently signing on with the Bad Ass Bunnies stable, this fight was seen as a key test for both women. It was expected to be an exciting, tooth and nail battle. On that front, Amanda admits she didn't hold up her end.

"First of all, I'm disappointed I didn't win," Seyfried said. "I know how tough Megan is, and I know beating her isn't easy. But, I wouldn't have gotten in the cage if I didn't expect to win." She took a sip of water as she collected her thoughts. "On top of that... like everyone else, I expected this to be a terrific fight. I was looking forward to being a part of it. So, I'm extremely disappointed that I didn't perform better."

Disappointed as she might have been, Amanda also let herself get into the flow of this burgeoning rivalry a little. "She's famous for her submission skills," she said of her friend, "but I noticed she had a few new weapons in the bag tonight. Those Bunnies are obviously having a big impact." She paused as a playful smile spread across her face. "We'll have to see what I can do about that," she finished.

For me, I understand that every night can't be your best night. For whatever reason, Amanda came out a little flat tonight, and Megan certainly has the skills to take advantage of that. It happens. Amanda's already talking about next time, which is exactly what I want to hear. So, the biggest disappointment for me is that the UMMA safety guidelines will now force Amanda out of her next event. Thanks, Freida. Much appreciated.

Welcome back, Tiz! Though Ashley Tisdale returned from injury in time to help get The Aly Cats off on the right foot, her first true test came tonight, as she faced off against the similarly ranked Carrie Underwood. And she passed that test with flying colors. Carrie is a very tough opponent, but Ash wasn't going to be denied in this one. Though she wasn't as precise as she usually is, she made up for it with brute force and sheer determination. A slick takedown by Underwood looked like it might be trouble, but it amounted to nothing as Ashley tied her up and even countered with a little ground and pound action of her own.

Determination was still the order of the day at the post-fight press conference. "I just... I wanted this," Tisdale said. "The injury had me laid up for so long, I just needed to get back in there and show what I can do. I lost a lot of ground while I was out, and I just want to get back to where I was, both physically and mentally. Tonight was a great start."

That kind of heart is what's gotten Tiz where she is. If you look past the designer clothes and the purple highlights in her hair, you find a young woman who refuses to quit, no matter what the odds. It was that determination that kept her standing long after she should have fallen against Kristin Kreuk, and her reward was a pair of broken ribs. I hated to see that happen to her, and part of me wishes she'd been smarter about it. But that's just not her way, and I've finally come to accept that reality. Her ability to gut it out has won her quite a few fights the experts say she should have lost. I wouldn't dare try to take that away from her.

After a few bumps in the road along her comeback trail, Krystal Forscutt needed a big win to get things going again. Meanwhile, opponent Serinda Swan has been in free fall, dropping nine consecutive fights coming into tonight. Having opened her career with eleven straight losses, Krystal could certainly commiserate with her opponent. But, there is no mercy in the cage, and she made sure she kept her foot firmly pressed on her battered opponent's throat until the win was secured.

"I feel for her," Krystal said afterward. "She probably doesn't want to hear that, and I don't blame her. But, the fact it is, I know what she's going through. I know how hard it is. But, I'm past that now, and I don't have the opportunity to be sympathetic. I have to worry about what I need to do to keep moving up."

I hate to see a fighter take a beating in the cage, but, like Krystal, I have my own concerns to worry about. My focus is first and foremost on the women of RSI. So, I hope Serinda gets things turned around... as long as it doesn't come at the expense of one of my girls.

Hmmm... maybe that other shoe did finally drop for Jennifer Stano. After a meteoric rise that was ignited with back-to-back wins over the legendary Doutzen Kroes, tonight's loss means Jen has now dropped three in a row. On top of that, this is the second recent fight that Stano has lost via submission.

Ladies and gentlemen, I think we've found a weakness.

Fact is, tonight's fight made it pretty apparent that Jennifer has problems in the submission defense area. Not only did opponent Lindsay Ellingson eventually make Jen tap out, but it was also obvious that worrying about defending Lindsay's submission game had Stano tied up in knots. Constantly watching for the takedown took Jen completely out of her own strategy, resulting in nearly no offense from the still-developing fighter. You can't win if you're constantly playing your opponent's game, and no one knew it better than Stano.

"This one's on me," she said flatly. "We had a strategy. We've been working on it for weeks. I went out there tonight, and..." She stopped and shook her head disgustedly. "I don't know what the hell I was doing, but it wasn't what we planned."

There was one member of the RSI team that wasn't too surprised at how things went for Jen.

"Submission defense is a hard thing to master," said fighter development guru Stephanie McMahon. Steph has some very recent knowledge on the topic, having fallen to the rejuvenated and reinvented Kim Kardashian by submission only a few weeks ago. "No matter how good your defense is," she said, "it only takes one slip up for the fight to be over. It's hard to maintain the focus you need to pull that off while also executing your own game plan."

McMahon was confident that Stano will get through this, though.

"Jen's a hard worker, and she's blessed with a lot of talent. She just has to stay patient, and it'll come together for her."

Let's hope it comes together soon. I don't know how patient Jen can stay if she keeps losing like this.

Unfortunately, the story was almost exactly the same for Anna Kendrick, as she was forced to submit to Emmy Rossum. Just like Jen, Anna was totally focused on defending Emmy's submission game. To her credit, she did a great job of it... holding off Rossum well into the third round. But, even if she'd managed to survive to the end of the fight, Anna was still going to lose because she never really mounted any offense of her own. On top of that, her one track focus on Rossum's submission skills left her open to a number of striking attacks... an area in which Kendrick is usually so solid. All in all, she looked overmatched, which is an uncommon feeling for her.

But, if anyone can simply suck it up and deal, it's Anna.

"That must have been fun for the fans," she said afterward. "To watch Emmy slap me around the cage for most of the fight... I'm sure that had a lot of the men standing at attention."

I admit that even I thought the young actress might have been making a sexual reference with that statement. She was quick to clarify, though.

"Then, she made me cry like a little bitch," she continued. "I'm sure that helped the crowd release whatever pent up feelings they may have had."

Yep. She was definitely making a sexual reference.

Let it never be said that RSI is lacking for character. Not with Anna around.

The fantasies of her male (and maybe even some female) fans aside, Anna knew she didn't get the job done, tonight. She also knew that her life was about to get pretty miserable.

"You wanna bet Steph and Danni team up on me when we get back to the gym?" she asked.

Indeed, Anna finds herself in a particularly unenviable position. Not only will she have to deal with the demanding Steph McMahon, but she will also have Danneel Harris, RSI team leader and unofficial head of our welterweight group, to contend with.

What did Danni say when told of Anna's comment?

"I think she's right to be worried."

It's been swell knowing you, Anna.

What's a girl gotta do to get a decision around here, anyway? Once again faced with the torturous task of dealing with young submission artist Kat Dennings, Allira Cohrs did everything she needed to do. Her defense was solid as she kept Dennings at bay throughout the full three rounds, but, unlike Jennifer and Anna, Allira executed her own game plan to perfection.

Who landed the most strikes? Allira, by a wide margin.

Who won the clinch game? Easily Allira.

Who displayed the greater striking accuracy? Again, the answer is Cohrs.

Who did the judges declare to be the winner? Dennings.

Somebody help me understand.

True, the decision was about as close as you can get. But, that's not a consolation. It shouldn't have been close. Every aspect of the fight that should factor into a decision went Allira's way. The only pro for Dennings was that she controlled the action better on the ground. And what good did that do her? None. Allira stuffed everything Kat threw at her, even making her opponent sweat out a couple of submission attempts, just for good measure.

It won't help her in the win department, but, in my opinion, Allira won this fight. She did everything she needed to do to get the victory. She was tough. She was smart. And, as far as I'm concerned, she was robbed.

At long last... VICTORY! Despite a dedication and physical prowess that had people talking from the minute RSI signed her, Michelle Lewin has been finding nothing but frustration in the cage. She was solid in both of her first two fights, carrying both of them through to the cards. But, both times, she came up on the losing end of the decision. Both decisions were legitimate... Michelle did well, but each of her opponents were clearly better. You could almost see the frustration mounting on the world renowned fitness star. And tonight, she unloaded all of it right into Sara Mutch's face.

Michelle's approach tonight had little to do with technique and everything to do with raw ferocity. Losing wasn't sitting well with her, and had no interest in working her way through to a decision. She let loose from the opening bell, and Mutch just wasn't prepared to deal with it. No one will question Sara's toughness, as she withstood a helluva beating in the nearly three minutes the fight lasted. But, try as she might, she had no answer for Lewin.

When it was over, Michelle seemed happy, but overly so. For someone who had just scored her long-awaited first victory, her response was surprisingly muted. "I'm happy to get the win," she said. "I was tired of thinking about it, really." Despite her overwhelming success tonight, the fledgling fighter seemed determined to keep her eye on the big picture. "Tonight I proved that I can do it, but there's a long way to go until I'm happy. I need to be able to win consistently, and I need to be able to do it against increasingly difficult competition."

I like a fighter who's driven to succeed, but I think I'm going to ask Danni to take over Michelle's training. She's clearly absorbing too much of Steph McMahon's personality.

That's all for this event, but the young'uns get their shot next time as the focus falls on the FAC. Injuries have stalled the rivalry between Julianne Hough and Vikki Blows, but everyone is healthy and ready to go now. Something tells me it will be just as intense as it always was as Jules gets a shot at reclaiming the bantamweight title. Speaking of rivalries, it's been a while since Aly Michalka and India Reynolds have faced each other, but they'll soon get reacquainted with the hard way. And Xenia Deli will get another chance to topple featherweight champion Emma Watson. Emma made it look easy the last time, which only means Xenia will be hungrier this time around. Emma knows her opponent has nothing to lose and everything to gain, so you can bet she'll be ready for anything. Join us then, and see how it all works out.
