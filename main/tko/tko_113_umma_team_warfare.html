When the Going Gets Tough

Both RSI teams had a tough time of it here in Edmonton. Britney's team saw two of its most dependable members dealt one-sided losses, including the one suffered by the team leader, herself. Elisha's team, meanwhile, mostly spun it's wheels as a trio of experienced fighters came out on the losing end. Though both teams managed to increase their overall point total, neither member of the gruesome twosome was in a particularly good mood. So, I decided they would have a special guest (or two) for this edition of the pre-recorded team rundown.

(as Britney and Elisha enter the room)

Richelle: Hello ladies.

Britney: Richelle? What are you doing here?

Elisha: See? I told you she was gonna be pissed.

Richelle: Guys...

Britney: Why? My team didn't screw up.

Richelle: Guys...

Elisha: What the fuck is that supposed to mean? At least I didn't...

Richelle: GUYS!! I'm not pissed. Now, shut up and SIT DOWN!!

Elisha: Really? Because you look pretty pissed.

Britney: Yeah. I don't think I've ever seen you that color before.

Richelle: Well, I wasn't pissed before you two idiots got here.

Britney: We do have that effect on you.

Richelle: (Looks from Britney to Elisha, then points at the empty chairs on the opposite side of the table) Sit.

(Britney and Elisha take their seats)

Richelle: I'm not mad at anyone. You guys had a tough event, and I just thought I'd help you get a little perspective.

Elisha: The perspective is this sucks.

Richelle: The perspective is that both of you have done a fantastic job getting your teams to this point. But, both teams are moving up the rankings, and that means things are going to keep getting harder. That doesn't mean anyone has failed.

Britney: It feels like failure, to me.

Richelle: I know. But the truth is that, even in this event, you both succeeded. Elisha, this was easily the worst performance your team has had so far...

Elisha: Gee... thanks.

Richelle: ...but you guys still came out on top. You won five out of eight, and still managed to increase your overall point total. On a night when you guys couldn't seem to get much momentum going, you still held your own. I've seen the updated standings. Tonight didn't hurt you much.

Elisha: Really? We held our ranking?

Richelle: You slipped one spot.

Elisha: Who jumped over us?

Richelle: (points over at Britney)

Elisha: Oh... shit.

Britney: (shaking her head) Don't worry about taking shit from me. I didn't have anything to do with it.

Richelle: Sure you did, but we'll get back to that. Let's breakdown the performances, shall we? And, to help us do that, I thought we should get the cold hard facts from someone who knows.

(Gestures toward the door as Stephanie McMahon walks through)

Britney and Elisha: (in unison) Steph!

Stephanie: (smiling) Hello, little blonde peoples.

Elisha: Cute, Steph.

Britney: Yeah... you're hilarious.

Richelle: I dunno. I kinda liked it.

Britney: Of course you did.

Richelle: OK, Steph... let's get right to it. Walk us through the match-ups for Elisha's team tonight.

Stephanie: Well, first up was Danica... and I have to admit that was a little bit ugly.

Richelle: I agree. And it's not the first time for her, either.

Stephanie: No, it isn't. And, she was decent, tonight. But, she just can't seem to put it all together.

Elisha: Danica lays it all out there, every time. You can't ask for more than that.

Stephanie: Actually, I can. Danica has the tools to be much better than what she's been showing. I love her heart as much as anyone, but she needs to get her technique straightened out. She can't keep going like this.

Richelle: Again, I agree. And I think we may need to change things up a bit with her.

Elisha: You're not thinking of benching her, are you?

Richelle: (shrugs) I'm not sure yet. But, we've given her chances to fight her way out of it, and it's not working. Maybe a step back is exactly what she needs.

Elisha: Do I get a say in this?

Richelle: Of course. But now's not the time. Let's move on.

Elisha: That would be Kelly.

Richelle: Steph?

Stephanie: She was good. Her defense was excellent, and she was patient and waited for her chances. And when she got them, she made them count. Truth is, she was supposed to win this fight, and she did.

Richelle: Kelly's been solid, lately. She's definitely making a case for herself.

Elisha: That brings us to Roselyn.

Stephanie: Like Danica, that one wasn't pretty. Roselyn should have performed much better, but you have to give some of the credit to her opponent, Victoria Justice. She came out strong and caught Roselyn flat-footed. And that was really all there was to it. Roselyn hung tough for a while, but the truth is this one was decided in those first thirty seconds.

Elisha: That one really hurt. Roselyn has been so constant for us.

Richelle: (nods) That's why I'm really not worried about it. She had a bad night. It happens.

Stephanie: Next up, I'll do Amanda and Daniela together, because it was the same story for both of them. Both were in a situation where they absolutely should have won, and they did. And we all know that's not as easy as it sounds.

Britney: Big expectations.

Elisha: And a big blow to the team if you lose.

Richelle: And little reward if you win. It's a pretty thankless job, but they both handled it. Just like they usually do.

Stephanie: Next was Petra, who's performance was....

Richelle: ...scintillatingly abbreviated.

Britney: Oh... my God! That was some move!

Stephanie: It was a little showy, for my taste.

Elisha: C'mon, Steph... nobody saw that spinning backfist coming. I don't even think Katy Perry knew what hit her!

Stephanie: Fine. Showy, but effective.

Richelle: And so, that brings us to Torrie Wilson. And, there's really nothing to discuss. When Torrie came to RSI, she was on the verge of just giving up. Steph here... she felt like Torrie had more in her, and she was right. Torrie has really turned things around... to the point where she's been just about unstoppable. Tonight, Kim Kardashian snuck a punch in there that landed right on the button. I'm sure Torrie's a little embarrassed by it, but it happens.

Britney: Yeah... tell me about it.

Stephanie: Don't start that whining shit.

Britney: I'm not whining. I fucked up. I'll own it.

Stephanie: From whiner to martyr. That was fast.

Elisha: Hey! Take it easy, Steph!

Richelle: OK, I guess we're gonna do this now. Fine, then. Yes, Britney... you're situation is exactly the same. Hilary Swank, who isn't exactly a rookie by the way, caught you with a shot that ended your night before it even got started.

Britney: And that cost my team big. I'm supposed to be the leader, and I let them down.

Stephanie: Enough! Alright? Just... enough. Brit, you know damn well I'd tell you if you fucked up. But, that's not how it went. Hilary took her shot, and it landed perfectly. Nine times out of ten, you take that punch and come back looking for more. Tonight, she caught you just right. Shit happens. Sometimes, you just have to take it and move on.

Britney: But...

Stephanie: But nothing. You didn't let us down. No one is disappointed in you. You're the one who got us where we are. You're the team lead, and your girls are behind you. So just shake it off, and let's go.

Britney: (laughs) Well, shit Steph... when you put it like that...

Richelle: There's no other way to put it, Britney. Let it go and move on. Speaking of which, let's finish up Elisha's team by selecting our Fighter of the Night.

Elisha: I think I'd have to go with Kelly...

Richelle: And that's why I'm not letting you pick, this time.

Elisha: Wait... what?

Richelle: Well, you're obviously gonna get it wrong, so...

Elisha: What do you mean? Kelly was good.

Richelle: She sure was. Amanda, Daniela, Petra... all fantastic. But, there was only one fighter who went toe-to-toe with a former champion, and came away with the victory. Only one who owned the fight... stand-up, groundwork, clinch... it didn't matter. Only one who scored with over seventy percent of her strikes. Only one who...

Elisha: (blushing) Jesus... would you STOP?

Richelle: (laughing) OK, OK... fine. Britney... tell her who her fighter of the night was.

Britney: (throwing an arm around her friend) Without a doubt, it was Elisha Cuthbert!

Elisha: You guys are too much.

Stephanie: No, you had it all going on tonight, Leesh.

Elisha: Thanks, Steph. It always feels good when the game plan works, right?

Stephanie: Yeah. And it's rare, too. So, just enjoy it.

Elisha: (smiling) I am.

Richelle: OK, so that brings us to Britney's team. We've already covered the unlucky fate of the captain... shall we finish up the bad news?

Britney: Oh, yeah... Aly.

Elisha: I really can't believe this happened to her again.

Richelle: Steph, what did you see?

Stephanie: I saw Aly going head-to-head with yet another top-notch opponent. Heidi Klum is the champion for a reason, and she rattled Aly early. Aly battled like the warrior she always is, but she just never recovered.

Britney: (shaking her head) I felt awful. Aly was spent by the end of the first round. But, she just never gives up. Heidi did the smart thing and played it safe, and it turned into a couple of rounds of torture.

Stephanie: I don't know what to say. The kid just never catches a break.

Richelle: You don't have to say anything. Aly doesn't have anything to answer for or make excuses about. She trains hard, she comes ready and she fights like there's no tomorrow. I've made no secret of the fact that she never gets an easy fight. But Aly seems to love it. I'm sure she'd disappointed, right now. But, she's a lot like you two clowns... she puts it all out there, win or lose.

Elisha: Clowns?

Britney: Yeah... how come you can't just say something nice about us, without throwing in the insults?

Richelle: I like to try and keep things in balance. Call it ego management.

Elisha: (thinks for a moment) Yeah, OK... I can see that.

Britney: (nods) That does seem like a good idea.

Richelle: Well, at least you guys are self-aware.

Stephanie: Can we just move along?

Richelle: Sure. What other notes do you have for us?

Stephanie: The rest of Britney's team really looked fantastic. Danni, Emily, Tiffany... the odds were heavily in their favor, even though they were all facing dangerous opponents. But, they all took care of business. Julianne and Catherine had tougher match-ups on paper, but they were both exceptional. To be honest, the team was really solid, overall.

Richelle: Gee, Steph... I feel like you left somebody out.

Stephanie: (closes her eyes and sighs) Please don't do this to me.

Richelle: Oh, but I must. Britney, didn't your team have a fighter coming back off a bad injury?

Britney: (smiling) It just so happens that we did.

Richelle: If I remember right, that fighter had been struggling before. In fact, she was just starting to turn things around when the injury happened. People thought that might be the last straw, for her.

Britney: That's what I recall.

Richelle: I'm sure she had a lot of doubters, especially since that fighter came up against the reigning SCQ super heavyweight champion in this event... a rival she has struggled badly against recently.

Britney: Yep. The experts weren't giving her much of a chance.

Richelle: (looks over at Steph while still talking to Britney) And how did that all turn out?

Britney: She. Kicked. Ass.

Elisha: Hell yeah, she did!

Stephanie: Oh, c'mon...

Richelle: (still looking at Steph) Britney... who was your Fighter of the Night?

Britney: Stephanie Fucking McMahon.

Stephanie: You guys are killing me.

Richelle: You can play it off if you want, Steph, but you rocked it tonight. Not only did you overcome Veronika Zemanova in a dominating win, but you backstopped your team. You were the reason the team jumped as far as it did.

Elisha: (rolling her eyes) Yeah.. thanks for that, Steph.

Britney: (nodding) Aly and I struggled, and you stepped up and made the difference.

Richelle: Which is exactly what the team competition is all about. Somebody falters... the others pick up the slack. Your team needed you tonight, and you were there for them.

Stephanie: I was just happy to get the win.

Richelle: So, let's hear it... how does it feel?

Stephanie: I can't lie... it feels pretty awesome. I haven't had a big win in such a long time. This really feels good.

Richelle: So, maybe you'll put off that retirement for a while?

Stephanie: Retirement, my ass.

Richelle: That's what I thought. Well, that about does it for this event. Next up is the lingerie tournament, and event that was an enormous success for RSI last year. Danneel Harris, Julianne Hough and Alyson Michalka will all be returning to defend their titles, along with seventeen other fighters looking to bring home some hardware. We'll see you then.

Elisha: (to Britney) Now what was that crack about my team screwing up?

Britney: I didn't say that. I said my team DIDN'T screw up.

Elisha: Yeah, but that makes it sound like we did.

Britney: All I know is that we moved up, and you guys moved down.

Elisha: You moved up because Steph saved your ass.

Britney: It's a team competition, Leesh. There is no "I" in team.

Elisha: Especially for you. Because you lost.

Britney: Fuck off, you little bitch.

Richelle: (to Stephanie) Explain to me again why this is working.

Stephanie: (shrugs) Some things just defy explanation.
