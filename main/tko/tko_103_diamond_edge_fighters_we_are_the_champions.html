TKO Issue: #103
Date: March 2014
Franchise: Diamond Edge Fighters
Event: We Are The Champions
Location: Colisee Pepsi (Quebec City, Quebec, Canada)

Rivalry Renewed

It seemed destined to happen. With 16 victories in a row, including 2 dominating wins over the current divisional champion, it felt like this one was over before it started. The Britney train would roll through, and the Queen would be back on top. Britney Spears would be crowned as champion of the DEF middleweight division, the final scene in what has been hottest stretch of her long UMMA career. But, this isn't a scripted story. The reality is that the DEF already has a middleweight champion, and Malin Akerman reminded everyone tonight of why that belt is wrapped around her waist. She stopped Brit cold and left no doubt that she's not going down without one helluva fight.

Malin's gameplan for this bout was nothing short of perfect. She was smart and she was quick... chipping away at Britney while staying clear of the singer's fearsome power. When Akerman recently signed on with Hellfire, she said she needed to get more focused. Well, it's clear now that she aimed that focus with laser-like precision on this matchup with Britney. Malin was prepared for everything Brit tried, and she was in control throughout the fight. It pains me to say it, but Malin was clearly the better fighter, tonight. And, I wasn't the only one who thought so.

"I had a feeling this was coming," said Britney afterward. "I've had it pretty easy in my last two fights with Malin. That's not like her, and I was pretty sure there was a time coming when she was going to step up and fight back." Then, with a wry smile, she added, "I just wish it hadn't been tonight." Though Spears was not at all pleased with how this one turned out, she was also far from defeatist. "I'm disappointed in how I performed tonight, but I think this was more about Malin than it was about me," she said. "She stepped up her game tonight," she added, a note of respect in her voice. "She let me know she's still the champion. And, she's right. She deserves that title until I can prove otherwise." Then, the smile disappeared and her voice hardened as she added, "But, trust me... I plan to prove otherwise."

Clearly, Britney was taking the loss much better than I was. And, I wasn't the only one having a hard time with it. "God damn that Malin," seethed Elisha Cuthbert. "She's been getting slapped around left and right, so I don't know how she won this fight. She just got lucky, that's all. She doesn't deserve this championship. I don't know how she pulled this off, but the wrong person came out of that cage with the belt." Uhm... uh-oh.

When told of her friend's obviously controversial comments, Britney failed to see any controversy at all. "Leesh said that?" Spears asked with a laugh. "Jesus... she's an emotional nut, isn't she?" After a moment, Britney offered an explanation for Cuthbert's little tirade. "Elisha's very emotional, and she was just upset. I guarantee she's going to apologize for that comment," said Brit. "I know for a fact that she has as much respect for Malin as I do. She's the champion. Period." Then, Spears let her competitive side show itself once again. "I'm excited that Malin's back on her game. I'm looking forward to battling her for this championship. I love the thrill of fighting a tough opponent, and there's no one tougher than Malin in this division." That one should make for a fun conversation during tomorrow's training session, I though to myself. But, Britney wasn't done.

"Elisha shouldn't have said what she said. I'm sure she already regrets it. But, make no mistake: I love that she said it," said the decorated musical artist. "I know in my heart I'm eventually going to win this championship, and Elisha's the reason why," she added emphatically. "Because there is nothing... absolutely nothing... that gives you the kind of confidence that comes from having someone like Elisha backing you up." Britney's eyes misted over as she went on. "She may be a nut, but she's MY nut. When the shit hits the fan, I know she's going to be there. There's no substitute for that." She paused a moment to collect herself, then added with a smile, "Of course, I only say shit like this when she's not in the room. So, that should tell you something, too."

Meanwhile, Danni Harris bounced back nicely after an uncharacteristically early exit from the recent Hit the Beach tournament. With Kristanna Loken unable to make her scheduled titled defense against Danni, Harris was left to face off against the ever dangerous Jennifer Lamiraqui.

This one was a trap fight if ever I saw one. Though the ranking difference between she and Danni is wide, Jennifer is no rookie. She's capable of giving Danni a tough fight, even when Harris is at her best. With no belt on the line, I worried Danni would ease up and make things too easy for Lamiraqui. But, as usual, my worries were for naught. Danni was as crisp as always, and she took care of business in her trademark, no nonsense style.

Afterward, Danni only had one thing on her mind. "I want that shot at Kristanna," she said, a glint of excitement in her eyes. "It's been a long time since I've had this kind of competition in my own division. Kristanna has clawed her way up, literally. In our first title match, she had nothing. She lasted only a couple of minutes, and I basically embarrassed her," continued RSI's team leader. "But, she kept coming, again and again. She got better each time, and, now, she has the belt. So, I have a lot of respect for what she did and how she did it. I can't wait to match up with her again. I think it's going to be quite a show."

Now, Danni is always deferential to her opponents, even those that are clearly not on her level. But, I have to be honest... I've never heard her talk about an opponent the way she talks about Kristanna. It takes a lot to earn that kind of respect from someone as experienced and accomplished as Danni, but Kristanna has clearly shown her a little something extra in that cage.

It seems the ever-evolving Danica Patrick is just full of surprises, isn't she? Though still struggling to establish herself as championship material, Danica has certainly shown she's capable of big things. With Patrick's ferocious punching power well known, opponent Rachael Leigh Cook sought to take things to the ground. Danica had her way early, but Rachael eventually got the takedown she was looking for. I wondered how the NASCAR driver would respond, and I was stunned when she immediately scored the reversal. She then seemingly took over the fight, grinding it out with Cook to improve her position until she had the actress helplessly pinned. With her opponent at her mercy, Danica unloaded with a brutal ground'n'pound session... hammering away at Rachael's body and face. It wasn't long before referee Natasha Yi was forced to step in and put a stop to things.

An impressive win for Danica, but, much like Danni, her mind was already on her next likely matchup afterward. "I have to find a way to deal with Tila Tequila," lamented Patrick. "Her style has proven very tough for me to handle. She's gotten the better of me too many times now, and it's time for that to change." No question, Tila's go-for-broke submission style has made Danica's life miserable on a couple of occasions. Can she continue to improve her ground game to handle it? Or, can she find another way? It's certainly clear she needs to try something different, because her current strategy just isn't working.

Speaking of nemesis-s... Err, nemeses? Nemisi? Christ, I don't know. Speaking of people who make your life difficult, Petra Cubonova has been heading down a bad road with Denise Milani. Prior to tonight, she had faced off against Milani twice, losing both times. But the RSI heavyweight got things turned around in this one. Utilizing a capable striking game and a ground game that far exceeds what most of the RSI fighters possess, Petra was running the show the whole time. Cubonova looked very relaxed and prepared in this one, but she also seemed pretty relieved to get the win.

"I am happy to win," said the Czechian beauty. "I have trouble with Denise in the past. To win, is... how you say? Monkey off my butt?" Uh, well... close enough. While the words may not be perfect, the thought is dead on. Healthy competition with another fighter can be good for both the athletes and the fans. But, if you're constantly coming out on the losing end, it becomes a big distraction. Denise still has the edge in this one, but Petra proved she's up to the challenge. So, the monkey is off her butt, her back and wherever else he was hanging around.

Carrying the first losing streak of her young career, Holly Bricken stepped into the cage tonight looking to get back on track against Jessica Simpson. Unfortunately, another brutal scratch caused yet another injury stoppage, ending things all too quickly for Jessica. Though Holly seemed calm and focused as things got started, the bout was just too short to really get a feel for how her game is developing. Still, a win is a win.

We wrap up the report for this event with yet another fine win from rookie Cobie Smulders, as she used her superb striking game to pick apart the far more experienced Sophia Bush. Though Sophia has struggled at times, she's shown herself to be a capable fighter, picking up wins over Vanessa Hudgens, Tiffany Mulheron and even Cheryl Cole. She figured to be a tough matchup for Cobie, but Smulders was having none of it. Making full use of her height advantage, Cobie scored at will with vicious punches, overwhelming Bush in only a minute. Though she still has much to learn, Cobie has definitely established herself as one to keep an eye on.

I feel compelled to give a follow-up on the Stephanie McMahon situation, though I'm really not sure what to say. Steph reported to the training facility the next day, as if nothing had happened. Just walked past my office, bag in hand, and said not a word. I walked to the office door and looked after her, but she just continued on into the locker room without even looking back.

Well, how do you like that? Without so much as a "kiss my foot" or "have an apple."

Seeking to avoid doing something stupid, I decided to check-in with Danni before saying anything to Steph. But I got no help there, either.

"She's here, isn't she?" was the chippy response I got when I queried her during our regular morning meeting.

What the hell?

As she gathered her things to leave, I pressed on.

"Hey... did I do something?"

"No," she said with an exasperated sigh. "It's just got me pissed, that's all."

As she went to leave, she paused in the office doorway. "We failed her, you know?"

The line more a statement than a question... her voice so flat and cold, I struggled to figure out how to respond. It mattered little because, next thing I knew, she was gone.

What the fuck is going on around here, anyway?

At any rate, we're back to the status quo, at least for now. Danni took the letter Steph had given me before I had the chance to open it, and I haven't seen it since. Since Steph is back with us, I could care less what happened to it. I just hope somebody fills me in sometime soon. I am, as I have always been, extremely proud to count Stephanie as a member of the RSI family. If we're not getting the job done for her, I will do whatever it takes to make it right.

Some big fights coming up for RSI at the next event, as the women of the ESL take the stage. Tiffany Mulheron will step in to defend her featherweight crown against it's previous owner, Nadine Coyle. If these two go at it like they did last time, To The Death might be more than just the name of the event. Also on tap, yet another meeting between Irina Sheik and Emily VanCamp. This will be Em's 6th matchup with Irina since joining RSI. Is it getting old? Hell, no! Though these matches are usually unpredictable, they are always hugely entertaining. And that's why you watch, right? Speaking of reasons to watch, rookie Jennifer Stano is going to get another shot at UMMA legend Doutzen Kroes. Stano shocked the world when she took Kroes down at the Hit the Beach tournament. Can she do it again? I can't lie, it'll be very tough for her to pull it off, especially since Doutzen is likely to be pretty pissed about that last upset. But, geez... what if she does it? I'll need a whole stack of dictionaries and thesauri just to come up with words. We'll see you then.
