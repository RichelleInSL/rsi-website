This is a story I co-wrote with the manager of a competing UMMA stable. Early on, it's pretty obvious we didn't know exactly what we were doing. But we figured it out as we went, and I think the final product is something we could both be proud of. Oh, and we had a ton of fun putting it together, too.

Anyway... after another tough event for the Dragon's Den stable, stable leader Sophia Bush is left searching for ways she can get her group on the right track. She gets the idea to seek advice from an old friend who happens to have just the kind of experience she needs.

The problem? Well, it seems the friend she has in mind is very busy leading a competing stable.
